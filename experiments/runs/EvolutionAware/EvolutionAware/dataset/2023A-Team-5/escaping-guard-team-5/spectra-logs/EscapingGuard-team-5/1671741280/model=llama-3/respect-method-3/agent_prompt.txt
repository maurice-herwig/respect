The system, EscapingGuard, interacts with its environment to control the movement of a robot within a defined area. The environment does not have direct control over the robot's position, as the variables `robotX` and `robotY`, which represent the robot's coordinates, are controlled by the system.

Initially, the robot is positioned at coordinates (D, 0), where D is a constant defined as 7. The system ensures that the robot moves in a way that its x and y coordinates change by at most 1 unit at a time.

The system guarantees that the robot will infinitely often visit three specific locations: T1 at coordinates (0, 0), T2 at coordinates (D, D), and T3 at coordinates (0, D). This means that the robot's movement pattern must include regular visits to these points.

There are no specified assumptions about the environment's behavior, implying that the system must be able to operate correctly under any environmental conditions. The system's guarantees focus solely on its ability to control the robot's movement and ensure it visits the specified locations infinitely often.
