The system interacts with an environment that controls the intruder's position, specified by `intruderX` and `intruderY`, while the system controls the positions of two robots, with initial positions `robotX0` and `robotY0` for the first robot, and `robotX1` and `robotY1` for the second robot.

The system guarantees that whenever either robot is at a Y-position of 1, its X-position will be either less than 2 or greater than 5, for both robots. 

In other words, if a robot's Y-position is 1, the system ensures the robot's X-position is restricted to the ranges less than 2 or greater than 5, mirroring the environmental assumption about the intruder's movement.
