The system consists of a robot with two positions, (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1), operating within a grid of dimensions DX by DY, where DX and DY are defined as 5 units. The environment controls an elevator, which moves vertically along the Y-axis. The elevator's position is restricted to change by at most 1 unit at a time.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that the elevator occasionally returns to the bottom (position 0) and also occasionally reaches the top (position DY).

The system guarantees that whenever the robot's X-position is 0, its Y-position will match the elevator's position, for both robot positions (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1). This means that when either robot is at the leftmost position, its vertical position will always be the same as the elevator's current position.
