The Patrolling system is a reactive system that controls the position of a robot within a defined area. The system has two outputs: the x-coordinate of the robot's position, referred to as robotX, and the y-coordinate of the robot's position, referred to as robotY. Both coordinates are restricted to a grid with dimensions 0 to DX (4 units) for the x-axis and 0 to DY (4 units) for the y-axis.

There are no specified environment-controlled inputs, assumptions about the environment, or guarantees required from the system. The initial positions of the robot are not defined. No safety, liveness, or next-state requirements are specified for the system. The system's behavior is currently undefined.
