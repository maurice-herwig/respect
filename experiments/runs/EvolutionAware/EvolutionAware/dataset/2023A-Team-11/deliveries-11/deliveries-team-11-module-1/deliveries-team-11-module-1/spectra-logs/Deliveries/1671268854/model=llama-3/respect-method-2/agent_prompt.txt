The system, Deliveries, involves a robot's movement within a defined grid. The grid has dimensions DX by DY, where DX is 5 and DY is 5. The robot's position is represented by two sets of coordinates: the initial position (robotX0, robotY0) and the next position (robotX1, robotY1). The x-coordinates (robotX0 and robotX1) are restricted to values between 0 and DX (inclusive), and the y-coordinates (robotY0 and robotY1) are restricted to values between 0 and DY (inclusive). The system controls these robot position variables. 

Note that the environment-controlled inputs are not specified in this part of the specification. The assumptions about the environment's behavior and the guarantees required from the system are also not defined here. The initial conditions, safety requirements, liveness requirements, and next-state/update rules are not provided in this portion of the specification.
