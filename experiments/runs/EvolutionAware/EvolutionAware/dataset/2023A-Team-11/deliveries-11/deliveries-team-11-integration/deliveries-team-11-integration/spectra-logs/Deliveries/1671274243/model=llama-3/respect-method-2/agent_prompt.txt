The system, called Deliveries, involves a robot's movement within a defined grid. The grid has a size of 5 units in both the x and y dimensions. The system controls the robot's positions, with two sets of coordinates: an initial position (robotX0, robotY0) and a subsequent position (robotX1, robotY1). The x-coordinates (robotX0 and robotX1) are restricted to values between 0 and 5, and the y-coordinates (robotY0 and robotY1) are also restricted to values between 0 and 5. 

The environment does not have any explicitly defined inputs in this part of the specification. 

There are no assumptions about the environment's behavior or guarantees about the system's behavior defined in this part of the specification. 

The initial conditions for the system are that the robot's initial x and y positions (robotX0 and robotY0) and subsequent x and y positions (robotX1 and robotY1) are defined within the specified grid boundaries. 

No safety requirements, liveness requirements, or next-state/update rules are defined in this part of the specification. 

The system-controlled outputs are the robot's positions, specifically the initial and subsequent x and y coordinates.
