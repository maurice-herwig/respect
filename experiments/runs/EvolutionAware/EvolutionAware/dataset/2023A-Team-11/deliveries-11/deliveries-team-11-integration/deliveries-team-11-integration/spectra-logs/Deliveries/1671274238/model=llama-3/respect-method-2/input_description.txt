The system, Deliveries, involves a robot's movement within a defined grid. The grid has dimensions DX by DY, where DX is 5 and DY is 5. The robot's position is represented by two sets of coordinates: the initial position (robotX0, robotY0) and the subsequent position (robotX1, robotY1). The system controls these coordinates, which are restricted to within the grid boundaries, ranging from 0 to DX for the x-coordinate and 0 to DY for the y-coordinate. 

Note that the specification does not provide details on the environment's behavior, assumptions about the environment, or guarantees required from the system beyond the declaration of the robot's position variables. The initial conditions and any potential updates to these positions are also not defined in this part of the specification.
