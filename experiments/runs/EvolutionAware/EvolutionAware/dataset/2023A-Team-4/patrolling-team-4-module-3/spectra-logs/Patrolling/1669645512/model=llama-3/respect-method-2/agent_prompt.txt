The Patrolling system controls a robot's position, defined by its x and y coordinates, within a grid of size 4x4. The environment does not have direct control over the robot's position. The system must ensure the robot visits the charging place, located at position (4,0), under certain conditions.

The environment is assumed to allow the robot to function correctly for at least 15 time steps when it is not at the charging place. 

The system guarantees that if the robot reaches the charging place, it will stay there for at least 3 time steps. Additionally, whenever the robot is not at the charging place for 15 consecutive time steps, the system ensures that the robot will eventually reach the charging place. 

The robot's initial position is not specified, and there are no constraints on its movement other than those related to visiting the charging place. The system is responsible for updating the robot's position, but the specifics of its movement between time steps are not defined.
