The Patrolling system controls a robot's position, defined by its x and y coordinates (robotX and robotY), within a grid of size 4x4. The environment does not directly control any variables in this specification. 

The system is expected to operate under the assumption that it can reliably move the robot for at least 15 time steps without failing to reach the charging place when not already there.

The system must guarantee that whenever it reaches the charging place (located at coordinates (4,0)), it will remain there for at least 3 time steps before potentially leaving. Additionally, if the robot is not at the charging place, the system must ensure that it will reach the charging place within 15 time steps. 

There are no explicitly defined initial conditions or next-state relationships for the robot's position in this specification. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of its ability to navigate to and remain at the charging place under the given assumptions.
