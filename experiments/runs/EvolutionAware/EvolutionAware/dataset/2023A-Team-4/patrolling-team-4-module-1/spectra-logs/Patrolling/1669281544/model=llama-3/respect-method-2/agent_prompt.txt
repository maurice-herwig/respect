The Patrolling system is a reactive system that controls the position of a robot within a grid. The grid has a size of 4x4, with x-coordinates ranging from 0 to 4 (DX) and y-coordinates ranging from 0 to 4 (DY). The system controls two outputs: the x-coordinate of the robot's position (robotX) and the y-coordinate of the robot's position (robotY), both of which are restricted to the grid's dimensions. There are no specified environment-controlled inputs, assumptions about the environment, or guarantees required from the system. The initial positions of the robot are not defined.
