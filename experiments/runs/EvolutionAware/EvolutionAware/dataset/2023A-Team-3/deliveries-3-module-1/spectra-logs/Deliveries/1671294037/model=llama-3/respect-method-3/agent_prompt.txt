The system is an elevator that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the elevator's vertical position, represented by `elevatorY`, which ranges from 0 to 5. The system controls the positions of a robot at two different times, represented by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, with `robotX0` and `robotX1` ranging from 0 to 5 and `robotY0` and `robotY1` ranging from 0 to 5.

The environment is assumed to move the elevator vertically in a way that its position either increases by 1, decreases by 1, or remains the same at each step. Additionally, the environment is assumed to visit the ground floor (`elevatorY = 0`) and the top floor (`elevatorY = 5`) infinitely many times. 

The specification does not impose any specific requirements on the system's behavior in terms of guarantees, initial conditions, safety requirements, liveness requirements, or next-state/update rules. It only defines the system's and environment's variables and the assumptions on the environment's behavior.
