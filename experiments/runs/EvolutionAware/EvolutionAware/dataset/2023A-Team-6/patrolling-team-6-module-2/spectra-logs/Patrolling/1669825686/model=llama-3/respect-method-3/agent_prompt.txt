The Patrolling system controls a robot's position in a two-dimensional space with dimensions up to 4 units in both the x and y directions. The system has control over the robot's x and y coordinates, denoted as robotX and robotY, respectively. 

The environment's behavior is not explicitly restricted in this specification. 

The system guarantees that the robot's x-coordinate, robotX, will always be 1. 

There are no specified initial conditions, liveness requirements, or next-state relationships beyond this safety requirement. The robot's y-coordinate, robotY, and the environment's behavior are not subject to any defined constraints or requirements in this partial specification.
