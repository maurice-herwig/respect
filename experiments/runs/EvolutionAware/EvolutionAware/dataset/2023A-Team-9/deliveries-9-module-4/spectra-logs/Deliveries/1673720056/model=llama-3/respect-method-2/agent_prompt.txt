The system, Deliveries, interacts with its environment and maintains several variables to track its state. The environment-controlled inputs are not explicitly defined in this specification, while the system-controlled outputs include the robot's coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1) and two boolean indicators (elevatorAndGround0, elevatorAndGround1).

The system assumes that the environment will behave in a way that is not explicitly constrained by this specification.

The system guarantees that initially, both elevatorAndGround0 and elevatorAndGround1 are set to false, indicating that the elevator and ground are not connected at the start. 

The system operates within a defined grid, where the x-coordinate ranges from 0 to DX (5) and the y-coordinate ranges from 0 to DY (5), and the robot's coordinates are constrained within these bounds. However, the initial values of the robot's coordinates are not specified.

In this initial state, the system does not impose any additional safety or liveness requirements beyond the initial conditions of the elevatorAndGround variables. The next-state relationships and any updates to the robot's coordinates or other system variables are also not defined in this specification.
