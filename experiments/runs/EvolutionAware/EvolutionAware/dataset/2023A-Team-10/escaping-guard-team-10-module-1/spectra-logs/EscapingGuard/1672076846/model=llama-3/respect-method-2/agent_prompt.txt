The system, referred to as EscapingGuard, interacts with its environment in a two-dimensional space with a maximum dimension size of 7 units (D = 7). 

The environment controls two types of inputs: 
- Obstacles, which are arrays of 4 pairs of dimensional coordinates, and 
- targets, which are arrays of 3 pairs of dimensional coordinates.

The system controls a single output: 
- the position of a robot, represented by two dimensional coordinates.

No specific assumptions are made about the environment's behavior, nor are any guarantees required from the system defined in this part of the specification. 

Initially, the robot's position and the locations of obstacles and targets are not specified. 

No safety, liveness, or next-state requirements are defined for the system or environment in this part of the specification.
