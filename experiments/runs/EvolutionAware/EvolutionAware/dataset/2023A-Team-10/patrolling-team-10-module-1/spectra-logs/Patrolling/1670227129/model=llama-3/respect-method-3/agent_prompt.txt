The Patrolling system operates within a defined area of dimensions 4x4, where the robot's position is represented by its x and y coordinates, `robotX` and `robotY`, respectively. The environment provides information about three goals, each with a pair of coordinates, `goals`, and a boolean array `isGoalBlocked` indicating whether each goal is blocked.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that each goal can be either blocked or not blocked at any point in time. Specifically, the environment will infinitely often have the first goal blocked and not blocked, the second goal blocked and not blocked, and the third goal blocked and not blocked.

The system controls the robot's position, `robotX` and `robotY`, within the defined area. However, the specification does not provide any guarantees about the system's behavior in response to the environment's inputs. 

Note that `robotX` and `robotY` are the system-controlled outputs, while `isGoalBlocked` and `goals` are the environment-controlled inputs. The initial conditions and next-state relationships for the robot's position are not specified in this partial specification.
