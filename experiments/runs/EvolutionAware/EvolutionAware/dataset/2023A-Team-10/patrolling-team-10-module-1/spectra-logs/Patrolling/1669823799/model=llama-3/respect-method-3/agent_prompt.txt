The Patrolling system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the x and y coordinates of three goals, represented by `goalsX` and `goalsY`. The system controls the x and y coordinates of a robot, represented by `robotX` and `robotY`, as well as a boolean array `isGoalBlocked` that indicates whether each goal is blocked.

The environment is assumed to provide valid goal coordinates within the specified ranges. 

The system guarantees that the first goal is never blocked, as indicated by `isGoalBlocked[0]` always being false. For the second and third goals, the system guarantees that each goal is sometimes blocked and sometimes not blocked, meaning that the blocked status of these goals will change infinitely often. 

The system does not impose any specific initial conditions or next-state relationships on the robot's position or the goals' coordinates beyond the guarantees related to the blocked status of the goals.
