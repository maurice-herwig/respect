The system is designed to interact with an environment where an intruder is present. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by `intruderX` and `intruderY` coordinates, which range from 0 to 7. The system, on the other hand, controls its own positions, `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, as well as a counter `turns` that tracks the number of turns.

Initially, the system ensures that the `turns` counter is set to 0. The system also guarantees that the `turns` counter will reach 3 and remain at that value infinitely often.

Whenever the intruder remains stationary, i.e., its `intruderX` and `intruderY` coordinates do not change, the system increases the `turns` counter by 1 in the next state. 

Note that the environment's behavior is not restricted in any way, except for the implications of the system's guarantees on the environment's actions. The system's behavior is defined in terms of its responses to the environment's actions, without imposing any constraints on those actions themselves.
