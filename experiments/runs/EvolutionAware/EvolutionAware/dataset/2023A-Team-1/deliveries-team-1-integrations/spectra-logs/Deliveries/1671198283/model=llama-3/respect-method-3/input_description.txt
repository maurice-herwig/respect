The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment and controls the positions of two robots. The environment-controlled inputs are not explicitly defined, while the system-controlled outputs are the positions of the two robots, represented by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, which are coordinates within a grid of size 5x5.

The system guarantees that initially, both robots are positioned at y-coordinate 5. 

At all times, the system ensures that the robots move in a way that their x and y coordinates can either increase by 1, decrease by 1, or remain the same. This movement rule applies to both robots. 

There are no specified assumptions about the environment's behavior, and no liveness conditions are defined for the system. The system's behavior is solely defined by the initial positions of the robots and the constraints on their movement.
