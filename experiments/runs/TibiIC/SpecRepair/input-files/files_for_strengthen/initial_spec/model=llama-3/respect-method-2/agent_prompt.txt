The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car (carA), emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green (greenA) for carA.

The environment is expected to initially have no car present and no emergency situation. The system is required to initially have the traffic light set to not green.

It is assumed that a car will be detected at the traffic light infinitely often, and the police will not be present infinitely often. 

The system guarantees that the traffic light will be green infinitely often and that there will be no car present at the traffic light infinitely often.

When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system ensures that the car remains present until the traffic light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and the traffic light is green, the system ensures that the car will move and no longer be present at the next time step. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car (carA), emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light (greenA).
