Use $respect-method-2.

The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic flow. The environment controls three inputs: `carA`, indicating the presence of a car, `emergency`, indicating an emergency situation, and `police`, indicating police presence. The system controls one output: `greenA`, indicating whether the traffic light is green for car A.

The environment is assumed to initially have no car present and no emergency situation. Additionally, the environment is expected to have a car present infinitely often. 

The system guarantees that it will initially have the traffic light set to not green. The system also guarantees that the traffic light will be green infinitely often, and that there will be no car present infinitely often.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the car will still be present at the next time step. On the other hand, if a car is present and the traffic light is green, the car will not be present at the next time step.

Note that the `emergency` and `police` inputs do not have any specified effect on the system's behavior in this specification.
