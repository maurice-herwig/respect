The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car (carA), emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light (greenA).

Initially, the environment is expected to have no car present and no emergency situation. The system is initially expected to have the traffic light in a non-green state.

The environment is assumed to periodically detect the presence of a car and to not have police present at all times. 

The system guarantees that the traffic light will be green at all times when the environment satisfies the assumptions, and that there will be no car present at the traffic light at all times.

When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, and there is no emergency, the car will remain idle until the traffic light turns green. However, when a car is present, the traffic light is green, and there is no police presence, the car will move, indicating its presence will no longer be detected.

The environment and system must satisfy these conditions to ensure the correct functioning of the TrafficE2 system.
