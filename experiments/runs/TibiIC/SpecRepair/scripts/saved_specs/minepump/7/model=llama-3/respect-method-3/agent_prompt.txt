The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

Initially, the system assumes that there is no high water and no methane present, and the pump is not active.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will either continue to be present or the pump will be activated at the next step.

Additionally, the system guarantees that if methane is present, it will either continue to be present or the pump will be deactivated at the next step if high water is present.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was active in the previous step and remains active, the water level will decrease at the next step. 

It is also assumed that if there is no high water, the environment will either continue to have no high water or there will be no methane. 

The system's behavior is defined by these guarantees and assumptions, ensuring a specific interaction between the environment and the system. The pump's activation is determined by the presence of high water and methane, according to the specified rules.
