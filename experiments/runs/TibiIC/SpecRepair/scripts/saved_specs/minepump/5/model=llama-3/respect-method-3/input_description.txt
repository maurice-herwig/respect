The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump is on and still on at the next time step, then the water level will decrease to not being high. Additionally, the environment is assumed to maintain a consistent state regarding the presence of high water, either continuing with no high water or ensuring that if there is no high water, then there is also no methane.

The system must guarantee that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump at the next time step, unless the pump is already off, in which case it remains off. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the system must ensure that if methane is still present at the next time step, the pump will be turned off.

In summary, the system's primary goal is to manage the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane, ensuring safe operation under the given assumptions about the environment's behavior. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, and the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
