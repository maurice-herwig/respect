The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to start in a safe state, where there is no high water and no methane present. Initially, the pump is also not operating.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will operate the pump if methane is also present or about to be present. Additionally, if methane is detected, the system will stop the pump at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump has been operating and continues to operate, the high water condition will be resolved at the next opportunity. Furthermore, the environment ensures that if the pump is operating, either the high water condition or the methane condition (or both) will be absent.

In summary, the Minepump system is designed to respond to the presence of high water and methane by operating or stopping the pump according to specific rules, while relying on the environment to satisfy certain assumptions about the behavior of the high water and methane conditions. The system's operation is guided by the need to safely manage the pump in response to the monitored conditions.
