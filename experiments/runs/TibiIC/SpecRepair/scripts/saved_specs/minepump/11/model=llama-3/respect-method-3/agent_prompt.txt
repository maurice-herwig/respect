The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump.

Initially, the environment is assumed to be in a safe state, with no high water and no methane present. In this initial state, the pump is required to be off.

The system is expected to behave as follows: if high water is detected, the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the pump is already off. If methane is detected, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump has been on and continues to be on, the high water condition will be resolved at the next opportunity. Additionally, if there is no high water, the environment will either continue to have no high water or will have no methane.

In summary, the system must respond to high water and methane conditions by controlling the pump, while the environment is expected to provide safe initial conditions and behave in a way that allows the system to effectively manage the pump. The system's primary goal is to ensure the pump is operated safely in response to the monitored conditions.
