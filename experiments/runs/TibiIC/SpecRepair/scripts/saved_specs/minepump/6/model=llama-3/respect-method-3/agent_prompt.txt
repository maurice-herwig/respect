The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump.

Initially, it is assumed that there is no high water and no methane present. In this initial state, the pump is guaranteed to be off.

The system guarantees that if high water is detected, the pump will either be off or will turn on at the next opportunity. Additionally, if methane is detected, the pump will either be on or will turn off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, the high water condition will be resolved at the next opportunity. Furthermore, the environment is assumed to ensure that if there is no high water, then either there will still be no high water or there will be no methane at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system is designed to respond to high water and methane conditions by controlling the pump, while the environment ensures certain conditions are met to facilitate the system's operation. The system's behavior is constrained by the initial conditions, safety requirements, and the environment's assumptions, which together ensure the proper functioning of the mine pump.
