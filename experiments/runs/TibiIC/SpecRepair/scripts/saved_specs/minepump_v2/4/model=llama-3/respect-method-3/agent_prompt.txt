The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: if the pump is on and still on at the next moment, then the water level will decrease. Additionally, if there is no high water, then either there will be no high water or there will be no methane at the next moment.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: 
- If high water is present, the pump must be off initially, but it will turn on at the next moment.
- If methane is detected, the pump must turn off at the next moment.
 
These requirements ensure the system's safe operation in response to environmental conditions.
