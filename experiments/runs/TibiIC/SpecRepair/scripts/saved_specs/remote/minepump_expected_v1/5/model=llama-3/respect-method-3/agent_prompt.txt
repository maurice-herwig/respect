The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is initially off.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: if the pump is on and still on at the next moment, the water level will decrease. Additionally, if there is no high water, the environment will either maintain this condition or ensure there is no methane.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: 
- If high water is present, the pump must be off, but it will turn on at the next moment.
- If methane is detected, then either the methane remains present or the pump will turn off at the next moment.

In general, the system's pump operation is influenced by the environmental conditions of high water and methane, with specific rules governing how the pump responds to these conditions.
