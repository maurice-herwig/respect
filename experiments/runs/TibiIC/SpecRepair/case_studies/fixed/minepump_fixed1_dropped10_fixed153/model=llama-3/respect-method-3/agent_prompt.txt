The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls two inputs: high water levels and methane presence. The system controls one output: the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever methane is present, the pump is initially off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if no methane is detected, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

Note that the system's behavior regarding the high water level input is not specified. The system's initial state and any potential liveness requirements are also not defined in this specification.
