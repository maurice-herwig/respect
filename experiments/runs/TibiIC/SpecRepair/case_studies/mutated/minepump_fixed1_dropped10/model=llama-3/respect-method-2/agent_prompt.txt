The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment determines whether there is high water and whether methane is present. The system determines whether the pump is operating.

It is assumed that the environment will not activate the pump. 

The system must satisfy two requirements. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be activated at the next step. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be activated at the next step. 

Note that the specification does not describe any initial conditions or liveness requirements for the system. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of the next-state relationships between the presence of methane and the activation of the pump.
