The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not activate the pump directly; instead, the system decides when to activate the pump. This assumption implies that if the pump is currently off, it was also off in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or the water is not high. This ensures that the pump is activated when necessary to handle methane in the presence of high water. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state. This prevents unnecessary pump operation when there is no methane.

These guarantees ensure the system responds appropriately to the environment's conditions regarding methane presence and water level, while also considering the system's own previous state regarding pump operation.
