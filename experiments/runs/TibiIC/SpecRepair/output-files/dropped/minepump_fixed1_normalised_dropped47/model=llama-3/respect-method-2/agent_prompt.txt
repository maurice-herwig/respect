The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating in the previous state, then it will not be in a high water condition in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be operating in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be operating in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly dependent on the presence or absence of methane in the environment, with the additional constraint that the environment's high water condition is related to the system's previous pump operation.
