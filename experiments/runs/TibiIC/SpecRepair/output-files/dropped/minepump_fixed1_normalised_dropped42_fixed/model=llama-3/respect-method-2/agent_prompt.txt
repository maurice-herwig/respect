The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if methane is both present and absent (which is impossible), and it was the case that the pump was not operating in the previous state, then either the high water condition will be false in the next state, or the pump was operating in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be operating in the next state, or the high water condition must be false. Secondly, if methane is absent, then the pump must not be operating in the next state.

Note that the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. The specification does not describe any initial conditions or explicit liveness requirements beyond these guarantees.
