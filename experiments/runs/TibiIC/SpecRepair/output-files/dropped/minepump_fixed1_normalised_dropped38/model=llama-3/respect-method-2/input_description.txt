The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the detection of high water and methane, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not active in the previous state, then it will not be the case that high water is present in the next state, unless the pump was active in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be activated in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not detected, then the pump must not be activated in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the presence or absence of methane, under the assumption that the environment's high water condition is related to the previous state of the pump.
