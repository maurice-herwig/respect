The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. In response, the system controls a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not running in the previous state, then it will not be in a high water condition in the next state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be running in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be running in the next state. These conditions must always hold, ensuring the system responds appropriately to the presence or absence of methane. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions, and the system-controlled output is the state of the pump.
