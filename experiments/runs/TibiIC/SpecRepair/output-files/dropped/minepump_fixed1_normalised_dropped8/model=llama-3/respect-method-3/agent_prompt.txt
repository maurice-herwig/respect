Use $respect-method-3.

The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment is assumed to never activate the pump. 

Given this environment, the system must satisfy two requirements. Firstly, it must always be the case that either methane is present or the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be activated in the next state. 

Note that the pump is controlled by the system, while the presence of high water and methane are controlled by the environment.
