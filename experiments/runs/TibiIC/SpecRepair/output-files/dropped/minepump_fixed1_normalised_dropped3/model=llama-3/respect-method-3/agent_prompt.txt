The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that it will not cause the water level to rise if the pump is not operating.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be turned on at the next step.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane levels, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
