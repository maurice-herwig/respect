The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not running in the previous state, then either the water level will not be high in the next state or it was not high in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be running in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be running in the next state. 

In essence, the system ensures that the pump operates based on the presence or absence of methane, under the assumption that the environment's water level changes are related to the pump's previous state.
