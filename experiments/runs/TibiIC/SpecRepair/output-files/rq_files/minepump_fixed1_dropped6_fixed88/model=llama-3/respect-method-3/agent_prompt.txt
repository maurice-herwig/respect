The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the pump's state.

It is assumed that the environment will not activate the pump if it was already active in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be activated in the next state, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state.

In summary, the Minepump system is required to control the pump based on the presence of methane and high water, while adhering to the environment's constraint on pump activation.
