The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane becomes present, then either the water is not high or the pump is not running. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next step. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the state of the pump. 

There are no specified initial conditions or liveness requirements for this system. The specified guarantees define the safety requirements that the system must satisfy in response to the environment's inputs, under the given assumption about the environment's behavior.
