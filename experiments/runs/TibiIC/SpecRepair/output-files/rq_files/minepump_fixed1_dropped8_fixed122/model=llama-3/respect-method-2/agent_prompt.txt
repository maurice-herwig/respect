The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels and the presence of methane. The environment controls the occurrence of these conditions, which are represented by the variables "highwater" and "methane". The system, in response, controls a pump, which is either on or off.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump is currently off, it will remain off, or if it was off in the previous state, it will stay off.

The system guarantees two main behaviors. Firstly, if the water level is not high, then the pump will be on in the next state, or if the pump is on, the water level will not be high in the next state. This ensures that the pump operates appropriately in response to water levels.

Secondly, the system ensures that if methane is present or not, the pump will be off in the next state. This condition always holds, regardless of the methane level, indicating a safety precaution where the presence or absence of methane directly influences the pump's state in the following moment.

In summary, the Minepump system is designed to manage a pump based on environmental conditions such as water levels and methane presence, with specific rules governing its operation to ensure safety and appropriate response to these conditions.
