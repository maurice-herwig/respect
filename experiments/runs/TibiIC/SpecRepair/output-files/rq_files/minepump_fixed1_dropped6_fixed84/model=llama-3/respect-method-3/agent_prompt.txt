The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls these conditions, while the system controls the state of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane is present, it was not the case that the pump was previously on.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, or there must not be high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's conditions of high water and methane presence, with specific rules governing when the pump should be turned on or off in response to these conditions.
