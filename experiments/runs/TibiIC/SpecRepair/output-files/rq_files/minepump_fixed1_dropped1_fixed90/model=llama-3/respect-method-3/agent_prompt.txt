The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently on, then the next state will either have no high water or the pump was previously off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In essence, the system's pump operation is directly tied to the presence or absence of methane, with the environment's behavior influencing the pump's state through the relationship between the pump's operation and the water level.
