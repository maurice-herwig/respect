The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating, then the presence of high water will not persist indefinitely; whenever the pump is off, the absence of high water will eventually follow.

The system must satisfy two requirements. Firstly, if methane is present, the system must ensure that the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, the system must ensure that the pump will be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
