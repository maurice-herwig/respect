The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump. 

The system assumes that the environment will not allow high water to rise if it was previously not present, unless the pump is off. 

The system guarantees two behaviors. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the environment inputs are the high water and methane conditions, and the system output is the state of the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the assumption about the environment's control of the high water condition and the guarantees about the pump's operation in response to methane and high water conditions.
