The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

The environment provides two inputs: high water and methane indicators. The system has one output: the pump's status.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: if the pump is on and remains on, then the water level will decrease in the next state. Additionally, if there is no methane, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane, indicating a relationship between methane presence and water level.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: if the water level is high, then in the next state, the pump must either be turned on or off. Furthermore, if methane is detected, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system's pump operation is influenced by the environment's high water and methane conditions, with specific constraints on how the environment and system interact to control the pump and water level.
