The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water level is not high and the pump is not running, then if the water level becomes not high, it will remain not high as long as the pump remains off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next step, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the presence of methane and the water level, while the environment's behavior is restricted regarding the water level when the pump is off.
