The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls two inputs: high water level (highwater) and methane presence (methane). The system controls one output: the pump activation (pump). 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever the water level was not high in the previous state, the pump must be off. 

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump will be activated in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump will be deactivated in the next state. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is turned on when methane is detected and turned off when methane is not present, under the assumption that the pump is initially off when the water level is not high.
