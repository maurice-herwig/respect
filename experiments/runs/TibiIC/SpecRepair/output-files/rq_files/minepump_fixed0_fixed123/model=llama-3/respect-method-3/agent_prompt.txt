The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. 

The system assumes that when the pump is continuously active, the high water level will decrease in the next state. Additionally, the system assumes that if there is no high water, then either there will still be no high water or there will be no methane in the next state.

Given these assumptions, the system guarantees two behaviors. Firstly, if the water level is high, the pump must be off initially, but it will turn on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is detected, then if the water level is high, the pump must be off in the next state.

Initially, there are no specified conditions. At all times, the system must adhere to the guarantees regarding pump operation based on water and methane levels. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's conditions, and it must respond accordingly to ensure safe operation.
