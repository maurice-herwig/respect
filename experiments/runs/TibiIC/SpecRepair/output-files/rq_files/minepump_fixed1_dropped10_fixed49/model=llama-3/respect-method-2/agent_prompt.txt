The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment provides two inputs: a signal indicating whether there is high water and a signal indicating the presence of methane. The system has one output: the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if there is high water, it will not turn on the pump. 

The system guarantees two behaviors. Firstly, if methane is present, the system will turn on the pump at the next opportunity. Secondly, if there is no methane, the system will turn off the pump at the next opportunity. 

These requirements imply that the system will manage the pump based on the presence or absence of methane, while also considering the environment's constraint on pump operation during high water conditions.
