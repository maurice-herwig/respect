The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and whether there is methane. The system determines whether the pump is on or off.

It is assumed that the environment will not turn the pump on if it was previously off, and if the pump is on, the environment will keep it on or turn it off.

The system guarantees that at any point, it will either keep the pump off or turn it on at the next step. 

Additionally, the system ensures that if there is high water or methane present, it will turn the pump off at the next step, unless both high water and methane are absent. 

In summary, the system's behavior is constrained by the environment's conditions and its own previous state, ensuring safe operation of the pump in response to the monitored conditions.
