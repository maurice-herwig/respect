The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that whenever there is high water, the pump will not be operating.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, at the next step, the pump must be turned on unless there is no methane.

In summary, the system's pump operation is constrained by the environment's conditions of high water and methane presence, with specific rules governing when the pump should be turned on in relation to these conditions.
