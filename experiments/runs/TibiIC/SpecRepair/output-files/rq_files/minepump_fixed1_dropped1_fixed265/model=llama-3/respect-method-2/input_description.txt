The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when there is no high water, the next state will also have no high water, unless the pump is turned off.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step, or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the presence of methane and high water, while the environment controls these two conditions, with the assumption that high water will persist unless the pump is turned off.
