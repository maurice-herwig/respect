The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and whether there is methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that the environment behaves in such a way that if the pump was on in the previous state and remains on, then the water level will decrease, moving away from a high-water condition, in the next state. Additionally, it is assumed that if the pump is on, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane present.

The system guarantees that if the water level is high, it will turn on the pump in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is detected, then the system guarantees that either the water level is high or it will turn off the pump in the next state. 

In summary, the system is designed to manage the pump based on the conditions of high water and methane presence, ensuring safe operation under the given environmental assumptions.
