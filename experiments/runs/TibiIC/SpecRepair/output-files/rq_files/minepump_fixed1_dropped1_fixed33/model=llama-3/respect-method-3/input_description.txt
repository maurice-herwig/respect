The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating, then either the water will not be high in the next state or the pump will remain off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on in the next state or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
