Use $respect-method-3.

The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not have high water when the pump is operating, and it is also assumed that when methane is present, the pump will not operate.

The system guarantees that whenever methane is present, the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water is not high. Additionally, the system ensures that methane is never present.

Initially, there are no specified conditions. At all times, the system must adhere to the guarantee that methane is absent. The system must also ensure that when methane is present, it will take action to turn on the pump, unless the water level is low, and it must do so in a way that respects the environment's constraints on high water and methane presence.
