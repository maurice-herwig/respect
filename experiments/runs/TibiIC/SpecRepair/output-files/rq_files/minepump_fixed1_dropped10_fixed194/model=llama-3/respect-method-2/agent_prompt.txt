The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever the water level is high, the pump is not operating. 

The system must guarantee two conditions: 
- If methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity.
- If methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system's pump operation is determined by the presence or absence of methane, while the environment ensures that the pump does not operate when the water level is high.
