The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment determines the levels of water and methane. The system must ensure that if methane is present, then either the pump will be activated in the next state or there is no high water. Additionally, the system guarantees that if the pump is to be activated in the next state, then there is no methane present.

It is assumed that the environment behaves in such a way that whenever there is high water, the pump is not activated.

In summary, the system's behavior is constrained by the environment's conditions, and it must adapt its pump activation accordingly, while the environment is expected to follow a specific pattern regarding water levels and pump activation. The system's initial state and other specific conditions are not defined, but its ongoing operation must satisfy these constraints.
