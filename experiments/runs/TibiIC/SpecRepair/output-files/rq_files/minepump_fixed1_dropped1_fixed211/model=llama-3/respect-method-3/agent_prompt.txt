The Minepump system is designed to operate in an environment with two input conditions: high water levels and methane presence. The system controls a single output, the pump. 

The environment controls the high water level and methane presence. The system must ensure that if methane is present, the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity. Conversely, if methane is not present, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane is not present, then either the water level will not be high at the next opportunity or the pump was not previously running.

Initially, there are no specified conditions. At all times, the system must adhere to the guarantees regarding pump operation based on methane presence, and the environment must follow the assumption regarding methane, water levels, and pump operation.
