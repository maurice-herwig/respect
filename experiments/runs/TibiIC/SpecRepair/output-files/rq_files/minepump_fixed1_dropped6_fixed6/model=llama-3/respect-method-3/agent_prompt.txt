The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and whether there is methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that the pump remains off until it is turned on, meaning that if the pump was previously off and is currently off, then it was off in the previous state.

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system ensures the pump is managed based on the presence of methane and the water level, under the assumption that the pump's initial state and transitions follow a specific pattern.
