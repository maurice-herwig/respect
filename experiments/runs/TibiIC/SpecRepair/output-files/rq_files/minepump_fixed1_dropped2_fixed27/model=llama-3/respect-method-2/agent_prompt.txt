The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and whether there is methane. The system determines whether the pump is on or off.

It is assumed that the environment will not turn the pump on if it was previously off, unless the pump was already on.

The system must behave as follows: if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must be no high water. Additionally, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is determined by the environment's methane and high water conditions, with specific rules governing when the pump should be on or off in response to these conditions.
