The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls these conditions, which are represented by the variables highwater and methane. The system controls a pump, which can be either on or off.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when the water is high, if the pump was previously off, it will remain off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, whenever the water is not high, the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, whenever the water is not high and there is no methane, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's conditions of high water and methane presence, with specific rules governing when the pump should be on or off in response to these conditions.
