The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is expected to behave in the following ways: when the pump is on, if it remains on, the water level will decrease in the next state. Additionally, if there is no high water, then either there will be no high water or there will be no methane in the next state.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: if the water level is high, then either methane is present or the pump will turn on in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is present, then either methane remains present or the pump will turn off in the next state.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. The system's behavior is defined by the guarantees that it must satisfy in response to the environment's inputs, under the given assumptions about the environment's behavior.
