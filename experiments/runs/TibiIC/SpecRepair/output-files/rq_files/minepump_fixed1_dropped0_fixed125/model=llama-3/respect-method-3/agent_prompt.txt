The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that whenever methane becomes present, if the pump was not operating in the previous state, then high water will not be present in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state. 

These requirements ensure that the system responds appropriately to the presence or absence of methane and high water, while the environment ensures that certain conditions are met when methane appears.
