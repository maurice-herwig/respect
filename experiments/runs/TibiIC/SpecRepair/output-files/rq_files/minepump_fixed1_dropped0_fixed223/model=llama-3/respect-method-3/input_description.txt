The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when the water is high, it will eventually become not high if the pump was not previously on.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or the water must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the presence of methane and high water, while the environment is expected to eventually lower the water level when it is high, provided the pump was not previously on.
