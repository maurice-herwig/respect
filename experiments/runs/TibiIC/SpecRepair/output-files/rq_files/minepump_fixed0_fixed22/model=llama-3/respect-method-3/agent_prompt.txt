The Minepump system is designed to operate in an environment where water levels and methane presence are monitored. The environment controls the water level, indicated by the "highwater" signal, and the presence of methane, indicated by the "methane" signal. The system controls the operation of a pump, indicated by the "pump" signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, if the pump is currently on and was also on in the previous state, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, if the pump is off, then either the water level is low or there is no methane present.

The system must guarantee certain behaviors. If the water level is high, then in the next state, the pump must be either on or off. If methane is present, then the pump must be off in the next state.

In summary, the system must respond to the environment's water level and methane signals by controlling the pump appropriately, while the environment must follow certain rules regarding the water level and methane presence when the pump is on or off.
