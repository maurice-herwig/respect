The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`carA`) and road B (`carB`), as well as the occurrence of emergencies (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greenA`) and road B (`greenB`).

The environment is assumed to always have cars present on both roads A and B at some point, and it is never in a permanent state of emergency. Additionally, when a car is present on road A and the light is not green, the car will still be present on the next step, and similarly for road B. Conversely, when a car is present on road A and the light is green, the car will not be present on the next step, and the same applies to road B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both green at the same time. It also ensures that each road's traffic light will be green at some point. Furthermore, during an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

In summary, the system manages the traffic lights to safely and fairly allow cars to pass through the intersection, while also responding to emergency situations by halting all traffic.
