Use $respect-method-2.

The system is a robot that operates within a defined grid of dimensions DX by DY, where DX and DY are both 7 units. The robot's position is controlled by the system and is represented by two coordinates: robotX and robotY, which are both within the range of their respective dimensions.

The environment does not have any explicitly controlled inputs in this specification. 

The system must always move its robot either one unit to the right, one unit to the left, or stay in the same position horizontally, as indicated by the possible next values of robotX. Similarly, the system must always move its robot either one unit up, one unit down, or stay in the same position vertically, as indicated by the possible next values of robotY.

When the robot's x-coordinate is 2, its y-coordinate must be 7. When the robot's x-coordinate is 5, its y-coordinate must be 0. These conditions must always hold true.

Initially, the robot is positioned at (0, 0).

It is guaranteed that the robot will eventually reach the position (7, 7) infinitely often.
