The TrafficA1 system controls two traffic lights, greenA and greenB, in response to the presence of cars, carA and carB, which are controlled by the environment.

The system guarantees that both traffic lights are never on at the same time. If there is no car at location A, then the traffic light greenA must be off, and similarly, if there is no car at location B, then the traffic light greenB must be off. 

Additionally, the system ensures that each traffic light will turn green infinitely often. This means that regardless of the presence of cars, both greenA and greenB will be turned on repeatedly over time. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence or absence of cars at locations A and carB, while the system-controlled outputs are the states of the traffic lights greenA and greenB.
