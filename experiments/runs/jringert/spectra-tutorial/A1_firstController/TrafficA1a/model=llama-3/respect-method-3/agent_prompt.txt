The system controls two traffic lights, greenA and greenB, which indicate when traffic from respective directions A and B is allowed to proceed. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, indicated by carA and carB, respectively.

Initially, it is assumed that there is a car at intersection A and no car at intersection B. Additionally, it is assumed that cars will always be present at both intersections infinitely often.

The system guarantees that initially, both traffic lights are off. At all times, the system ensures that both traffic lights are never on simultaneously. If there is no car at intersection A, the system guarantees that greenA will be off, and similarly, if there is no car at intersection B, the system guarantees that greenB will be off.

Furthermore, the system guarantees that greenA will be on infinitely often and greenB will be on infinitely often, ensuring that traffic from both directions will be allowed to proceed at some point.
