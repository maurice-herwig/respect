The TrafficL4 system controls the traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The system has two outputs: greenA and greenB, which indicate whether the traffic lights are green for roads A and B, respectively. The environment provides two inputs: carA and carB, which indicate whether there are cars present on roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever there is a car on road A, there will be a car on road B within 0 to 3 time units.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for both roads A and B will not be green at the same time. 

Additionally, the system ensures that if there is a car on road A and the traffic light for road A is not green, then the traffic light for road A will turn green within 4 time units.
