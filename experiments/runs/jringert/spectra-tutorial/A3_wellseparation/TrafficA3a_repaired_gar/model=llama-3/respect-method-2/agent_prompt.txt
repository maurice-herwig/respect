The system controls two traffic lights, greenA and greenB, which are boolean indicators of whether each light is currently green. The environment provides input through two boolean variables, carA and carB, indicating the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if a car is present at intersection A and the light is not green, the car will still be present at the next time step. The same assumption applies to intersection B. Additionally, it is assumed that cars will never be present at both intersections A and B simultaneously.

The system guarantees that the two traffic lights will never be green at the same time. Furthermore, the system ensures that if a car is present at intersection A, the light at A will be green, and if a car is present at intersection B but not at A, the light at B will be green. These conditions must always hold, ensuring safe and coordinated traffic flow.
