The system controls two traffic lights, greenA and greenB, which indicate when cars can proceed from intersections A and B, respectively. The environment controls the presence of cars at these intersections, represented by carA and carB.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if a car is present at intersection A and the greenA light is not on, the car will remain at intersection A in the next time step. Similarly, if a car is present at intersection B and the greenB light is not on, the car will remain at intersection B in the next time step. Additionally, the environment ensures that either there are no cars at both intersections A and B, or both greenA and greenB lights are off whenever cars are present at both intersections.

The system guarantees that the greenA and greenB lights will never be on at the same time, ensuring safe traffic flow by preventing simultaneous green lights at intersections A and B.
