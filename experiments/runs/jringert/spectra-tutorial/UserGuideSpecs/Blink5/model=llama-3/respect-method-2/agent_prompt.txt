If the system is not at a station, the counter resets. The system is designed to blink exactly 5 times while at a station, after which no further blinking is required. 

In summary, the Blink5 system is required to blink its light up to 5 times whenever it is at a station, with the blinking process starting from the initial state and resetting when the system leaves the station.
