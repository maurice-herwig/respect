The Minepump system is designed to interact with an environment that provides two inputs: highwater and methane, which indicate the presence of high water and methane, respectively. The system controls one output: pump, which represents the activation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to start in a safe state, where both highwater and methane are false. Initially, the pump is also deactivated.

The system guarantees that it will behave as follows: if the environment indicates high water and no methane, the pump will be activated at the next step. On the other hand, if the environment indicates the presence of methane, the pump will be deactivated at the next step.

The environment is also assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously active and is still active, the highwater condition will be resolved at the next step.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is activated when there is high water and no methane, and deactivated when there is methane, while the environment ensures that the highwater condition is resolved when the pump is active.
