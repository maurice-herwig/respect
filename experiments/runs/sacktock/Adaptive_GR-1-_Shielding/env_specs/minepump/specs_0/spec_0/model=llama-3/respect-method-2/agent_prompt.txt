The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels and the presence of methane. The environment controls the occurrence of these conditions, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

Initially, it is assumed that there is no high water and no methane present. Under these initial conditions, the system must ensure that the pump is not active.

The system is required to behave in the following manner: if the water level becomes high, the pump must be activated at the next opportunity. Conversely, if methane is detected, the pump must be deactivated at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously active and remains active, the water level will decrease at the next opportunity. Additionally, it is assumed that the environment will not simultaneously have high water and methane present.

These requirements define the expected interaction between the environment and the Minepump system, ensuring safe and proper operation of the pump in response to changing environmental conditions.
