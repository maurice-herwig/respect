The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls two inputs: `highwater` and `methane`, which indicate the presence of high water and methane, respectively. The system controls one output: `pump`, which represents the state of a pump.

Initially, the environment is assumed to be in a safe state, where there is no high water and no methane. In this initial state, the pump is required to be off.

The system guarantees that if high water is detected and there is no methane, the pump will be turned on at the next step. On the other hand, if methane is detected, the pump will be turned off at the next step.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently on and was also on in the previous step, the high water condition will be resolved at the next step.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is controlled based on the presence of high water and methane, while the environment is expected to resolve the high water condition when the pump is operating continuously.
