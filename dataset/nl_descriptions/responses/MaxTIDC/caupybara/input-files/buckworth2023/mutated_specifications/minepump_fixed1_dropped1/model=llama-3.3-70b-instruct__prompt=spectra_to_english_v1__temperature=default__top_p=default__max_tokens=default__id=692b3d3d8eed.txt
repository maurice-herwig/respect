The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating, then the water will not be high in the next state, or the water will become not high in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be operating in the next state or the water must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be operating in the next state.

There are no specified initial conditions or liveness requirements for the system. The system's behavior is defined solely by the safety requirements stated above, which must always hold. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
