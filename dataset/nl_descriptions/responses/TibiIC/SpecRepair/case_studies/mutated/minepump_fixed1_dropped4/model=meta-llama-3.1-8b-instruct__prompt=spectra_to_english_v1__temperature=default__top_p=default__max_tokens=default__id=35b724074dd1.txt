The Minepump system must operate in conjunction with its environment, which provides two inputs: a boolean indicating whether the water level is high and a boolean indicating the presence of methane. The system's sole output is a boolean indicating whether the pump is activated.

The environment's behavior is assumed to be restricted by the condition that the pump must always be deactivated. 

The Minepump system guarantees two behaviors: 

Firstly, whenever methane is present or the next state of the pump is activated, the system must ensure that the water level is not high. 

Secondly, the system must guarantee that methane is never present. 

These guarantees must hold under the assumption that the environment's behavior is restricted as specified.
