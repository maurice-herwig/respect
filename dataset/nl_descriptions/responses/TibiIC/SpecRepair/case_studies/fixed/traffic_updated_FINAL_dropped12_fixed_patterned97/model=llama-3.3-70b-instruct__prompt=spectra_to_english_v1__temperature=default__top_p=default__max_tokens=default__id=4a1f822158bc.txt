The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the traffic light for car A is red, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the traffic light for car B is red, car B is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. The environment is also assumed to move car A when the traffic light for car A is green, and to move car B when the traffic light for car B is green, unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for cars A and B are not both red at the same time, unless there is an emergency. When there is no emergency, the system ensures that if car A is present, the traffic light for car A will eventually turn green. If car B is present, the system ensures that either the traffic light for car A or car B will eventually turn green. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system responds to the presence of cars by changing the state of the traffic lights. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will change its state to wait for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will change its state back to waiting for a car to arrive. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state.
