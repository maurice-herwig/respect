### **Minepump System Requirements**

This system models a mine pump that responds to water and gas levels in an underground environment.

**Inputs (controlled by the environment):**
- `highwater`: Indicates whether the water level in the mine has reached a critical high point.
- `methane`: Indicates whether dangerous methane gas is detected in the mine.

**Output (controlled by the system):**
- `pump`: Determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

**Assumptions on the environment:**
- The environment guarantees that whenever methane is detected (`methane = true`), the pump must already be off (`pump = false`). In other words, the system will never be in a state where both methane is present and the pump is running.

**System guarantees:**
1. If methane is detected (`methane = true`) at any time, the system must ensure that the pump will be on (`pump = true`) in the very next step. This implies that the pump must activate immediately after methane is detected, regardless of the water level.
2. If no methane is detected (`methane = false`) at any time, the system must ensure that the pump will be off (`pump = false`) in the very next step, meaning the pump must deactivate immediately once methane is no longer present.

The system's behavior is strictly defined in terms of its next-state response to methane detection, without additional constraints on initial conditions or long-term liveness. The pump's operation is tied solely to the presence or absence of methane in each step.
