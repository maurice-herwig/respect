The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, an emergency situation, and police intervention. The environment controls the variables "car" (indicating whether a car is present), "emergency" (indicating an emergency situation), and "police" (indicating police intervention). The system controls the variable "green" (indicating whether the traffic light is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not always be present, and there will be times when they are not intervening. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is not moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the light is green, the environment is assumed to ensure that if a car was present, it will either move or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green often when a car is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will always be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and responds accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will stay green as long as a car is present, after which it may turn red again. The system ensures that it will regularly return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car.
