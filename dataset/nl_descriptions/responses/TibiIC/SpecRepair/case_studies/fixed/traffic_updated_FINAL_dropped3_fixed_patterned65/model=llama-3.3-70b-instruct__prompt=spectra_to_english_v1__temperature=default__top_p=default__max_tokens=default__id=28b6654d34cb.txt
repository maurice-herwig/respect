The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road B is red, then the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. 

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to a car on a road by turning the corresponding traffic light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not waiting for a response. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car on the road or the traffic light is already green. If a car appears on the road and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for a response. The system remains in this waiting state until the traffic light turns green, at which point it transitions back to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will repeatedly respond to cars on the roads.
