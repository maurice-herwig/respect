The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to environmental inputs. The environment controls three boolean inputs: `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which signals an emergency situation. The system controls two boolean outputs: `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B, respectively.

The system assumes that when an emergency situation occurs, the cars at both intersections will either remain idle or move only when their respective traffic lights are green. Specifically, if an emergency situation is in effect, the car at intersection A will not move unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency situation persists. Similarly, the car at intersection B will not move unless the traffic light at intersection B is green or the car is already moving or the emergency situation persists. Additionally, when an emergency situation is in effect, the cars will move only when their respective traffic lights are not green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time. If a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, if a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. However, during an emergency situation, the system will keep the traffic lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green, but only if the emergency situation is not in effect. If the emergency situation is in effect, the system will keep the traffic lights red. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
