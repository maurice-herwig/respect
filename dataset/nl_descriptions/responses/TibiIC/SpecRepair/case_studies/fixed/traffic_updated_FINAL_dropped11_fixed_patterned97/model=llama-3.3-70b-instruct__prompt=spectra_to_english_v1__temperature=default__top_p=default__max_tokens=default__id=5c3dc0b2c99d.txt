The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that the emergency signal is usually false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if car A is present, it will either remain present, the emergency situation will persist, the traffic light at intersection A will be green, or car A will not be present at the next time step. Similar assumptions are made for car B. Furthermore, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if car A or car B is moving, it will either continue to move, the emergency situation will persist, or the traffic light at the corresponding intersection will be red. 

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. If a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. A similar guarantee is made for intersection B. When an emergency situation occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights will be red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green, while ensuring that the traffic lights are never both red at the same time. The system repeatedly returns to its initial state after responding to the presence of a car.
