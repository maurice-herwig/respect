The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road A at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing or the light for road A is green. Similarly, for road B, when an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road B at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing, the light for road B is green, or there was no car on road B previously. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that there is no car on road A at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing or the light for road A is no longer green. The environment also ensures that when an emergency occurs, the light for road B will not be green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. However, when an emergency occurs, the system will keep the lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present on a road or the corresponding light is green when a car is present. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. The system returns to the initial state when the light turns green. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it periodically checks for the presence of cars and responds accordingly.
