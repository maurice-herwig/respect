The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Firstly, when the traffic light is not green, the car will either remain idle or become present only if there is an emergency or if the car is already present. Secondly, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or become present only when the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This is achieved through a pattern of behavior where the system initially starts in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is green when the car is present. If the car becomes present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green. The system's behavior is constrained by the assumptions on the environment and the guarantee that it will often respond to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green.
