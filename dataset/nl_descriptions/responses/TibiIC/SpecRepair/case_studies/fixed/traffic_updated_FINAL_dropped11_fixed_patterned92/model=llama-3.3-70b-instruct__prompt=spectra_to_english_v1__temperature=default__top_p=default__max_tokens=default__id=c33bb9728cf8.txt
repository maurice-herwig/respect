The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that it is always the case that there will be a point in the future where an emergency is not present. Additionally, when an emergency is present, cars at intersection A will not move until the emergency has passed, the traffic light at A is green, or the car is no longer present. Similarly, when an emergency is present, cars at intersection B will not move until the emergency has passed or the traffic light at B is green. Furthermore, when an emergency is present and the traffic light at A is green, the car at A will move. Likewise, when an emergency is present and the traffic light at B is green, the car at B will move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. Specifically, the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at B green. During emergency situations, both traffic lights will be red.

The system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will respond by turning the corresponding traffic light green, but only if the traffic light is not already green. If the traffic light is already green, the system will continue to keep it green as long as the car is present. The system will repeatedly turn the traffic light green in response to the presence of a car, ensuring that the traffic light is green whenever a car is present.
