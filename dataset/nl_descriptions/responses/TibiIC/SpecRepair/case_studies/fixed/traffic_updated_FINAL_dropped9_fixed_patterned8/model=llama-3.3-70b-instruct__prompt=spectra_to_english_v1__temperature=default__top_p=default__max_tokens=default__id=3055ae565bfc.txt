The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as emergency situations, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when a car is approaching intersection A, it will either stop or an emergency will occur, or the traffic light at intersection A will be green, or there will be no car approaching; similarly, when a car is approaching intersection B, it will either stop or an emergency will occur, or the traffic light at intersection B will be green, or there will be no car approaching. Additionally, if an emergency occurs, it will persist. Furthermore, when a car is at intersection B, it will either not be moving or it will not move in the next step, or the traffic light at intersection B will not be green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green; similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green. This guarantee is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to arrive, and then responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. The system will continue to turn the traffic light green as long as the car is present, and will return to waiting when the car is no longer present.

Initially, the system is in a waiting state. The system's behavior is constrained by safety requirements that ensure the traffic lights are turned green only when a car is present, and that the system returns to waiting when a car is no longer present. The system also satisfies liveness requirements that ensure the traffic lights will eventually turn green when a car is present. The next-state relationships are defined such that the system will transition between waiting and responding states based on the presence of cars and the current state of the traffic lights.
