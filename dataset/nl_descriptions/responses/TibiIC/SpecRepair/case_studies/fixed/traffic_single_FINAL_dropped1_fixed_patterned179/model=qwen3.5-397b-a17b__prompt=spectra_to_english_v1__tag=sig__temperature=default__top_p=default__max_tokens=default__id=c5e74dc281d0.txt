

Specification name: TrafficE2

Environment variables:
- car: boolean
- emergency: boolean
- police: boolean

System variables:
- green: boolean

The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light signal based on environmental inputs regarding vehicle presence and emergency services. The environment provides inputs indicating whether a car is present, whether an emergency is active, and whether police are present. The system controls the green light output.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment behavior is restricted by the following assumptions:
1.  The police signal is not permanently active; it must be false infinitely often over time.
2.  Whenever the car is absent, either the light is green or the car is absent.
3.  Whenever the car will be absent in the next step, either the police signal is active, the car will be absent in the next step, or the light is not green.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
1.  **Initial Condition:** The system starts in a ready state.
2.  **Response Requirement:** Whenever the car is present, the traffic light must eventually turn green. The system must ensure this response occurs repeatedly, such that the condition where the car is present without the light being green cannot persist indefinitely.
