The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (not green), the car is expected to be idle. If the car is moving and the traffic light turns red, the car will stop, unless the police intervene.

The environment is also assumed to ensure that if the car is about to stop moving when the traffic light is green, it will either be because the police have intervened or the car will actually stop moving, or the traffic light will turn red.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is formalized by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of two states: S0 and S1. Initially, the system is in state S0. The system remains in state S0 as long as there is no car present or the car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to state S1. In state S1, the system will turn the traffic light green, allowing the system to transition back to state S0. The system will infinitely often return to state S0, ensuring that the traffic light will periodically turn green when a car is present.

In summary, the TrafficE2 system is designed to control a traffic light in response to the presence of a car, while accounting for the potential presence of police and emergency situations, and ensuring that the traffic light periodically turns green when a car is present. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
