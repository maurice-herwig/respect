The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A do not start moving unless the traffic light at A is green or another emergency situation occurs. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection B do not start moving unless the traffic light at B is green or another emergency situation occurs. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A move when the traffic light at A is green, and cars at intersection B move when the traffic light at B is green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at B green. During emergency situations, both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive at an intersection. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. The system remains in this state until the car has passed, at which point it returns to the waiting state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that cars are allowed to pass through the intersection in a timely manner.
