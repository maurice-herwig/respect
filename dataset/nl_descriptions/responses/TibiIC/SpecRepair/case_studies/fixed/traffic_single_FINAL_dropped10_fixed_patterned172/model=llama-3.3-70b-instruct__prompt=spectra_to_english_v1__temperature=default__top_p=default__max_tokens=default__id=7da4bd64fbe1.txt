The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that there will be periods when the police are not present, and the car will not be constantly moving when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car remains stationary. Additionally, when the police are present, the car is either not moving or will not move in the next moment.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be periods when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the car's presence and the traffic light's state, updating its state accordingly. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence again. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic light turns green in response to the car's presence.
