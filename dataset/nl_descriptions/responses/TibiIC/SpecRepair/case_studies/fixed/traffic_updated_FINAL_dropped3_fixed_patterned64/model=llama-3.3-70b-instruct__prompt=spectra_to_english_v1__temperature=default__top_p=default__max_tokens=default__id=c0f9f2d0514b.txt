The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when the emergency signal is active, the traffic light at intersection B is either green or the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green; and when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green. The system responds to the presence of cars by transitioning through a series of states, initially starting in a state where the traffic light is either red or green when there is no car present, and then transitioning to a state where the traffic light turns green when a car is present, and finally returning to the initial state when the traffic light is green and the car is no longer present. The system ensures that it will infinitely often return to the initial state.
