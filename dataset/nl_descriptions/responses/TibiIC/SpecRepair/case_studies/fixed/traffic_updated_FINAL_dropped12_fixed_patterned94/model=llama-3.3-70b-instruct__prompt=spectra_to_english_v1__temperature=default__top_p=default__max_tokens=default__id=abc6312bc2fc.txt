The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal, denoted by `emergency`. The system-controlled outputs are the states of the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation occur infinitely often, and when the traffic light is red for car A, car A will not be present unless there is an emergency. Additionally, when the traffic light is red for car B, car B will not be present unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is about to turn green. The environment also ensures that when car A is present and the traffic light is green, car A will move, and when car B is present and the traffic light is green, car B will move unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both traffic lights red at the same time unless there is an emergency, and when car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car A green. When car B is present, the system will eventually turn either the traffic light for car A or car B green. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both traffic lights will be red.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it will do so in a way that ensures the traffic light will eventually turn green when a car is present. The system will also ensure that the traffic lights are updated correctly based on the presence of cars and emergency situations.
