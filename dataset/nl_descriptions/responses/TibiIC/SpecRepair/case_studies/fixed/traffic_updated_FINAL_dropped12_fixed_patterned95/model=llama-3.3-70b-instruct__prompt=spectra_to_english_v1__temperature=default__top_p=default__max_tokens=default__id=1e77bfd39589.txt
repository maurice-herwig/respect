The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car A is idle unless there is an emergency. Car B is also idle when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the light is green for car B. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green, car A moves and car B moves unless there is an emergency or the light is not green for car B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights are never both red when there is no emergency. When car A is present, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light green for car A. When car B is present, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light green for either car A or car B. During an emergency, both traffic lights are red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the expected conditions are met, such as the traffic light being green when a car is present. If these conditions are not met, the system transitions to a different state, where it waits for the expected conditions to be satisfied before transitioning back to the initial state. The system always eventually returns to the initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
