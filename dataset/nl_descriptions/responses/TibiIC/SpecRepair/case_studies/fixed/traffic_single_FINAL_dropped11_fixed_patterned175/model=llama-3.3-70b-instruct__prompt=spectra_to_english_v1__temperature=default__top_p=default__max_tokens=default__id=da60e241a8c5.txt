The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Firstly, it is assumed that the police will not be present infinitely often. Additionally, the environment is expected to ensure that if a car is not present, it will not appear unless there is an emergency, the traffic light is green, or the car was already present. Furthermore, the environment should ensure that if a car was previously present, it will not be present now unless there are police, the car was not present previously, or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light will be green, but it may take some time. The system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car, and it will remain in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car appears and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light is green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.
