The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency status. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency is declared, the environment will not have a car on road A in the next state if the light is not green for road A, unless the emergency persists or the car is already present. Similarly, for road B, when an emergency is declared, the environment will not have a car on road B in the next state if the light is not green for road B, unless the emergency persists, the car is already present, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency is declared, the environment will ensure that if road A's light is green, a car on road A will move, unless the emergency persists or the light turns red. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if road B's light is green during an emergency, the car on road B will move.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light green for road A. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light green for road B. However, during an emergency, the system will keep the lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system periodically responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
