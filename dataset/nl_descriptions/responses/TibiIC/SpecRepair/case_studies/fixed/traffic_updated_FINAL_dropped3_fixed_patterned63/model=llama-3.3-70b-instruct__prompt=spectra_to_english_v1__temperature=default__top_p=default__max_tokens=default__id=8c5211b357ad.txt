The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the traffic light for road B is either green or the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

More specifically, the system's response to the presence of cars on the roads can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system repeatedly cycles through these states, ensuring that the traffic light for each road turns green whenever a car is present on that road. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic lights continue to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
