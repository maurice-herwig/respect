The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, car A will either remain idle or move to the next state where it is present, unless the traffic light for car A is green. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, car B will either remain idle or move to the next state where it is present, unless the traffic light for car B is green. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present, car A will move when its traffic light is green, unless the emergency situation persists or car A is already moving. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its traffic light green. The same applies to car B. When an emergency situation is present, both traffic lights will be red.

The system also follows specific rules. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. When a car is present and its traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. When the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state. 

In the context of the traffic lights, the system's behavior can be understood as follows: when a car is present, the system will turn its corresponding traffic light green, and when the traffic light is green, the car will move. The system ensures that the traffic lights are never both red at the same time, and when an emergency situation is present, both traffic lights will be red. The system's behavior is designed to respond to the presence of cars and emergency situations, while ensuring safe and efficient traffic flow.
