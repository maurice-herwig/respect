

Specification name: TrafficE2

Environment variables:
- car: boolean
- emergency: boolean
- police: boolean

System variables:
- green: boolean

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment is assumed to adhere to the following constraints:
1.  The police signal is false infinitely often.
2.  Whenever a car is present and no emergency is active, the car remains present in the next step.
3.  The police signal may only become active in the next step if the police signal is already active, the car is currently absent, or the car becomes absent in the next step.
4.  The emergency signal may only become active in the next step if the police signal is active, the car is currently absent, or the car becomes absent in the next step.
5.  If the emergency signal is inactive, then either the police signal is active, the car is currently absent, or the car becomes absent in the next step.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
1.  **Response to Car:** The system must respond to the presence of a car. Specifically, if a car is present while the green light is off, the system must eventually turn the green light on. The system must ensure this response cycle completes infinitely often.
2.  **Car Absence:** The system guarantees that the car signal is false infinitely often.
