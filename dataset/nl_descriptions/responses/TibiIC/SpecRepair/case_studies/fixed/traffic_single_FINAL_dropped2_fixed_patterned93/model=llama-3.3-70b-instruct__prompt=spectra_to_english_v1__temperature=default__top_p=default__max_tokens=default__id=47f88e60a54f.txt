The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in a specific way: when the traffic light is red, the car will either not be present, be present and then move away, or an emergency situation will occur, allowing the car to remain. Additionally, when a car is not present, it will either remain absent or move away when the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system initially starts in a state S0. The system remains in state S0 as long as there is no car present or the car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to state S1. The system returns to state S0 when the traffic light turns green, indicating a response to the car's presence. The system must infinitely often return to state S0, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
