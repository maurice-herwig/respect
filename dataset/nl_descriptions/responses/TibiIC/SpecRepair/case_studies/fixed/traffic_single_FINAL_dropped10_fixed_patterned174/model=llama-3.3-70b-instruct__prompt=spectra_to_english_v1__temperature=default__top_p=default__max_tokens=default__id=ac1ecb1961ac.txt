The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will either not be moving or will stop moving.

The system must satisfy certain guarantees. The traffic light must turn green whenever a car is present, and this must happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system must ensure that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will stay green as long as the car is present. If the car is no longer present, the system may turn the light back to not green. The system will repeatedly return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
