The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment. The environment controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system controls the traffic light's green status.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that police will not be present infinitely often, and when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will remain present until either an emergency occurs or the car leaves. Additionally, when police are present or no car is present, the car will not move, but when the light is green and no police are present, the car is expected to move.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is present, and there will be periods where no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to a car's presence. The system then continuously checks for a car's presence and its own green status, updating its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it waits for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the light remains green while a car is present, the system stays in its initial state. If the light turns green while no car is present, the system also stays in its initial state, waiting for a car to arrive.
