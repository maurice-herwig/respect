The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, whenever the pump is on, the environment will either have low water or no methane.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, then either methane is present or the pump will be turned on in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is present, then the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the environment controls the high water and methane levels, and the system controls the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions, and it must guarantee certain responses to the environment's conditions, specifically regarding the pump's operation in relation to high water and methane presence.
