The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The system guarantees that when high water is present, the pump will be off initially, but will turn on at the next opportunity. 

Additionally, if methane is detected, the system guarantees that if high water is present, the pump will turn off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was on previously and is still on, the water level will decrease at the next opportunity. 

The environment is also assumed to maintain a consistent state regarding the presence of high water, either by keeping the water level low or by ensuring that methane is not present when the water level is low.

The system and environment variables are: 
- Environment-controlled inputs: high water and methane presence
- System-controlled output: pump operation
