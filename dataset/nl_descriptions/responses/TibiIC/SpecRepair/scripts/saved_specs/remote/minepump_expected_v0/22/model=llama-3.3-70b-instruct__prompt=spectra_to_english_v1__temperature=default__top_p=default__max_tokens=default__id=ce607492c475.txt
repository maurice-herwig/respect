The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system's pump is initially off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump is on, it will not cause the water to remain high indefinitely. Additionally, the environment is assumed to prevent high water and methane from being present indefinitely when there is no high water.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump either immediately or at the next opportunity. On the other hand, if methane is detected, the system will ensure the pump is off, either immediately or at the next opportunity.

In general, the system's pump operation is determined by the presence of high water and methane. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions, ensuring a safe and coordinated interaction between the system and its environment.
