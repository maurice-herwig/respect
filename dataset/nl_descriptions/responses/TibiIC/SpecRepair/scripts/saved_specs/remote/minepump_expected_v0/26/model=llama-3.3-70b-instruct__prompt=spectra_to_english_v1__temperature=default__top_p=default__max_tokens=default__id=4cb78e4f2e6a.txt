The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water level and methane presence, while the system controls the pump operation.

Initially, it is assumed that there is no high water and no methane present. Correspondingly, the system guarantees that the pump is initially off.

The system is designed to operate under certain assumptions about the environment. Specifically, it is assumed that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, it is assumed that if the pump is on, then either the water level is low or there is no methane present.

The system must satisfy certain guarantees. If the water level is high, then either there is methane present or the pump will be turned on in the next state. Furthermore, if there is methane present, then the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the Minepump system is required to manage the pump operation based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane presence, under the given assumptions and guarantees, to ensure safe and proper operation. The environment-controlled inputs are high water and methane presence, and the system-controlled output is the pump operation.
