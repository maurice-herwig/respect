The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump is on and still on at the next moment, the water level will decrease. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that if the pump is on, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane.

The system must guarantee that when high water is detected, it will turn on the pump, unless it is already off. If methane is detected, the system must ensure that either the methane persists or the pump is turned off at the next moment.

In general, the system's pump operation is determined by the environment's conditions: when high water is present, the pump should be on, and when methane is detected, the pump's operation depends on the persistence of the methane.
