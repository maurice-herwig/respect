The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in a way that when the pump is on and still on at the next moment, the water level will decrease. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that if the pump is on, then either there is no high water or there is no methane.

The system must ensure that when high water is present, it will turn on the pump at the next moment. Furthermore, if methane is detected, then the system must either turn off the pump at the next moment when high water is present, or ensure that the pump is off at the next moment.

In this system, high water and methane are the environment-controlled inputs, and the pump is the system-controlled output. The system's behavior is defined based on these inputs, with specific requirements for initial conditions, safety, and next-state relationships.
