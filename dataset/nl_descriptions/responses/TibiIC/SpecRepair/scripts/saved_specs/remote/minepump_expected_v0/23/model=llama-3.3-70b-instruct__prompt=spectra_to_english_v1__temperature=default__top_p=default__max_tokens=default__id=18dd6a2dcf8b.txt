The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump at the next opportunity. 

Additionally, if methane is detected, the system ensures that either high water is present, the pump is off, or the pump will be turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, it will ensure that there is no high water at the next moment. 

The environment also guarantees that if there is currently no high water, then either there will still be no high water or there will be no methane.

The system's pump and the environment's high water and methane levels are the key variables in this interaction, with the system aiming to safely manage the pump based on the environmental conditions.
