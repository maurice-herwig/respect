The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump is on and still on at the next moment, the water level will decrease. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that if the pump is on, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane present.

The system guarantees that if high water is detected, it will turn on the pump, but only after the high water condition is met. If methane is detected, the system will keep the pump off, even at the next moment.

In general, the system's pump operation is dependent on the environmental conditions of high water and methane, with the goal of safely managing the pump in response to these conditions.
