The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to start in a safe state, where there is no high water and no methane present. Initially, the pump is also off.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump if methane is also present or about to be present. Additionally, if methane is detected, the system will turn off the pump at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump has been on and continues to be on, the high water level will decrease at the next step. Furthermore, the environment is assumed to maintain a safe state where if there is no high water, then either there will continue to be no high water or there will be no methane present.

In summary, the system's primary goal is to manage the pump's operation in response to the environment's conditions, ensuring safe operation by avoiding the simultaneous presence of high water and methane, while the environment ensures that its conditions evolve in a way that supports the system's safe operation.
