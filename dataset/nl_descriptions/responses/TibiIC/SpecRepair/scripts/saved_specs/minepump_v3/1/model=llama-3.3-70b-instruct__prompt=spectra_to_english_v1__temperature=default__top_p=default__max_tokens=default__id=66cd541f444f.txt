The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is on and remains on, then the high water will be removed by the next state. Additionally, the environment is assumed to not introduce high water or methane when there is no high water.

The system must guarantee that if high water is present, it will continue to be present until the pump is turned on. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the system must turn off the pump in the next state.

In general, the environment's behavior is restricted such that it does not introduce high water when there is no high water, unless methane is present. The system's operation is guided by the need to respond to high water and methane conditions by controlling the pump appropriately.
