The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, whenever the pump is on, the environment is assumed to either have low water or no methane.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is detected, then either high water is present or the system will turn off the pump in the next state.

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's high water and methane conditions, with the system ensuring the pump's operation is appropriate given these conditions, and the environment ensuring its behavior is consistent with the system's operation.
