**Minepump System Requirements**

This reactive system controls a mine pump in response to two environment-controlled inputs: the detection of **high water** and the presence of **methane gas**.

**Inputs from the Environment:**
- **High water (`highwater`)** – A boolean signal indicating whether the water level in the mine has risen to a critical level.
- **Methane (`methane`)** – A boolean signal indicating whether dangerous methane gas has been detected.

**Output from the System:**
- **Pump (`pump`)** – A boolean signal that determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

**Initial Conditions:**
- At startup, the environment ensures that there is no high water (`highwater = false`) and no methane (`methane = false`).
- The system starts with the pump turned off (`pump = false`).

**Assumptions About the Environment:**
1. If the pump runs continuously for two consecutive time steps, the environment guarantees that high water will clear (`highwater = false`) in the next state.
2. The environment ensures that if there is no high water (`highwater = false`), then either high water will remain absent **or** methane will be absent in the next state (though the two need not occur simultaneously).

**System Guarantees:**
1. **Safety in High Water:**
   - If high water is detected (`highwater = true`), the system must either keep the pump off **or** turn it on in the next state. However, the pump cannot remain on immediately while high water is present.
2. **Methane Detection:**
   - If methane is detected (`methane = true`), the system must turn the pump off in the next state to prevent potential ignition risks.

The system must uphold these guarantees at all times, provided the environment adheres to its assumptions.
