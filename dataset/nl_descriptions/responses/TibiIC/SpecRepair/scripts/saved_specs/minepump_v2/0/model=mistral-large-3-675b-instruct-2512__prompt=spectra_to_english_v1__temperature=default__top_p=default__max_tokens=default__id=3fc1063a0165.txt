### **Minepump System Requirements**

This system controls a pump in an environment where water levels and methane gas pose safety risks.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment** provides two boolean inputs:
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether the water level is dangerously high.
  - `methane`: Indicates whether dangerous levels of methane gas are present.
- The **system** controls one boolean output:
  - `pump`: Determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Initial Conditions**
At system startup:
- The water level is not high (`highwater = false`).
- No methane is detected (`methane = false`).
- The pump is initially off (`pump = false`).

#### **Environment Assumptions**
The environment must behave as follows:
1. If the pump runs continuously for two consecutive time steps, the water level will no longer be high in the next step.
2. If the water level is not high, then either it remains not high, or methane is not present (i.e., both cannot become `true` simultaneously).

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must enforce the following rules at all times:
1. **Pump Activation Rule**: If the water level becomes high, the system must either:
   - Immediately detect methane (making pumping unsafe), **or**
   - Activate the pump in the next time step (if methane is not present).
2. **Pump Shutdown Rule**: If methane is detected, the pump must be turned off in the next time step.

The system must ensure these guarantees hold whenever the environment respects the stated assumptions.
