The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is initially off.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is on and remains on, the water level will decrease in the next state. Additionally, whenever the pump is on, the environment will either have no high water or no methane.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump the next time, unless methane is already present. If methane is present, the system guarantees that it will turn off the pump the next time.

In summary, the environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions, and it must guarantee specific responses to the environment's conditions, particularly regarding the presence of high water and methane.
