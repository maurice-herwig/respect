The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, the environment is assumed to maintain a low water level or remain free of methane.

The system must operate under the following rules: if high water is present, then the pump must be on either currently or in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the system must turn off the pump in the next state.

In summary, the environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions and must satisfy the specified guarantees regarding pump operation in response to high water and methane detection.
