

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

**Requirements Description**

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs indicating water levels and methane presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the environment signals that the water level is not high (`highwater` is false) and no methane is detected (`methane` is false). The system initializes with the pump turned off (`pump` is false).

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment is constrained by the following behaviors:
*   Whenever the pump was active in the previous step and remains active in the current step, the environment must ensure that the water level becomes not high (`highwater` is false) in the next step.
*   At all times, if the water level is not high (`highwater` is false), then either the water level is not high or methane is not detected (`methane` is false).

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
*   At all times, if the water level is high (`highwater` is true), the system must ensure that either the water level is high or the pump is activated (`pump` is true) in the next step.
*   At all times, if methane is detected (`methane` is true), the system must ensure that either the water level is high (`highwater` is true) or the pump is deactivated (`pump` is false) in the next step.
