

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs regarding water levels and methane presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the water level is not high and no methane is present. The system initializes with the pump off.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment behaves according to the following constraints:
1. If the pump was on in the previous step and remains on in the current step, the water level will not be high in the next step.
2. At all times, if the water level is not high, then either the water level is not high or methane is not present.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
1. At all times, if the water level is high, then either the pump is currently off or it will be on in the next step.
2. At all times, if methane is present, then either methane is present or the pump will be off in the next step.
