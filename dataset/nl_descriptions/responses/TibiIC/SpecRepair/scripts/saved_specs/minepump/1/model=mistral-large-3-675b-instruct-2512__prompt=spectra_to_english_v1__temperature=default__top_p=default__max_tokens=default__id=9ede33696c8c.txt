### **Minepump System Requirements**

This specification describes a reactive minepump system that interacts with its environment to manage water levels and prevent hazards in the presence of methane gas.

#### **Environment and System Variables**
- **Environment-controlled inputs:**
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether the water level has reached a critical high point.
  - `methane`: Indicates whether dangerous levels of methane are detected in the environment.

- **System-controlled output:**
  - `pump`: Controls whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Initial Conditions**
- At the start of operation:
  - The water level is low (`highwater = false`).
  - No methane is detected (`methane = false`).
  - The pump is initially turned off (`pump = false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The system relies on the following assumptions about the environment’s behavior:
1. **Effect of Pumping on Water Level:**
   If the pump runs continuously (i.e., `pump = true` at two consecutive time steps), the water level will eventually decrease (`highwater = false` in the next step).

2. **Safe Operating Conditions:**
   If the water level is low (`highwater = false`), then either the water remains low or no methane is present (`methane = false`).

#### **Guarantees Provided by the System**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
1. **Pump Activation for High Water:**
   If the water level becomes high (`highwater = true`), the system must either keep the pump off (which is unsafe) or, in the next step, activate the pump (`next(pump = true)`). *(Note: This guarantee appears trivial as currently stated, as it permits either inaction or activation without strict enforcement of pumping.)*

2. **Immediate Pump Shutdown on Methane Detection:**
   If methane is detected (`methane = true`), the system must turn the pump off (`pump = false`) in the next step to prevent ignition hazards.
