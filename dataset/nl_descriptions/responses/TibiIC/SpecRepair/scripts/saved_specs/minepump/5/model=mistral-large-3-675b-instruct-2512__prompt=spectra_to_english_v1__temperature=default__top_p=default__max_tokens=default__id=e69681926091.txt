### **Minepump System Requirements**

This system models the interaction between a mine environment and a pump controller. The environment provides two inputs: `highwater`, indicating whether water levels are critically high, and `methane`, indicating the presence of dangerous gas. The system controls a single output: `pump`, which determines whether the pump is active.

#### **Initial Conditions**
- At startup, the environment ensures that there is neither high water (`highwater = false`) nor methane (`methane = false`).
- The system ensures that the pump is initially off (`pump = false`).

#### **Environment Assumptions**
1. If the pump has been running continuously in two consecutive steps, the environment guarantees that the water level will decrease in the next step (`highwater = false`).
2. If the water level is not high (`highwater = false`), then at least one of the following holds in the current state:
   - The water level remains low (`highwater = false`).
   - There is no methane (`methane = false`).

#### **System Guarantees**
1. **Pump Control Based on Water Level:**
   Whenever the water level becomes high (`highwater = true`), the system ensures one of the following:
   - The pump is immediately turned off (`pump = false`).
   - The pump will be turned on in the very next step (`next(pump = true)`).

2. **Pump Control Based on Methane:**
   Whenever methane is detected (`methane = true`), the system guarantees:
   - Either methane remains present in the current state (`methane = true`), or
   - The pump will be turned off in the next step (`next(pump = false)`).
   *(Note: This guarantee simplifies to always ensuring that if methane is present, the pump will turn off in the next step, unless methane remains detected indefinitely.)*
