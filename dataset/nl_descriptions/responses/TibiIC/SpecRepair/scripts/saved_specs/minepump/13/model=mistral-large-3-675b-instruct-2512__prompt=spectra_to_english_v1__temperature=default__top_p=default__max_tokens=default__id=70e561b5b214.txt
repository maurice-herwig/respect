### **Minepump System Requirements**

This specification describes a **minepump system** that regulates water levels in an environment where methane may be present.

#### **Inputs (Environment-Controlled)**
- **High water (boolean):** Indicates whether the water level in the mine has reached a dangerous height.
- **Methane (boolean):** Indicates whether hazardous methane gas is detected in the mine.

#### **Outputs (System-Controlled)**
- **Pump (boolean):** Controls whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Initial Conditions**
- At startup, the water level is **not high** (`highwater = false`), and **no methane** is detected (`methane = false`).
- The pump must be **off** (`pump = false`) at initialization.

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
1. **Pump Effect on Water Level:**
   - If the pump remains **on** (`pump = true`) in two consecutive steps, the water level will eventually **decrease** (`next(highwater = false)`).
2. **Safe Pumping Conditions:**
   - The pump is **only active** when either **no methane is present** (`methane = false`) or **the water level is not high** (`highwater = false`).

#### **System Guarantees (Required Behavior)**
1. **Pump Activation in High Water:**
   - Whenever the water level becomes **high** (`highwater = true`), the pump **must eventually turn on** (`next(pump = true)`) **unless methane is present** (`methane = true`).
2. **Pump Deactivation in Methane Presence:**
   - If **methane is detected** (`methane = true`), the pump **must turn off in the next step** (`next(pump = false)`).
