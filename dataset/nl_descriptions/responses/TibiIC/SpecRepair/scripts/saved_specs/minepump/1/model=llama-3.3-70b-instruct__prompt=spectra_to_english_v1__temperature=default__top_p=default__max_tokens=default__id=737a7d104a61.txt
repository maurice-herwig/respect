The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system's pump is initially off.

The environment is expected to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, the environment is assumed to never introduce high water or methane without bound, specifically, if there is no high water, then either there will be no high water or no methane in the next state.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will ensure the pump is on in the next state. Furthermore, the system ensures that if methane is detected, the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions, and it must guarantee specific responses to the environment's conditions, particularly regarding the presence of high water and methane.
