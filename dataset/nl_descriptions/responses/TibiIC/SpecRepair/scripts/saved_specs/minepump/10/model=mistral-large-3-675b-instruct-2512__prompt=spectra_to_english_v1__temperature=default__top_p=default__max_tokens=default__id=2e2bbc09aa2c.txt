### **Minepump System Requirements**

This specification describes a **minepump system** that operates in an environment where water levels and methane gas levels pose hazards.

#### **Environment-Controlled Inputs**
- **High water (`highwater`)** – A signal indicating whether the water level is dangerously high (controlled by the environment).
- **Methane (`methane`)** – A signal indicating whether dangerous levels of methane gas are present (controlled by the environment).

#### **System-Controlled Output**
- **Pump (`pump`)** – A control signal determining whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Initial Conditions**
- The system **starts in a safe state**, where the water level is not high (`highwater = false`), no methane is detected (`methane = false`), and the pump is off (`pump = false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
1. If the pump runs continuously (`pump` remains `true` in consecutive steps), the environment ensures that the water level will eventually lower (`highwater` becomes `false` in the next step).
2. If the water level is low (`highwater = false`), then either it remains low **or** no methane is present (`methane = false`).

#### **Guarantees Enforced by the System**
1. **Water Handling**:
   - Whenever the water level becomes high (`highwater = true`), the system **must** either:
     - Keep the water level high in the next step, **or**
     - Activate the pump (`pump = true`) in the next step.
   - *(Note: This is a simplified safety condition; a more practical version would likely always require pump activation when water is high.)*

2. **Methane Safety**:
   - If methane is detected (`methane = true`), the system **must** either:
     - Keep methane levels dangerous in the next step, **or**
     - Shut off the pump (`pump = false`) in the next step.
   - *(Similarly, a stricter version would likely always require immediate pump deactivation on methane detection.)*

The system ensures that its responses to water and methane conditions are consistent with safety constraints, though the given guarantees allow some flexibility in how strictly the pump reacts.
