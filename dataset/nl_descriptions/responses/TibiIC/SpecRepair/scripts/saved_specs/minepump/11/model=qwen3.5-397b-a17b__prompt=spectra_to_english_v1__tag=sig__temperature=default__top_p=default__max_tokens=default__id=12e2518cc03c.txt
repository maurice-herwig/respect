

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs indicating water levels and methane presence. The variables `highwater` and `methane` are inputs controlled by the environment, while `pump` is an output controlled by the system.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the environment must indicate that the water level is not high and no methane is present. The system must initialize with the pump turned off.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment is assumed to adhere to the following behavior:
*   If the pump has been on for two consecutive steps (it was on in the previous step and remains on in the current step), the environment must ensure that the water level is not high in the next step.
*   It is always assumed that whenever the water level is not high, either the water level is not high or methane is not present.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements during operation:
*   Whenever the water level is high, either the pump is currently off, or it must be on in the next step.
*   Whenever methane is detected, the pump must be off in the next step.
