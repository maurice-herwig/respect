The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to be in a safe state, with no high water and no methane present. Correspondingly, the system starts with the pump turned off.

The system guarantees that whenever high water is present, it will turn on the pump at the next opportunity. Additionally, if methane is detected, the system ensures that if the water is not high, it will turn off the pump at the next opportunity when methane is present.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously on and is still on, the water level will decrease at the next step. Furthermore, the environment is expected to ensure that if the pump is on, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane present.

In summary, the Minepump system is designed to respond to its environment by controlling the pump based on the presence of high water and methane, while the environment is expected to follow certain rules regarding the effects of the pump operation on the water level and methane presence.
