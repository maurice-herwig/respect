The Minepump system is designed to operate in an environment where water levels and methane presence are monitored. The environment controls the water level, indicated by the highwater signal, and the presence of methane. The system controls the pump, which is used to manage the water level.

Initially, the environment is assumed to have normal water levels and no methane presence, and the system's pump is off.

The system is required to operate under the following conditions: if the water level is high, then either methane is present or the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity. Additionally, if methane is detected, the pump will be turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously on and is still on, the water level will be lowered at the next opportunity. Furthermore, if there is no methane, then either the water level is normal or it will remain normal.

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's water level and methane presence, with the goal of managing the water level while considering the presence of methane. The environment's behavior is restricted to ensure that the system can operate effectively.
