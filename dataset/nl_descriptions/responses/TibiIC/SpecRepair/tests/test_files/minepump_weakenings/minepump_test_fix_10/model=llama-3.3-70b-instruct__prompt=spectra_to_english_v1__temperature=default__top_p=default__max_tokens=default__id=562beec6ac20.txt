The Minepump system operates in an environment with two controlled inputs: high water level (highwater) and methane presence (methane). The system controls one output: the pump activation (pump).

The environment is assumed to initially be in a safe state, with no high water and no methane. Initially, the system's pump is also off.

The system guarantees that whenever the water level is high, it will turn on the pump at the next opportunity. Additionally, if methane is detected, the system ensures that either the methane remains present or the pump is turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously on and is still on, the water level will be low at the next moment. Furthermore, the environment is assumed to ensure that if there is no methane, then either the water level is low or there is still no methane.

In summary, the Minepump system is designed to respond to high water levels by activating the pump and to methane presence by potentially turning off the pump, under the assumption that the environment behaves in a specific way regarding water levels and methane presence.
