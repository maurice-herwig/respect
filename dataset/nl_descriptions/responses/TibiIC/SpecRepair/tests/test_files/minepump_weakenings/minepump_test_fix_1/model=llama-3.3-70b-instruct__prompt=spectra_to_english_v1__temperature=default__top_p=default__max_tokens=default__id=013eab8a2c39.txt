The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water level and methane presence, while the system controls the pump operation.

Initially, it is assumed that there is no high water and no methane present. Under these initial conditions, the system guarantees that the pump is not operating.

The system assumes that the environment will behave in certain ways. Specifically, if the pump was previously operating and is still operating, the environment will ensure that the water level will not be high in the next state. Additionally, the environment assumes that if the pump is operating, then either the water level is not high or there is no methane present.

The system guarantees certain behaviors in response to the environment. If the water level is high, the system will ensure that the pump will be operating in the next state, as long as the water level remains high. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the system guarantees that the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the Minepump system is designed to operate the pump based on the water level and methane presence in the environment, with specific guarantees about its behavior in response to these conditions, and assumptions about how the environment will behave. The environment-controlled inputs are the high water level and methane presence, and the system-controlled output is the pump operation.
