The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity. 

Additionally, the system ensures that if methane is detected, then either the methane remains present or the pump is turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and remains on, the high water condition will be resolved at the next opportunity.

It is also assumed that the environment will ensure that if the pump is on, then either there is no high water or there is no methane.

In summary, the system's purpose is to control the pump in response to the environment's high water and methane conditions, while the environment is expected to behave in a way that allows the system to effectively manage these conditions. The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions, and the system-controlled output is the pump operation.
