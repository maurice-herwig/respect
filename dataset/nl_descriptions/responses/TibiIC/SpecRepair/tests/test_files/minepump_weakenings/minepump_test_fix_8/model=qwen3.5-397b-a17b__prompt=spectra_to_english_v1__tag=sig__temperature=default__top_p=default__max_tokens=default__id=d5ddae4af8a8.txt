

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs regarding water levels and methane gas presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the water level is not high (`highwater` is false) and no methane is detected (`methane` is false). The system initializes with the pump off (`pump` is false).

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment adheres to the following constraints:
1. If the pump was on in the previous step and remains on in the current step, the water level must become not high (`highwater` becomes false) in the next step.
2. Whenever the pump is on, either the water level is not high (`highwater` is false) or methane is not present (`methane` is false).

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following behaviors:
1. If the water level is high (`highwater` is true), the system must turn the pump on (`pump` becomes true) in the next step.
2. If methane is detected (`methane` is true), the system must ensure that either the water level is high (`highwater` is true) or the pump is off (`pump` becomes false) in the next step.
