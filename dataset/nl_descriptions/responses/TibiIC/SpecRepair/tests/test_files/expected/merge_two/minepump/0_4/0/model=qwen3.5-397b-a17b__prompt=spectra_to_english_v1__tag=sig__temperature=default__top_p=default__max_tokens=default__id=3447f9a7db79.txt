

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs regarding water levels and methane presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the beginning of operation, the water level is not high and methane is not present. The system starts with the pump off.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment is constrained by the following requirements:
1. If the pump was on in the previous step and remains on in the current step, then in the next step, the water level must not be high.
2. At all times, either the water level is not high, methane is not present, or the pump is off.
3. Infinitely often, either the water level is not high or methane is not present.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following behavior:
1. Whenever the water level is high and either the pump is currently on or methane is not present, the system must turn the pump on in the next step.
2. Whenever methane is present, the system must turn the pump off in the next step.
