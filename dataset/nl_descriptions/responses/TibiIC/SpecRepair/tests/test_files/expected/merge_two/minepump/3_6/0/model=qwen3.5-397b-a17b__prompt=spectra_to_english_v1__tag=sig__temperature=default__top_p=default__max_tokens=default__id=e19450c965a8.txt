

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

This specification describes a reactive system that controls a pump based on environmental inputs regarding water levels and methane presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the environment indicates that the water level is not high and methane is not present. Simultaneously, the system ensures the pump is off.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements regarding the pump control:
1. Whenever the high water level signal is true, the pump must be turned on in the next step.
2. Whenever methane is present and the water level is not high, the pump must be turned off in the next step.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment behavior is restricted by the following constraints:
1. If the pump was on in the previous step and remains on in the current step, the high water level signal must become false in the next step.
2. At any time the pump is on, either the high water level signal is false or the methane signal is false.
3. Infinitely often, either the high water level signal is false or the methane signal is false.
