

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

**Requirements Description**

The Minepump system controls a pump based on environmental inputs indicating water levels and methane presence.

**Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation, the environment indicates that the water level is not high (`highwater` is false) and methane is not present (`methane` is false). The system initializes with the pump off (`pump` is false).

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment behaves according to the following constraints:
1. If the pump has been on continuously from the previous step into the current step, the environment ensures that the water level will be low (`highwater` is false) in the next step.
2. At all times, at least one of the following conditions holds: the water level is low (`highwater` is false), methane is absent (`methane` is false), or the pump is off (`pump` is false).
3. Infinitely often, either the water level is low (`highwater` is false) or methane is absent (`methane` is false).

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:
1. Whenever the water level is high (`highwater` is true), the system must turn the pump on (`pump` is true) in the next step.
2. Whenever methane is present (`methane` is true), the system ensures that either the pump will be off (`pump` is false) in the next step, or the pump is currently off (`pump` is false) while the water level is high (`highwater` is true).
