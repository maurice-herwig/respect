

Specification name: Minepump

Environment variables:
- highwater: boolean
- methane: boolean

System variables:
- pump: boolean

The Minepump system manages a pump based on water levels and methane presence. Initially, the environment indicates that the water level is not high and there is no methane, while the system ensures the pump is off.

The system must satisfy the following guarantees: whenever the water level is high, the pump must be on in the next step. If methane is present and the water level is not high, the pump must be off in the next step.

The environment is subject to the following assumptions: if the pump is on in the current step and was also on in the previous step, the water level must be not high in the subsequent step. Whenever the pump is on, either the water level must not be high or there must be no methane. Additionally, infinitely often, either the water level is not high or there is no methane.
