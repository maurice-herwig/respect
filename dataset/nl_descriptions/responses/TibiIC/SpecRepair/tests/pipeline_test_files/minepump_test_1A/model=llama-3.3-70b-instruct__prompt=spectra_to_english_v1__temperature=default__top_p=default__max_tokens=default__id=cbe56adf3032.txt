The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system's pump is off.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will decrease in the next state. Additionally, if the pump is currently on and off at the same time (which is not possible in a normal scenario but is considered for the sake of assumption), then either the water level or the methane level will decrease.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: if high water is present, the system will keep the pump on as long as the high water persists. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the system will turn off the pump in the next state.

In summary, the environment controls the high water and methane levels, and the system controls the pump operation. The system's goal is to manage the pump based on the environmental conditions, ensuring safe operation by avoiding pumping when methane is present and continuing to pump when high water is present.
