The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that whenever the pump is turned on and then off, or if it was previously off, the environment will ensure that the next state will not have high water if the pump was previously on and is now off, or if the pump remains off.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step, or there must not be high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In summary, the system must manage the pump operation based on the presence of methane and high water, under the assumption that the environment behaves in a specific way when the pump is turned on and off.
