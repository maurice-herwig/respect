### **Minepump System Requirements**

The *Minepump* system monitors and controls a pump in response to environmental conditions, specifically water levels and methane presence.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment** provides two inputs:
  - *Highwater*: A signal indicating whether water has reached a critical level.
  - *Methane*: A signal indicating whether methane is detected in the environment.
- The **system** controls one output:
  - *Pump*: A signal that activates or deactivates the pump.

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must behave as follows:
- Whenever the *highwater* signal fluctuates between *false* and *true*, the next state must either return *highwater* to *false* **or** the pump must have been off in the immediately preceding state. This ensures that rapid oscillations in water level do not occur without the pump having a chance to respond.

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure the following behavior under all conditions where the environment adheres to its assumptions:

1. **Methane Safety Guarantee**
   - If methane is detected (*methane = true*), the pump must be activated (*pump = true*) in the next state.

2. **Methane Clearance Guarantee**
   - If no methane is detected (*methane = false*), the pump must be deactivated (*pump = false*) in the next state.

The system does not impose any explicit initial conditions or liveness requirements beyond these safety guarantees.
