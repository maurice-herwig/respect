### **Minepump System Requirements**

The **Minepump** is a reactive system that manages water levels in a mine while accounting for the presence of methane gas. The system interacts with its environment through two inputs and one output:

- **Environment-controlled inputs:**
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether the water level in the mine is dangerously high (true) or safe (false).
  - `methane`: Indicates whether methane gas is detected in the mine (true) or not (false).

- **System-controlled output:**
  - `pump`: Controls whether the water pump is active (true) or inactive (false).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must adhere to the following constraint:
- If the pump is turned off (`pump=false`) and then turned on (`pump=true`) in the next step, the environment must ensure that the water level does not remain high (`highwater=false`) in the following state. Alternatively, in the immediately preceding state, the pump must have already been off (`PREV(pump=false)`).

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following behavioral requirements:

1. **Safety Guarantee (Methane or Pump Control):**
   At all times, at least one of the following must hold:
   - Methane is detected (`methane=true`).
   - The pump will be activated in the next state (`next(pump=true)`).
   - The water level is not high (`highwater=false`).

2. **Safety Guarantee (Pump Deactivation in Safe Conditions):**
   If methane is not detected (`methane=false`), the pump must be deactivated (`pump=false`) in the next state.

The system has no explicitly stated initial conditions or liveness requirements beyond these guarantees. Its behavior is strictly defined by the given constraints on transitions and environmental inputs.
