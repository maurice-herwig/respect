The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is initially off and then turned on, the water will not be high in the next state, unless the pump was already on and the water was high.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, the pump must be turned on in the next state, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if there is no methane, the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
