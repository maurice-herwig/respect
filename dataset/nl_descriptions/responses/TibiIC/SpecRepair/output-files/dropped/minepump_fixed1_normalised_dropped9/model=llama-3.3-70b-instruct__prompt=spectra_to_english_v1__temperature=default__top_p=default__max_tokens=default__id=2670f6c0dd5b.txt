The Minepump system operates in an environment where high water levels and methane presence are monitored. The environment controls the detection of high water and methane, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that it will not increase the water level if the pump is not activated. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, or the pump will be activated at the next step, or the water level is not high, then these conditions must always hold true. Secondly, if methane is not detected, then the pump must not be activated at the next step. 

In summary, the system ensures safe operation by regulating the pump based on environmental conditions, while the environment ensures that water levels do not increase without pump activation.
