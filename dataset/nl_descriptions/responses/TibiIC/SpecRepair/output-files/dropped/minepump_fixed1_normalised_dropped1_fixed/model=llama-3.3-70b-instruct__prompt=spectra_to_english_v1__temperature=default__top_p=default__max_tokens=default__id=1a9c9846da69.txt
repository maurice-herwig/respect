The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the levels of water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating, then the water level will not remain high in the next state, unless the pump remains off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump is turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if no methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump is turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system-controlled pump must respond to the environment-controlled water and methane levels according to these rules, with the environment following its assumed behavior regarding water levels when the pump is off.
