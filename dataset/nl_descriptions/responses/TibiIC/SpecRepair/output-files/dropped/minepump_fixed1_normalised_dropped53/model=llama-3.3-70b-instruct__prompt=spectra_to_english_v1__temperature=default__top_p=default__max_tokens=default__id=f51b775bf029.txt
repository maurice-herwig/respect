The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. In response, the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating in the current state, then the high water condition will not be present in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be operating in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be operating in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly dependent on the presence or absence of methane in the environment, under the assumption that the environment's high water condition is related to the system's previous pump operation.
