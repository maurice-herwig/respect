The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the `highwater` and `methane` conditions. The system controls the `pump` output. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water is not high in the previous state, then it will not be high in the next state, unless the pump was on in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, or the pump will be on in the next state, or the water is not high, then this condition must always hold. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be on in the next state.

In other words, the system ensures that the pump's state in the next moment is determined by the current presence of methane and the water level, while the environment's behavior regarding the water level is constrained by the pump's previous state.
