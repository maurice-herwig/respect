The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not operating, then the next state will either have no high water or the pump will still be off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must turn on at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must turn off at the next step.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
