The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump's operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not keep the water level high indefinitely if the pump is not operating, meaning that if the pump is not running, the water level will eventually drop.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

These conditions must always hold, ensuring the system's safe operation in response to the environment's conditions.
