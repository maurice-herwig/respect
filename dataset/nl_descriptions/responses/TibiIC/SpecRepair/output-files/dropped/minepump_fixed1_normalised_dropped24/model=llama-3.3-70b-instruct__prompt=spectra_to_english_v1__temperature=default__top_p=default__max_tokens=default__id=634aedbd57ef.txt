The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. In response, the system controls a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that the pump is not activated initially or remains deactivated until it is activated by the system. 

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state.

In summary, the system ensures safe operation of the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane presence, under the assumption that the pump's initial state or previous state is deactivated when not explicitly activated by the system.
