### **Minepump System Requirements**

This system controls a pump in a mining environment, interacting with sensor inputs from its surroundings.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment** provides two inputs:
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether water levels have risen to a critical level.
  - `methane`: Indicates whether dangerous methane gas has been detected.
- The **system** controls a single output:
  - `pump`: Determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment is assumed to never activate the pump directly—meaning `pump` remains `false` at all times from the environment's perspective.

#### **System Guarantees**
Under the given assumptions, the system must ensure the following:

1. **Safety Guarantee (Guarantee 1.1):**
   The pump must never run if either:
   - Methane is present (`methane = true`), **or**
   - Water levels never reach a critical level (`highwater = false`).

2. **Safety Guarantee (Guarantee 2.1):**
   If methane is ever detected (`methane = true`), the pump must **immediately** turn off in the next state (`!next(pump)`)—meaning it cannot remain active in the subsequent step. If no methane is present, the pump may either stay on or turn off, with no restrictions beyond the first guarantee.
