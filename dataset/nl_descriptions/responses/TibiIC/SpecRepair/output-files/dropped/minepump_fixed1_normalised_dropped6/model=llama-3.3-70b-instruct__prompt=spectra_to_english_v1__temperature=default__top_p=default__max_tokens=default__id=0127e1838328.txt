The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that it will not cause the water level to rise if the pump is not operating.

In other words, the system must ensure that the pump is operated appropriately based on the presence of methane and the water level, while the environment ensures that the water level does not rise unnecessarily when the pump is off. 

The system's operation is constrained by the environment's behavior, specifically the levels of water and methane, which are the environment-controlled inputs, and the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. 

At all times, the system must adhere to these rules to ensure safe and correct operation, given the environment's constraints.
