The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A (cara), the presence of car B (carb), and emergency conditions (emergency). The system-controlled outputs are the traffic lights for car A (greena) and car B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, car A will not start moving if the traffic light is red, unless the emergency condition persists or the traffic light turns green. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, car B will not start moving if the traffic light is red, unless the emergency condition persists, the traffic light turns green, or car B is already present. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, car A will move if the traffic light is green, unless the emergency condition persists or car A is not present. Car B will also move if the traffic light is green, unless car B is not present or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that if car A is present, the traffic light for car A will eventually turn green, and if car B is present, the traffic light for car B will eventually turn green. The system ensures this by maintaining a state machine that responds to the presence of cars A and B. Initially, the system is in a state where it expects no cars to be present. If a car is present and its corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars A and B.
