The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars A and B are assumed to behave in specific ways: car A will not move when the light is red, and car B will also not move when the light is red. Furthermore, when the emergency light is on and the traffic light is green, car A is assumed to move, and car B is also assumed to move.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light green for that car, and similarly for intersection B. The system also guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the light green for that car, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions about car behavior during emergencies. The system will continue to cycle through states, turning the lights green for cars that are present and turning them red when an emergency occurs or when a car is not present. Overall, the system is designed to safely and efficiently manage traffic flow in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
