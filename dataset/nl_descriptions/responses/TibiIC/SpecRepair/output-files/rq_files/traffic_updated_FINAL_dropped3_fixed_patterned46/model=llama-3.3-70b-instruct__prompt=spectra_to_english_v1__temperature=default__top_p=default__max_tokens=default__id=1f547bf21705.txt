The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is present, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when an emergency is present, if the traffic light for road B is red, then an emergency is present; when an emergency is not present, if there is a car on road A and the traffic light for road A is green, then the car on road A will move and not be present in the next state, or the traffic light for road A will turn red; similarly, when an emergency is not present, if there is a car on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, then the car on road B will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: at all times, at least one of the traffic lights for roads A and B is red; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road A green; when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green; once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state, ready to respond to the next car on the road. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars on the roads in a timely manner.
