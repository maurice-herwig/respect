The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, and when a car is present and its light is red while the other car's light is green, the car remains present in the next time step. Additionally, when a car is present and its light is green, the car moves and is no longer present in the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system eventually responds by turning its green light on. Similarly, when car B is present, the system eventually responds by turning its green light on. If the emergency signal is active, both green lights are turned off.

The system's response to a car's presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system eventually turns the corresponding green light on when the car is present. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states: one where the car's presence does not require an immediate response, and one where the car's presence requires a response. The system remains in the first state as long as the car is not present or its green light is on, and transitions to the second state when the car is present and its green light is off. The system returns to the first state when the car's green light is turned on. The system always eventually returns to the first state, ensuring that the car's presence is eventually responded to.
