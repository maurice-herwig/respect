The Minepump system interacts with an environment that provides two input signals: `highwater` and `methane`, indicating the presence of high water and methane, respectively. The system controls a single output, `pump`, which determines whether the pump is active or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when it signals that the water is not high, it will continue to signal that the water is not high at the next step, unless the pump is inactive.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be active at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be inactive at the next step.

In summary, the system must ensure that the pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and high water, while the environment is expected to provide consistent signals about the water level when the pump is not running.
