The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A (`cara`), the presence of car B (`carb`), and an emergency signal (`emergency`). The system-controlled outputs are the green light signals for car A (`greena`) and car B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the emergency signal is not always on, and it will eventually be turned off. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car A will not move unless there is an emergency or it is already moving. The environment also ensures that if there is an emergency, car B will either stop moving or the green light for car B will be turned on. Furthermore, when the green light is on, car A will move, and car B will move unless there is an emergency or the green light for car B is off.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights will never both be red at the same time unless there is an emergency. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. When car B is present, the system will eventually turn on either the green light for car A or the green light for car B. If there is an emergency, the system will turn off both green lights. 

The system also follows specific rules. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives and the corresponding green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to turn on. Once the green light turns on, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state. 

Overall, the TrafficE2 system is designed to safely and efficiently manage traffic flow in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
