The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal, represented by `emergency`. The system-controlled outputs are the states of the traffic lights for cars A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning that there are periods where `emergency` is false. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency, and car B is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is green. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green, car A is assumed to move, and car B is assumed to move unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is not green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red when there is no emergency. When car A is present, the system eventually responds by setting the traffic light for car A to green. When car B is present, the system eventually responds by setting either the traffic light for car A or car B to green. During an emergency, both traffic lights are red. 

The system also ensures that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars in a specific way. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system transitions back to the initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
