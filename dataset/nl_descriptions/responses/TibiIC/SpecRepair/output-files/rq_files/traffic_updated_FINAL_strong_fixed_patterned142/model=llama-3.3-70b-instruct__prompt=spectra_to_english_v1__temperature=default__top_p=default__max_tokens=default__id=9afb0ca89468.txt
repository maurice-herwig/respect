The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light is red for A but green for B, car A will remain idle and not move. Similarly, when car B is present and the light is red for B but green for A, car B will remain idle and not move. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and no longer be present. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of a pattern of response to stimuli. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any stimulus. If a stimulus, such as the presence of a car, occurs and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This pattern of response ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its light green.
