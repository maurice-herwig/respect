The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning there will be times when it is not emergency; when car A is present and the light is red for A but green for B, car A will remain present in the next state; similarly, when car B is present and the light is red for B but green for A, car B will remain present in the next state; when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state; and when car B is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for A and B are never both red at the same time; when car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green; when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green; and when the emergency signal is active, both lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when a car is present. This pattern works by tracking the system's state and ensuring that when a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will turn the light green, and once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
