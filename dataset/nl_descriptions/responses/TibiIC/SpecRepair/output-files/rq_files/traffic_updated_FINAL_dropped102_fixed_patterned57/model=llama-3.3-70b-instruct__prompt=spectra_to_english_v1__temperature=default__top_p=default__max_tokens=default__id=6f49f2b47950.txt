The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, it will not activate cara and carb simultaneously in the next state, and if either cara or carb is inactive, it will remain inactive or become active in the next state. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, the environment will ensure that if the light is green for either road, the corresponding car signal will become inactive in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when the emergency signal is inactive, the light will not be green for road B. The system also guarantees that if a car is present on road A and the light is not green for road B, the light will become green for road A. Similarly, if a car is present on road B and the light is green for road B, the system will maintain this state. Furthermore, when the emergency signal is inactive, the system ensures that both lights are not red simultaneously.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to car presence signals. The system's response to car presence signals is defined by a pattern where it remains in a state as long as the car presence signal is inactive or the corresponding light is green. If the car presence signal becomes active and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the light to become green. Once the light becomes green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence signal. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to this initial state, allowing it to continuously respond to car presence signals.
