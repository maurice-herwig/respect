The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, and when the light is red for car A, car A is idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the light is red for car B, car B is idle unless there is an emergency. The environment also ensures that when the light is green, cars A and B move.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red when there is no emergency, and that if car A is present, the light will eventually turn green for car A. If car B is present, the light will eventually turn green for either car A or car B. When an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is such that when a car is present, the corresponding light will turn green, and when the light is green, the car will move. The system will always eventually return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. 

In the event of an emergency, the system prioritizes safety by ensuring both lights are red. The system's behavior is designed to balance the needs of cars A and B while responding to emergencies and ensuring safe traffic flow.
