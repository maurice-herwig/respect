The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the levels of high water and methane. The system must ensure that if methane is present, the pump will be activated at the next opportunity, unless the water level is not high. Additionally, if there is no methane, the system must ensure the pump is deactivated at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane becomes present and the pump was previously off, then the water level will not be high at the next moment.

Initially, there are no specified conditions. At all times, the system must adhere to the guarantees regarding pump activation and deactivation based on methane presence and water level. The system's behavior is solely defined by its response to the environment's conditions, specifically the presence of methane and the water level, with the goal of safely operating the pump.
