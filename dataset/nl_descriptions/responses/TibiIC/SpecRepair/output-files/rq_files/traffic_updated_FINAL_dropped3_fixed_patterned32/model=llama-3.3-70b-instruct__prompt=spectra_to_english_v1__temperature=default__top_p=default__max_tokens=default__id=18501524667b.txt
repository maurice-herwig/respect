The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, indicated by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if the light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when an emergency is in effect, the emergency condition persists or the light for road B is green; when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red at the next step; and when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road B green.

More specifically, the system's response to a car on either road A or B can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to a car. If a car is present and the corresponding light is green, the system remains in this ready state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the ready state. The system will infinitely often be in the ready state.
