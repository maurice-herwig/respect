The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic lights. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for both cars A and B.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A, the presence of car B, and emergency situations. The system-controlled outputs are the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and car A is idle when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency. Additionally, car B is idle when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is green. When the traffic light is green, car A moves, and car B moves unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is red.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. When there is no emergency, the traffic lights for cars A and B are not both red. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the green light for car A on. If car B is present, the system will eventually turn either the green light for car A or car B on. When there is an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning the corresponding green lights on. The system ensures that if a car is present, it will eventually turn the green light on, and if the green light is on, the car will move. The system also ensures that it will return to the waiting state after a car has passed. 

In all cases, the system prioritizes emergency situations by turning both traffic lights red when an emergency occurs.
