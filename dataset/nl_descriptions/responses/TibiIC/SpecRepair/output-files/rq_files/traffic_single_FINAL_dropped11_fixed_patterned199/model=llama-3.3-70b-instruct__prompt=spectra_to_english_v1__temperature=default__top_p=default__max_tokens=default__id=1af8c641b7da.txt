The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions frequently, and when a car is present, it should either be an emergency, the traffic light should be green, or the car should not be moving towards the traffic light. Additionally, when the car is not moving, it should be because either there is a police intervention, the car is not present, or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light turns green, unless the car is no longer present. The system's response to the car's presence is modeled as a state machine with two states: one where the system is waiting for the car to arrive or the traffic light to turn green, and another where the system is waiting for the traffic light to turn green after a car has arrived. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state.

Initially, the system starts in the waiting state. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to this waiting state, and it will do so infinitely often. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and it will remain green until the car is no longer present or another condition is met. The system's behavior is designed to ensure that cars are allowed to pass through the traffic light safely and efficiently.
