The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to be present or waiting for the corresponding green light to be turned on. If a car is present and its green light is on, the system will remain in the waiting state. If a car is present and its green light is off, the system will move to the state where it waits for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system will return to the waiting state. The system will infinitely often be in the waiting state.
