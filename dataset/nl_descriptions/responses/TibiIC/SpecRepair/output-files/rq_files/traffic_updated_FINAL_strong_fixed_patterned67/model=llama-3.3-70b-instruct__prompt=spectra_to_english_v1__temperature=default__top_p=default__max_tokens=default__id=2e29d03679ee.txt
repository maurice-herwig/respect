The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. The environment also ensures that when a car is present and its light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are not both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when the car is present. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or its light is already green. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. The system returns to the initial state when the light turns green. The system is always eventually in the initial state, ensuring that it will respond to the car's presence.
