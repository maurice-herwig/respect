The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that the emergency signal is usually false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if a car is present at intersection A, it will either remain present, the emergency situation will persist, the traffic light at intersection A will be green, or the car will not be present. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B.

The environment also ensures that when the emergency signal is true and the traffic light at an intersection is red, a car will not move to that intersection. Conversely, when the emergency signal is true and the traffic light at an intersection is green, a car will move from that intersection.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. If a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. A similar guarantee is made for intersection B. When an emergency situation occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the traffic light turns green when a car is present. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car. The system remains in this state until the traffic light turns green, at which point it returns to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green.
