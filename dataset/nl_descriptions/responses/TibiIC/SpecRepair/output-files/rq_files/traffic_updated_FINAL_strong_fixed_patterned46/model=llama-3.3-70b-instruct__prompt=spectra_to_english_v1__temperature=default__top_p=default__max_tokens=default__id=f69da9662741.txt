The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected to be in a non-emergency state. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the environment will keep the car present until the light turns green, unless an emergency occurs for car A. When a car's light is green, the environment will move the car to a not-present state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn a car's light green when the car is present, and will keep the light green until the car is no longer present. This pattern is followed for both cars A and B.
