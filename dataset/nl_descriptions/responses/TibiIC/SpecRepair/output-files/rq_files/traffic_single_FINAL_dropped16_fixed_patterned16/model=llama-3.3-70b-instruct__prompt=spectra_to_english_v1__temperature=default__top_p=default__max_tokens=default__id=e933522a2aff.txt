The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: when the traffic light is not green, the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, when the police are present, the car will not move unless the traffic light is green, in which case the car will stop moving at the next opportunity.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present. This guarantee is fulfilled by the system responding to the presence of a car in a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the car's presence. If a car is present and the light is green, or if no car is present, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the car's presence again. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to this initial responsive state.

The traffic light starts in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. The system ensures that the traffic light turns green infinitely often when a car is present, allowing the car to pass through the intersection.
