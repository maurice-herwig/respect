The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, indicated by greena and greenb.

The environment-controlled inputs are cara (car A is present), carb (car B is present), and emergency (an emergency situation is occurring). The system-controlled outputs are greena (the traffic light for car A is green) and greenb (the traffic light for car B is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation is occurring, if car A is not present, it will not become present at the next time step unless the traffic light for car A is green or the emergency situation is still occurring. Similarly, when an emergency situation is occurring, if car B is not present, it will not become present at the next time step unless the traffic light for car B is green, the emergency situation is still occurring, or car B was previously present. Additionally, when an emergency situation is occurring, if car A is present, it will move (i.e., become absent) at the next time step unless the emergency situation is still occurring or the traffic light for car A is not green. Similarly, when an emergency situation is occurring, if car B is present, it will move (i.e., become absent) at the next time step unless the traffic light for car B is not green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when car A is present, the system will eventually respond by setting the traffic light for car A to green, and when car B is present, the system will eventually respond by setting the traffic light for car B to green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by setting the corresponding traffic light to green, unless the car is no longer present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a safety requirement that ensures the system will always be in one of two states: a state where the system is ready to respond to the presence of a car, or a state where the system is waiting for the car to be served (i.e., for the corresponding traffic light to be set to green). The system will always eventually return to the ready state.
