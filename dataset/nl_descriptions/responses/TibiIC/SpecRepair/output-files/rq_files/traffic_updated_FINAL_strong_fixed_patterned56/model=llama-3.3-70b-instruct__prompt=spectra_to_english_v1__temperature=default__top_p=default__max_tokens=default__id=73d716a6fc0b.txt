The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have emergency conditions frequently, meaning that it will eventually always be in a non-emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light for car A is red while the light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the light for car B is red while the light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both lights red at the same time, ensuring that at least one of the lights is always green. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for car A green. The same guarantee applies to car B. Moreover, during an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when a car is present. This pattern involves an internal state machine that tracks whether the system is currently responding to the presence of a car or not. The system will always eventually return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's safety conditions ensure that it will always maintain a consistent state and respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. Over time, the system will infinitely often be in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
