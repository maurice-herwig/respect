The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions often, meaning that there will be times when there are no police interventions. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green (i.e., red), the car should either be idle or there should be an emergency situation, or the car should not be present. Furthermore, when the car is moving and the traffic light turns green, the car should continue moving or come to a stop.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light will turn green at some point.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system's behavior is constrained by safety requirements that dictate how it should respond to the car's presence and the traffic light's state. Specifically, when the system is not responding to the car's presence and the car is present but the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. When the system is responding to the car's presence and the traffic light is green, it will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence.
