The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and there should be periods where the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car should not be present unless there is an emergency or the car was already present. If there was an emergency in the previous moment, the car should not be moving unless there is a police presence or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green, unless the car is no longer present. The system's response to the car's presence can be in one of two states: either it is immediately responsive, or it becomes responsive after a car has been present while the light was not green. In the latter case, the system will turn the traffic light green once it becomes responsive, and then return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, where it is immediately responsive to the presence of a car.

Initially, the system is in its initial state, where it is immediately responsive to the presence of a car. The system's state is updated based on the presence of the car and the traffic light's state, ensuring that it always eventually returns to its initial state.
