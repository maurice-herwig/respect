The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the police are not present, the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the light is green. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will not move when the light is green, or if the car is already moving, it will stop.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light turns green when a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
