The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever there is high water, the pump is not operating. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, if the pump is to be turned on at the next step, then there must be no methane present.

In other words, the system ensures that the pump's operation is dependent on the presence of methane and high water, while the environment ensures that the pump is off when there is high water. The system's guarantees are designed to prevent the pump from operating under certain conditions, prioritizing safety in the presence of methane and high water.
