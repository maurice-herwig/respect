The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually cease. Additionally, when a car is present at a red light, it will remain idle until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present at a green light, it will move and its presence will no longer be detected at the next instant. However, car B will only move when its light is green and car A's light is also green.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B will never both be red at the same time. When a car is present, its corresponding green light will eventually turn on. If an emergency occurs, both green lights will turn off. The system also ensures that if an emergency is in effect, both green lights will remain off.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is such that it will eventually turn on the corresponding green light when a car is detected, and it will keep the light on until the car moves. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ready to respond to new car detections.
