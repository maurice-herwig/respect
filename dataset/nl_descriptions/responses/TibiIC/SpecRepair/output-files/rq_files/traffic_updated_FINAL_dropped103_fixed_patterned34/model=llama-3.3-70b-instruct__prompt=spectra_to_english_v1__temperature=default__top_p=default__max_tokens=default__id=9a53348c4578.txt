The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the occurrence of emergencies, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars on road A are assumed to either not be present, become present at the next time step, or the light for road A is green. Similarly, for road B, cars are assumed to either not be present or become present at the next time step when an emergency occurs and the light for road B is green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light for a road is green, cars on that road are assumed to either not move, move at the next time step, or the light turns red.

The system guarantees several behaviors. Firstly, it ensures that both traffic lights are never red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system eventually responds by turning the light for road A green. The same response is guaranteed for road B when a car is present on it. During emergencies, the system ensures that if the emergency condition is false, then at least one of the lights is green, but if an emergency is true, both lights must be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system eventually turns the corresponding light green when a car is present. This pattern involves the system tracking its state (whether it is currently responding to a car's presence or not) and updating this state based on the presence of cars and the current state of the lights. The system always eventually returns to a state where it is ready to respond to new cars.
