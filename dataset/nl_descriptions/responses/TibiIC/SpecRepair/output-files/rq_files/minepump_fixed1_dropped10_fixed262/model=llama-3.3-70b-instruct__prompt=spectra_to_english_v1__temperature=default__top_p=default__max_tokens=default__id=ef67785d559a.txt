The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump's operation.

It is assumed that when the water is high, the pump will be off.

The system must guarantee two conditions: 
- If methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity.
- If methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump's operation is determined by the presence or absence of methane, while also respecting the constraint imposed by high water levels.
