The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. When car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. When car A's light is green, car A will move and its presence will be removed. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also ensures that when car A is present, its light will eventually turn green, and when car B is present, its light will eventually turn green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must respond to the car's presence. Once the car's light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
