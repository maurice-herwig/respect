The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the police are about to arrive, the car should either not be present or should not be moving. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if the police are present, the car is either not moving or will not move, or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light will turn green at some point.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present, the system remains in this ready state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the ready state. If the traffic light does not turn green while the system is in the waiting state, it remains in that state.

The system is designed to periodically return to the ready state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of a car. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
