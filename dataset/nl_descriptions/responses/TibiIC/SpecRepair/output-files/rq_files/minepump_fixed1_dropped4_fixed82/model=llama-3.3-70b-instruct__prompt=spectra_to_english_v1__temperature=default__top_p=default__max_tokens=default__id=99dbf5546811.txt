The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment determines the levels of high water and methane. The system must ensure that if methane is present, then either the pump will be activated in the next state or there is no high water. Additionally, the system must always ensure that methane is not present.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when there is high water, the pump is not activated. Furthermore, when methane is present, the pump is not activated.

In summary, the system's output is the pump's state, and the environment's inputs are the high water and methane levels. The system guarantees certain conditions based on these inputs, under the assumptions that the environment will not activate the pump when there is high water or methane present.
