The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and no longer be present at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency occurs, both lights will be red. 

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
