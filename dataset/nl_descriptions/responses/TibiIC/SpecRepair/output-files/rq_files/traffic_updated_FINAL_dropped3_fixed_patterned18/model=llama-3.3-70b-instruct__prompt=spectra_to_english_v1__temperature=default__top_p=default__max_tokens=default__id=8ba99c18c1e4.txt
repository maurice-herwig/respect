The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and the emergency signal (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road B is red, then the emergency signal must still be active; when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next step; and when the emergency signal is not active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road, the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green, and once the light is green, it will stay green until the car is gone or the system decides to turn it red; if a car is present and the light is not green, the system will turn the light green at some point in the future. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
