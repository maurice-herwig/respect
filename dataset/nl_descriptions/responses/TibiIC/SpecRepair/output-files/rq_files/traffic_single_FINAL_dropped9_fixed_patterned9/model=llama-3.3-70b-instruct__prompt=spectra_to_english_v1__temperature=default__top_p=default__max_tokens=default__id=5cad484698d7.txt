The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have police presence infinitely often, and when the police are present, the car will not start moving when the traffic light is red. Additionally, when the police are present or there is no car, or the car will not move next, or the traffic light is not green, the car will move.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car or the traffic light is green when there is a car. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car. 

The system's safety requirements ensure that it will only transition between its states based on the presence of a car and the state of the traffic light, and it will always eventually return to its initial state.
