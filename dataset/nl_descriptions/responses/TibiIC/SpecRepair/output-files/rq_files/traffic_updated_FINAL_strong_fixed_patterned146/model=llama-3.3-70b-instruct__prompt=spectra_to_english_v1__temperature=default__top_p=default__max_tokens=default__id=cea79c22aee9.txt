The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green and car A is present, car A will move and no longer be present. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

In general, the system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding light green, while ensuring that the lights are never both red and handling emergencies by turning both lights red. The system's behavior is such that it will periodically return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars, ensuring that cars are given the opportunity to pass through the intersection.
