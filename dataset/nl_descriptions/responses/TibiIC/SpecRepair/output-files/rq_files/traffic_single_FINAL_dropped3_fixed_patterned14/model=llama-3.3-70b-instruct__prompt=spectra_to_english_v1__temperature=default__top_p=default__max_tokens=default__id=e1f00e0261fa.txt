The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when there is no emergency, if the car is not present or the traffic light is green, the car will not appear in the next moment. Furthermore, if the car is not present, it will either remain absent or the traffic light will not be green in the next moment.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If a car is present and the traffic light is green, or if no car is present, the system will remain in its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to cars and turn the traffic light green as needed.
