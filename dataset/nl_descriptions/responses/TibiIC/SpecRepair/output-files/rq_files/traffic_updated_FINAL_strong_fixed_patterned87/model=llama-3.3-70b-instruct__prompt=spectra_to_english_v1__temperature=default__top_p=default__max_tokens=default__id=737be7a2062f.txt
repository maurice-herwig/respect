The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for car A and car B, denoted by greenA and greenB, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following way: emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that it is always the case that there will be a point in the future where there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it will remain present until its light turns green. When car A is present and its light is green, it will move and no longer be present. When car B is present, its light is green, and there is no emergency, it will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During emergency situations, both lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows: when a car is present, the system will transition through a series of states to ensure that the car's light is eventually turned green. The system starts in an initial state where it is not responding to the car's presence. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to be turned green. Once the car's light is green, the system will transition back to the initial state. This process repeats to ensure that the car's presence is always eventually responded to with a green light.
