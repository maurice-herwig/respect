The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and when the police are present, if the car is present and the light is not green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when the car is present and the light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present infinitely often.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is green, the system ensures that the car moves. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. The system always tries to return to its initial state, where it can respond to the presence of a car.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
