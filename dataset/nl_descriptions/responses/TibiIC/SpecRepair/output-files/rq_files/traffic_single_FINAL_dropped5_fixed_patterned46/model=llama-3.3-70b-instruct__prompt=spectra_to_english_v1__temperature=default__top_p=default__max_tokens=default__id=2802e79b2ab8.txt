The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, when the traffic light is red (i.e., not green), the car should not start moving unless the light turns green or the car remains stationary. Additionally, when the police are present or there is no car, the car should not move unless the traffic light is green or the car stops moving.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior as a state machine with two states, S0 and S1. Initially, the system is in state S0. The system remains in state S0 as long as there is no car present or the car is present and the light is green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to state S1. In state S1, the system will turn the light green, allowing the system to transition back to state S0. If the light remains green, the system will stay in state S0. This pattern ensures that the system will periodically turn the traffic light green when a car is present.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee is to periodically turn the traffic light green in response to the presence of a car, while the environment's assumptions restrict the car's behavior based on the traffic light's state and police presence.
