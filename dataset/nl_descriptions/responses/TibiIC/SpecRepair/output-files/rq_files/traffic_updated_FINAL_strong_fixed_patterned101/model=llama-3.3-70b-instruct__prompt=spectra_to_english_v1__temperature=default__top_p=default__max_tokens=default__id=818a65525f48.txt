The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is on, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is on, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously checks the presence of cars and the emergency signal, updating its state accordingly to ensure that the green lights are controlled correctly. The system ensures that if a car is present, its corresponding green light will be turned on, and if an emergency occurs, both green lights will be turned off.
