The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and when a car is approaching, it should either be an emergency, the traffic light should be green, or there should be no car. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the environment should ensure that either police are present, there is no car, or the traffic light remains not green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state. In this state, if a car is present, the system should turn the traffic light green. If the system fails to turn the traffic light green when a car is present, it transitions to a different state, where it remains until the traffic light is turned green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system should infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically checks for and responds to the presence of cars. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of periodically turning the traffic light green in response to a car's presence is the core requirement, ensuring a safe and responsive traffic management system.
