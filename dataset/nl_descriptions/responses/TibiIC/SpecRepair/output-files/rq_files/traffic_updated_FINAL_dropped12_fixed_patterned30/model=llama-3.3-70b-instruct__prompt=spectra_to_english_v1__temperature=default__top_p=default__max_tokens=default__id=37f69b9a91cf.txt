The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies.

The system is expected to behave under certain assumptions about the environment. Specifically, emergencies should not occur frequently, and when the lights are red, cars on road A should not be present unless there is an emergency. Additionally, when there is an emergency, cars on road B should either be present during the emergency or the light for road B should be green. Cars on both roads should move when their respective lights are green.

The system must guarantee certain behaviors. When there is no emergency, the light for road B should not be green if the light for road A is not green. If there is a car on road A, the system should eventually respond by setting the light for road A to green. If there is a car on road B, the system should respond by setting either the light for road A or road B to green. During an emergency, both lights should be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is governed by a pattern where it initially waits for a car to be present, then responds by setting the corresponding light to green, and eventually returns to the waiting state. This pattern ensures that the system periodically responds to the presence of cars. 

In terms of environment-controlled inputs, `cara`, `carb`, and `emergency` are controlled by the environment, while `greena` and `greenb` are controlled by the system. The system's behavior is designed to ensure safe and responsive traffic light control under the given assumptions and guarantees.
