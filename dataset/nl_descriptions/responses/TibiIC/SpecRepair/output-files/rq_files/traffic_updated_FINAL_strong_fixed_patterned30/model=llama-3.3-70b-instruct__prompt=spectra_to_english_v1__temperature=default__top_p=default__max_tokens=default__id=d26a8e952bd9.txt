The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and car A is not present, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car is not present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. Whenever an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the car's presence by turning its light green. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to the car's presence or not. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or its light is already green. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. In this state, the system will turn the car's light green, and then transition back to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will always be ready to respond to the car's presence.
