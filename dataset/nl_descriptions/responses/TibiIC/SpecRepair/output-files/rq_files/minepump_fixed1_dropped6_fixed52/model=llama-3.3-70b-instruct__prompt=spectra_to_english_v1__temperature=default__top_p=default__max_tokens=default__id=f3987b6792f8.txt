The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the pump.

It is assumed that the environment will not turn the pump on if it was already on in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on in the next state or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off in the next state. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and high water, while adhering to the constraint that the pump cannot be turned on if it was already on.
