The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road A at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing or the light for road A is green. Similarly, for road B, when an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road B at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing, the light for road B is green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that either the emergency is still ongoing, there is no car on road A at the next time step, or the light for road A is no longer green. For road B, when an emergency occurs, the environment will ensure that the light for road B is not green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. However, during an emergency, the system will keep the lights red.

The system's response to cars on the roads follows a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to cars on the roads by turning the corresponding lights green.
