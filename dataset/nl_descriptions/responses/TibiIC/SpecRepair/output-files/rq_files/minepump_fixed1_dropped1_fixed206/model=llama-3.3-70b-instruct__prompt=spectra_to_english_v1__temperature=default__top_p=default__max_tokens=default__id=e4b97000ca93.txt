The Minepump system operates in an environment where water levels and methane presence are monitored. The environment controls the water level, indicated by the highwater signal, and the presence of methane. The system controls the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when the water level is about to rise (i.e., highwater is about to become true), if the pump is off, then the water level will not rise in the next instant.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on in the next instant or the water level must be low. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off in the next instant.

In summary, the system must ensure that the pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and the water level, while the environment ensures that the water level does not rise when the pump is off and about to rise.
