The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and when the traffic light is green, a car should not start moving if it was not already moving, unless the light turns red. Additionally, when the police are present, or there is no car, or the car is moving, or the light is red, the car should not start moving.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. The system also ensures that there will be periods where no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described by its response to the presence of a car: if a car is present and the light is green, the system will remain in this state; if a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green; once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
