The TrafficE2 system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the traffic light, indicating whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will remain present until either an emergency occurs, the car leaves, or the light turns green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, either there is no car or the car will move when the light turns green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when a car is present, the light will eventually turn green. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and adjusts its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it waits for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it periodically returns to this initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.
