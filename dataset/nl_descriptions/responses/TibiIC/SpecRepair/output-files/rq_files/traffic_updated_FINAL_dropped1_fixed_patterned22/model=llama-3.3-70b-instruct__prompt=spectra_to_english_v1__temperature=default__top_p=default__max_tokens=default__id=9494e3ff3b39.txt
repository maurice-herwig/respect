The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency variable will be false most of the time. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: if car A is present when the light is red, it will either remain present or the emergency will end or the light will turn green; if car B is present when the light is red, it will either remain present or the light will turn green or car B will not be present; if car A is moving when the light is green, it will either stop moving or the emergency will end or the light will turn red; and if car B is moving when the light is green, it will either stop moving or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will behave in certain ways. Specifically, the system ensures that the lights at intersections A and B are not both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light green for intersection A, and similarly for intersection B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will turn the light green for that intersection if it is not already green. The system will keep the light green until the car leaves or an emergency occurs. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. The system will periodically return to its initial state, waiting for a car to arrive. Overall, the system is designed to safely and efficiently manage traffic flow in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
