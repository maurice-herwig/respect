The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police intervention infinitely often, and when a car is present and the light is red, the car should remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car is present, the light is green, and there is no police intervention, the car should move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees two main behaviors. Firstly, it should often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system should ensure that the light turns green, allowing the car to pass. Secondly, the system guarantees that there will be periods where no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to any car. The system's behavior can be described by its state transitions: if the system is not responding to a car and either no car is present or a car is present and the light is green, it remains in this state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system should infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it regularly resets and prepares to respond to new cars.
