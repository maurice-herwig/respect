The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the state of the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when an emergency is in effect, the traffic light for road B is always green or the emergency condition persists; when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. The system responds to the presence of cars on each road by transitioning through a series of states to ensure that the traffic lights turn green when cars are present. The system always returns to its initial state after responding to a car's presence.
