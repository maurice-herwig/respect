The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A presence), `carb` (car B presence), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, car A will not be present in the next state, unless the light for car A is red or the emergency situation persists. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, car B will not be present in the next state, unless the light for car B is green, the emergency situation persists, or car B is already not present. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for car A is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state, unless the emergency situation persists or the light for car A turns red. Similarly, when an emergency occurs and the light for car B is green, car B will move and not be present in the next state, unless the emergency situation persists, the light for car B turns red, or car B is already not present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the light for car B will not be green. The system will also respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding green light on. Specifically, when car A is present and the light for car B is not green, the system will turn the green light on for car A. When car B is present and the light for car B is green, the system will keep the green light on for car B. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both lights will not be red at the same time.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to trigger a response or waiting for the response to be satisfied. If a car is present and the corresponding green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the response to be satisfied. If the response is satisfied, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state.
