The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain idle and not move until its green light turns on. Similarly, when car B is present and its green light is red while car A's green light is on, car B will remain idle. However, when the green light for a car is on, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state.

The system guarantees that the green lights for cars A and B are never both off at the same time. When a car is present, the system will eventually turn on its corresponding green light. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. The system also ensures that if a car is present and its green light is on, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to trigger its green light or waiting for the green light to be turned on. If a car is present and its green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
