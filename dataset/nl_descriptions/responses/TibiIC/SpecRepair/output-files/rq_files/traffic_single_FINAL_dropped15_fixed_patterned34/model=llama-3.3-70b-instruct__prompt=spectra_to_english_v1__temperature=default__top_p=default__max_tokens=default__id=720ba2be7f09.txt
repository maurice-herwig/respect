The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: when the traffic light is not green, the car will not move unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, once the car starts moving, it will continue to move until the traffic light turns green, unless there is an emergency or the car is not moving.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green often when a car is present, and there will be periods where no car is present. The system also ensures that when a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then enters a cycle where it waits for a car to arrive and turns green when one does, and then returns to waiting for the next car. If a car arrives when the traffic light is not green, the system will remember this and turn the light green as soon as possible. The traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is present. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.
