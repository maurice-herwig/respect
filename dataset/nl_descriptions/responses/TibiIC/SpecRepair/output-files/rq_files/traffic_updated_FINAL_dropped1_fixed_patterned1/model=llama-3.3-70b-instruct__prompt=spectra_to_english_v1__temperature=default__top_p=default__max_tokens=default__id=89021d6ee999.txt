The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, the environment is assumed to ensure that if car A is not present initially, it will not be present in the next state, or the emergency situation will still be present, or the traffic light at intersection A will be green. Similar assumptions are made for car B. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when an emergency situation occurs and car A is present, it will not be present in the next state, or the emergency situation will still be present, or car A will not be present initially, or the traffic light at intersection A will not be green. Similar assumptions are made for car B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency situation, the system will ensure that both traffic lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green. If the traffic light is already green, the system will continue to keep it green as long as the car is present. If the car leaves, the system will turn the traffic light red. This behavior is repeated indefinitely, with the system responding to the presence of cars at both intersections.
