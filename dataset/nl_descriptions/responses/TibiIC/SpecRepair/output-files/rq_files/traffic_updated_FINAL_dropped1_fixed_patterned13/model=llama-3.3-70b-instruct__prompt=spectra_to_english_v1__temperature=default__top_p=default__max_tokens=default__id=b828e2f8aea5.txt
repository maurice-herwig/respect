The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when an emergency is present, cars at intersection A will either not be present in the next state, or will be present and the traffic light at A will be green, unless the emergency persists. Similar assumptions are made for cars at intersection B. Furthermore, when an emergency is present and a car is at an intersection with a green light, the car will either not be present in the next state or the traffic light will turn red, unless the emergency persists.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at A will eventually turn green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at B will eventually turn green. During an emergency, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that cars are eventually allowed to pass through the intersection.
