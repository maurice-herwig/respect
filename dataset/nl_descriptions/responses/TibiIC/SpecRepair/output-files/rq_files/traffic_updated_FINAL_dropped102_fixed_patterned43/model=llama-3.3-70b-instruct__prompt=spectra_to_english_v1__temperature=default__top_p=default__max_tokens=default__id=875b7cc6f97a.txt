The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for roads A (greena) and B (greenb).

The system assumes that when an emergency occurs, cars on road A will not be present initially and will not appear in the next state if the light is red. Similarly, it assumes that when an emergency occurs, cars on road B will either not be present initially, or if present, they will not be present in the next state if the light is red, or the light will be green, or no car will be present. The system also assumes that during an emergency, if a car is present on road A when the light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state, unless the light turns red. A similar assumption is made for road B, where during an emergency, if a car is present when the light is green, it will either move and not be present in the next state, or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees that when there is no emergency, the traffic light for road B will not be green. Additionally, the system guarantees that if a car is present on road A and the light for road B is red, then the light for road A will be green. If a car is present on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will maintain this state. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system ensures that if a car appears on a road and the corresponding light is not green, it will eventually turn green. The system maintains a pattern of response where it prioritizes turning the light green when a car is present and the light is not already green, and it will return to a state of readiness once the light has turned green. This pattern of response is repeated infinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
