The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the system is in an emergency state, the environment is assumed to not have cars approaching intersection A or B when their respective lights are red, unless the emergency situation persists or the light turns green. The environment is also assumed to have cars moving away from intersection A or B when their respective lights are green during an emergency situation.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A or B, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If an emergency situation occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern where it initially waits for a car to arrive, then responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green when the car is present, and returns to the waiting state when the car is no longer present or the light is turned green. This pattern ensures that the system always eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
