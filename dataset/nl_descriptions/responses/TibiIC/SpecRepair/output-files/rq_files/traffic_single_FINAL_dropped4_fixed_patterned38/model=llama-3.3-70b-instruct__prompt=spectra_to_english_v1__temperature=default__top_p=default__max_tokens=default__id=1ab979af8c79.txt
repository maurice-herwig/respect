The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence infinitely often, and when the traffic light is red, a car should not be present unless there is an emergency or the car was already present. Additionally, when a car is present, it should either move or not be present when the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and then transitions between two states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light's state. The system remains in the initial state if the car is not present or if the car is present and the traffic light is green. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a secondary state. The system returns to the initial state when the traffic light is green, and it remains in the secondary state if the traffic light is not green. The system should infinitely often be in the initial state.

The system's initial condition is that it starts in the initial state. The system's safety requirements are defined by the allowed transitions between the two states, based on the presence of the car and the traffic light's state. The system's liveness requirement is that it should infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that the traffic light will eventually turn green in response to the presence of a car.
