The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic lights. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the occurrence of an emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when the light is red for car A, car A is idle unless an emergency is occurring or about to occur. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, car B is either idle or the light is green for car B. Cars A and B are also assumed to move when their respective lights are green, unless an emergency is occurring.

The system guarantees certain behavior. When there is no emergency, the light for car B is not green if the light for car A is not green. The system also ensures that when car A is present, the light for car A eventually turns green. If car B is present, the system guarantees that either the light for car A or the light for car B eventually turns green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, both lights are red.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding lights green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system remains in this state until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red. The system always returns to its initial state after responding to a car's presence.
