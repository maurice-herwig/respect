The Minepump system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water is high, then either the water will become not high at the next moment or the pump will be off. 

The system guarantees two conditions: 
- If methane is present, then the pump will be turned on at the next moment.
- If methane is not present, then the pump will be turned off at the next moment.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
