The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The car is also expected to move when the traffic light is green, unless it was already moving or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to cars infinitely often.
