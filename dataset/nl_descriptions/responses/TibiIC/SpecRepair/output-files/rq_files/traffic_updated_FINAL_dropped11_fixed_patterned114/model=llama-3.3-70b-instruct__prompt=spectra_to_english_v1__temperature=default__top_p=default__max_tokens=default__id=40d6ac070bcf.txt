The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the system is about to enter an emergency state, cars at intersection A are assumed to be idle, meaning they are either already stopped or will stop, unless the light is green for them or they are already stopped. Similar assumptions are made for cars at intersection B. Furthermore, when the system is about to enter an emergency state, cars at both intersections are assumed to move when their respective lights are green, unless the emergency situation is already present or the light is about to turn red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light green for that car. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light green for that car. During emergency situations, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green when a car is present. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states: one where the system is ready to respond to a car, and another where the system is waiting for the car to be served. The system will always eventually return to the ready state.
