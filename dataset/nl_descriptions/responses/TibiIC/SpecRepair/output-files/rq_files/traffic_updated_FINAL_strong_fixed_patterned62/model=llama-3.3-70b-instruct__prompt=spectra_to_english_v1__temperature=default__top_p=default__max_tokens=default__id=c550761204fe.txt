The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and an emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have an emergency signal most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. When car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency signal is present, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green, but only after it has not been responding previously. If the system is responding to a car and the car is still present but its light is not green, the system will continue to not respond until the car's light is green. The system will always eventually return to a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
