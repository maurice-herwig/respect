The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain idle and not move. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is on, car B will remain idle and not move. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is on, car A will move and no longer be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its green light is on.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both green lights are turned off.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B follows a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and its corresponding green light is on, the system remains in this state. If a car is present but its green light is not on, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system returns to its initial state. The system repeatedly returns to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars A and B.
