The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a present car has a green light. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must respond to the car's presence. Once the system responds by turning the car's light green, it returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
