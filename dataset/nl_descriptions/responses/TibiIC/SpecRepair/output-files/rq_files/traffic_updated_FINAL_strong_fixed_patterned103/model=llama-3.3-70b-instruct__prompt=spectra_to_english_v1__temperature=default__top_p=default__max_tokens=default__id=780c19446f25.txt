The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning there will be times when it is not emergency. When car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next time step. When car A's light is green and car A is present, car A will move and not be present in the next time step. The same applies to car B: when its light is green and it is present, it will move and not be present in the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually make car A's light green. The same applies to car B: when it is present, the system will eventually make its light green. During an emergency, both car A's and car B's lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B can be described as follows: the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light is green, the system returns to its initial state, indicating that it has responded to the car's presence. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars A and B.
