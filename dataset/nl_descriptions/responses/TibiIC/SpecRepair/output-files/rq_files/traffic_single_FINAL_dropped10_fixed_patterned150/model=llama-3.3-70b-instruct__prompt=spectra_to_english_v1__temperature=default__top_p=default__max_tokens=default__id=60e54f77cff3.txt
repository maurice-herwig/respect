The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that there will be periods where police are not present, and this will happen infinitely often. Additionally, the car will not start moving when the light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the light is green, the car will either not be present, be accompanied by police, or will stop moving at the next opportunity.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green infinitely often, and this will happen in response to the presence of a car. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be periods where there is no car present, and this will also happen infinitely often.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the light green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state, as long as the car is no longer present or the light has turned green in response to the car's presence. If the light is green and the car is still present, the system will remain in the waiting state until the car is gone or the light turns red again.
