The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides input signals indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B (`cara` and `carb`) and an emergency status (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the light for road B is green; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road B green.

The system's response to cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the waiting state. This pattern ensures that the system responds to cars on the roads by turning the corresponding lights green.
