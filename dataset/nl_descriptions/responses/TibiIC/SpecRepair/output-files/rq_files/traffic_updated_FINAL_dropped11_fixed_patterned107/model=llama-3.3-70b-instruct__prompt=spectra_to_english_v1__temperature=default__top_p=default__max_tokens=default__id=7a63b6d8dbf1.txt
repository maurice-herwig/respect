The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either remain idle or move only when the traffic light is green, and similarly for cars at intersection B. 

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A to green, and similarly for intersection B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both traffic lights to red.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. When a car is present at an intersection and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars by eventually turning the corresponding traffic light to green.
