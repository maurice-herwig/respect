The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light is red for A but green for B, car A will remain idle until the light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and the light is red for B but green for A, car B will remain idle until the light turns green. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
