The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as indicated by the fact that it is always the case that eventually, there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A do not move until the emergency is over, the traffic light at A turns green, or the car is no longer present. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection B do not move until the emergency is over or the traffic light at B turns green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at A is green, cars at intersection A move and are no longer present. Similarly, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at B is green, cars at intersection B move and are no longer present.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. Specifically, the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at B green. During an emergency, both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present or the corresponding traffic light is green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the initial state. The system always eventually returns to the initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to the presence of cars.
