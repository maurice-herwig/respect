The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if there is high water, the pump will not be activated. 

The system guarantees the following behavior: if methane is detected, the pump will be activated at the next opportunity, and if methane is not detected, the pump will be deactivated at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is turned on when methane is present and turned off when methane is not present, under the assumption that the environment does not activate the pump when there is high water.
