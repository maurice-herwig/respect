The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently on and was also on in the previous state, then in the next state, the water will not be high.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if the water is high and there is no methane, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

Note that the variables "highwater" and "methane" are controlled by the environment, while the variable "pump" is controlled by the system.
