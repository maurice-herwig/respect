The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the light for road B is green; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car on the road or the light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
