The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or become present only if there is an emergency or the car is already present. Additionally, when the traffic light is green, the car will either be absent or will move (i.e., will not be present in the next state) unless the police or an emergency situation is present.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is formalized by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of its response to the car's presence. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system is designed to control a traffic light in response to the presence of a car, with certain assumptions about the environment's behavior, and guarantees that it will periodically turn the traffic light green when a car is present.
