The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green, car A will move and its presence will be removed. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light is green, the system will transition back to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
