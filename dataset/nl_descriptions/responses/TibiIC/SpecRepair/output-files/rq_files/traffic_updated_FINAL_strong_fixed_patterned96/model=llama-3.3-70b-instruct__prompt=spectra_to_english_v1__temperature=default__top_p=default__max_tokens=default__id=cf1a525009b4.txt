The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B: when car B is present and its light is green, car B will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B: when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the conditions for responding to car presence are met. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence. The system always eventually returns to this initial state, ensuring that it can continually respond to car presence.
