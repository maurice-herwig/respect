The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the system is in an emergency state, the environment is expected to behave in certain ways: if road A's light is red, the environment will either not have a car on road A or will have a car on road A at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or road A's light turns green. Similarly, if road B's light is red, the environment will either not have a car on road B or will have a car on road B at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends. Furthermore, when the system is in an emergency state and road A's light is green, the environment is expected to either end the emergency situation, have no car on road A at the next time step, or have road A's light turn red. The same expectations apply to road B.

Similarly, if there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn road B's light green. When an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's behavior can be described by a pattern of response to the presence of cars, where it will turn a road's light green when a car is present on that road, and will keep the light green until the car is no longer present or the emergency situation ends. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ensuring that it always responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
