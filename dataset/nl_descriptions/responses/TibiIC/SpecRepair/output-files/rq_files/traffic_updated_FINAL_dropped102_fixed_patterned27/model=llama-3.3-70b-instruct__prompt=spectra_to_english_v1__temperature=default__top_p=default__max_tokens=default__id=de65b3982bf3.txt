The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and whether an emergency is occurring (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car on road A, and it will either not introduce a car on road B, or the light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency is occurring, if a car is present on road A, it will eventually move, unless the light for road A is red. Similarly, if a car is present on road B during an emergency, it will eventually move, unless the light for road B is red or there is no car on road B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both lights red unless an emergency is occurring, in which case both lights may be red. If a car is present on road A and the light for road B is red, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. If a car is present on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will keep the light for road B green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: if a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. If the light is already green when a car is present, the system will keep it green. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ready to respond to new cars on the roads.
