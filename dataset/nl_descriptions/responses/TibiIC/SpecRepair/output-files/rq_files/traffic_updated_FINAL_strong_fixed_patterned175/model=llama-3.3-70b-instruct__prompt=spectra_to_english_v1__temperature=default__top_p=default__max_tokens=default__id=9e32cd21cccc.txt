The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation occur infinitely often, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected. The environment also ensures that car B will remain idle when its light is red and there is no emergency.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or if a car is present and its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will respond to a car's presence infinitely often.
