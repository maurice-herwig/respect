The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to environmental inputs. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is true, car A will not be present in the next state; when the emergency signal is true, car B will either not be present in the next state or will be present and the traffic light for car B will be green; when the emergency signal is true and car A is present, car A will not be present in the next state if the traffic light for car A is not green; and when the emergency signal is true and car B is present, car B will not be present in the next state if the traffic light for car B is not green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when the emergency signal is false, the traffic light for car B will not be green; when car A is present and the traffic light for car B is not green, the traffic light for car A will be green; when car B is present and the traffic light for car B is green, the system will respond accordingly; and when the emergency signal is false, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

In particular, when car A is present, the system will eventually respond by setting the traffic light for car A to green if the traffic light for car B is not green. Similarly, when car B is present and the traffic light for car B is green, the system will respond accordingly. The system ensures that the traffic lights are updated in a way that satisfies these guarantees, with the initial state being such that the system is ready to respond to the environmental inputs.
