The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. When car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. When car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and its presence will be removed. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to a car's presence by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to a car's presence or not. The system starts in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence, and it will remain in this state as long as no car is present or its corresponding light is already green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state until the car's light is turned green. The system will always eventually return to the state where it is not responding to any car's presence.
