The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state will have a car on road A, unless the emergency ends or a car is already on road A. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state will either have a car on road B, the emergency will continue, the light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the next state will either have the emergency end, no car on road A, or the light for road A will turn red. The same logic applies to road B: when an emergency occurs and the light for road B is green, the next state will either have the emergency continue, no car on road B, or the light for road B will turn red.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the light for road B will not be green. If a car is present on road A and the light for road B is not green, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. If a car is present on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will maintain this state. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both lights will not be red at the same time.

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a set of safety rules that dictate the next state of the system based on the current state and the presence or absence of cars on the roads. The system will always eventually return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
