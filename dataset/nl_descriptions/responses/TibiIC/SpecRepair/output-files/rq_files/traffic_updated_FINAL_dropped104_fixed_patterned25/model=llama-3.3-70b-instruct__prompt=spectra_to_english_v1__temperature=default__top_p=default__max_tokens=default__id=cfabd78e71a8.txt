The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as an emergency signal. The system, in turn, controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A, the presence of car B, and the emergency signal. The system-controlled outputs are the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, car A will not be present in the next state unless the emergency signal is still active or the green light for car A is on. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, car B will not be present in the next state unless the emergency signal is still active, the green light for car B is on, or car B is not currently present. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, if car A is present, it will move and not be present in the next state unless the emergency signal is still active or the green light for car A is off. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, if car B is present, it will move and not be present in the next state unless the green light for car B is off.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of cars A and B by turning on their respective green lights. Specifically, the system will ensure that whenever car A is present, the green light for car A will eventually be turned on, and whenever car B is present, the green light for car B will eventually be turned on. This response is governed by a state machine that ensures the system always returns to a safe state after responding to the presence of a car. Initially, the system is in this safe state. The system remains in the safe state as long as no car is present or the corresponding green light is on. If a car is present and its green light is not on, the system transitions to a responding state, where it ensures the green light is turned on, and then returns to the safe state. The system will infinitely often be in the safe state.
