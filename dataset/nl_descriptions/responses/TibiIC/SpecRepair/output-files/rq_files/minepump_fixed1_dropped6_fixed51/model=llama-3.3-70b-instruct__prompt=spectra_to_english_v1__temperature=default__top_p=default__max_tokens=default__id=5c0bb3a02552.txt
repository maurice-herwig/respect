The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that whenever methane becomes present, the pump was not running in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must not be high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system's behavior is constrained by the environment's signals (high water and methane presence), and it must control the pump accordingly to meet the specified safety requirements. The pump's state is determined based on the current and future presence of methane and the absence of high water.
