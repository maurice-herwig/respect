The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the levels of water and methane, which are indicated by the highwater and methane signals, respectively. The system controls the pump signal.

It is assumed that when methane is present, the pump will be off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is turned on when methane is detected and turned off when methane is not detected, under the assumption that the environment does not activate the pump when methane is present.
