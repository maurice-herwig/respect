The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, it will also remain present in the next time step. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, it will move and not be present in the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to trigger a response or waiting for the response to be fulfilled. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to the state where it is waiting for the response to be fulfilled. Once the response is fulfilled, i.e., the car's light turns green, the system will return to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
