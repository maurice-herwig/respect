The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not always on, and when a car is present at a red light, it remains present until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car is present at a green light, it moves and is no longer present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system eventually turns the green light for car A on. Similarly, when car B is present, the system eventually turns the green light for car B on. If an emergency signal is present, both green lights are turned off.

The system also ensures that car A and car B are given the opportunity to move when their respective green lights are on, and that the system responds to the presence of cars by turning their green lights on, as long as the emergency signal is not present. 

In the initial state, the system is not waiting for a car to be served. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or the green light is on for a present car. If a car is present and its green light is off, the system transitions to a waiting state until the green light is turned on, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system always eventually returns to the initial state.
