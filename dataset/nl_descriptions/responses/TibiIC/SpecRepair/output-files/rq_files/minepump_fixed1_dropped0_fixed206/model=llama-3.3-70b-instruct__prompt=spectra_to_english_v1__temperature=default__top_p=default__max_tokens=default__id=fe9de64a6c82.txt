The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if the water level is not high and the pump was not operating in the previous state, then if the water level becomes not high, it will remain not high in the next state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on in the next state or the water level must be low. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state. 

Note that the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
