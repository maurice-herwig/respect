The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if methane becomes present, then either the water level must be low when the pump is not operating, or the water level must be low at the next time step.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be activated at the next time step, unless the water level is low. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated at the next time step.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
