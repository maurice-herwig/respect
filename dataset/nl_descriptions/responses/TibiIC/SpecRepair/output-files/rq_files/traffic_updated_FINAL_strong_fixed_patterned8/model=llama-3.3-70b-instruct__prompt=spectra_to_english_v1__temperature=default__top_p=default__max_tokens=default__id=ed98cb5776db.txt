The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

In general, the system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding light green, unless an emergency occurs. The system's response to a car's presence can be in one of two states: either the car's light is already green or appropriate, or it is not, and the system will transition between these states as necessary to ensure the car's light is turned green when the car is present, while also considering the emergency signal and the other car's state. The system will always return to a state where it is ready to respond to a car's presence.
