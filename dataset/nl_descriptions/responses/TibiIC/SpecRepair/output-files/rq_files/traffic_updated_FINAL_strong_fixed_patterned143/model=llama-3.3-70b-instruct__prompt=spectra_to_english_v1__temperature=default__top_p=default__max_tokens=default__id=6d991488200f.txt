The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, and when a car is stopped at a red light, it remains stopped until the light turns green. Specifically, if car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, if car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. However, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and no longer be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During an emergency, both lights will be red. 

In general, the system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding light green, while ensuring safe operation by never having both lights red and responding appropriately to emergency situations and car movements.
