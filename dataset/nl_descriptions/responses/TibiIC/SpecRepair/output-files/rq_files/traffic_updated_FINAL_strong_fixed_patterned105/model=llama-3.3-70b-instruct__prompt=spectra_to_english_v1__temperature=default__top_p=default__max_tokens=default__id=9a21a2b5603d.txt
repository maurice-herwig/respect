The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A's light is green and car A is present, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of a pattern where a stimulus (a car being present) leads to a response (its corresponding light turning green). This pattern has an initial state where the stimulus is not present or is present and the response is given. The system then transitions between states based on the presence of the stimulus and the response. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
