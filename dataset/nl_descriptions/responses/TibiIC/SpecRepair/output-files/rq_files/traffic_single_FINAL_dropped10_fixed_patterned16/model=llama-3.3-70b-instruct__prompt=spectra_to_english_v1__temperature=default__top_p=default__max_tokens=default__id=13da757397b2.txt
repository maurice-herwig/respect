The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (i.e., not green), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will either not be moving or will stop moving.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when it does, the car will be allowed to move. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where the traffic light is not green, and the car is not moving. The system then responds to the presence of the car and the state of the traffic light. When the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green, allowing the car to move. Once the traffic light is green, it will stay green until the car is no longer present or the traffic light is turned red again. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green and the car is allowed to move.
