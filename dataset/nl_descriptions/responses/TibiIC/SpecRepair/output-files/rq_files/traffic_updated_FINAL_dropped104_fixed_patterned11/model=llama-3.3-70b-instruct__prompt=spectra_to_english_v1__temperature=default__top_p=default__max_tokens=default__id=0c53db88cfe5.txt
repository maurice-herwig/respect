The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which indicates whether an emergency situation is present. The system controls the outputs `greena` and `greenb`, which indicate whether the traffic lights for cars A and B are green, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation is present, car A will not be present in the next time step unless the traffic light for car A is green or the emergency situation continues. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, car B will not be present in the next time step unless the traffic light for car B is green, the emergency situation continues, or car B is not present. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present, car A will move away (i.e., will not be present in the next time step) unless the emergency situation continues or the traffic light for car A is not green. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, car B will move away unless the traffic light for car B is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of cars A and B by turning their respective traffic lights green. Specifically, whenever car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car A green, and whenever car B is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car B green. This response is governed by a state machine that ensures the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its traffic light green. The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is safety-critical, meaning that it must always respond correctly to the presence of cars, and it must do so in a way that ensures the traffic lights are turned green infinitely often when cars are present.
