The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency status. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the emergency status. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency status is true, if the light for road A is not green, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency status is true, the environment will either maintain the emergency status or have the light for road B green; when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road A, the environment will either have no car on road A at the next time step or the light for road A will not be green; similarly, when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road B, the environment will have no car on road B at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to cars on the roads by eventually turning the corresponding lights green.
