The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the occurrence of emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when the traffic light is red, car A is idle unless there is an emergency. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car B is idle unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is about to turn green. Car A moves when the traffic light is green, and car B moves when the traffic light is green, unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. When there is no emergency, the traffic lights at intersections A and B are not both red. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. If car B is present, the system will eventually turn either the traffic light at intersection A or intersection B green. When there is an emergency, the traffic lights at both intersections are red.

The system also follows specific rules for responding to cars. When a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green, as long as the car remains present. This response is guaranteed to happen in a way that ensures the traffic light is turned green in a timely manner. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to cars. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it periodically returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.
