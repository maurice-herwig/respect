The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there are no emergencies. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment is expected to behave in certain ways: if a car is on road A when the light is red, it will either stay there, move when the light turns green, or an emergency will persist; similarly, if a car is on road B when the light is red, it will either stay there or move when the light turns green, or an emergency will persist. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light is green for road A, the car on road A is expected to move, and similarly for road B.

The system guarantees several behaviors. Firstly, it ensures that the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. Secondly, when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light green for road A, and similarly for road B. Thirdly, during an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green when a car is present, and will keep the light green as long as the car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car to be served, and will stay in this state until the light is turned green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car on either road A or road B.
