The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence infinitely often, and when a car is present, it should either be an emergency situation, the traffic light should be green, or the car should not be moving towards the traffic light. Additionally, when a car is not moving, it should either be because the police are present, the car is not approaching the traffic light, or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system's behavior is safe and consistent. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the car's presence and the traffic light's state, updating its state accordingly. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, ready to respond to cars. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
