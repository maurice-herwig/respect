The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, as well as an `emergency` flag. The system controls the outputs `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that the `emergency` flag is typically false. Additionally, when the `emergency` flag is true, the environment is assumed to not have cars approaching intersection A or B when the corresponding traffic light is red, unless the emergency situation persists or the traffic light turns green. The environment is also assumed to have cars move when the traffic light is green, unless an emergency situation occurs.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A or B, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both traffic lights red. The system also ensures that when a car is present at an intersection, the traffic light will turn green in response, and this response is modeled using a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the car's presence.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's behavior is defined by a set of safety and liveness requirements that ensure the traffic lights are controlled correctly based on the inputs from the environment.
