The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will not move unless the traffic light is green or the emergency situation persists, and cars will move to the next state of being present or not. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection B will not move unless the traffic light is green or the emergency situation persists. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars will move and their presence will change. The same applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. The same applies to intersection B. Furthermore, when an emergency situation is present, both traffic lights will be red.

The system also follows specific rules. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. When the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to its initial state. 

In the presence of cars and emergency situations, the system's traffic lights will respond accordingly, ensuring safe and efficient traffic flow.
