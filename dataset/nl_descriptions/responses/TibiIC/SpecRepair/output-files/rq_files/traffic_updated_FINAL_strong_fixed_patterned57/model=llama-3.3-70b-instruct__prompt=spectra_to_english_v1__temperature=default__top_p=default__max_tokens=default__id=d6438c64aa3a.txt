The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: either it is ready to respond to a car's presence, or it is waiting for the car's light to turn green after the car's presence has been detected but its light has not yet turned green. The system will infinitely often be in the state where it is ready to respond to a car's presence. 

In each state, the system's next state and actions depend on the current state and the presence of cars. If the system is ready to respond and a car is not present or its light is already green, it will remain in the ready state. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. Once in the waiting state, the system will turn the car's light green, allowing it to move to the ready state again.
