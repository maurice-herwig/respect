The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

It is assumed that if the pump is off, it will remain off unless the environment changes in a way that requires it to turn on, and if it was off in the previous state, it will stay off or turn on based on the current conditions.

The system guarantees two main behaviors. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must turn on at the next opportunity, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must turn off at the next opportunity. 

These guarantees ensure the pump's operation is directly influenced by the presence or absence of methane, with consideration for the water level. The system's behavior is constrained to react to the environmental conditions in a way that prioritizes safety, as indicated by its response to methane and water levels.
