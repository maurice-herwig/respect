The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that it will never have both green lights off at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B. Whenever an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its green light on. This pattern starts in an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system repeatedly returns to this initial state, ensuring that it responds to the car's presence over time.
