The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The environment controls the variables "car" (indicating a car is present), "emergency", and "police", while the system controls the variable "green" (indicating the traffic light is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, allowing for periods where the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car will not start moving unless the light turns green or the car remains stationary. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, or there is no car, or the car does not move forward, the traffic light will not be green, or the car will not move forward when the light is not green.

The system guarantees two main behaviors. Firstly, it ensures that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Secondly, the system guarantees that there will be periods where no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously monitors the presence of a car and adjusts the traffic light accordingly, following specific rules to ensure safe and responsive behavior. When a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition into a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the light remains green when a car is present, the system stays in its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often return to its initial state, indicating that the traffic light has turned green in response to a car.
