The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (i.e., not green), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The car is also expected to move when the traffic light is green, unless the police are present or the car is already stopped. If the police or an emergency situation arises when the traffic light is green, the car will stop. When there is no emergency, the car will also stop if the police are present.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly over time. The system also ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. This cycle repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present.
