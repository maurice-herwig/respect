The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if car A is not present or the traffic light at intersection A is green, then car A will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency persists. Similar assumptions are made for car B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at intersection A or B is green, the corresponding car will move, meaning it will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency persists.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time, meaning that at least one of greena or greenb is true. The system also guarantees that when car A is present, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green, and similarly for car B. Furthermore, the system guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both traffic lights will turn red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any cars. When a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green. The system will continue to keep the traffic light green as long as the car is present. If the car is no longer present, the system will turn the traffic light red. This behavior is repeated indefinitely, with the system always responding to the presence of cars at the intersections. In emergency situations, the system prioritizes turning both traffic lights red.
