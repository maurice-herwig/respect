The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, car A is present, and car B's light is red, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. Whenever an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the respective light green when a car is present. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or its light is already green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. The system returns to its initial state once the car's light is green, ensuring that the car's presence is eventually responded to with a green light. The system repeatedly returns to its initial state, ensuring that it always eventually responds to the presence of cars A and B.
