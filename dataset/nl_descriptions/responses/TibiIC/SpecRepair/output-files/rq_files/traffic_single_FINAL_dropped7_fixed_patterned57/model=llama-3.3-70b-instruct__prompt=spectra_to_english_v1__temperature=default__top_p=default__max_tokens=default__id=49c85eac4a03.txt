The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car will not start moving unless it was already moving. The environment is also expected to behave such that when the police are present or there is no car, the car will either not move or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, infinitely often. Additionally, there will be times when there is no car present.

The system's behavior is further defined by the following rules. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. If the traffic light is already green when a car arrives, the system will remain in its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
