The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present at a red light, it will remain idle until the light turns green. However, when a car is present at a green light and an emergency is imminent, the car will move and clear the intersection before the emergency arrives.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present, the corresponding green light will eventually turn on. If an emergency occurs, both green lights will turn off. The system also ensures that when a car is present and its corresponding green light is on, the car's presence will lead to the green light being sustained until the car moves or an emergency occurs.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn on the green light when a car is present, and will keep the light on until the car moves or an emergency occurs. This pattern also ensures that the system will return to its initial state after responding to a car's presence.
