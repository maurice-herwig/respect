The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: the police intervention is not ongoing indefinitely, and there are periods when the police are not present. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent, become present, or an emergency situation will occur, unless the traffic light is red, in which case the car will not become present unless an emergency occurs or it was already present. The car is also assumed to be idle when the traffic light is not green, meaning it will not move unless the light turns green.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green often when a car is present, ensuring a response to the car's presence. This guarantee is fulfilled through a specific pattern of behavior: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the car's presence. The system then enters a cycle where it remains in this responsive state as long as the car is not present or the light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it awaits the light turning green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial responsive state. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to this responsive state, allowing it to consistently respond to the presence of cars. 

The initial condition of the system is that it starts in this responsive state, ready to handle the presence of cars. The safety requirements of the system dictate how it transitions between states based on the presence of the car and the state of the traffic light, ensuring that the system always responds appropriately to the car's presence and the traffic light's state. The liveness requirement ensures that the system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the car's presence, guaranteeing that cars will periodically have the opportunity to pass through the intersection when the traffic light turns green.
