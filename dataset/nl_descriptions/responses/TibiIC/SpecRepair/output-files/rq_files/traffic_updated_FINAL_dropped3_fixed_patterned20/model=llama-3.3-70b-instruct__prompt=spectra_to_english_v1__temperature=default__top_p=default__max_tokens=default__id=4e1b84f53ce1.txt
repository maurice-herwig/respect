The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on input from the environment. The environment controls three inputs: `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which signals an emergency situation. The system controls two outputs: `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is declared, if the light at intersection A is red, then there should be no car at intersection A, or the light at intersection A should be green. Similarly, when an emergency is declared, if the light at intersection B is red, then the emergency signal should still be active, or the light at intersection B should be green. Additionally, when an emergency is declared, or when there is no car at intersection A, or when the light at intersection A is red, then the car at intersection A should move or not be present in the next state. A similar assumption is made for intersection B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars at the intersections follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system transitions back to its initial state. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system eventually responds to the presence of cars at the intersections.
