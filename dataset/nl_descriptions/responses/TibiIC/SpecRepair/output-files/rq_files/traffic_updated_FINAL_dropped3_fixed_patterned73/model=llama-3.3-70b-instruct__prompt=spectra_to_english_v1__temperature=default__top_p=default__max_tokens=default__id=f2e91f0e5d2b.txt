The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the emergency status, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The system ensures that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency status is true and the light for road A is red, there is no car on road A; when the emergency status is true, the light for road B is either green or the emergency status remains true; when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will turn red, or the car will not be present on road A at the next time step; and when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move or not be present on road B at the next time step.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system responds to the presence of a car on a road by eventually turning the corresponding light green. The system remains in this waiting state as long as there is no car present or the light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the waiting state. The system will infinitely often return to the waiting state.
