The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergencies (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain idle or the traffic light for road A will be green, unless the car moves. Similarly, for road B, if a car is present, it will either remain idle or the traffic light for road B will be green, unless the car moves. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for a road is green, the car on that road will move, unless the emergency persists or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that if the emergency is ongoing, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars on the roads follows a pattern where it prioritizes turning the traffic light green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it will turn the traffic light green. Once the traffic light is green, the system remains in this state until the car is no longer present, at which point it transitions back to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars on the roads in a timely manner.
