The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when an emergency situation occurs, cars A and B will not move unless their respective lights are green or the emergency situation persists. Additionally, when the lights are green, cars A and B will move unless an emergency situation occurs.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car B green. During an emergency situation, both lights will be red. 

The system responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their respective lights green, following a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This pattern repeats to ensure that the system responds to the presence of cars A and B in a timely manner. 

In all cases, the system ensures that the traffic lights operate safely and respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
