The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. In emergency situations, both traffic lights will be red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is not present or its light is already green, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the car's light green. Once the car's light is green, the system returns to its initial state, where it can respond to the presence of cars again. The system will infinitely often be in this initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
