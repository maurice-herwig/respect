The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when no police are present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green and police are about to arrive, the car should either be absent or about to arrive. The environment is also expected to ensure that if a car is present, it will either move or the traffic light will turn green.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. The system must also ensure that the traffic light operates in a way that responds to the presence of a car. Specifically, when a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green, and this will happen repeatedly.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present.
