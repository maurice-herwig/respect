The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, indicated by greena and greenb.

The environment-controlled inputs are cara (car A present), carb (car B present), and emergency (emergency situation). The system-controlled outputs are greena (green light for car A) and greenb (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation occurs, car A will not be present at the next time step unless the emergency situation continues or the green light for car A is on, or car A is already present. Similarly, when an emergency situation occurs, car B will not be present at the next time step unless the emergency situation continues, the green light for car B is on, or car B is not present. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, if car A is present, it will move (i.e., not be present at the next time step) unless the emergency situation continues or the green light for car A is off. The same applies to car B: when an emergency situation occurs, if car B is present, it will move unless the green light for car B is off or car B is not present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: whenever car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A, and whenever car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. This is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to be present. If a car is present and its corresponding green light is not on, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is on, the system returns to its initial state, repeating this pattern indefinitely. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to its initial state, indicating that the green light has been turned on in response to a car being present.
