The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When the police are not present, the car will be present at the next time step, unless there is an emergency or the car is already absent. Additionally, when the car is present, it will either remain present or move at the next time step, unless the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. If the traffic light remains green, the system stays in the waiting state.

The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, where it is not responding to the car's presence, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions. This means that the system will periodically return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
