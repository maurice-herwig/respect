The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green, car A will move and its presence will be detected as false in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. In the event of an emergency, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern is initially in a state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state as long as the car is not present or its light is already green. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars A and B.
