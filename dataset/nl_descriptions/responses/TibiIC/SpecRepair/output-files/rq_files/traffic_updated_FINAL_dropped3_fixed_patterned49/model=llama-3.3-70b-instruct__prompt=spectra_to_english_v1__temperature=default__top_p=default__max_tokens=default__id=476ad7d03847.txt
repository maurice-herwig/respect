The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when there is an emergency, the traffic light for road B should be red or there should be an emergency; when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. 

In the scenario where a car is present on a road, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. This response is modeled as a state machine that initially waits for a car to be present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. The system ensures that it periodically returns to this initial waiting state.
