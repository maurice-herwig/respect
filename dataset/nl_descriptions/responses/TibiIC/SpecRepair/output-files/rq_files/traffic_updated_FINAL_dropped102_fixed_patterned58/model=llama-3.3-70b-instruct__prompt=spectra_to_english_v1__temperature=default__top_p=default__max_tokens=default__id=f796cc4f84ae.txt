The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A is present), `carb` (car B is present), and `emergency` (an emergency situation is occurring). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (the traffic light for car A is green) and `greenb` (the traffic light for car B is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation occurs, car A will not be present in the next state if it is not already present, and car B will either not be present in the next state or will be present and the traffic light for car B will be green, or car B will not be present. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, car A will move away if the traffic light for car A is green, and car B will either move away or not be present if the traffic light for car B is green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the traffic light for car B will not be green. If car A is present and the traffic light for car B is not green, then the traffic light for car A will be green. If car B is present and the traffic light for car B is green, then the system will respond accordingly. When there is no emergency, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

The system also satisfies a responsiveness pattern, which ensures that when a certain condition is met (e.g., car A is present and the traffic light for car B is not green), the system will eventually respond by setting the traffic light for car A to green. This pattern is satisfied by the system alternating between two states, ensuring that the system will eventually respond to the condition. 

In the initial state, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's safety conditions ensure that it will always respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations, and its liveness conditions ensure that it will eventually respond to certain conditions.
