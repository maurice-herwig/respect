The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will either remain present, an emergency will occur, or the car will leave. The environment is also expected to ensure that when a car is present and the police are not, the car will move when the light turns green, unless the police arrive or the car leaves.

The system guarantees that the green light will be on at some point when a car is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and the state of the green light. If a car is present and the light is green, the system remains in its current state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to cars by turning the green light on at some point when a car is present.
