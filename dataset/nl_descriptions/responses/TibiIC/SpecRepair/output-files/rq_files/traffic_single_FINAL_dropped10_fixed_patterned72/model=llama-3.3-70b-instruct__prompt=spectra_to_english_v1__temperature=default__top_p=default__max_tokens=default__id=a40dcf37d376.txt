The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car remains still. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, the car will either not be present or will not move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior is constrained by safety rules that dictate how it responds to the presence of a car and the state of the traffic light. Specifically, when a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If a car is present and the light is green, the system will remain in its initial state. If the light is not green and no car is present, the system will also remain in its initial state. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to the presence of a car.
