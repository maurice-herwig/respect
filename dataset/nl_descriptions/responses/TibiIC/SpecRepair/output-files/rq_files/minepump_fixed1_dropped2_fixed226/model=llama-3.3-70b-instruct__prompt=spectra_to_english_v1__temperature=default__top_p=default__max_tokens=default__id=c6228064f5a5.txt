The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if the pump is not operating, it will remain off unless it was previously off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if there is no methane, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. The system's behavior is defined by the guarantees, which dictate how the pump should respond to the environmental conditions.
