The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car B is on, car A will not move until its own green light turns on. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car A is on, car B will not move until its own green light turns on. Furthermore, when the green light for a car is on, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next state.

The system guarantees that the green lights for cars A and B are never both off at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

In general, the system responds to the presence of cars by eventually turning on the corresponding green light, while ensuring safe operation during emergencies and normal conditions.
