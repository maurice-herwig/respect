The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever methane is present, the pump is initially off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions: 
- If methane is detected, then the pump must be turned on at the next step.
- If methane is not detected, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, although high water does not influence the specified behavior. The system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
