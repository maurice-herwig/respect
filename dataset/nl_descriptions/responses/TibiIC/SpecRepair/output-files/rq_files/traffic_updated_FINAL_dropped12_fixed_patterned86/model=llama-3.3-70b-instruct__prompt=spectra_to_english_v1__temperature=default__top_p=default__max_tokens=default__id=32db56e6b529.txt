The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the traffic light is red for road A, the environment ensures that there is no car on road A or an emergency is occurring. For road B, if an emergency is happening, then either the emergency continues or the traffic light for road B turns green. The environment also ensures that if a car is on road A and the traffic light is green, the car will move and not remain on the road. Similarly, for road B, if there is no emergency and a car is present, the car will move and not remain on the road if the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are not both red when there is no emergency. If there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. If there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn either the traffic light for road A or road B green. When an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights turn red. 

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: if a car is present on a road, the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green, and once the light is green, it will remain so until the car has passed. This pattern repeats infinitely, ensuring that cars on both roads are given the opportunity to pass through the intersection.
