The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the occurrence of emergencies, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. When the emergency light is on, cars on road A will either move next or the light will turn green, or the emergency will still be present. Similarly, when the emergency light is on, cars on road B will either move next or the light will turn green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when the emergency light is on and the traffic light for road A is green, either the emergency will still be present, there will be no car on road A, or the car on road A will move next and the light will turn red. The same conditions apply to road B.

The system must guarantee certain behaviors. The traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When there is a car on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road A green. The same applies to road B. During an emergency, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to a state where it is responding to the car. The system will remain in this state until the traffic light turns green, at which point it will return to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car on either road A or B by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
