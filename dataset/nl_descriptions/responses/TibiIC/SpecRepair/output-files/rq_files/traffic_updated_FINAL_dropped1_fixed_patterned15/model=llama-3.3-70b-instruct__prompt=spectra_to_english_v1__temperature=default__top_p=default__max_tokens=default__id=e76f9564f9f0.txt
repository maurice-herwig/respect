The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red at intersection A, a car will not be present at intersection A if an emergency is not present and the light is not about to turn green. Similarly, when the traffic light is red at intersection B, a car will not be present at intersection B if the light is not about to turn green and a car is not already present. Furthermore, when an emergency is present and the traffic light is green at intersection A, a car will move and will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency is still present or the light turns red. The same assumption applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behavior. Specifically, the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green at intersection A. The same guarantee applies to intersection B. Additionally, when an emergency is present, both traffic lights will be red.

The system starts in an initial state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will turn the traffic light green at that intersection if it is not already green. The system will continue to keep the traffic light green until the car has passed or an emergency occurs. If the traffic light is not green when a car arrives, the system will turn it green as soon as possible. The system will always return to its initial state after a car has passed or an emergency has ended.
