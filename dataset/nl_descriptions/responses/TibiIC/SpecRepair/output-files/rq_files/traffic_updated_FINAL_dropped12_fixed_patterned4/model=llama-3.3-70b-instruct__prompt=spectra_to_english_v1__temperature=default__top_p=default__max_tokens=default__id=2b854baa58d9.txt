The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A is present), `carb` (car B is present), and `emergency` (an emergency situation is occurring). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (the traffic light for car A is green) and `greenb` (the traffic light for car B is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation occur frequently, and when car A is about to appear, it will not be during an emergency situation or when car A is already present. Additionally, when car B is about to appear, it will either be during an emergency situation or when the traffic light for car B is green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that car A and car B move when their respective traffic lights are green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency situation, the traffic light for car B will not be green if the traffic light for car A is not green. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by setting the traffic light for car A to green. When car B is present, the system will eventually respond by setting either the traffic light for car A or car B to green. During an emergency situation, both traffic lights will be red.

The system also guarantees that when there is no emergency situation, at least one of the traffic lights will be green. The initial condition is that the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the traffic lights are set to green in response to the presence of cars, and that the system returns to its initial state after responding to the presence of cars. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
