The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on input from the environment. The environment controls three inputs: `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which indicates whether an emergency situation is present. The system controls two outputs: `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation is present, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there should be no car at intersection A; when an emergency situation is present, if the traffic light at intersection B is red, then the emergency situation should still be present; when an emergency situation is present or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red at the next time step; and when an emergency situation is present or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green; and when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green.

The system's response to the presence of cars at the intersections follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive; if a car arrives and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green; once the traffic light turns green, the system transitions back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state.
