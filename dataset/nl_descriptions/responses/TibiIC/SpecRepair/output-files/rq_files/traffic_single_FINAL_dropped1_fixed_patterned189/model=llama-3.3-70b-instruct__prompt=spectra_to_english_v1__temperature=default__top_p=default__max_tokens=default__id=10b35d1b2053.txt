The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions often, meaning that there will be times when police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (not green), the car should be idle, meaning it is not present. The car should also move when the traffic light is green, unless there is a police intervention or the car is already not present.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system's behavior is consistent: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the car's presence. The system then follows specific rules to determine its next state based on the car's presence and the traffic light's state. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ensuring that it can consistently respond to the car's presence.

In terms of specific requirements, the system must always follow these rules: when the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system must move to a state where it will turn the traffic light green; when the traffic light is green, the system must stay in its current state if the car is not present or if the car is present and the traffic light should remain green; and when the system is in the state where it will turn the traffic light green, it must turn the traffic light green and move back to its initial state.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's behavior is designed to ensure safe and consistent interaction with the environment, based on the specified assumptions and guarantees.
