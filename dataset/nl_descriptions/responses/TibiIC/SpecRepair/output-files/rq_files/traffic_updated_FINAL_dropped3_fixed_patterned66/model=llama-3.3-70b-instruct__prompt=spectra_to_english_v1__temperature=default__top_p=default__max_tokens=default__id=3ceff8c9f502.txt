The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency mode. The environment controls the presence of cars on both approaches (cara and carb) and the emergency mode (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for both approaches (greena and greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency mode is active, if the traffic light for approach A is red, then there is no car on approach A; when the emergency mode is active, if the traffic light for approach B is red, then the emergency mode remains active; when the emergency mode is not active or there is no car on approach A, the car on approach A will move or the traffic light for approach A will be red at the next time step; and when the emergency mode is not active or there is no car on approach B, the car on approach B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for both approaches are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on approach A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for approach A to green; and when a car is present on approach B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for approach B to green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light to green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
