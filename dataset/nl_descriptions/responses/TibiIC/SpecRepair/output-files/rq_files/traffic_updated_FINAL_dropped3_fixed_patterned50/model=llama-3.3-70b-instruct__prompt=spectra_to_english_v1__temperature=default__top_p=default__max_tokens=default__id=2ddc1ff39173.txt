The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when there is an emergency, the traffic light for road B should be green or the emergency condition should persist; when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next step; and when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. 

In the case of a car on either road A or B, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green after the car's presence is detected, and this response is repeated whenever a car is present. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the corresponding traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system will infinitely often return to this initial state, ensuring that it continually responds to the presence of cars on both roads.
