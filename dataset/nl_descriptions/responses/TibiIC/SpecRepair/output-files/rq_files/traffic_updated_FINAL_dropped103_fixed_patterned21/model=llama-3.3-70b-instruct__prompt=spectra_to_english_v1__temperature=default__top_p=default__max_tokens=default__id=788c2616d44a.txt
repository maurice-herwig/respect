The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, as well as an `emergency` signal. The system controls the outputs `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the emergency signal is not always active, meaning there are times when it is false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, the environment ensures that if a car is present at intersection A, it will either remain present or the traffic light at intersection A will be green at the next time step, unless the emergency signal remains active. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B. Furthermore, when the emergency signal is active and the traffic light at an intersection is green, the environment ensures that the car at that intersection will move, meaning it will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency signal remains active or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. It ensures that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at an intersection, the system eventually responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights turn red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green, and it will remain green until the car is no longer present. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
