The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and an emergency flag. The system-controlled outputs are the green light indicators for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, car A will not be present in the next time step unless the emergency is still in effect or the green light for car A is on. Similarly, when an emergency is in effect, car B will not be present in the next time step unless the emergency is still in effect, the green light for car B is on, or car B is not currently present. Additionally, when an emergency is in effect, car A will move and not be present in the next time step unless the emergency is still in effect or the green light for car A is off. Car B will also move and not be present in the next time step unless it is not currently present or the green light for car B is off.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of cars A and B by turning on the corresponding green lights. Specifically, when car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then enters a cycle where it remains in this state as long as the corresponding car is not present or the green light is on. If the car is present and the green light is off, the system transitions to a state where it must turn on the green light. Once the green light is on, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to the presence of cars.
