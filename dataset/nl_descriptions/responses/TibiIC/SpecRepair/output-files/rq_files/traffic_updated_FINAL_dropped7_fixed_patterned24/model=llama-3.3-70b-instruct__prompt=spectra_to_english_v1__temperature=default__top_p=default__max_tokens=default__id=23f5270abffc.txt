The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B (cara and carb) and whether there is an emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the light for road A is red, the car on road A will not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green, unless the car is already moving. Similarly, when there is an emergency, if the light for road B is red, the car on road B will not move until the emergency is over, the light turns green, or the car is already moving and was previously not moving. Additionally, when there is an emergency, if the light for road A is green, the car on road A will move until the emergency is over or the light turns red. The car on road B will move when its light is green during an emergency.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. However, during an emergency, the system ensures that it is always safe, but it does not explicitly turn the lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is as follows: if a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars.
