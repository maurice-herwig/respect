The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The environment controls the inputs "car" (indicating a car is present), "emergency" (indicating an emergency situation), and "police" (indicating police are present). The system controls the output "green" (indicating the traffic light is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is green, the car will either remain present or become present at the next time step, unless the light turns red or there is no car. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if the police are present or there is no car, the car will either not be present at the next time step or the light will be red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously monitors the presence of a car and adjusts the traffic light accordingly. If a car is present and the light is green, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it will periodically return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars repeatedly.
