The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the light is red for car A, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the light is red for car B, car B is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency or the light is about to turn green for car B. The environment is also assumed to ensure that car A moves when the light is green for car A, and car B moves when the light is green for car B, unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time, unless there is an emergency. When there is no emergency, the system ensures that if car A is present, the light will eventually turn green for car A. If car B is present, the system ensures that the light will eventually turn green for car B, but only if car A is not present or its light is also green. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green for a car if it is present, and will keep the light green until the car is no longer present. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
