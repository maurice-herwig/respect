The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars A and B are assumed to behave in specific ways: car A will not move when the light is red unless there is an emergency or the light turns green, and car B will not move when the light is red unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light is green for a car, that car is assumed to move, meaning it will not be present at the intersection in the next state.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green. Specifically, if car A is present, the system will respond by turning the light green for car A, and similarly for car B. The system also guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding lights green, and it does so in a way that ensures the lights are not both red at the same time. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to cars and emergencies.
