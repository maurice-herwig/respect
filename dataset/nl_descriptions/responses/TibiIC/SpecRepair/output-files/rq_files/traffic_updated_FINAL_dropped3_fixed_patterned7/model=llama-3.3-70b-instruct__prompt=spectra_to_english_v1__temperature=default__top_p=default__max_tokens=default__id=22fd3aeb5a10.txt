The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency flag. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency flag is set, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there should be no car at intersection A; when the emergency flag is set, if the traffic light at intersection B is red, then the emergency flag should still be set; when the emergency flag is not set or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will be red at the next time step; and when the emergency flag is not set or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green; and when there is a car at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars in this way. Additionally, the system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, where it is not responding to a car.
