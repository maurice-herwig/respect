The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light is red and there is no emergency, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, car B will remain present in the next state. On the other hand, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

In the initial state, the system starts with a default state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a pattern where it prioritizes turning the light green when a car is present, and it will keep trying to turn the light green until it succeeds. Once the light is green, the system will return to its default state after the car has moved.
