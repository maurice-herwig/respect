The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls these conditions, which are represented by the variables "highwater" and "methane". The system controls a pump, which is either on or off, represented by the variable "pump".

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when the water is high, it will eventually become not high if the pump was not previously on.

The system guarantees two behaviors. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity or the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump will be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and the water level, while the environment ensures that high water levels are temporary if the pump is not used. The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions, and the system-controlled output is the state of the pump.
