The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane becomes present, it will not be accompanied by high water, unless the pump is not operating.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if no methane is detected, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly tied to the presence or absence of methane, with the environment's behavior constrained to avoid certain combinations of methane and high water when the pump is not operating. The system-controlled pump output is determined by the environment-controlled methane input, according to the specified guarantees.
