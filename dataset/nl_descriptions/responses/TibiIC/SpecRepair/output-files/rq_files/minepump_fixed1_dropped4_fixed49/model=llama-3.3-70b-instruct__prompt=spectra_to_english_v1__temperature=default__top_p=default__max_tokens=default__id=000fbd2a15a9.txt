The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that when there is no high water, the pump will be off, as controlled by the environment.

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must be no high water. Secondly, the pump must be turned on at the next step unless there is no methane.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump's state is determined based on the presence of methane and high water, under the assumption that the environment ensures the pump is off when there is no high water.
