The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car will not start moving, and if a car is already present, it will not move unless the light turns green. The environment is also assumed to behave such that if a car is not present, it will either remain absent or the traffic light will be green when it appears.

The system guarantees certain behavior. It ensures that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

The system's behavior can be described in terms of its response to the presence of a car. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or if a car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car appears and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
