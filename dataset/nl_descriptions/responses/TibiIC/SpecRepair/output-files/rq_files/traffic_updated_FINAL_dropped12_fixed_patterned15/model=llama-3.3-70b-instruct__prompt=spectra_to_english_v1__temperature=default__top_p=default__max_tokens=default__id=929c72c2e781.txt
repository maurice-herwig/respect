The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic lights. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when the traffic lights are red, car A is idle unless there is an emergency. Additionally, when the traffic lights are red, car B is idle unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is about to turn green. The environment also ensures that car A moves when its traffic light is green, and car B moves when its traffic light is green, unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees certain behavior. When there is no emergency, the traffic lights for cars A and B are not both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its traffic light green. If car B is present, the system will eventually turn either its traffic light or car A's traffic light green. When there is an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red. The system also ensures that whenever car A or car B is present, it will eventually respond by turning the corresponding traffic light green, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the cars are not present or their traffic lights are green. If a car is present and its traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system repeatedly cycles through these states, ensuring that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars.
