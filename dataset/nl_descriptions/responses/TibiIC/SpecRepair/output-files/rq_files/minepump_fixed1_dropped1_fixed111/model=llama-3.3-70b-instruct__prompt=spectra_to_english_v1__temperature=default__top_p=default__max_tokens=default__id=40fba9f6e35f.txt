The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if the pump is off, then the next state will either have no high water or the pump will remain off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump will be turned on in the next state or there is no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system must ensure that the pump is managed appropriately based on the presence of methane and water, while the environment must ensure that the absence of the pump does not lead to high water if the pump remains off.
