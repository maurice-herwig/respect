The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the traffic light is green, the car will either remain present or the traffic light will remain green, or the car will not be present at the next time step. Additionally, if there is a police presence, or if there is no car, or if the car does not move at the next time step, or if the traffic light is not green, then the car will either not be present or not move when the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a state machine that starts in an initial state. In this state machine, if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a next state where it remains until the traffic light turns green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state, indicating that it will periodically respond to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of periodically turning the traffic light green in response to a car's presence is the primary requirement, and this is influenced by the environment's assumptions about car behavior when the traffic light is green and when there is a police presence.
