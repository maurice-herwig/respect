The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently off, it will remain off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next step. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

Note that the presence of high water does not affect the system's behavior as specified.
