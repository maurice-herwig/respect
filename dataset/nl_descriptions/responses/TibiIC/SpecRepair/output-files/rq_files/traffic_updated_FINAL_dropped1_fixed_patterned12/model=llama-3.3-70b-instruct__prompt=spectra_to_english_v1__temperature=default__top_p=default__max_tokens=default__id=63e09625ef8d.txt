The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as the occurrence of emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A do not move until the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency persists. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection B do not move until the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection B is green or there are no cars at intersection B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A move and do not stop until the next time step, unless the emergency persists or the traffic light at intersection A turns red. Likewise, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at intersection B is green, cars at intersection B move and do not stop until the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection B turns red.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. Specifically, the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection B green. During emergency situations, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present or the corresponding traffic light is green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must respond by turning the traffic light green. Once the traffic light is green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
