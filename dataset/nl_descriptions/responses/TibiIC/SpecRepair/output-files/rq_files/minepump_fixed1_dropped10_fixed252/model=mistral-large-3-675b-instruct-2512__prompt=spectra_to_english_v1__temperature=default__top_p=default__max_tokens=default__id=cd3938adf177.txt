**Requirements Description for the Minepump System**

The minepump system regulates a pump based on water levels and the presence of methane gas in its environment.

**Environment-Controlled Inputs:**
- `highwater`: A signal indicating whether the water level is high.
- `methane`: A signal indicating whether methane gas is detected.

**System-Controlled Output:**
- `pump`: A signal that controls whether the pump is on (`true`) or off (`false`).

**Assumptions on the Environment:**
- Whenever methane is absent (`methane=false`), the environment ensures that the pump is off (`pump=false`).

**Guarantees Provided by the System:**
1. **Safety Guarantee (Pump Activation):**
   The system ensures that if methane is detected (`methane=true`), the pump will be turned on in the next step (`pump=true`).
2. **Safety Guarantee (Pump Deactivation):**
   The system ensures that if methane is not detected (`methane=false`), the pump will be turned off in the next step (`pump=false`).

The system continuously monitors methane levels and adjusts the pump state accordingly, ensuring it reacts immediately to changes in methane detection. The water level input (`highwater`) is monitored but does not directly influence the stated guarantees under the given assumptions.
