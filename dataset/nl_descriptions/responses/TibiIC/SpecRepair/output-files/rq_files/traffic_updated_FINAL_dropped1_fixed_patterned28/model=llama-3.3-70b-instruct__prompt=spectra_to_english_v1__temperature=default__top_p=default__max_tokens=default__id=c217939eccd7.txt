The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as the occurrence of emergency situations, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when an emergency is present, cars at intersection A will either move or remain idle until the emergency passes or the light turns green. Similarly, when an emergency is present, cars at intersection B will either move or remain idle until the emergency passes or the light turns green. Furthermore, when an emergency is present and the light is green, cars at intersection A will move and not remain idle. Likewise, when an emergency is present and the light is green, cars at intersection B will move and not remain idle.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. At all times, at least one of the traffic lights at intersections A and B must be red. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green. During emergency situations, both traffic lights must be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light will eventually turn green when a car is present. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that it will always eventually return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car.
