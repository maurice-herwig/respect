The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the traffic light is red at intersection A, a car will not be present at intersection A unless an emergency situation is occurring, and if an emergency situation occurs, a car will be present at intersection A at the next time step. Similarly, when the traffic light is red at intersection B, a car will not be present at intersection B unless an emergency situation is occurring or the traffic light at intersection B is green, and if an emergency situation occurs, a car will be present at intersection B at the next time step. Additionally, when the traffic light is green at intersection A, a car will not be present at intersection A unless an emergency situation is not occurring, and if an emergency situation is not occurring, a car will not be present at intersection A at the next time step. The same assumption applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B will not both be red at the same time, unless an emergency situation is occurring. When a car is present at intersection A and the traffic light at intersection B is red, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green. When a car is present at intersection B and the traffic light at intersection B is green, the traffic light at intersection B will remain green. During an emergency situation, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will always respond to the presence of cars by eventually turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it will do so in a way that ensures the traffic lights are not both red at the same time, unless an emergency situation is occurring. The system will also ensure that the traffic lights return to a safe state after an emergency situation has passed.
