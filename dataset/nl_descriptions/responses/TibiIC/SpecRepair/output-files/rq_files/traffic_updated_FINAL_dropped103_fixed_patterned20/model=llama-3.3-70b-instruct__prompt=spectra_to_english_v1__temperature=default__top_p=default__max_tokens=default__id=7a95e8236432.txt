The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as the occurrence of an emergency (`emergency`). The system controls the state of the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that emergency situations do not occur frequently, and that when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A will not move until the emergency is over or the traffic light turns green, and will move when the traffic light turns green. Similarly, cars at intersection B will not move until the emergency is over or the traffic light turns green, and will move when the traffic light turns green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency, both traffic lights will be red.

The system responds to the presence of cars at intersections A and B according to a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will turn the traffic light green if it is not already green. The system will keep the traffic light green as long as the car is present, and will turn it red when the car leaves. This pattern ensures that the traffic light turns green in response to the presence of a car, and stays green until the car leaves.
