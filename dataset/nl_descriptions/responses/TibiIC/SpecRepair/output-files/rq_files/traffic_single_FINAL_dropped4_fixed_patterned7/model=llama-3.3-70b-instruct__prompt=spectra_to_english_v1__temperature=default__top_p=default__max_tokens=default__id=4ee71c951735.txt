The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent, become present, or an emergency will occur, and this condition will hold at all times. The car is also assumed to be idle when the traffic light is not green, unless it is already moving, and it will move when the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is characterized by a pattern where the system initially waits for a car to arrive. If a car is present and the traffic light is green, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green infinitely often when a car is present. 

The traffic light is initially not green, and the system ensures that it will turn green at some point when a car is present, as long as the environment satisfies the specified assumptions.
