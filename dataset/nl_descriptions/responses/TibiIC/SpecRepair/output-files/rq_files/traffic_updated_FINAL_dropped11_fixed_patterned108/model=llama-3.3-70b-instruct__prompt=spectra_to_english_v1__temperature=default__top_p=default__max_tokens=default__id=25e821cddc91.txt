The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain idle or the traffic light for road A will be green, or the emergency will persist. Similar assumptions are made for road B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for a road is green, the environment ensures that the car on that road will move or the emergency will persist, or the traffic light will turn red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. A similar guarantee is made for road B. The system also guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both traffic lights will be red.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of a car on a road follows a specific pattern: if a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green. Once the traffic light is green, it will remain so until the car is no longer present on the road. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars on the roads in a timely and safe manner.
