The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until its light turns green. However, when a car's light is green and there is no emergency, the car will move and its presence will be removed at the next time step. If a car's light is green and the emergency signal is present, the car's behavior is not specified.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. If the emergency signal is on, both green lights will be off.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. When a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light is green, the system will transition back to its initial state, indicating that it has responded to the car's presence. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of both cars A and B.
