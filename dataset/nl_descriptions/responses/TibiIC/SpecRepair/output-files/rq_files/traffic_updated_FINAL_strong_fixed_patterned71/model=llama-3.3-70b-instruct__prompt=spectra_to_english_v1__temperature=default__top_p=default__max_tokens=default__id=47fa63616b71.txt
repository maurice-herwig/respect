The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. The system also ensures that whenever a car is present, its light will be turned green in response, but this response is not immediate and may take some time.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn a car's light green when the car is present, and the system will return to its initial state after responding to the car's presence. This pattern ensures that the system will infinitely often be in a state where it is ready to respond to car presence.
