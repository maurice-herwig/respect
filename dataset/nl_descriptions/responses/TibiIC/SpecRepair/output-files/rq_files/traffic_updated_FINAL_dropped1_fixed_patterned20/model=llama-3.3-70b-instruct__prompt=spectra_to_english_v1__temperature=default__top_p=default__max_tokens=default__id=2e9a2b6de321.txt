The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that emergency situations do not occur frequently. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present, the environment ensures that if a car is present at intersection A, it will either remain present or the traffic light at intersection A will be green, or the emergency situation will end before the next time step. Similarly, if a car is present at intersection B, it will either remain present or the traffic light at intersection B will be green, or the car will not be present in the next time step. The environment also ensures that when an emergency situation is present and a car is present at an intersection with a green light, the car will either move or the emergency situation will end, or the traffic light will turn red before the next time step.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency situation, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green if it is not already green. If the traffic light is already green, the system will keep it green as long as the car is present. If the car leaves the intersection, the system will turn the traffic light red. The system will repeat this process indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic lights are never both red at the same time and that cars are given a green light when they are present at an intersection.
