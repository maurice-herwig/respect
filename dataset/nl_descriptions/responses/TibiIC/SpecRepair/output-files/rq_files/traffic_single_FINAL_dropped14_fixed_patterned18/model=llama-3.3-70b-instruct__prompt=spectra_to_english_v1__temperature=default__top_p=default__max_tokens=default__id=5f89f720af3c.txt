The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is not present when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the traffic light is green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the traffic light is green, if a car is present, it will move, and when the traffic light is not green, the car will not move.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green when a car is present. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and then based on the presence of a car and the state of the traffic light, it transitions between two states. In the initial state, if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a next state where it remains until the traffic light turns green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, indicating that it will periodically respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of responding to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green ensures safe and efficient traffic flow.
