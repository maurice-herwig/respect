The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: emergencies do not occur frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, it will remain present until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed. In normal situations, when car B's light is red and there is no emergency, car B will remain present until its light turns green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly for car B. If an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also ensures that whenever a car is present, its corresponding light will be turned green in response, as long as the environment behaves according to the assumptions.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn a car's light green when the car is present, and will return to a state where it is ready to respond to the next car presence. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to a car's presence, as long as the environment behaves according to the assumptions.
