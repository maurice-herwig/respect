The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not activate the pump if it was already active in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or there must be no high water. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state.

In summary, the system ensures safe operation of the pump based on the presence of methane and high water, under the assumption that the pump is not continuously activated by the environment.
