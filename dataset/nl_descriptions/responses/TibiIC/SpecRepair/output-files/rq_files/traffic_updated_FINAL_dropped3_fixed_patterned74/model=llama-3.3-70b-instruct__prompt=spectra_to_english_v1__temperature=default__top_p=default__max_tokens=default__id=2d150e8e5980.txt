The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and the emergency signal (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the emergency signal remains active or the traffic light for road B is green; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move (i.e., its presence will change) or the traffic light for road A will be red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

More specifically, the system's response to the presence of cars on each road can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars on each road.
