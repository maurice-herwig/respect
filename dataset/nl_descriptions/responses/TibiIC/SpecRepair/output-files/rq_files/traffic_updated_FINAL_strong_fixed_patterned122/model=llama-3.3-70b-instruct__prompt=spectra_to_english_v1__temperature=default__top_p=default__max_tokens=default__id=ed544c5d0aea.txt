The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergency conditions frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: at any given time, at least one of the traffic lights will be red. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency condition is present, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's behavior is also governed by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their corresponding lights green. The system ensures that it will always eventually respond to a car's presence by turning its light green. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system is designed to safely and efficiently manage traffic flow for two cars in response to their presence and emergency conditions, while ensuring that the traffic lights are always in a safe state.
