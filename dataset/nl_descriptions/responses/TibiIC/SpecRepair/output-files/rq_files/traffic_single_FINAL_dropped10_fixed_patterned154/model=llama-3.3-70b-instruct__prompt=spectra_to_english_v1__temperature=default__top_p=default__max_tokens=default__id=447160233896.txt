The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that there will be periods when no police intervention is occurring, and this will happen infinitely often. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the environment will either not have a car present, have an emergency, or keep the car present in the next moment, as long as the light remains red. The environment is also expected to behave such that when the police are present, or there is no car, the car will either not be present in the next moment or remain absent.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. It ensures that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be periods when no car is present, and this will also happen infinitely often.

The system's behavior regarding the traffic light is defined by a pattern that responds to the presence of a car. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to a car's presence. The system then follows specific rules: if a car is not present or is present and the light is green, the system remains in the ready state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the ready state. If the light remains red while a car is present, the system stays in the waiting state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the ready state, allowing it to respond to cars when they are present.
