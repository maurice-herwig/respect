The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it remains present until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it remains present until its light turns green. If an emergency occurs while a car is present and its light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, both traffic lights will turn red.

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of its response to the presence of cars. When a car is present, the system will respond by turning its corresponding light green, but only if the other light is not already green. This response is guaranteed to happen eventually, but the system may delay its response if the other light is currently green. Once the system has responded by turning a light green, it will keep it green until the car is no longer present or an emergency occurs.
