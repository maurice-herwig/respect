The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road A at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing or the light for road A is green. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road B at the next time step unless the emergency is still ongoing, the light for road B is green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that either the emergency is still ongoing, there is no car on road A at the next time step, or the light for road A is no longer green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when an emergency occurs, the light for road B is not green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. At all times, at least one of the lights for roads A and B must be red. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road B green. However, during an emergency, the system will keep both lights red.

The system's response to cars on the roads is governed by a pattern that ensures the lights are turned green in response to cars. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to be turned green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. This pattern ensures that the system regularly returns to its initial state, waiting for cars to arrive.
