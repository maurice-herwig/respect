The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic lights. The environment controls three inputs: `cara` and `carb`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which signals an emergency situation. The system controls two outputs: `greena` and `greenb`, which represent the state of the traffic lights at intersections A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning that there are infinitely many times when the emergency signal is false. Additionally, when the traffic light at intersection A is red, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when there is an emergency, car B is assumed to be idle unless the traffic light at intersection B is green. Furthermore, when the traffic light at an intersection is green, the corresponding car is assumed to move.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. Firstly, it ensures that when there is no emergency, the traffic light at intersection B is not green. Secondly, when car A is present, the system eventually responds by setting the traffic light at intersection A to green. When car B is present, the system responds by setting either the traffic light at intersection A or intersection B to green. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both traffic lights are red. 

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously checks for the presence of cars and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to this initial state, allowing it to continuously respond to the presence of cars.
