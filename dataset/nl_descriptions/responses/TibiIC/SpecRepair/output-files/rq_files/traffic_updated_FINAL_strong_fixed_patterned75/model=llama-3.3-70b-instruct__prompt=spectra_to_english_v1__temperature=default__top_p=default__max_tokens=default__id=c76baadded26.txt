The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly for car B. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also ensures that if a car is present and its light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it continuously responds to the presence of cars.
