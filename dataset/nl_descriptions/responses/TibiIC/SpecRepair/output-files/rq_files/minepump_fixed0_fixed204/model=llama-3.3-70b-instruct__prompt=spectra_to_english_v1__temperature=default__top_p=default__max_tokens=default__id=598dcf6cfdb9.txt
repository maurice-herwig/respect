The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: if the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, when the pump is on, the environment ensures that either there is no high water or there is no methane.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: if the water is high, then either methane was previously present or the pump will be turned on in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system's purpose is to control the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane presence, while the environment ensures certain conditions are met when the pump is operating. The system's behavior is defined by its response to these environmental conditions, ensuring the pump is managed appropriately to handle high water and methane detection.
