The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by the variables carA and carB, and emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by the variables greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present on road A and the light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the light is red, and there is no emergency, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present on either road and the corresponding light is green, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. The same applies to road B: when a car is present, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. During emergency situations, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that it will turn the corresponding light green, but only after the car has been waiting for some time. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will remember this and turn the light green at a later time, ensuring that the car gets a chance to pass. The system periodically returns to its initial state, ready to respond to new cars.
