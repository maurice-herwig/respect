The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for roads A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have frequent emergency situations, and it is also assumed that when the lights are red, car A will not move unless there is an emergency or it is already moving. Additionally, when there is an emergency, car B will either continue to have an emergency or the green light for road B will be on. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when car A's light is green, car A will move, and when car B's light is green, car B will move unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees that the lights will not both be red at the same time when there is no emergency. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for road A. When car B is present, the system will eventually turn on either the green light for road A or road B. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of cars by turning on the corresponding green light, and it will continue to do so as long as the cars are present. If a car is present and its corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green, and it will stay in this state until the light turns green. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
