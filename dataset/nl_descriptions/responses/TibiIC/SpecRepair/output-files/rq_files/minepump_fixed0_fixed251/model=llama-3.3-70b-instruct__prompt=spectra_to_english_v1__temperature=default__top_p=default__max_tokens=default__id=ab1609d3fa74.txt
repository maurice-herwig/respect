The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and whether there is methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that the environment behaves in such a way that if the pump was on in the previous state and remains on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, it is assumed that if the pump is off, then either there is no high water or there is no methane.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if the water level is high, the pump must be on, or it must turn on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is detected, the pump must turn off in the next state.

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane, while the environment is expected to behave in a way that respects certain constraints on the relationships between the pump state and these conditions.
