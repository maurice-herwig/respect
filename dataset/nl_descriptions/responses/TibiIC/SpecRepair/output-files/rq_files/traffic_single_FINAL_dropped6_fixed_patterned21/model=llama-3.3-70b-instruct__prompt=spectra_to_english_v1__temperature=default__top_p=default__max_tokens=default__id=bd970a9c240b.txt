The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. The car is also expected to move when the light is green, unless the police are present or the car is already not moving.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. The system ensures that when a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green. The system also ensures that the absence of a car is a recurring event.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. If the light is already green when a car arrives, the system remains in its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, indicating that the traffic light has turned green in response to a car's presence.
