The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red and the police are present, if a car is waiting, it will continue to wait. If the traffic light turns green and a car is present, the car will move, and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. The system ensures that whenever a car is waiting, the traffic light will eventually turn green, allowing the car to proceed. This is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system monitors the presence of the car and adjusts the traffic light accordingly.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and adjusts the traffic light. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car to pass. Once the car has passed (indicated by the green light being on), the system returns to its initial waiting state. This pattern of response ensures that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present, allowing the car to proceed safely.
