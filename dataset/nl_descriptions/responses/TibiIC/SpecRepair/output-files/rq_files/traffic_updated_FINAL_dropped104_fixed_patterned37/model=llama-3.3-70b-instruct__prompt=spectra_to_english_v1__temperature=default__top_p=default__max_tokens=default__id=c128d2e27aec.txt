The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency mode. The environment-controlled inputs are `cara` and `carb`, indicating the presence of cars from directions A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, indicating whether the system is in emergency mode. The system-controlled outputs are `greena` and `greenb`, representing the traffic lights for directions A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency mode is active, a car from direction A will not approach if the light is red, unless the emergency mode ends or the light turns green. Similarly, a car from direction B will not approach if the light is red, unless the emergency mode ends, the light turns green, or there is no car from direction B. Additionally, when the emergency mode is active, a car from direction A will move if the light is green, unless the emergency mode ends or the car is not present. A car from direction B will also move if the light is green, unless the car is not present or the light turns red.

The system guarantees that whenever a car approaches from direction A, the traffic light for direction A will eventually turn green, and similarly for direction B. This guarantee is fulfilled through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to approach. If a car approaches and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it ensures the light turns green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, repeating this pattern infinitely often. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to approaching cars.
