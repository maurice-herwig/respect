The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either not be present, or to become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency situation persists. Similar assumptions are made for cars at intersection B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at an intersection is green, cars at that intersection are assumed to either not be moving or to stop moving at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars in a timely manner. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car. The system remains in this state until the traffic light turns green, at which point it returns to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system periodically returns to a state where it is not responding to a car, allowing it to respond to new cars in a timely manner.
