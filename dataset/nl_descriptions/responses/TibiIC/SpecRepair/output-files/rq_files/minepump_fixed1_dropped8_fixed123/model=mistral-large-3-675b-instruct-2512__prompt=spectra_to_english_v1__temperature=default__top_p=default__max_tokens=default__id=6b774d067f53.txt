### **Minepump System Requirements**

This specification describes a **minepump system** that regulates water levels in a mine in response to environmental conditions. The system interacts with its environment as follows:

#### **Environment-Controlled Inputs (Uncontrollable by the System)**
1. **High water level (`highwater`)** – Indicates whether the water level in the mine is dangerously high.
2. **Methane presence (`methane`)** – Indicates whether methane gas is detected in the mine.

#### **System-Controlled Output (Controllable by the System)**
1. **Pump operation (`pump`)** – Determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The system assumes the following about the environment’s behavior:
- Once the pump is turned **off** (`pump = false`), it remains **off** in the next step unless the system explicitly turns it back on.

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure the following under all circumstances where the environment behaves as assumed:

1. **Pump Control Decision**
   - At every step, the system must decide whether to **turn the pump on** (`pump = true`) or **keep it off** (`pump = false`) in the next state.

2. **Safety Condition**
   - The pump must **never activate in the next step** (`next(pump) = false`) if **both** of the following conditions hold simultaneously:
     - The water level is high (`highwater = true`).
     - Methane is detected (`methane = true`).

This requirement prevents the pump from operating in hazardous conditions where methane and high water could pose an explosion risk.

The specification does not impose additional constraints on initial conditions or long-term liveness properties. The system’s behavior is solely defined by the above guarantees as long as the environment adheres to its assumption.
