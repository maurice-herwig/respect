The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when a car is present, it will either continue to be present or disappear at the next time step, unless there is an emergency, in which case the car's presence may change. Additionally, when a car is not present, it will either remain absent or disappear at the next time step, unless the traffic light is green, in which case the car may appear.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and then responds to the car's presence and the traffic light's state in the following way: if the car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it remembers the car's presence; if the car is present and the light is green, the system stays in its current state; if the system is in the remembering state and the light is green, it returns to its initial state; and if the system is in the remembering state and the light is not green, it stays in that state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state. 

The traffic light is initially not green, and the system's response to the car's presence is determined by its current state and the traffic light's state. The system's next state is determined by its current state, the car's presence, and the traffic light's state.
