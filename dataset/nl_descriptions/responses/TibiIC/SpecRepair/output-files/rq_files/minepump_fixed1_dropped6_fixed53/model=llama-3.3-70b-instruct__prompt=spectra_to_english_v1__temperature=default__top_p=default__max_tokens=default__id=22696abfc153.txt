The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that when there is no methane, the pump was not operating in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next state or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next state.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump operates based on the presence of methane and high water, while the environment's behavior is restricted such that the absence of methane implies the pump was previously off.
