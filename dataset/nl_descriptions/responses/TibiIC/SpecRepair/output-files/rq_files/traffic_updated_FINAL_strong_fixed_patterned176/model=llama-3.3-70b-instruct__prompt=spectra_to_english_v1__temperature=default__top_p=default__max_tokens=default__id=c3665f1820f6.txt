The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. On the other hand, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will make the car move, i.e., it will not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are not both red at the same time. Whenever car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, whenever car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the corresponding light green. If the system is not responding to a car's presence and the car is present, it will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.
