The Minepump system operates in an environment where high water levels and methane presence are detected. The environment-controlled inputs are high water levels and methane presence, while the system-controlled output is the pump's operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when the water level is not high in the next state, it will remain not high in the subsequent state, unless the pump is operating.

The system guarantees that if methane is present, then either the pump will be activated in the next state or the water level is not high. Additionally, the system ensures that if methane is not present, then the pump will not be operating in the next state.

In summary, the system is designed to control the pump based on the presence of methane and high water levels, with specific constraints on its operation to ensure safe and correct behavior.
