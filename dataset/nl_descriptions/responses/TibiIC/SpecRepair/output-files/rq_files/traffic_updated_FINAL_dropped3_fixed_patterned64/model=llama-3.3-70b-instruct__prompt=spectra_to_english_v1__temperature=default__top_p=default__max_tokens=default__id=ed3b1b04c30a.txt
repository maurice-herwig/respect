The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when the emergency signal is active, the traffic light at intersection B is either green or the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green; and when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to a car's presence by turning the corresponding traffic light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is already green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this responding state until the traffic light is turned green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to cars' presence over time.
