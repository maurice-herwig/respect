The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if the pump is currently on and was also on in the previous state, then in the next state, the water will not be high.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if the water is high and there is no methane, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

Note that the variables "highwater" and "methane" are controlled by the environment, serving as inputs to the system, while "pump" is controlled by the system, serving as an output.
