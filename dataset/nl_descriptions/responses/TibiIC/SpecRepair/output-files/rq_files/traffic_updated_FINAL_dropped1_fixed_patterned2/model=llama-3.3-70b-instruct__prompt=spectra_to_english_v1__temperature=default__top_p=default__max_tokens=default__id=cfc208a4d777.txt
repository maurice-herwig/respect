The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that the emergency variable will be false most of the time. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, the environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: if a car is present at intersection A, it will either move or the emergency situation will end, and if a car is not present at intersection A, it will not appear until the emergency situation ends or the traffic light at intersection A turns green. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B will never both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green, and similarly for intersection B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both traffic lights red.

The system also follows a specific pattern of behavior when responding to the presence of cars. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to appear. If a car appears and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This pattern of behavior ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars in a predictable and reliable way.
