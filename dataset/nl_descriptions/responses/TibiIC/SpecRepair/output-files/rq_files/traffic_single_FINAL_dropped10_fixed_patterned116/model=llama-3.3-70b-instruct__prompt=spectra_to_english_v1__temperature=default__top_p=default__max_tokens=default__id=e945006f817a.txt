The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that there will be times when the police are not present, and this will happen infinitely often. Additionally, when the police are not present, the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will either not be moving or will stop moving.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present, and this will also happen infinitely often.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the light green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system remains in its initial state as long as there is no car present or the light is green when a car is present. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
