The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the traffic light, which can be either green or not. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The system assumes that the environment will not have police interventions often, and when the traffic light is not green, the car will remain idle. Additionally, the system assumes that when the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move, but if an emergency occurs while the car is moving, it will stop. If there is no emergency, the car will also stop after moving while the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will often be green when a car is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will often be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or if a car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green in this state, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars.
