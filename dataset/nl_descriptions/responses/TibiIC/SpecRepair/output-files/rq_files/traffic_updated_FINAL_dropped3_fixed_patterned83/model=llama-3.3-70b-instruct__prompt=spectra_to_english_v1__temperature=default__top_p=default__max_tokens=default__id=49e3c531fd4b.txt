The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, indicated by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if the light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; similarly, when an emergency is in effect, if the light for road B is red, then the emergency must still be in effect. Additionally, when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red, and similarly for road B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green, and similarly for road B. The system responds to the presence of cars on each road by turning the corresponding light green, but only after any preceding cars have been cleared, as indicated by a pattern of response that ensures the light turns green when a car is present and turns back to red after the car has moved.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the light is already green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car to be cleared. Once the car is cleared or the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system repeatedly returns to its initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars on each road.
