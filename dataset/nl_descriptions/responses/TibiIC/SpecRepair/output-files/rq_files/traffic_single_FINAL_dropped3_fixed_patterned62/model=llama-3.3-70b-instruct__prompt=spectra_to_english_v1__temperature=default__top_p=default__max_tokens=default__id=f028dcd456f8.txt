The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is guaranteed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the police are present, if the car is not moving, it will either start moving or the traffic light will turn green, or the car will remain still. The environment also ensures that when the car is not moving, it will either continue not moving or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. 

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's response to a car's presence is governed by a set of rules that ensure the traffic light turns green when a car is present, and remains green until the car is no longer present or the traffic light turns red. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. 

In terms of safety, the system ensures that the traffic light will only turn green when a car is present or when it is already green and a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. 

The system also ensures that it will infinitely often return to its initial state, indicating that it is ready to respond to the presence of a car.
