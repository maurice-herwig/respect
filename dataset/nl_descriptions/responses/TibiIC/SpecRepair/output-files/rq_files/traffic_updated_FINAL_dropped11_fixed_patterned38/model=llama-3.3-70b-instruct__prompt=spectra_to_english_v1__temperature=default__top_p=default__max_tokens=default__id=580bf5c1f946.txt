The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have the emergency signal active often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false at some point in the future and remain false for some duration. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, cars on road A will not move unless the light is green or the emergency signal remains active, and cars on road B will not move unless the light is green or the emergency signal remains active. Furthermore, when the emergency signal is active and the light is green for road A, the car on road A will move, and similarly for road B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never be red for both roads at the same time, ensuring that at least one road has a green light. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road A, and similarly for road B. The system also guarantees that when the emergency signal is active, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is responding to the car. Once the light is turned green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to cars on both roads.
