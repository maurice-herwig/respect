The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that when there is no high water, the pump will be off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless there is no high water. Secondly, if the pump is to be turned off at the next opportunity, then there must be no methane present. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's conditions of high water and methane, with specific rules governing when the pump should be on or off in response to these conditions.
