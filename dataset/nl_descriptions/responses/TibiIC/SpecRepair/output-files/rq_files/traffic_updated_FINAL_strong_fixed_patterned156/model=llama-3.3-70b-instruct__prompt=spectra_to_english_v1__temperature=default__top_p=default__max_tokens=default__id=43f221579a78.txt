The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is not present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B: when it is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. In the event of an emergency, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of a car is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the car's light green when the car is present, and the car's light will remain green until the system decides to turn it off. This pattern ensures that the system will always return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
