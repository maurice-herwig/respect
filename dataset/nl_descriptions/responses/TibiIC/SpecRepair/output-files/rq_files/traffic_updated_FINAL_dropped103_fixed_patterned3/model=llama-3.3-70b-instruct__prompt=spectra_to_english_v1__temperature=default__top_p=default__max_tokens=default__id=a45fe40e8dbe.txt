The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation occurs, cars A and B will either remain idle or move only when their respective lights are green. Additionally, when the lights are red, cars A and B will not move unless an emergency situation is ongoing.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights will never be green for both cars A and B at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During emergency situations, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described in terms of initial conditions, safety requirements, and liveness requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The system's liveness requirements ensure that it will eventually turn the lights green for cars A and B when they are present.

If a car's light is already green, the system will keep it green as long as the car is present. The system will also ensure that if an emergency situation occurs, both lights will be turned red. The system's behavior is designed to ensure safe and efficient traffic flow, even in emergency situations.
