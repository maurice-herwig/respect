The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that the police will not be present infinitely often, and when an emergency is declared, the car will either be present in the next state or the emergency will still be declared or the traffic light will be green. Additionally, when the car is moving, it is assumed that it will eventually stop.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green when a car is present. This is achieved through a pattern of behavior where the system starts in an initial state and transitions between two states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light. The system ensures that if a car is present, the traffic light will be green, and if the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will try to turn the traffic light green. The system will return to its initial state once the traffic light is green.

Initially, the system starts in its initial state. The system's behavior is constrained by safety requirements that dictate the allowed transitions between states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light. The system also ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, indicating that it will regularly return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
