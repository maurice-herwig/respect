The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The environment controls the inputs: whether a car is present, whether there is an emergency, and whether the police are present. The system controls the output: whether the traffic light is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green and a car is present, the car will remain present in the next time step. When the traffic light is green, a car is present, and there is no police intervention, the car will move and not be present in the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will periodically turn green when a car is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and adjusts its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it will periodically return to this initial state, allowing it to respond to cars and turn the light green as needed.
