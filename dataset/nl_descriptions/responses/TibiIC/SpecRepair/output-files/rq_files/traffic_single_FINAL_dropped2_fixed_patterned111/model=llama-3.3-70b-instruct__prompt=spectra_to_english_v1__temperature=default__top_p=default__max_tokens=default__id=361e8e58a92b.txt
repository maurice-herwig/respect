The TrafficE2 system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in a specific manner: when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or become present only if there is an emergency or the traffic light is not green. Additionally, when the car is present and the traffic light is red (not green), the car will either remain present or move only if the traffic light turns green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This is achieved through a pattern of behavior where the system initially starts in a state (S0) where it is not responding to the car. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state (S1) where it is waiting for the opportunity to turn the light green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state (S0). The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state (S0), where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light (green or not). The system's guarantee of often responding to the car's presence with a green light is the primary requirement, supported by the environment's assumptions about car behavior in relation to the traffic light's state.
