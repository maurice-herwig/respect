The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if car A is not present, it will not become present at the next time step unless the emergency signal is still true or the traffic light for car A is green. Similarly, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if car B is not present, it will not become present at the next time step unless the traffic light for car B is green or car B is already present. The environment also ensures that when the emergency signal is true, if car A is present, it will not remain present at the next time step unless the emergency signal is still true or the traffic light for car A is not green. A similar assumption is made for car B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. Whenever car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car A green. Similarly, whenever car B is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car B green. When an emergency situation occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. The system remains in this state as long as the corresponding car is not present or its traffic light is green. If a car is present and its traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state.
