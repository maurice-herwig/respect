The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that police intervention will not occur infinitely often. Additionally, when the police do intervene, if the traffic light is not green, the car will be present in the next state, or the car will not be present. It is also assumed that if a car is present, it will either move or the traffic light will be green, and if the car is not moving, it will not be present or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green infinitely often, and whenever a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green. The system also guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

The system starts in an initial state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.
