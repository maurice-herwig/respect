**Minepump System Requirements**

The *minepump* is a reactive system designed to manage a pump in response to environmental conditions—specifically, the presence of high water and methane gas.

### **Environment-Controlled Inputs and System-Controlled Outputs**
- The **environment** controls two boolean signals:
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether water levels in the mine are dangerously high (`true`) or safe (`false`).
  - `methane`: Indicates whether methane gas is detected (`true`) or not (`false`).
- The **system** controls one boolean output:
  - `pump`: Determines whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The system relies on the following constraints on the environment’s behavior:
1. If the pump remains **continuously active** (`pump = true` for two consecutive steps), the environment must **eventually lower the water level** (`highwater` will become `false` in the next step).
2. If **methane is not detected** (`methane = false`), then either:
   - The water level is **not high** (`highwater = false`), **or**
   - Methane remains **undetected** (`methane = false`) indefinitely.

These assumptions ensure that the environment does not impose impossible conditions (e.g., forcing the pump to run indefinitely in high water without any eventual drainage or making contradictory methane/water state demands).

### **System Guarantees**
The system must enforce the following behaviors in response to the environment:

1. **Pump Control Under High Water**
   - If **high water is detected** (`highwater = true`), the system must **either**:
     - Immediately **turn off the pump** (`pump = false`), **or**
     - Ensure the pump is **turned on in the next step** (`next(pump) = true`).

2. **Pump Control Under Methane Detection**
   - If **methane is detected** (`methane = true`), the system must **either**:
     - Immediately **turn on the pump** (`pump = true`), **or**
     - Ensure the pump is **turned off in the next step** (`next(pump) = false`).

These guarantees ensure the pump reacts safely to hazardous conditions, either immediately or in the next step. There are no explicit initial conditions or liveness requirements beyond these constraints.
