The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the light for road A is red, then there are no cars on road A; when there is an emergency, the light for road B is either green or there is still an emergency; when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will turn red at the next step; and when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

More specifically, the system responds to the presence of cars on each road by turning the corresponding light green, following a pattern where the system initially waits for a car to arrive, then turns the light green when a car is present, and turns the light red again when the car is gone. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to cars on each road in a timely manner.
