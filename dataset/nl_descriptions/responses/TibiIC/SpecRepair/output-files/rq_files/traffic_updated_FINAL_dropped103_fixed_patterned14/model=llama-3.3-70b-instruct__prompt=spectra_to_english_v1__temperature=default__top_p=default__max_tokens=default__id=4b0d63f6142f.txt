The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars on road A are assumed to either not be present or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light for road A is green or the emergency situation persists. Similarly, cars on road B are assumed to either not be present or become present at the next time step when an emergency occurs, unless the traffic light for road B is green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for road A is green, cars on road A are assumed to either not move or move away at the next time step, unless the emergency situation persists. The same assumption applies to cars on road B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. The same guarantee applies to road B. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present on a road or the corresponding traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the traffic light green. Once the traffic light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
