The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment provides two inputs: highwater and methane, which indicate whether the water level is high and whether methane is present, respectively. The system has one output: pump, which indicates whether the pump is operating.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way such that if methane becomes present, the pump was not operating in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next state, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next state. 

These guarantees ensure that the pump operates in response to the presence of methane and the water level, while the assumption restricts how the environment can change the presence of methane.
