The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B: when it is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. However, if an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence follows a pattern where it prioritizes turning the corresponding light green when a car is present. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it waits for the light to turn green before returning to its initial state. This pattern ensures that the system periodically returns to its initial state, allowing it to continuously respond to the presence of cars.
