The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, based on the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the light for road B must be green; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light will eventually turn green when a car is present. The system will repeatedly turn the light green when a car is present, as long as the car remains present.
