The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when an emergency situation occurs, car A will not move if the light is red, unless the emergency situation continues or the light turns green. Similarly, car B will not move if the light is red during an emergency situation. Additionally, during an emergency situation, car A will move if the light is green, unless the emergency situation continues or the light turns red. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights will never both be red at the same time, and if car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car A. The same applies to car B. When an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: if a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will stay green until the car is no longer present. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
