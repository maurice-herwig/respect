### **Minepump System Requirements**

#### **Environment and System Variables**
The environment monitors two inputs:
- **Highwater**: A signal indicating whether the water level is dangerously high (*true* when high).
- **Methane**: A signal indicating whether methane gas is present (*true* when detected).

The system controls one output:
- **Pump**: A command to turn the pump on (*true*) or off (*false*).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must satisfy the following restriction:
- Whenever methane is detected (*methane = true*), the environment ensures that either:
  - The highwater signal will become *false* in the next state, **or**
  - The pump is already off (*pump = false*).

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure the following behaviors at all times:

1. **Pump Control During Methane or Low Water**:
   The system ensures that either:
   - Methane is detected (*methane = true*), **or**
   - The pump will turn on in the next state (*pump = true*), **or**
   - The highwater signal is *false*.

2. **Pump Shutdown When Methane is Safe**:
   Whenever methane is **not** detected (*methane = false*), the system will turn the pump off in the next state.
