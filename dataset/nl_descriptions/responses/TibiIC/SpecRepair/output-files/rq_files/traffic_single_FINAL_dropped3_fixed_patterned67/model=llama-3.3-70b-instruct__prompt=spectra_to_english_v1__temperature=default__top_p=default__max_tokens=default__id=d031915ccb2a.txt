The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the police arrive, if the traffic light is not green and there is no car, then a car will appear in the next state. It is also assumed that if there is a car, it will either move or the traffic light will be green, and if the car is not moving, it will not be present when the traffic light is red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when it does, if there is a car, the car will be allowed to pass. The system also ensures that there will be times when there is no car.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to appear. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green, allowing the car to pass. If a car appears when the traffic light is not green, the system will remember this and turn the traffic light green in the next state, allowing the car to pass. If the traffic light is green and there is no car, the system will remain in this state. The system will periodically return to its initial state, waiting for a car to appear.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.
