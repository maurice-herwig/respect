The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and there should be periods where the police are not present. Additionally, a car should not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. If there was an emergency in the previous moment, the car should not move unless there is a police presence or the light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the light green when a car is present. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the light turns green, but it may take some time.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the light is already green when a car is present, the system remains in its initial state. The system ensures that it will periodically return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
