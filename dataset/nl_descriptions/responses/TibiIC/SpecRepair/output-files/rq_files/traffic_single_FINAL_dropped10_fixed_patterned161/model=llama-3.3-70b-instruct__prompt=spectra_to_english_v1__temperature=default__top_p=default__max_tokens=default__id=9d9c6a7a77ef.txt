The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, with the environment providing input signals. The environment-controlled inputs are "car", indicating the presence of a car, "emergency", indicating an emergency situation, and "police", indicating the presence of police. The system-controlled output is "green", indicating whether the traffic light is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the traffic light is green, a car will move unless the police are present or the car is not present, and this condition will continue to hold even if the police or an emergency situation arises.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly over time. The system also guarantees that there will be times when there are no cars present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system will remain in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it repeatedly responds to the presence of cars.
