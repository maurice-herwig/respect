The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is not present when the light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the light is green, the environment is assumed to ensure that if a car is present, either the police are present, the car is not moving, or the car will not be present at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light turns green when a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. The system also guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the light green, but only after the car has been waiting for some time. Once the light is green, it will remain green until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red again. The system ensures that the light is green infinitely often when a car is present, allowing the car to pass through the intersection. 

The system also ensures that the absence of a car is a recurring event, allowing for other traffic to use the intersection.
