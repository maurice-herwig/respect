The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present in the next time step. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, and the other car is not present, the car will move and not be present in the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behavior: it will never have both green lights off at the same time, and when a car is present, its corresponding green light will eventually turn on. The system also guarantees that when an emergency situation occurs, both green lights will be off.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. When a car is present, the system will eventually turn on the corresponding green light. The system will keep its green light on until the car moves or an emergency situation occurs. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. The system will always ensure that at least one green light is on, unless an emergency situation occurs. 

The presence of car A and car B, and the emergency signal are the environment-controlled inputs, while the green lights for car A and car B are the system-controlled outputs.
