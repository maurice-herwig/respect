The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergency conditions often, meaning that eventually, the emergency condition will always be false. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its green light is red while car A's green light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A or car B is present and its respective green light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees certain behaviors: the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the green light for car A to green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the green light for car B to green. During emergency conditions, both green lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car by turning its green light to green. If a car is present and its green light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the green light to green. Once the green light is turned to green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of the car again. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its green light to green.
