The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally expected to be in a non-emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and car A is present, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves (i.e., is no longer present) in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car by turning its light green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the next car presence. The system always eventually returns to this initial responsive state.
