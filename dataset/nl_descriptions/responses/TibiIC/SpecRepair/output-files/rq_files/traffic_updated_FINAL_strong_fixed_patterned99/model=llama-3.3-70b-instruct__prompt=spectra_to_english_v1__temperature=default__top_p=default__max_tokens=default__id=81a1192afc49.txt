The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence signal will be reset.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B will not both be red at the same time. If a car is present, its corresponding green light will eventually turn on. When an emergency situation occurs, both green lights will turn off. The system also ensures that if a car is present and its light is green, the car's presence signal will be reset at the next time step.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning on their corresponding green lights.
