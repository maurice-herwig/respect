The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false at some point in the future and remain so for some duration. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red and there is no emergency, it will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next state. Conversely, when car A is present and its light is green, it will not be present in the next state, and the same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are not both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
