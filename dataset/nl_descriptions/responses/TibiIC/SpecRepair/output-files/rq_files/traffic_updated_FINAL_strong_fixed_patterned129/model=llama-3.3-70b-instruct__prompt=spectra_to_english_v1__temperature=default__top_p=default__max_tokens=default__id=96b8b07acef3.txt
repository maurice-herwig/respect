The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a present car has a green light. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, where it can respond to the presence of cars again. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
