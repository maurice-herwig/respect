The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light for car A is red while the light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the light for car B is red while the light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. If car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A to green. The same guarantee applies to car B. During an emergency, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is based on a pattern where the system initially is in a state waiting for a car to be present. If a car is present and its corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, indicating that the car has been serviced. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their corresponding lights to green.
