The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, when the traffic light is not green, the car will either not be present, be present and remain present, or an emergency situation will occur, and this condition holds at all times. Additionally, when a car is not present, it will either remain absent or the traffic light will not be green at the next time step, and this condition also holds at all times.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This guarantee is formalized by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in response to the car's presence. Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system then enters a cycle where it either remains in this state if the car is not present or if the car is present and the light is green, or it transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green if the car is present and the light is not green. Once in this waiting state, the system will remain there until the light turns green, at which point it will return to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence.
