The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if there is high water, the pump will not be activated. 

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be activated at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be activated at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is activated when methane is detected and deactivated when methane is not detected, under the assumption that the environment does not activate the pump during high water conditions.
