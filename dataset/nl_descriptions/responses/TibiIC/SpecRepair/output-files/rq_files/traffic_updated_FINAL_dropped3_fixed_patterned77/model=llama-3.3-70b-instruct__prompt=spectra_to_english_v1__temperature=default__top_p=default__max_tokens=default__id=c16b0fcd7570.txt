The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides input signals indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road B is red, then the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road B green. The system's response to a car's presence is defined by a pattern where the system initially waits for a car, then responds to the car's presence by turning the corresponding light green, and eventually returns to waiting for the next car. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in a state where it is waiting for a car.
