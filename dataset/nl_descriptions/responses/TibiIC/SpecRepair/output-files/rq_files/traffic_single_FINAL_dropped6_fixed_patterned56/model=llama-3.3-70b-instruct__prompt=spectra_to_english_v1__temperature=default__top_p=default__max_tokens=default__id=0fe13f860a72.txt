The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that police presence will not be ongoing indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the police are not present, the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will not move when the light is green, or if the car is already moving, it will stop when the light turns red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green often enough to allow the car to pass when it is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for the car to arrive. The system then responds to the car's presence by turning the green light on, but only if the car is present. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, allowing the car to pass. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that the car can pass through the intersection repeatedly.
