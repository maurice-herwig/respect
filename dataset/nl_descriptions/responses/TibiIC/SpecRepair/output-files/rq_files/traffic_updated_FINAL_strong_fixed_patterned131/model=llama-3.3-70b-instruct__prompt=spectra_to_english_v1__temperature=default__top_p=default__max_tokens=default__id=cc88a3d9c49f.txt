The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. The environment also ensures that when car A is present and its green light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the green light for car A to green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the green light for car B to green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both green lights to red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding green light to green. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the presence of a car. The system will remain in this state as long as the car is not present or its green light is already green. If a car is present and its green light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will respond to the presence of cars A and B.
