The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. Whenever car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. The system also ensures that if car A or car B is present, it will respond to their presence by turning its corresponding light green, but only after any previous response has been completed.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars A or B. The system's response to the presence of a car is to turn its corresponding light green, but it will only do so when it is in a state where it is ready to respond, and it will return to this ready state after responding. The system will always eventually return to this ready state.
