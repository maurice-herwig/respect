The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, meaning there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when an emergency is occurring, the car will not start moving until the traffic light turns green, unless the emergency situation continues. Furthermore, once the traffic light turns green, the car is expected to move.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light will turn green at some point.

The system's behavior can be described in terms of its initial state and its ongoing operation. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the presence of the car and the state of the traffic light. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is attempting to respond to the car. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system periodically responds to the presence of cars by turning the traffic light green.

In terms of specific requirements, the system must always ensure that if a car is present and the traffic light is green, it remains in a state where it is responding to the car's presence.
