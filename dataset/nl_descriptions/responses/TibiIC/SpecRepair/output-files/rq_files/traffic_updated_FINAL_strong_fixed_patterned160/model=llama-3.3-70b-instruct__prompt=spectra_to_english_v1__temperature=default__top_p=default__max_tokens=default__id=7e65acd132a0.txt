The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is eventually always the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will ensure that the car is not present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B: when it is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Furthermore, whenever there is an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is governed by a pattern that ensures the system responds to the environment's signals. Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the environment's signals. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their corresponding lights green, and it does so in a way that ensures the lights are never both red at the same time. The system repeatedly returns to its initial state, allowing it to continuously respond to the environment's signals.
