The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when there is an emergency, the traffic light for road B should be green or the emergency situation should persist. Additionally, when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A should move or the traffic light for road A should turn red at the next step. Similarly, when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B should move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

More specifically, the system's response to the presence of cars on the roads can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars again. The system will always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
