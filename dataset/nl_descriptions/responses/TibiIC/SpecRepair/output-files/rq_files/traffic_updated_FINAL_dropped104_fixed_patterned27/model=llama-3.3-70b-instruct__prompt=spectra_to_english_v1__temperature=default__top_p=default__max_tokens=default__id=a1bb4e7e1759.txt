The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency status. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the emergency status. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if a car is present on road A, it will either remain present, the emergency will continue, or the traffic light for road A will be green. Similarly, for road B, if a car is present, it will either remain present, the emergency will continue, the traffic light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency is in effect, if a car is present on road A and the traffic light for road A is green, the car will either not be present in the next state, the emergency will continue, or the traffic light for road A will not be green. A similar assumption applies to road B: if a car is present on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, the car will either not be present in the next state or the traffic light for road B will not be green.

The system guarantees that if a car is present on road A, the traffic light for road A will eventually be green, and if a car is present on road B, the traffic light for road B will eventually be green. This guarantee is achieved through a pattern of response where the system initially assumes a state (S0) where either there is no car present or the traffic light is green if a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to another state (S1) and remains in this state until the traffic light is green, at which point it transitions back to the initial state (S0). The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state (S0), indicating that the traffic light will periodically be green when a car is present.
