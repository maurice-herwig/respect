The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: when the traffic light is red (not green), the car should not start moving unless the light turns green or the car remains idle. Additionally, when the police are present or there is no car, the car should not move unless the traffic light is green or the car stops moving.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of two states, S0 and S1. Initially, the system is in state S0. The system remains in state S0 as long as there is no car or the car is present and the light is green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to state S1. In state S1, the system waits for the light to turn green and then returns to state S0. The system repeatedly returns to state S0, ensuring that the traffic light is often green when a car is present.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of often turning the traffic light green when a car is present ensures safe and efficient traffic flow.
