The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in a specific manner: when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or become present only when the traffic light is green. Additionally, when the police are present, the car will either be absent or will not move when the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a state machine that ensures the system remains in a safe state. Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. 

The system's safety requirements dictate that it will remain in this initial state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. If the traffic light remains red while in this waiting state, the system will stay in this state. 

The system is also required to infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically resets and prepares to respond to the car's presence again. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
