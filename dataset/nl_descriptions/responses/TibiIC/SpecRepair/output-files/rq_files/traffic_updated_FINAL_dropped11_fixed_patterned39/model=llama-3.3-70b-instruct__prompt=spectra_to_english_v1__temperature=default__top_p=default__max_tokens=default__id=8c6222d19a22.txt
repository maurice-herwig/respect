The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain idle or move next, unless the light for road A is green or the emergency ends. Similar assumptions are made for road B. The environment also ensures that when the light is green for a road and an emergency occurs, cars on that road will move or the emergency will end.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green. The same guarantee is made for road B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green when a car is present. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states: one where it is ready to respond, and another where it is waiting for the car to be served. The system remains in the ready state as long as there is no car or the light is already green. When a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to the waiting state and remains there until the light is turned green, at which point it returns to the ready state. The system will infinitely often be in the ready state.
