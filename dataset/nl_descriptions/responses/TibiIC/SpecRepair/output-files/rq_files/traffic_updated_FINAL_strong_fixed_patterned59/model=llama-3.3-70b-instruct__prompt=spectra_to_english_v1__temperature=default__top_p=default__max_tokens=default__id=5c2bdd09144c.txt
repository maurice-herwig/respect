The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. When car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by turning its light green. The same guarantee applies to car B. During an emergency, both lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when the car is present. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is responding. Once the system is responding and the car's light is green, it returns to the non-responding state. The system always eventually returns to the non-responding state, ensuring that it responds to the car's presence.
