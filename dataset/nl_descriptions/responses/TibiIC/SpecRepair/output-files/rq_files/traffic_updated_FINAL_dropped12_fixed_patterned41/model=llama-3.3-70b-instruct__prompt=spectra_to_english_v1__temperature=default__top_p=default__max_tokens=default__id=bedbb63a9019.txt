The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic lights. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the light is red for car A, it is assumed that car A will be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the light is red for car B, it is assumed that car B will be idle unless there is an emergency or the light turns green for car B. The environment is also assumed to behave in a way that when car A is present and the light is green, car A will move, and when car B is present and the light is green, car B will move unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees that the lights will not both be red at the same time, unless there is an emergency. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car A. When car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car B, but only if the light is not already green for car A. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green, and it will do so in a way that ensures the light will eventually turn green when a car is present. The system will also ensure that the light will turn red when a car is no longer present or when there is an emergency. Overall, the system is designed to safely and efficiently manage the flow of traffic in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations.
