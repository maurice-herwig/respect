The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it remains idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it remains idle until its light turns green. However, when car A's light is green, it moves and its presence is no longer detected. Car B behaves similarly when its light is green and there is no emergency.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system eventually responds by turning its light green. The same response is guaranteed for car B when it is present. If an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both lights turn red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of a car is to eventually turn its corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures it can repeatedly respond to the presence of cars over time.
