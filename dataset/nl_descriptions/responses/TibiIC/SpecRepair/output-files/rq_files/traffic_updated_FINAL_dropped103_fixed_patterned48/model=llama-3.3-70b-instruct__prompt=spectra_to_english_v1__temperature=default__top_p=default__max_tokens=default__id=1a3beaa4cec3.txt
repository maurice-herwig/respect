The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the system is in an emergency state, the environment is assumed to not have car A or car B moving when the corresponding traffic light is red, and to have car A or car B moving when the corresponding traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car A green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for car B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any cars. When a car is present and its traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system transitions back to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their corresponding traffic lights green.
