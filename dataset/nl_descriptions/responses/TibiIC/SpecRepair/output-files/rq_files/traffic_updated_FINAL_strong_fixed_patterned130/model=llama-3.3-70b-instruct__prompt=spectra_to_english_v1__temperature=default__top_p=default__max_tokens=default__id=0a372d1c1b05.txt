The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have emergency situations occur frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present until its light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red. 

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or if a car is present and its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars.
