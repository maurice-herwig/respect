The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning there are times when it is not emergency. When car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, and there is no emergency, it will remain present until its light turns green. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, it will move and no longer be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both green lights off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. During an emergency, both green lights will be turned off.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and its green light is on, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and its green light is off, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car's green light to turn on. Once the green light turns on, the system returns to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely, ensuring that the system eventually responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning on their corresponding green lights.
