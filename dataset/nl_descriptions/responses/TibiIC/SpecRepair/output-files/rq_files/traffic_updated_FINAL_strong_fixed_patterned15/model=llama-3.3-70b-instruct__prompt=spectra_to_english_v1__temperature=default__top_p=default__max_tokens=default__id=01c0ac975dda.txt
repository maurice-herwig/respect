The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergency situations frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain idle. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: at any given time, at least one of the traffic lights will be red. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its traffic light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its traffic light green. During emergency situations, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern called pRespondsToS, which ensures that when a car is present, its corresponding traffic light will be turned green in a timely manner. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system then transitions between states based on the car's presence and the traffic light's status, ensuring that the traffic light is turned green when the car is present and turned red when the car is no longer present. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the car's presence over time.
