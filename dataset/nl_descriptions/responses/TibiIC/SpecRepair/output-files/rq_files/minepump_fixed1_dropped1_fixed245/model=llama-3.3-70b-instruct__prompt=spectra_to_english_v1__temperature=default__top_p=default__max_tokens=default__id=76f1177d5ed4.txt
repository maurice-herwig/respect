The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the `highwater` and `methane` conditions. The system controls the `pump` output. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever methane is present, either the water level will not be high in the next state or the pump will not be activated. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if no methane is detected, then the pump must not be activated in the next state.

In summary, the system ensures safe operation of the pump based on the presence of methane and water level, under the assumption that the environment's behavior regarding methane presence and water level is constrained as specified. The environment-controlled inputs are `highwater` and `methane`, and the system-controlled output is `pump`.
