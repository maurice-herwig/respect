The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either remain idle or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green. Similarly, cars at intersection B are assumed to either remain idle or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection B is green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A are assumed to either not be present, become absent at the next time step, or the traffic light at intersection A is red. A similar assumption is made for cars at intersection B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
