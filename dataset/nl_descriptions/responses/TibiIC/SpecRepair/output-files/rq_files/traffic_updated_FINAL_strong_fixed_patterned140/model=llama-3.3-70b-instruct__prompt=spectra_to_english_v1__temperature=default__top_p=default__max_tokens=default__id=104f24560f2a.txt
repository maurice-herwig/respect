The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain idle and not move. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is on, car B will remain idle and not move. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is on, it will move and no longer be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its green light is on.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. In emergency situations, the system will turn off both green lights for car A and car B. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is such that when a car is present, the system will eventually turn on the corresponding green light, and this response is persistent in the sense that the system will continue to respond to the car's presence until the car is no longer present.
