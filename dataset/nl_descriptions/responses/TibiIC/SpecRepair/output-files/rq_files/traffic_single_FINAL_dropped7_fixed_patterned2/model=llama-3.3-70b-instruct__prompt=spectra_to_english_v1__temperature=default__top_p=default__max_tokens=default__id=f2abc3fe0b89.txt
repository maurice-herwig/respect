The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or the traffic light will turn green before the car appears. The environment is also assumed to behave such that when the police are present, the car will either be absent or will not move, or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to appear. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. If a car appears when the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to this initial state. 

The traffic light will be green infinitely often when a car is present, ensuring that cars are allowed to pass through the intersection regularly.
