The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green (i.e., red), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car remains still. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, the car is either not present or will not move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light green. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described by a set of rules that determine its next state based on the current state and the presence of a car. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car.
