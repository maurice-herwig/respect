The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the levels of water and methane. The system must ensure that if methane is present, then either the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity or there is no high water. Additionally, the system must always ensure that there is no methane.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when there is high water, the pump is not turned on. Furthermore, when methane is present, the pump is not turned on.

In summary, the system's output is the state of the pump, and the environment's inputs are the levels of water and methane. The system must adhere to the guarantees of handling methane presence and absence while considering the environment's assumed behavior regarding high water and methane levels.
