The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is not present when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The environment is also expected to ensure that when a car is present, it will either be accompanied by police, or the car will move or the traffic light will turn red at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behavior. Firstly, it ensures that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, infinitely often. Secondly, the system guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described by a pattern of response to the car's presence. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of a car. 

The system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal, while the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence.
