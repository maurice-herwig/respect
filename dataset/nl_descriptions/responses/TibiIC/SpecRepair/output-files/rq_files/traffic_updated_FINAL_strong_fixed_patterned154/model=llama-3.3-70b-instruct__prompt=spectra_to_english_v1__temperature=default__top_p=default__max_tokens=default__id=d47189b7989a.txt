The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. When a car is present and the corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Specifically, if car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, if car B is present, its light is red, and car A's light was red in the previous time step, car B will remain present in the next time step. On the other hand, when a car's light is green, the car will move and no longer be present in the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behaviors: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green, but only after the car has been waiting for some time. The system will repeatedly cycle between waiting for a car to arrive and turning the corresponding light green. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights to ensure safe and efficient flow of cars A and B, while responding to emergency situations and the presence of the cars.
