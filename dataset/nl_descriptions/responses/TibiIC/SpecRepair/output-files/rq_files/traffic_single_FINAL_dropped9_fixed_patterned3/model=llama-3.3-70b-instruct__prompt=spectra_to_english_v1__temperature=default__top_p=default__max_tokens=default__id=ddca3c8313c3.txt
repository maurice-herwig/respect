The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It should not have police presence indefinitely; there will be times when there is no police presence. Additionally, when the police are present, if a car is about to arrive, it must either arrive or not be present initially. The environment should also ensure that when the police are present, a car will either not move, not be present initially, move away, or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green when a car is present. This response is defined by a pattern that outlines the system's behavior in terms of its state. Initially, the system is in a state where it does not respond to the car's presence. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it awaits the car's passage. Once the car passes (indicated by the traffic light turning green), the system returns to its initial state. The system remains in this initial state as long as a car is not present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. The system will infinitely often be in this initial state, ensuring that it periodically resets to await new car arrivals.

In essence, the TrafficE2 system is designed to manage traffic flow by responding to the presence of cars while considering the constraints imposed by the environment, such as police presence and emergency situations.
