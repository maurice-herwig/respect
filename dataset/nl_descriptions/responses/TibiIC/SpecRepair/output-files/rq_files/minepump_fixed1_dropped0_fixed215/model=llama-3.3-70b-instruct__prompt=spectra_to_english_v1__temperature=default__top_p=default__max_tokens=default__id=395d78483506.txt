The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane is present, then either the water level will not be high in the next state or the pump was not running in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on in the next state or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the presence of methane and high water, under the assumption that the environment will behave in a specific way when methane is present.
