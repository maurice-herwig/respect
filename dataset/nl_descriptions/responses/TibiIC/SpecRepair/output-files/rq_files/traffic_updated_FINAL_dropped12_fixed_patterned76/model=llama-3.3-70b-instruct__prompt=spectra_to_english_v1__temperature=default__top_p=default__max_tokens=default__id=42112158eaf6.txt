The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the traffic light is red at intersection A, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the traffic light is red at intersection B, car B is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. The environment is also assumed to ensure that car A moves when the traffic light is green at intersection A, and car B moves when the traffic light is green at intersection B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are not both red at the same time, unless there is an emergency. When there is no emergency, the system ensures that at least one of the traffic lights is green. The system also guarantees that when car A is present, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green. When car B is present, the traffic light at either intersection A or B will eventually turn green. In the event of an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously monitors the presence of cars and emergencies, updating its state accordingly. When a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system transitions back to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
