The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police intervention. The environment controls the variables "car" (indicating whether a car is present), "emergency" (indicating an emergency situation), and "police" (indicating police presence). The system controls the variable "green" (indicating whether the traffic light is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that police will not be present infinitely often, and when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will either not move or an emergency will be in effect, or the car will not be present in the next state. Additionally, when a car is present and was present in the previous state, it will either move (not be present in the next state) or the light will not be green in the next state.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. The system's behavior regarding the traffic light is further defined by a pattern that ensures the light responds to the presence of a car. Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to a car's presence. The system then follows specific rules: if a car is present and the light is not green, it moves to a state where it waits for the light to turn green; if the light is green when a car is present, it stays in the initial state; if the light turns green while in the waiting state, it returns to the initial state. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to its initial state, where it can respond to a car's presence.
