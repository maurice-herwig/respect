The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that there will be periods when police are not present, and the car will sometimes not be present. Additionally, the car will only start moving when the traffic light is green, unless it was already moving. The car will also stop moving when the police are present or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and there will be periods when no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the car's presence and the traffic light's state, updating its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the car's presence.
