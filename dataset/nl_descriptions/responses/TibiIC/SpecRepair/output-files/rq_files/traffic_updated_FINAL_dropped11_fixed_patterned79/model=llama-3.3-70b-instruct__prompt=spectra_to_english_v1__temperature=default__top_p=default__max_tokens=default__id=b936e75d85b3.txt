The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is restricted in its behavior: if car A is not present and the light is red, then either the emergency situation continues, the light turns green for car A, or car A becomes present in the next state. Similarly, if car B is not present and the light is red, then either the emergency situation continues or the light turns green for car B in the next state. The environment is also assumed to behave in certain ways when the emergency variable is true and the lights are green: car A will move and may not be present in the next state, and car B will move and may not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for car A, and similarly for car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state. 

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in the waiting state. The safety requirements are that the system will not have both lights red at the same time, and that the system will respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding lights green. The liveness requirement is that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of cars. The next-state relationships are defined by the system's state transitions, which depend on the presence of cars and the state of the emergency variable.
