The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving, or the light is about to turn green. Additionally, when the light is red, the car will not move unless the police are present or the car is not moving, or the light remains red.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the light green when a car is present. This response is characterized by a pattern where the system initially waits for a car to arrive. When a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, indicating that it has responded to the car's presence. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, indicating that it will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars.

The system starts in its initial state, waiting for a car to arrive. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will only transition between its states in response to the presence or absence of a car and the state of the light. The system's liveness requirement ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, indicating that it will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
