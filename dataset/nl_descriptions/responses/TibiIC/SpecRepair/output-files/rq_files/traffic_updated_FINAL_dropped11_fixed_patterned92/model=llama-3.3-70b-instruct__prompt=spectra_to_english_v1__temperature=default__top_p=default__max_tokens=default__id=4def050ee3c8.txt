The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when an emergency is present, car A will not move until the emergency is over, the light at intersection A is green, or car A is already moving. Similarly, when an emergency is present, car B will not move until the emergency is over or the light at intersection B is green. Furthermore, when an emergency is present and the light at intersection A is green, car A will move, and when an emergency is present and the light at intersection B is green, car B will move.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. At all times, at least one of the traffic lights at intersections A and B must be red. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at intersection B green. During an emergency, both traffic lights must be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's presence. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's presence to be acknowledged. Once the car's presence is acknowledged by turning the corresponding light green, the system returns to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars at intersections A and B.
