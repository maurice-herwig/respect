The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars at points A and B (cara and carb) and the occurrence of an emergency. The system-controlled outputs are the states of the traffic lights at points A and B (greena and greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when the traffic light at point A is red, car A is idle unless an emergency is occurring. When the traffic light at point B is red, if an emergency is occurring, then either the emergency continues or the traffic light at point B turns green. Furthermore, when the traffic light at point A is green, car A moves, and when the traffic light at point B is green, car B moves unless an emergency is occurring.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at points A and B are never both red when there is no emergency. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at point A green. If car B is present, the system will eventually turn either the traffic light at point A or point B green. When an emergency occurs, both traffic lights turn red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously monitors the environment and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic lights respond to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
