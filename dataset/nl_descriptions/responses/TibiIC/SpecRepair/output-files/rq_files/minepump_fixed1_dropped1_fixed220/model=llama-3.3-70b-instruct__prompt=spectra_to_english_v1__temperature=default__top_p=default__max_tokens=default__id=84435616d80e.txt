The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls these conditions, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if there is no methane, then if the pump was not operating in the previous state, the water will not be high in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be operating in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must not be operating in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly dependent on the presence or absence of methane in the environment, with the additional constraint on the environment's behavior regarding water levels when the pump is not operating and there is no methane.
