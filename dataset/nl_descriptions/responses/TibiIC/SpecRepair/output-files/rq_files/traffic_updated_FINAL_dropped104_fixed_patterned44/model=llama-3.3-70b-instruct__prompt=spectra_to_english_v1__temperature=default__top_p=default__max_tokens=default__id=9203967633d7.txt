The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency condition. The environment controls the presence of cars on approaches A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the emergency condition, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights for approaches A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency condition is true, if a car is approaching from direction A, it will either continue to approach, the emergency condition will persist, or the traffic light for approach A will be green. Similarly, when the emergency condition is true, if a car is approaching from direction B, it will either continue to approach, the emergency condition will persist, the traffic light for approach B will be green, or there will be no car approaching from direction B. Additionally, when the emergency condition is true, if a car is approaching from direction A and the traffic light for approach A is green, the car will either stop approaching, the emergency condition will persist, the traffic light for approach A will turn red, or the car will continue to approach but the traffic light for approach A will turn red. A similar assumption is made for approach B.

The system guarantees that whenever a car approaches from direction A, the traffic light for approach A will eventually turn green, and whenever a car approaches from direction B, the traffic light for approach B will eventually turn green. This guarantee is satisfied by the system entering a state where it responds to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars.
