**Minepump System Requirements**

The *Minepump* system controls a pump in response to two environment-controlled inputs: a **high water level** signal and a **methane presence** signal. Both inputs are boolean, with `true` indicating detection (high water or methane present) and `false` indicating their absence.

### Assumptions on the Environment
The environment must satisfy the following condition:
- If the high water level is *not* detected in the next state (`highwater = false`), then either this condition persists in the following state, or the pump must currently be off (`pump = false`). This ensures the environment does not abruptly reintroduce high water unless the pump has been inactive.

### System Guarantees
The system controls the pump (`pump`) and must ensure the following requirements hold at all times:

1. **Safety Guarantee:**
   - The pump must either **remain off** in the next state, **methane must be present**, or **high water must not be detected**. This prevents the pump from activating when unsafe conditions persist.

2. **Methane Response Guarantee:**
   - If **methane is not detected**, the pump must **turn off** in the next state. This ensures the pump never runs while methane is absent, prioritizing safety over water removal.

No initial conditions or liveness requirements are specified. The system’s behavior is solely defined by the above guarantees and their interplay with the environment’s inputs.
