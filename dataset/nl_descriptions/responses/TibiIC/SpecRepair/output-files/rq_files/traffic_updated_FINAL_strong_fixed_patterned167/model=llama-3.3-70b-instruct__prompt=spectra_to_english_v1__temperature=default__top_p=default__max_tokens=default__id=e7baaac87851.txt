The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergencies often, and when a car is present at a red light, it will remain idle until the light turns green. Specifically, car A will not move when its light is red and there is no emergency, and car B will not move when its light is red. Conversely, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system must satisfy several guarantees. First, it must ensure that both traffic lights are never red at the same time. When car A is present, the system must eventually turn its light green, and similarly, when car B is present, the system must eventually turn its light green. During emergencies, the system must turn both lights red.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously monitors the presence of cars and the state of the emergency, updating its state and the traffic lights accordingly. The system ensures that whenever a car is present, its corresponding light will eventually turn green, and the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence.
