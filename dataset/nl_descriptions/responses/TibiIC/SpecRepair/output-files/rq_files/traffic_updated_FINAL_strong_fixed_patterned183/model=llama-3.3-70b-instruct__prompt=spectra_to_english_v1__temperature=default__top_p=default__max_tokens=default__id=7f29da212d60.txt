The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, represented by the variables carA and carB, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, represented by the variables greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency condition often, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other light is green, the car will remain idle until its light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees certain behavior: it will never have both lights green at the same time, and when a car is present, its corresponding light will eventually turn green. The system also guarantees that when an emergency condition is present, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. When a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning its corresponding light green. If the system is not responding to a car's presence and the car is present, it will move to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to cars' presence.
