The TrafficE2 system controls traffic flow based on input from the environment. The environment provides signals indicating the presence of cars A and B, as well as an emergency status. The system controls the green light indicators for cars A and B.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A, the presence of car B, and the emergency status. The system-controlled outputs are the green light indicators for cars A and B.

The system assumes that the environment will behave in the following ways: when car A is not present, it will not become present unless the green light for car A is on, an emergency is occurring, or car A is already present. Similarly, when car B is not present, it will not become present unless the green light for car B is on or an emergency is occurring, or car B is already present. Additionally, the system assumes that if an emergency is occurring, it will continue to occur. The system also assumes that if car B is not moving, it will either remain not moving or the green light for car B will be off.

The system guarantees that when car A is present, the green light for car A will be on, and when car B is present, the green light for car B will be on. This guarantee is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a signal from the environment. If the signal is present, the system responds by turning on the corresponding green light. If the signal is not present, the system remains in its initial state. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ensuring that the green light is turned on whenever the corresponding car is present.

Initially, the system is in its waiting state. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always respond correctly to the environment's signals, turning on the green light when a car is present and turning it off when the car is not present. The system's liveness requirement ensures that it will periodically return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to new signals from the environment.
