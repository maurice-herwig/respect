**Minepump System Requirements**

The Minepump system monitors and controls water levels in a mine while accounting for hazardous gas conditions. The environment provides two inputs: a `highwater` signal indicating whether water levels are dangerously high, and a `methane` signal detecting the presence of flammable gas. The system controls a single output, the `pump`, which drains water when activated.

**Environment Assumptions:**
The environment ensures that if the water level was *not* high in the previous moment, it will either remain at a safe level or the pump was inactive in the current moment. This prevents abrupt, unrealistic rises in water level unless the pump fails to operate.

**System Guarantees:**
1. **Safety Condition:** At all times, either:
   - Methane is detected, *or*
   - The pump will activate in the next moment, *or*
   - The water level is not high.
   This ensures the pump does not run in the presence of methane unless water levels remain dangerously high.

2. **Pump Shutdown:** If methane is *not* present, the pump must deactivate in the next moment. This prevents unnecessary pump operation when gas conditions are safe.
