The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not introduce methane when the pump is already operating. In other words, whenever methane becomes present, it must be that the pump was not operating in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

These guarantees ensure the system responds appropriately to the environment's conditions, specifically the presence or absence of methane, while considering the water level.
