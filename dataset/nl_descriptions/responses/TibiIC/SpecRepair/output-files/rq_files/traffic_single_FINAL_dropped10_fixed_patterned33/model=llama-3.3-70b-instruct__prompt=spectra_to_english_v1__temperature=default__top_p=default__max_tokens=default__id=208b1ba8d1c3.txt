The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and there should be periods where the police are not present. Additionally, the environment should ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the traffic light is red, the environment should ensure that the car does not move unless the police are present or the car was already moving.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and then transitions between two states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light. The system remains in the initial state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a secondary state. The system returns to the initial state when the traffic light is turned green. The system is required to infinitely often return to the initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green.
