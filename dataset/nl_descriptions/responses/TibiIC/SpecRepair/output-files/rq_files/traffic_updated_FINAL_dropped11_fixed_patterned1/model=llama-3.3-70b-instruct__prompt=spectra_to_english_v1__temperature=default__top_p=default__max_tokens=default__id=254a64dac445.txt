The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red at intersection A, car A is idle, meaning it will not move, unless an emergency is present or the light turns green. Similarly, when the traffic light is red at intersection B, car B is idle unless an emergency is present or the light turns green. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green at intersection A, car A moves, meaning it will not remain present, unless an emergency is present or the light turns red. The same applies to car B at intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green at intersection A. The same applies to intersection B. Additionally, when an emergency is present, the system will turn both traffic lights red.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any cars. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car to be served. Once the car is served, the system will transition back to its initial state. This cycle repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
