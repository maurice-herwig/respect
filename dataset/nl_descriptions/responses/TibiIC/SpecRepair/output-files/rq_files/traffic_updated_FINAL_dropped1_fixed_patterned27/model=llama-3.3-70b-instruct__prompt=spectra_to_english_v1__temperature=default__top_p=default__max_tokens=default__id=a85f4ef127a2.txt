The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, and when an emergency does occur, cars A and B will not move if their respective lights are red, unless the emergency persists or the light turns green. Additionally, when the emergency occurs and the light is green, cars A and B will move, unless the emergency persists or the light turns red.

The system must satisfy several guarantees. The traffic lights must never both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A or B, the system must eventually respond by turning the corresponding light green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, both lights must turn red.

The system starts in an initial state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is defined by a pattern, which ensures that when a car is present, the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green, and the light will remain green until the car is no longer present. This pattern is designed to ensure that the system responds to cars in a timely and consistent manner.
