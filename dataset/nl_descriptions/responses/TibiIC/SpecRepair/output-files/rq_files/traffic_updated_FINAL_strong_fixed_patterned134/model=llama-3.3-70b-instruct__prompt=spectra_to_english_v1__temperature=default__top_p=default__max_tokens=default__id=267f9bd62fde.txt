The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by carA and carB, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greenA and greenB indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present on road A and the light is red for road A but green for road B, the car on road A will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when a car is present on road B and the light is red for road B but green for road A, the car on road B will remain present in the next time step. Furthermore, when a car is present on road A and the light is green for road A, the car will move and not be present in the next time step. The same applies to road B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never be red for both roads at the same time, ensuring that at least one road always has a green light. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road A. The same guarantee applies to road B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green when a car is present. This pattern is initially in a state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the light is already green. If the car is present and the light is not green, the pattern transitions to a state where it is waiting for the system to respond. The system responds by turning the light green, which causes the pattern to return to its initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding light green.
