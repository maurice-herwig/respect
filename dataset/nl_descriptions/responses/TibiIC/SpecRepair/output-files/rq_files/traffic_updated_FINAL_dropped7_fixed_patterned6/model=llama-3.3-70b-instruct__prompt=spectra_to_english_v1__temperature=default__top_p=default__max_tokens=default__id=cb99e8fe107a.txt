The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides input signals indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state of cara will be true (car A is present) unless the emergency persists or the light for road A is green. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state of carb will be true (car B is present) unless the emergency persists, the light for road B is green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the next state of cara will be false (car A is not present) unless the emergency persists or the light for road A is no longer green. The environment also ensures that when an emergency occurs, the light for road B will not be green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. However, when an emergency occurs, the system ensures that the emergency condition is always false, implying that the system prioritizes emergency situations.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives on either road A or B, the system will turn the corresponding light green if it is not already green. The system will keep the light green as long as the car is present. If the car leaves while the light is green, the system will turn the light red. The system will repeatedly turn the lights green for roads A and B in response to the presence of cars on those roads.
