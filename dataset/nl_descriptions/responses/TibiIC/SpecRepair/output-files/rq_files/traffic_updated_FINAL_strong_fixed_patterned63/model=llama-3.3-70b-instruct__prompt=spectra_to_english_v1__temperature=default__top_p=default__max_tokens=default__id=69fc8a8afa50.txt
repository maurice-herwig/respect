The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when the car is present, and will keep the light green until the car moves. This pattern is initially in a state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it will move to a state where it is responding if the car is present and its light is not green. The system will return to the initial state when the car's light is green. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
