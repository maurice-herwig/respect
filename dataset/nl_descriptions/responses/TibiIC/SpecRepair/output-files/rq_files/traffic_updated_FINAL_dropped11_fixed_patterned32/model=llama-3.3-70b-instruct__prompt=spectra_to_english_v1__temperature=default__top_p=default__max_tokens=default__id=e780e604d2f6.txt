The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when an emergency situation occurs, cars A and B will not move unless their respective lights are green or the emergency situation persists. Additionally, when the lights are red, cars A and B will not move unless the emergency situation persists or their respective lights turn green. When the lights are green, cars A and B will move unless the emergency situation persists.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car B green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will be red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding light green, and it will do so in a way that ensures the light is turned green whenever the car is present, and the light is turned red when the car is not present. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system is designed to safely and efficiently manage traffic flow in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations, while ensuring that the traffic lights behave in a predictable and responsive manner.
