The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car at intersection A. Instead, the car will be introduced at the next time step. Similarly, when an emergency situation occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car at intersection B if the light at intersection B is red or if there is no car at intersection B. The environment is also assumed to remove cars from intersection A when the light is green and an emergency situation occurs, or when the light turns red. The same assumption applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency situation, the light at intersection B will be red. If a car is present at intersection A and the light at intersection B is red, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green. If a car is present at intersection B and the light at intersection B is green, the system will keep the light at intersection B green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will be red.

The system also follows a specific pattern of response to the presence of cars: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. If a car arrives and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely and predictable manner.
