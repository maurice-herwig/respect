The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green and there is no emergency, if a car is present, it will remain present until the traffic light turns green. On the other hand, when the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system must satisfy certain guarantees. The traffic light must turn green periodically when a car is present. Furthermore, the system must ensure that there will always be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system must always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green periodically when a car is present.
