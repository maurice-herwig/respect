The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and no longer be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it must turn the car's light green. Once the car's light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
