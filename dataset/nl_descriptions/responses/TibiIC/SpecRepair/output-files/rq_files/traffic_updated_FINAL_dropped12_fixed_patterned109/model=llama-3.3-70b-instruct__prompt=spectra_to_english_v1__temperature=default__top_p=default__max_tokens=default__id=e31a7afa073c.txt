The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars A and B, and whether there is an emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have emergencies often, and when car A is about to arrive, it should not be in an emergency state or have car A already present unless the light is about to turn green. Similarly, when car B is about to arrive, it should either be in an emergency state, have the light for car B green, or have car B not present. Additionally, when the lights are red, car A should not move unless there is an emergency, and car B should not move unless there is an emergency or its light is green. Conversely, when the lights are green, car A should move, and car B should move unless there is an emergency or its light is red.

The system guarantees certain behavior: when there is no emergency, the light for car B should not be green if the light for car A is not green. The system should also respond to the presence of cars A and B by turning their respective lights green. Furthermore, when there is an emergency, both lights should be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars A and B by turning their respective lights green, and it returns to the waiting state once the light is green. This ensures that the system always eventually returns to the waiting state, allowing it to respond to new arrivals. 

The system's behavior is designed to ensure safety and responsiveness to the environment's inputs, while also accounting for emergency situations.
