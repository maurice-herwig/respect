The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green and a car is present, the car will remain present until the traffic light turns green. When the traffic light is green, a car will move and no longer be present, unless an emergency situation arises while the car is moving, in which case the car will also move and no longer be present.

The system must guarantee certain behaviors. The traffic light must turn green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system must ensure that there will always be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present.
