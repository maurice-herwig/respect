The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false at some point in the future and remain false for some time. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red and there is no emergency, car A will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, car B will remain present in the next time step. Conversely, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will not be present in the next time step, and the same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same guarantee applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green, but only if the car is present and its light is not already green. If a car's light is not green when the car is present, the system will transition to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars again. The system will always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
