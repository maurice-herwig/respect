The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is assumed to behave in specific ways: if car A is not present, it will either remain absent or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green; if car A is present, it will move when the traffic light at intersection A is green, unless an emergency situation is ongoing; similar assumptions are made for car B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green; when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green; and when an emergency situation occurs, both traffic lights will turn red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern called pRespondsToS, which ensures that when a car is present, the corresponding traffic light will eventually turn green. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to the presence of a car. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. The system then enters a loop where it either remains in this state if the car is not present or if the traffic light is already green, or it transitions to a state where it is responding to the presence of a car if the car is present and the traffic light is not green. Once the system is responding to a car, it will remain in this state until the traffic light turns green, at which point it will return to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
