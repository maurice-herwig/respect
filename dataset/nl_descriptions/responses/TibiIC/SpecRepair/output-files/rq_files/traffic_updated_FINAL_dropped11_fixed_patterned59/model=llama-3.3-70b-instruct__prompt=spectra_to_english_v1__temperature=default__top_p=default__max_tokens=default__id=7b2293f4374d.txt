The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either not be present, be allowed to proceed (i.e., the light is green), or not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green. Similarly, cars at intersection B are assumed to either not be present, be allowed to proceed, or not move until the emergency is over. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light is green, cars at both intersections are assumed to move or not be present.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when a car is present. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and the corresponding light is green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this responding state until the corresponding light is turned green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to cars' presence over time.
