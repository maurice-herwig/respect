The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency condition active). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency condition is active, car A will not be present at the next time step unless the emergency condition is still active or the green light for car A is on. Similarly, when an emergency condition is active, car B will not be present at the next time step unless the emergency condition is still active, the green light for car B is on, or car B is not present. Additionally, when an emergency condition is active, car A will move (i.e., not be present) at the next time step unless the emergency condition is still active, car A is not present, or the green light for car A is off. Car B will also move (i.e., not be present) at the next time step unless car B is not present or the green light for car B is off.

The system guarantees that when car A is present, the green light for car A will eventually be turned on, and when car B is present, the green light for car B will eventually be turned on. This is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to be present, then responds by turning on the corresponding green light. The system will continue to turn the green light on as long as the car is present, and will turn it off when the car is no longer present. The system will always eventually return to a state where it is waiting for a car to be present.

Initially, the system is in a waiting state. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to be present or responding to a car being present. The system's liveness requirements ensure that it will always eventually return to the waiting state. The next-state relationships define how the system transitions between these two states based on the presence of cars and the state of the green lights.
