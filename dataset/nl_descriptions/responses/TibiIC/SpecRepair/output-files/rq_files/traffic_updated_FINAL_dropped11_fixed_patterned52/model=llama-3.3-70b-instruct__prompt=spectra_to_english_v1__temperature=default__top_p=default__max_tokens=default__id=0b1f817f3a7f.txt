The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment is assumed to behave in specific ways: if the light is red, cars on road A will not move until the emergency signal is false, the light turns green, or a car is already present; similarly, cars on road B will not move until the emergency signal is false or the light turns green. Conversely, when the emergency signal is true and the light is green, cars on both roads A and B will move, either immediately or at the next opportunity.

The system guarantees several behaviors. First, it ensures that both traffic lights are never red at the same time. Second, when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light green for road A. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light green for road B. Third, during an emergency, the system will turn both lights red.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives on a road, the system will turn the corresponding light green if it is not already green. If the light is already green, the system will keep it green as long as the car is present. If the car leaves, the system will turn the light red, but it will turn it green again when the next car arrives. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars on the roads in a predictable and safe manner.
