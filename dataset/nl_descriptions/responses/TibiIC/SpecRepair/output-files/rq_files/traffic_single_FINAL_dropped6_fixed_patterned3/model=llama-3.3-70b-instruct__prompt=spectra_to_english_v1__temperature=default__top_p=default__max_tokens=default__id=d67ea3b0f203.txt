The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will remain absent indefinitely. The environment is also assumed to follow specific rules regarding the car's movement: when the traffic light is red (not green), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the police are present. Furthermore, when the police are present, the car will either not be present or will not start moving.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. The system also guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior is constrained by safety rules: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. When the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. If a car is present and the light is green, the system will remain in its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.
