The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected to be in a non-emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light is red for A but green for B, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and the light is red for B but green for A, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. When a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. The system also ensures that when a car's light is green, the car will move, and when a car is present and its light is not green, it will remain idle until its light turns green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: ready to respond or waiting for a car's light to turn green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. If a car's light is green, the system will return to the ready state. The system will infinitely often be in the ready state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars.
