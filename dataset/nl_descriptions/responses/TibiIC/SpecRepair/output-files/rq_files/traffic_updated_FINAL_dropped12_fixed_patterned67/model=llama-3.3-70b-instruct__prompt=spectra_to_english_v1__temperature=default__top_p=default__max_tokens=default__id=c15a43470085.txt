The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have emergency situations frequently, and when car A is not present, it will not become present unless there is an emergency or it was already present. Additionally, when there is an emergency, the environment will either maintain the emergency state or have car B present with a green light. The environment also ensures that car A and car B move when their respective lights are green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will not have both lights red when there is no emergency, and it will turn the green light on for car A when car A is present. When car B is present, the system will turn on either the green light for car A or car B. The system also ensures that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior is also governed by the following rules: initially, the system is in a state where it responds to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the corresponding car is not present or its light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, where it responds to the presence of cars.
