The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of an emergency, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency situation ends, a car will be present at intersection A; when the emergency situation is present, a car will be present at intersection B if the light at intersection B is green or if there is no car at intersection B; when the emergency situation is not present, a car will not be present at intersection A if the light at intersection A is green; and when the emergency situation is present, a car will not be present at intersection B if the light at intersection B is green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights at both intersections will not be red at the same time, unless an emergency situation is present; if a car is present at intersection A and the light at intersection B is red, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green; if a car is present at intersection B and the light at intersection B is green, the system will keep the light at intersection B green; and if an emergency situation is present, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present at an intersection. The system will respond to the presence of a car at an intersection by turning the corresponding light green, and it will keep the light green until the car is no longer present. If a car is present at an intersection and the corresponding light is not green, the system will turn the light green. The system will also periodically return to its initial state, where it is waiting for a car to be present at an intersection.
