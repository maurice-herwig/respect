The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not the case that emergency situations occur. Additionally, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: if car A is not present when the light is red, it will become present at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or the light turns green; similarly, if car B is not present when the light is red, it will become present at the next time step, unless the light turns green or car B is already present. Furthermore, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is assumed to have cars A and B move when their respective lights are green, meaning that if a car is present when the light is green, it will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or the light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. The system also guarantees that when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green, and similarly for intersection B. Additionally, the system guarantees that when an emergency situation occurs, both traffic lights will be red.

The system has an initial condition where it starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
