The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not have high water when the pump is operating.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must not be high water. Secondly, if the pump is to be turned on at the next step, then methane must not be present.

In summary, the system's pump operation is constrained by the environment's high water and methane conditions, with specific rules governing when the pump can be turned on in relation to these conditions.
