The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. In response, the system controls a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that the pump is only turned on if it was previously off.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In essence, the system's pump operation is dependent on the environmental conditions of methane presence and water level, with specific rules governing when the pump should be turned on or off in response to these conditions.
