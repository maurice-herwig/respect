The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to environmental inputs. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables `cara` and `carb`, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When the emergency signal is not active, if the light for car A is red, car A will not be present at the next time step. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, if the light for car B is red or car B is not present, car B will not be present at the next time step. Additionally, when the emergency signal is not active, if car A is present and the light is green, car A will move and not be present at the next time step. Likewise, when the emergency signal is active, if car B is present and the light is green, car B will move and not be present at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights will never both be red unless the emergency signal is active or the light for car B is already red. If a car is present at intersection A and the light for car B is red, the system will eventually turn the light for car A green. If a car is present at intersection B and the light for car B is green, the system will keep the light for car B green. When the emergency signal is active, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green, but only if the other light is not already green. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system will remember this and turn the light green at some point in the future. The system will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars, turning the corresponding lights green and then resetting to wait for the next car.
