The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by the variables `cara` and `carb`, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by the variables `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road B is red, then the emergency signal remains active; when the emergency signal is not active, if there is a car on road A and the traffic light for road A is green, then the car will move and not be present on the next time step, or the traffic light for road A will turn red; when the emergency signal is not active, if there is a car on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, then the car will move and not be present on the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car on the road or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
