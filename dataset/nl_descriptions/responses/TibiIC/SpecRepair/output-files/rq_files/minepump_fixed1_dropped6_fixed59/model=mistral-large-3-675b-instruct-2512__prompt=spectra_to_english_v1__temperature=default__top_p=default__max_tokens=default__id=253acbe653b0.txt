### **Minepump System Requirements**

This reactive system controls a pump in response to environmental conditions detected by sensors.

**Environment-Controlled Inputs:**
- `highwater`: A signal indicating whether the water level is dangerously high.
- `methane`: A signal indicating the presence of explosive methane gas.

**System-Controlled Output:**
- `pump`: A signal determining whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

**Assumptions on the Environment:**
- The pump cannot turn itself on without the system issuing a command. Specifically, if the pump is off in the current state *and* was off in the immediately preceding state, it must have been off in the state before that (i.e., the environment cannot spontaneously activate the pump without the system's intervention).

**Guarantees Required from the System:**

1. **Safety Requirement:**
   - The pump must never be activated in the presence of methane. Specifically:
     - If methane is detected, either:
       - The pump will be turned *off* in the next state, *or*
       - The water level will *not* be high in the current state (making pump activation unnecessary), *or*
       - The pump is already off.
   - This ensures the pump does not operate while methane is present, preventing explosion risks.

2. **Pump Deactivation in Safe Conditions:**
   - If methane is *not* detected, the pump must be turned off in the next state.
   - This ensures the pump only runs when explicitly permitted by the system's safety logic.
