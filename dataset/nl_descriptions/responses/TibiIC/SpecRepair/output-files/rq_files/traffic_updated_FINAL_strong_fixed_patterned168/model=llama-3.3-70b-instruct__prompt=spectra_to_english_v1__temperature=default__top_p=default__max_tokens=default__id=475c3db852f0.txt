Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for car B green. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights for car A and car B are red.

This pattern involves the system being in one of two states, where it either immediately responds to the car's presence or delays its response until the car is still present and the light is not yet green.
