The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain idle and not move until its green light turns on. Similarly, when car B is present and its green light is red while car A's green light is on, car B will remain idle. However, when a car's green light is on, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both green lights are turned off.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present, the system will turn on its corresponding green light. If the green light is already on, the car will move, and its presence will be removed. If the green light is off, the system will wait until the car is still present before turning on the green light. The system will always return to its initial state after responding to a car's presence. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
