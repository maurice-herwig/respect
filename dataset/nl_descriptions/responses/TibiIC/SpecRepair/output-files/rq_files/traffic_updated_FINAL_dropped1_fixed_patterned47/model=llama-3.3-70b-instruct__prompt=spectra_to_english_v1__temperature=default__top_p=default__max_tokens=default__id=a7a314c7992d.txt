The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as the occurrence of emergency situations, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation occurs, cars at intersection A do not move unless the traffic light is green or the emergency situation persists. Similarly, when an emergency situation occurs, cars at intersection B do not move unless the traffic light is green or the car is not present. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs and the traffic light is green, cars at both intersections A and B move and do not remain stationary.

The system guarantees certain behavior. At all times, at least one of the traffic lights at intersections A and B is red. When a car is present at intersection A, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection B green. During emergency situations, both traffic lights are red.

The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as a car is not present or the corresponding traffic light is green. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the presence of the car. The system remains in this state until the traffic light is turned green, at which point it transitions back to the initial state. The system repeatedly returns to the initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
