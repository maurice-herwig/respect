The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence signal will be reset.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as a car is not present or its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars again. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
