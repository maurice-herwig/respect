The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car A present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car B present in the next time step. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the environment will ensure that the car moves and is no longer present in the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: either it is ready to respond to a car's presence or it is waiting for the corresponding light to turn green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. The system will return to the ready state when the car's light turns green. The system will always eventually return to the ready state.
