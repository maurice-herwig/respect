Here is the natural-language description of the **Minepump** system:

The system models a mine pump that interacts with its environment based on two input signals: **high water level** (`highwater`) and **methane presence** (`methane`). Both inputs are controlled by the environment, while the system controls the **pump** (`pump`), an output indicating whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

**Assumptions on the environment:**
The environment must ensure that whenever methane is detected at a given time step, the pump must have been off *either* at that same time step *or* in the immediately preceding step. This prevents unsafe conditions where methane and a running pump coexist.

**Guarantees provided by the system:**
1. At every time step, the system must enforce one of the following:
   - Methane is detected,
   - The pump will activate in the next time step, *or*
   - The water level is not high.
   This ensures the pump does not run unnecessarily while avoiding unsafe combinations of conditions.
2. Additionally, if methane is *not* detected at any time step, the pump must deactivate in the next time step. This ensures the pump responds quickly to changes in methane levels.

No initial conditions or additional liveness requirements (e.g., eventual activation) are specified for this system. The guarantees strictly govern the system's safety and immediate responses.
