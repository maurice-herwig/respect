The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency, both traffic lights must be red.
