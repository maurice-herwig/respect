The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: high water levels (highwater) and the presence of methane (methane). The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the highwater and methane conditions. The system controls the pump.

It is assumed that the environment behaves in the following ways: if the pump has been on and remains on, then the water level will be low in the next state. Additionally, if the water level is currently low, then either the water level remains low or there is no methane in the next state.

The system must satisfy the following requirements: if the water level is high, then either methane is present or the pump will be turned on in the next state. Furthermore, if methane is detected, then the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system must respond to the environment's conditions by controlling the pump according to these rules, under the given assumptions about the environment's behavior.
