The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It should not have police interventions frequently, meaning there will be times when police are not present. Additionally, the environment should ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. When the traffic light is green, the environment should allow the car to move, unless police are present or the car is not moving.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a specific pattern of behavior. Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the car's presence and its own state. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the right moment to turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence again. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in this initial state, ready to respond to the car.

Overall, the TrafficE2 system is designed to manage traffic flow in response to the presence of cars, emergencies, and police interventions, while ensuring safe and orderly traffic movement.
