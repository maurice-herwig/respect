The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green, car A will move and its presence will no longer be detected. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B when it is present. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. The system also ensures that whenever car A or car B is present, their respective lights will be turned green in response, but this response may be delayed.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the cars are not present or their lights are green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must respond to the car's presence. The system will periodically return to the initial state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
