The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment to control traffic flow. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the state of the green lights for cars A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, car A will not be present unless the green light for car A is active or the emergency signal remains active in the next state, or car A is already present and the emergency signal remains active. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, car B will not be present unless the green light for car B is active, the emergency signal remains active in the next state, or car B is not present. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, if car A is present, it will move and not be present in the next state unless the emergency signal remains active or the green light for car A is inactive. The same applies to car B: when the emergency signal is active, if car B is present, it will move and not be present in the next state unless the green light for car B is inactive.

The system guarantees the following behavior: whenever car A is present, the system will eventually activate the green light for car A, and whenever car B is present, the system will eventually activate the green light for car B. This is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to be present. If a car is present and its corresponding green light is not active, the system transitions to a state where it ensures the green light is activated. Once the green light is active, the system returns to its initial state, repeating this pattern infinitely often to ensure that the green lights are activated whenever cars are present. Initially, the system is in its waiting state. The system always maintains this pattern of response, ensuring that the green lights are activated in response to the presence of cars A and B.
