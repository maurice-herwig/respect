The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the detection of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if high water was present in the previous state, the pump must be off.

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump will be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not detected, the pump will be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is turned on when methane is present and turned off when methane is absent, while also considering the previous state of high water to determine the pump's operation.
