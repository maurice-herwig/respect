The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When the traffic light is not green, the car will not move unless there is an emergency. Additionally, once the car starts moving, it will continue to move until the traffic light turns green, unless an emergency occurs or the car stops on its own.

The system must guarantee two main behaviors. Firstly, it must ensure that the traffic light turns green often enough to allow the car to pass when it is present. This means that whenever a car is present, the system should eventually turn the traffic light green to let the car pass. Secondly, the system must ensure that there are periods where no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green to let the car pass. If the traffic light is green when a car is present, the system will keep the traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence again.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
