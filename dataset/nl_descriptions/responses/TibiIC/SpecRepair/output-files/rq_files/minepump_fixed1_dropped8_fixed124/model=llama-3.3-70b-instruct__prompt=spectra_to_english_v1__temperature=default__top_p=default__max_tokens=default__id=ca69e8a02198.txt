The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the levels of high water and methane. The system controls the pump's operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not turn the pump on if it was previously off, and once the pump is off, it will remain off until further changes.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if no methane is present, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

Note that the specification does not mention the "highwater" variable in the assumptions or guarantees, so its influence on the system's behavior is not defined.
