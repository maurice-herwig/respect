The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when an emergency is in effect, the traffic light for road B is always green or the emergency condition persists; when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next step; and when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. The system responds to the presence of cars on each road by turning the corresponding traffic light green, but only after any necessary waiting period. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive, and it remains in this state as long as there is no car or the traffic light is green. If a car arrives and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a waiting state, where it remains until the traffic light turns green. The system will infinitely often return to the initial waiting state.
