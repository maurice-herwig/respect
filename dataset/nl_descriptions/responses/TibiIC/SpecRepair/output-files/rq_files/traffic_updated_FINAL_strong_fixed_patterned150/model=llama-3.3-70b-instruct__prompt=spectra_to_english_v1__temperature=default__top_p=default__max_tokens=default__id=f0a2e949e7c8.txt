The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the light is red and there is no emergency, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, car B will remain present in the next state. On the other hand, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also guarantees that if car A is present, it will eventually turn its light green, and similarly for car B. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and its light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green for the car to proceed. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars again. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
