The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning there are times when it is not emergency; when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state; similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state; when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state; and when car B is present and its light is green, car B will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: at any given time, at least one of the green lights for car A or car B is off; when car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on; when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on; and when the emergency signal is active, both green lights will be off.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a pattern where it ensures that when a car is present, its corresponding green light will be turned on, and this pattern repeats as long as the car remains present. The system always returns to its initial state after responding to a car's presence. 

In general, the system ensures that the traffic lights operate safely and respond correctly to the presence of cars A and B, while also considering the emergency signal.
