The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and no longer be present. However, if an emergency occurs while car B is present and its light is green, car B may not move.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During emergency situations, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is such that if a car is present, its corresponding light will be turned green, and once the light is green, the car will move and the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence. If a car is present and its light is not turned green, the system will persist in trying to turn the light green until it succeeds.
