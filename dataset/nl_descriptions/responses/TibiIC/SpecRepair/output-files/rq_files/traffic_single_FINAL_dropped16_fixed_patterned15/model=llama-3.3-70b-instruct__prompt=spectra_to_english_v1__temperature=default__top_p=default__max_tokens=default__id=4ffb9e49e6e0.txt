The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: when the traffic light is not green, the car should not move unless there is an emergency or the car is already not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is green, the car should either not be present or should move on the next step.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system's behavior is safe and live. Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the car's presence and adjusts its state accordingly. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the car to pass. If the car passes while the light is green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often return to its initial state, where it is ready to respond to the car's presence.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not. The system's guarantee of responding to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green ensures that the traffic light will often be green when a car is present, provided the environment behaves according to the specified assumptions.
