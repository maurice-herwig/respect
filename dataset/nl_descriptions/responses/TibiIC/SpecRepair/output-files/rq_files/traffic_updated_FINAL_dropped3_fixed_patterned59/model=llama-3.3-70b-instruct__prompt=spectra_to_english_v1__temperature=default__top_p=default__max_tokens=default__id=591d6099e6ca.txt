The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as an emergency flag. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency flag is set, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when the emergency flag is set, if the traffic light at intersection B is red, then the emergency flag remains set; when the emergency flag is not set or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red at the next step; when the emergency flag is not set or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green; when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green.

The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the traffic light turns green when a car is present. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. This pattern ensures that the traffic light turns green infinitely often when a car is present.
