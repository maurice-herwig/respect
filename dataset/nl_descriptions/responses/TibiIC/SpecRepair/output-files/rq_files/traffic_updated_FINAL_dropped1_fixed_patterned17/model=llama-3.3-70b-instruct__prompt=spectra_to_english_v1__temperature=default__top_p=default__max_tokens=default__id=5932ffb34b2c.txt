The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will either not be present initially or will arrive at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection B will either not be present initially or will arrive at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection B is green. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A will either not be present initially or will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency situation is still present. The same assumption applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. The same guarantee applies to intersection B. When an emergency situation is present, the system will turn both traffic lights red. 

The system's behavior can be described in terms of its initial conditions, safety requirements, and liveness requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. The system will eventually return to the initial state when the traffic light turns green. This pattern of behavior applies to both intersections A and B.
