The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false at some point in the future. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain idle until the light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, it will also remain idle until its light turns green. On the other hand, when car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and its presence will be removed at the next time step. The same behavior applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same guarantee applies to car B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red.

The system's response to car presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will respond to a car's presence by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the system is currently responding to a car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or its corresponding light is already green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state until the car's light is turned green, at which point it returns to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to a car's presence by turning its corresponding light green.
