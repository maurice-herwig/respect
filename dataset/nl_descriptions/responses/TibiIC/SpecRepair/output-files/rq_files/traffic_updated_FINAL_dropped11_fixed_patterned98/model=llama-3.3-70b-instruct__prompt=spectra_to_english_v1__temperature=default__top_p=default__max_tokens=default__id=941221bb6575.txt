The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency situation will eventually become false. Additionally, when the emergency situation is true, car A will not move unless the light is green or the emergency situation persists, and car B will not move unless the light is green. Furthermore, when the emergency situation is true, car A will move when the light is green, and car B will move when the light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car A, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car B. During an emergency situation, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it responds to the presence of cars. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will remain in this state until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
