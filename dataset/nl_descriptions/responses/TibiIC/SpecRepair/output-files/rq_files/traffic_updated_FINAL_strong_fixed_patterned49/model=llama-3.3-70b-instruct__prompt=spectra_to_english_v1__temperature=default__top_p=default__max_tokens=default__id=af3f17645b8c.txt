The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green, car A will move and its presence will be removed, and the same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and the same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

In the absence of an emergency, the system will ensure that when a car is present, its corresponding light will be turned green, allowing it to move. The system's behavior is designed to respond to the presence of cars and emergency situations while maintaining safe and efficient traffic flow.
