The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The system assumes that when an emergency occurs, the next state of cara will be true, meaning a car will be present on road A. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state of carb will be true if the emergency persists, the light for road B is green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, the system assumes that during an emergency, if a car is present on road A, it will move when the light turns green, and similarly, a car on road B will move when its light turns green.

The system guarantees that when there is no emergency, the light for road B will be red. It also guarantees that if a car is present on road A and the light for road B is red, then the light for road A will turn green. Furthermore, if a car is present on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will maintain this state. The system ensures that during an emergency, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to cars on the roads follows a pattern where it prioritizes turning the light green for the road with a present car, under the conditions specified. The system will infinitely often return to a state where it can respond to the presence of cars, ensuring ongoing interaction with the environment.
