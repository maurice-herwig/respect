The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations should not occur frequently, meaning that there should be infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car A should not be present unless an emergency is occurring or about to occur. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when an emergency is present, car B will either remain idle or the traffic light at intersection B will turn green. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green, car A should move, and car B should move unless an emergency is present or the traffic light at intersection B is red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. When there is no emergency, the traffic light at intersection B should not be green. The system should also ensure that when car A is present, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green. When car B is present, the system should ensure that either the traffic light at intersection A or the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green. In emergency situations, both traffic lights should be red.

The system's behavior can be described in terms of initial conditions, safety requirements, and liveness requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is green. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. The system returns to the initial state when the traffic light turns green. The system should always satisfy these state transitions and should infinitely often return to the initial state, indicating that the traffic lights are responding to the presence of cars.
