The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will either remain present or an emergency situation will occur, or the car will leave. It is also assumed that when the police are present, or there is no car, the car will either not be present initially or will leave when the light is green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. The system's response to a car's presence is as follows: if a car is present and the light is green, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the light remains green while a car is present, the system stays in its initial state. If the light is not green while a car is present, the system stays in its waiting state until the light turns green. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars repeatedly.
