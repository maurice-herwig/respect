The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water is high, then either the water level will decrease or the pump will be deactivated at the next step.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be activated at the next step. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated at the next step. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the presence of methane, and the environment's behavior regarding high water levels has a specific relationship with the pump's operation. The system ensures that the pump's state is determined by the methane condition at the next step.
