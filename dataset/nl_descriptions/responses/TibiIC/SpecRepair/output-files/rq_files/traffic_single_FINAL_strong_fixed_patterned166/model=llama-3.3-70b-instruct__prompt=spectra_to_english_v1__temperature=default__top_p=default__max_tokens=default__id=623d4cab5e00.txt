The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the police are present and the traffic light is red, the car will remain idle. However, when the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move, and thus, the presence of the car will change.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present, the system remains in this ready state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the ready state. The system will infinitely often be in the ready state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
