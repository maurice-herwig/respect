The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency signals. The system controls the green light indicators for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain idle. Conversely, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and its presence signal will be reset.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If a car is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding light green. When an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also ensures that if car A or car B is present, it will respond to the presence by turning its corresponding light green, and this response will happen repeatedly over time.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to car presence signals. The system's response to car presence signals is governed by a set of rules that ensure it will repeatedly turn the corresponding light green when a car is present, and it will not turn the light green when no car is present. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence signal.
