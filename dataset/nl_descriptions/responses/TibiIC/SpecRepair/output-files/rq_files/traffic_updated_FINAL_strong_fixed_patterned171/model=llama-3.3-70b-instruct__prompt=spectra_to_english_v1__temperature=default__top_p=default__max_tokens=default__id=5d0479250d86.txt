The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when the car is present, and will return to its initial state once the light has been turned green. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car, as long as the environment behaves according to its assumptions.
