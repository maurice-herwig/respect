The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain idle or move forward when the light turns green, and similarly for road B. The environment also ensures that cars on both roads move forward when their respective lights are green during an emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green, and similarly for road B. During an emergency, the system ensures that both lights are red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on either road. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. The system will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars on either road, turning the corresponding light green when a car is present. This response will happen infinitely often, ensuring that cars on both roads are given the opportunity to pass through the intersection.
