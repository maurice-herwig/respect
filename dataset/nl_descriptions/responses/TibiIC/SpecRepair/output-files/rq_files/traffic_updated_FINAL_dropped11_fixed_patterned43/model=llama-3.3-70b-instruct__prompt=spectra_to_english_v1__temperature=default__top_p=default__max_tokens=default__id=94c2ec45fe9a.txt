The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as an emergency flag. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency flag will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the emergency flag is true, the environment will ensure that if a car is present on road A, it will either remain present, the emergency flag will still be true, or the light for road A will be green at the next time step. Similarly, for road B, if a car is present, it will either remain present, the emergency flag will still be true, or the light for road B will be green at the next time step. The environment also ensures that when the emergency flag is true, cars on both roads A and B will move when their respective lights are green.

The system guarantees that the lights for roads A and B will never both be red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. During an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars in a predictable and safe manner.
