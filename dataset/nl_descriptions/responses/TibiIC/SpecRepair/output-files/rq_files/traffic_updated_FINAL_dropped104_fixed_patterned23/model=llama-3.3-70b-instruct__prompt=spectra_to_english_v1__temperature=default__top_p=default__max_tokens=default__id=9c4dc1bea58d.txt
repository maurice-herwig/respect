The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency mode. The environment controls the presence of cars in both directions (cara and carb) and the emergency mode (emergency), while the system controls the traffic lights for both directions (greena and greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency mode is active, it will not allow a car to approach from direction A unless the traffic light for A is green or the emergency mode remains active, and if a car is already approaching from A, it will either continue to approach or the emergency mode will remain active. Similarly, for direction B, the environment will not allow a car to approach unless the traffic light for B is green, the emergency mode is active, or no car is approaching from B. Additionally, when the emergency mode is active, the environment will ensure that if a car is approaching from A, it will either stop approaching or the traffic light for A will turn red, and if a car is approaching from B, it will stop approaching or the traffic light for B will turn red.

The system guarantees that whenever a car approaches from direction A, the traffic light for A will eventually turn green, and similarly, when a car approaches from direction B, the traffic light for B will eventually turn green. This guarantee is achieved through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to approach, and then responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to a state where it is waiting for a car to approach.

Initially, the system is in a waiting state. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to approach or responding to a car's approach. If a car is approaching and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a responding state. If the traffic light is green while in the responding state, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system also ensures that it will infinitely often be in the waiting state.
