The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it is assumed that car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, car B is assumed to remain present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, it is assumed that the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same guarantee applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both lights turn red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a pattern where it prioritizes turning the light green for a present car. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it will turn the light green as soon as possible. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car presence. This pattern ensures that the system always eventually responds to the presence of cars by turning their respective lights green.
