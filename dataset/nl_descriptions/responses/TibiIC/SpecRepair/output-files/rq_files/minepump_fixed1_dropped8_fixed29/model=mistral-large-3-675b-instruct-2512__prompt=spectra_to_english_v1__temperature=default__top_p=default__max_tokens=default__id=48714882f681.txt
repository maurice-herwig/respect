### **Minepump System Requirements**

The Minepump system monitors and controls a pump in response to water and methane levels in an underground environment.

#### **Inputs (Environment-Controlled)**
- `highwater`: A signal indicating whether the water level is dangerously high.
- `methane`: A signal indicating whether dangerous levels of methane gas are detected.

#### **Outputs (System-Controlled)**
- `pump`: A signal controlling whether the pump is active (`pump=true`) or inactive (`pump=false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must ensure that whenever the water level becomes high (`highwater=true`), the pump must not have been running in the immediately preceding state.

#### **Guarantees Provided by the System**
The system ensures the following behavior:

1. **Pump Activation Safety:**
   If the water level is *not* high (`highwater=false`), the pump must either remain on or be activated in the next state.

2. **Pump Shutdown Safety:**
   If the water level is *not* high and no methane is detected (`highwater=false` and `methane=false`), the pump must either remain off or be deactivated in the next state.

The system enforces these guarantees at all times during operation.
