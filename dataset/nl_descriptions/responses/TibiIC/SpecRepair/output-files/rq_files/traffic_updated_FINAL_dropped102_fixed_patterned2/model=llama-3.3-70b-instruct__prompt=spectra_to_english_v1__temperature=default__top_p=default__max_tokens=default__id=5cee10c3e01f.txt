The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car on road A, and it will either not introduce a car on road B, or the traffic light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that there is no car on road A, or the traffic light for road A will turn red. Similarly, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for road B is green, the environment will ensure that there is no car on road B, or the traffic light for road B will turn red.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the traffic light for road B will be red. If a car is present on road A and the traffic light for road B is red, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. If a car is present on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, the system will continue to keep the traffic light for road B green. When there is no emergency, the system will ensure that at least one of the traffic lights is not green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system will always respond to the presence of cars on the roads in a way that ensures the traffic lights are turned green when necessary. Specifically, if a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green. The system will also ensure that if a car is not present on a road, the traffic light for that road can be turned red. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
