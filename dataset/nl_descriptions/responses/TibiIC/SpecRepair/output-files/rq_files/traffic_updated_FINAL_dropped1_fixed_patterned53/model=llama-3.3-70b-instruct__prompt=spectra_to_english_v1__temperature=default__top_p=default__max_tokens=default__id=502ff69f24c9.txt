The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that emergencies do not occur infinitely often. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars on road A will not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green, and cars on road B will not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green. Furthermore, when the light is green for a road during an emergency, cars on that road will move and then stop until the emergency is over or the light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road A. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road B. During an emergency, the system ensures that both lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive on either road. The system then responds to the presence of cars on the roads by turning the corresponding lights green, while ensuring that the lights are not both red at the same time. The system repeatedly cycles through this behavior, responding to the presence of cars on the roads and emergency situations.
