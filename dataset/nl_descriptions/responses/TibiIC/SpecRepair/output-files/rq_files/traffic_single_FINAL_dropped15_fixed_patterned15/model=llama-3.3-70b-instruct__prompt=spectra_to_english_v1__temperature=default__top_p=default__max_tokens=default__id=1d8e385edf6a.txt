The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: when the traffic light is not green, the car will not move unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, once the car starts moving, it will continue to move unless the traffic light turns green or an emergency occurs.

The system must guarantee two main behaviors. Firstly, it must ensure that the traffic light turns green often enough to allow the car to pass when it is present. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green to let the car pass. Secondly, the system must ensure that there are periods where no car is present, allowing for a steady flow of traffic.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green, allowing the car to pass. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car to pass. Once the car has passed, the system will return to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. The system will always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green often enough to allow cars to pass.
