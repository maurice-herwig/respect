The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when the emergency signal is active, the traffic light at intersection B is always green; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will be red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection A green; and when there is a car at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection B green.

The response of the system to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will respond to a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car or the traffic light is already green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car. The system remains in this state until the traffic light is turned green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to cars.
