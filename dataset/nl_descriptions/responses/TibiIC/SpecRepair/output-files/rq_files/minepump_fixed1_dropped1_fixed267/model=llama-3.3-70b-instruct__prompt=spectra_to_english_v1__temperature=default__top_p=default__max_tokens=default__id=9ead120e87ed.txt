The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the pump. The environment is expected to behave in a way that when methane is present, if the pump is not turned on at the next step, then the water level will not be high. 

The system must ensure two main guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

It is assumed that the environment will not allow methane to be present and the water level to be high at the same time if the pump is not turned on, unless the pump is already off. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is conditioned on the presence of methane and the water level, with specific requirements for when the pump should be turned on or off in response to these environmental factors.
