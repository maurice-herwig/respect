The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Firstly, when the police are not present, the car will be present in the next state if it is not already present, unless there is an emergency or the car is already absent. Secondly, if a car is present, it will either remain present or move in the next state, unless the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the presence of the car and its own state. If the system is not responding to the car and the car is present without the traffic light being green, it will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. If the system is waiting for the traffic light to turn green and the traffic light is indeed green, it will transition back to the initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence.
