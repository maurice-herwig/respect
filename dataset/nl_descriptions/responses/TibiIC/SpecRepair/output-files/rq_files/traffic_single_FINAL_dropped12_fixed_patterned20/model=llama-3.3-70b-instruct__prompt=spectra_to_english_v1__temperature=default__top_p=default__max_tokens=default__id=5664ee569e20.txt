The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when there is no emergency, if the car is not present or the light is green, the car will not start moving. However, if the car is present and the light is not green, the car will not move when there is no emergency. It is also assumed that when the police are present, or there is no car, or the car is not moving, or the light is not green, the car will either not move or the light will not be green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. The system also guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then enters a loop where it waits for a car to arrive and turns the light green when the car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it waits for the light to turn green before returning to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state.

In terms of environment-controlled inputs, the system receives information about the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.
