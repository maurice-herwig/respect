The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the high water level and methane presence. The system controls the pump operation. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever the water level is high, the pump is not operating. 

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next step. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the high water level and methane presence, while the system-controlled output is the pump operation. 

There are no explicitly stated initial conditions or liveness requirements in this specification. The system's behavior is defined solely by the guarantees that it must satisfy in response to the environment's inputs, under the given assumption about the environment's behavior.
