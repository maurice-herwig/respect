The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. In the event of an emergency, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the car's light green. Once the car's light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state.
