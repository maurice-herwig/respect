The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as the occurrence of emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that the emergency variable is usually false. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is present at intersection A, it will either remain present or the traffic light at intersection A will turn green at the next time step, unless the emergency situation persists. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency situation occurs and the traffic light at an intersection is green, the car at that intersection will move, meaning its presence will change at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time, meaning that at least one of the greena or greenb variables is true. The system also guarantees that when a car is present at an intersection, the corresponding traffic light will eventually turn green. Specifically, if a car is present at intersection A, the system will respond by setting greena to true, and similarly for intersection B. Furthermore, the system guarantees that when an emergency situation occurs, both traffic lights will be red, meaning that both greena and greenb will be false.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's safety requirements ensure that it always responds correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations, by transitioning between different states that reflect the presence or absence of cars and the state of the traffic lights. The system's liveness requirements ensure that it will always eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
