The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an `emergency` flag. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for cars at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency is declared, the environment will either keep the emergency flag set, allow a car to approach intersection A, or ensure the light at intersection A is green. Similarly, when an emergency is declared, the environment will either keep the emergency flag set, allow a car to approach intersection B, ensure the light at intersection B is green, or keep the car at intersection B from approaching. Additionally, when an emergency is declared and the light at intersection A is green, the environment will either keep the emergency flag set, ensure the car at intersection A does not approach, or turn the light at intersection A red. The environment is also assumed to keep the light at intersection B red when an emergency is declared.

The system guarantees certain behavior. At all times, at least one of the traffic lights must be red, meaning both lights cannot be green simultaneously. When a car approaches intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car approaches intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green. However, if an emergency is declared, the system will keep the emergency flag reset.

The system's response to cars approaching intersections A and B follows a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's approach. If a car approaches an intersection and the corresponding light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to cars approaching intersections A and B by eventually turning the corresponding light green.
