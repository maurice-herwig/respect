The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is assumed to behave in specific ways: if a car is not present at intersection A, it will either remain absent or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency situation ends; similarly, if a car is not present at intersection B, it will either remain absent or become present at the next time step, unless the traffic light at intersection B is green. The environment is also assumed to move cars when the traffic lights are green, meaning that if a car is present at an intersection and the traffic light is green, the car will move and the presence variable will become false at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time, meaning that at least one of greena or greenb will be true. The system also guarantees that when a car is present at an intersection, the corresponding traffic light will eventually become green. Specifically, if cara is true, greena will become true, and if carb is true, greenb will become true. Furthermore, when an emergency situation occurs, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will be red, meaning that both greena and greenb will be false.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green. The system ensures that once a car arrives, the traffic light will become green, and the system will then wait for the car to move before turning the traffic light red again. This behavior is repeated indefinitely, ensuring that cars are allowed to pass through the intersection when the traffic light is green.
