The **Minepump system** manages a pump in response to water level and methane gas detection in its environment. Below is a natural-language description of its behavior:

### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment controls**:
  - `highwater`: A signal indicating whether the water level is critically high.
  - `methane`: A signal indicating whether dangerous levels of methane gas are detected.
- The **system controls**:
  - `pump`: A signal determining whether the pump is active (`true`) or inactive (`false`).

### **Assumptions (Environment Behavior)**
The system relies on the environment behaving as follows:
1. **Pump Effectiveness**: If the pump is active for two consecutive time steps, the water level will decrease (`highwater` will become `false`).
2. **Methane Safety**: When no methane is detected (`methane = false`), either the water level is not high (`highwater = false`) or methane remains undetected in the next step.
3. **Pump Safety**: If the pump is active (`pump = true`), either the water level is not high (`highwater = false`) or no methane is detected (`methane = false`).
4. **Stable Conditions**: If the water level was not high in the previous step (`highwater = false`), either it remains not high or methane is detected (`methane = true`) in the next step.

### **System Guarantees**
The system ensures the following:
1. **High Water Response**: If the water level is high (`highwater = true`), the system will activate the pump (`pump = true`) in the next time step.
2. **Methane Safety**: If methane is detected (`methane = true`), the system will immediately deactivate the pump (`pump = false`) in the next time step.

The system continuously monitors the environment and adjusts the pump state accordingly to prevent flooding while avoiding operation in hazardous methane conditions.
