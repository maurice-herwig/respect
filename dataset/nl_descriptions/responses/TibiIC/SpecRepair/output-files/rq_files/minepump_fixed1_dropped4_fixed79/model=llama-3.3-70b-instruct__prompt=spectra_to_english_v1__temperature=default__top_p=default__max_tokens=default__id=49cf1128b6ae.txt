The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not have high water when the pump is operating.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or there must not be high water. Secondly, if the pump is to be turned off at the next step, then there must not be methane present.

Initially, there are no specified conditions. At all times, the system must adhere to the guarantees mentioned, given the assumption about the environment's behavior regarding high water and pump operation.
