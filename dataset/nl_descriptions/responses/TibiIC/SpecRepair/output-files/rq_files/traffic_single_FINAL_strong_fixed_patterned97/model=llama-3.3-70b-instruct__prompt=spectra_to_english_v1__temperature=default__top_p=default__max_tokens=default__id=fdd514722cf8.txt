The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, if a car is present, it will remain present until the light turns green. On the other hand, when the traffic light is green and a car is present, and there are no police, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system must guarantee certain behaviors. The traffic light must turn green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system must ensure that there will always be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system then enters a loop where it either remains in this state, waiting for a car to arrive, or transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. If a car is present and the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green in response to the presence of a car.
