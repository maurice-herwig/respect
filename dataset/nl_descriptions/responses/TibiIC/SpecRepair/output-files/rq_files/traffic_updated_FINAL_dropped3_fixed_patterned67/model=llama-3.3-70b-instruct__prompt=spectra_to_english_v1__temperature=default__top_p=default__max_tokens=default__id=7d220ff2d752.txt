The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on input from the environment. The environment provides signals indicating the presence of cars at intersections A and B (`cara` and `carb`) and an emergency signal (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B (`greena` and `greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there should be no car at intersection A; similarly, when the emergency signal is active, if the emergency signal remains active, then the traffic light at intersection B should be green. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active or there is no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A should move when the traffic light is green, and similarly for intersection B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green, and similarly for intersection B. 

The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will respond to the car's presence by turning the corresponding traffic light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is already green. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars.
