The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency flag. The system controls the state of the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is in effect, if road A's light is red, then there is no car on road A; when an emergency is in effect, road B's light is green; when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red at the next step; and when an emergency is in effect or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. These response guarantees are realized through a pattern of behavior where the system monitors the presence of cars and responds by turning the corresponding light green, ensuring that the light is turned green in response to a car's presence in a way that can be repeatedly satisfied over time.
