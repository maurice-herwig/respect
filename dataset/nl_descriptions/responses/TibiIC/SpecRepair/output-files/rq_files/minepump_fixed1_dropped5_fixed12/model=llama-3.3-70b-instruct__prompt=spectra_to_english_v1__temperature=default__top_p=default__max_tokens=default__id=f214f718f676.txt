The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water is high, then either the water will become not high in the next state or the pump will be off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the presence or absence of methane, while the environment's behavior regarding high water levels has specific constraints related to the pump's state.
