The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the high water and methane levels, while the system controls the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when there is no high water, the next state will also have no high water, unless the pump is turned off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step, or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system must control the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane, while the environment is expected to maintain certain constraints on the high water level based on the pump's state.
