The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, car A is present, and car B's light is red, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car is not present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light is turned green only when necessary. The system will always return to this initial state after responding to a car's presence. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in this initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
