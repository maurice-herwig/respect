The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergencies (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state will have a car on road A (cara) if the current state does not have an emergency or a car on road A. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state will have a car on road B (carb) unless there is an emergency, the light for road B is green (greenb), or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, if there is a car on road A, it will move (cara will become false) unless the light for road A is not green (greena is false). Similarly, when an emergency occurs, if there is a car on road B, it will move (carb will become false) unless there is an emergency, the light for road B is not green (greenb is false), or there is no car on road B.

The system guarantees the following behaviors: the traffic light for road B will not be green when there is no emergency. When a car is present on road A and the traffic light for road B is not green, the traffic light for road A will be green. When a car is present on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, the system will ensure this condition is met. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

In terms of responsiveness, the system ensures that when a car is present on a road, the corresponding traffic light will eventually turn green. This responsiveness is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding light green, returning to a state where it is ready to respond to the next car. The system will always eventually return to this ready state, ensuring that it can respond to cars on both roads.
