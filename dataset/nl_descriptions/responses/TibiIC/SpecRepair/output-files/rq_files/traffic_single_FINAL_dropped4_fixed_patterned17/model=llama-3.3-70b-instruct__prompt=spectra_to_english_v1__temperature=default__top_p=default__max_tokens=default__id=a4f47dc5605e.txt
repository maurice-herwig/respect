The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it is expected that police interventions do not occur infinitely often, and when the traffic light is not green, the car will either not be present, be present and not move, or an emergency situation will occur. Additionally, when the car is present and the traffic light is green, the car will move.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system's behavior is safe and responsive. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the car's presence and adjusts its state accordingly. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence again. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in this initial state, ready to respond to the car.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
