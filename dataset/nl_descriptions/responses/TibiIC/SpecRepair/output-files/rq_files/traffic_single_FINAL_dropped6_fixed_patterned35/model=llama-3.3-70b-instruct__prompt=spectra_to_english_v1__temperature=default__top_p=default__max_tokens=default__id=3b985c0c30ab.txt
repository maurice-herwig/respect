The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (i.e., not green), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, the car will either not be present or will not start moving, unless the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when it does, the car will be allowed to pass if it is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for the car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of the car by turning the traffic light green, allowing the car to pass. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will allow the car to pass and then return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green periodically.
