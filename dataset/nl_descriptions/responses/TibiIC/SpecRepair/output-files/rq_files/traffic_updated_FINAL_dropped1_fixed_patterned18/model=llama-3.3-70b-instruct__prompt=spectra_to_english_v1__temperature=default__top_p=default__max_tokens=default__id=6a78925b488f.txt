The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is approaching intersection A, it will either stop or the traffic light will turn green, and similarly for intersection B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency situation occurs and the traffic light is red, cars will not approach intersection A or B unless the light turns green or the emergency situation ends. Furthermore, during an emergency, the environment guarantees that cars will move away from intersections A and B when their respective traffic lights are green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car approaches intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green, and similarly for intersection B. The system also guarantees that during an emergency situation, both traffic lights will be red.

The system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. When a car approaches an intersection, the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green if it is not already green. If the traffic light is green and a car is present, the system will keep the light green until the car leaves. The system will periodically return to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars.
