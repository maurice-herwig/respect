The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and whether an emergency is occurring (`emergency`). The system controls the state of the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is occurring, it will not have a car on road A in the next state if the light for road A is not green, and it will either not have a car on road B in the next state or the light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency is occurring and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that there is no car on road A in the next state if the light for road A is not green. Similarly, for road B, when an emergency is occurring, the environment will either have no car on road B in the next state or ensure the light for road B is not green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the light for road B will be red. If there is a car on road A and the light for road B is red, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. If there is a car on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will maintain the light for road B as green. When there is no emergency, the system ensures that both lights are not red at the same time.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system always eventually returns to this initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
