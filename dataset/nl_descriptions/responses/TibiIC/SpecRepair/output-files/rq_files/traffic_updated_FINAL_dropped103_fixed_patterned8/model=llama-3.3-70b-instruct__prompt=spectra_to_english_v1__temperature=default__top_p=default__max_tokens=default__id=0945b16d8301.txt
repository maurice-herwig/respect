The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when an emergency situation occurs, car A will not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green, and car B will not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green. Additionally, when the light is green for car A or car B, the corresponding car will move during an emergency situation.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights will never both be red at the same time, and if car A or car B is present, the corresponding light will eventually turn green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will be red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light will turn green whenever a car is present, and will stay green until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red due to an emergency situation. The system will always eventually return to a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
