The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain idle. However, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A or car B is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding green light on. If an emergency occurs, both green lights will turn off. The system also ensures that when a car is present and its light is green, it will move and its presence will be removed.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives, the system will turn its corresponding green light on if it is not already on. If the car's light is already on, the system will keep it on until the car moves. If the car's light is off and the other car's light is on, the system will keep its light off until the other car moves or an emergency occurs. The system will always return to its initial state after a car moves or an emergency is resolved.
