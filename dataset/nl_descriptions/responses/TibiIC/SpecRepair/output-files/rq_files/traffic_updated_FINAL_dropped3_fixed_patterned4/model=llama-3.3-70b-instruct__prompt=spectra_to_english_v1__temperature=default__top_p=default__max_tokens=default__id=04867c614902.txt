The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency status. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and the emergency status (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency status is true, if the light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency status is true, the light for road B must be green if there is no emergency; when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will be red at the next time step; and when the emergency status is true or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on either road. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car on the road or the corresponding light is green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often return to the initial state, ensuring that it will periodically respond to the presence of cars on either road.
