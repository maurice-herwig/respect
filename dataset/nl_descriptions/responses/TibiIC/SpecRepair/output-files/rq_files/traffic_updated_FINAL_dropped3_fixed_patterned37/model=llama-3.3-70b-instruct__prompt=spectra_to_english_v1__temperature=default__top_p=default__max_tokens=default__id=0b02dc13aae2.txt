The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides input signals indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B (`cara` and `carb`) and an emergency status (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights, setting them to green for either road A (`greena`) or road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency status is active, if the light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when the emergency status is active, the light for road B should be green if there is a car on road B; when the emergency status is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red at the next step; similarly, when the emergency status is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually set the light for road A to green; when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually set the light for road B to green. The system responds to the presence of cars by setting the corresponding lights to green, following a pattern where the system initially waits for a car to appear, then responds by setting the light to green, and repeats this process whenever a car appears. The system always returns to its initial waiting state after responding to a car.
