The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and whether an emergency is occurring (`emergency`). The system controls the state of the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the traffic light for road A is red (i.e., `greena` is false), the environment will not have a car on road A unless there is an emergency or the car was already present. If there is no emergency, the environment will ensure that if a car is on road B, then either there is an emergency, the traffic light for road B is green, or the car will move. The environment also ensures that cars on both roads will move when their respective traffic lights are green.

The system guarantees several behaviors. First, it ensures that the traffic lights for roads A and B are not both red at the same time when there is no emergency. If a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. If a car is present on road B, the system will ensure that either the traffic light for road A or road B is eventually green. When there is no emergency, the system ensures that at least one of the traffic lights is green. 

In the case of an emergency, the system will turn both traffic lights red. The system's response to cars on the roads and emergencies is such that it will always eventually return to a state where it is ready to respond to new cars or changes in the emergency status. This means that once the system has responded to a car or an emergency, it will always eventually be ready to respond again, ensuring ongoing interaction with the environment.
