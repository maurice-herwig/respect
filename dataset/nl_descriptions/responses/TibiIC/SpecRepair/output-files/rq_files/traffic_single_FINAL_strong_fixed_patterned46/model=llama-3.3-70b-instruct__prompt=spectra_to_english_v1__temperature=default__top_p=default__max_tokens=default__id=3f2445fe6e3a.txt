The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be periods when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (not green), if a car is present, it will remain present in the next state. If the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move (not be present in the next state) regardless of whether an emergency situation arises or not.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and there will be periods when no car is present. Specifically, whenever a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light green. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will always be periods in the future when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system then continuously monitors the presence of a car and the state of the traffic light, updating its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the traffic light turns green in response to the presence of a car and that there are periods when no car is present.
