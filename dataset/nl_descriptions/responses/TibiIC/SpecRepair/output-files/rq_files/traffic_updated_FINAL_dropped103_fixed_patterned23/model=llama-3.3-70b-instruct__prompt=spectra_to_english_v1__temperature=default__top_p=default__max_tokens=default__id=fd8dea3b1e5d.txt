The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have frequent emergency situations, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the emergency situation is true, the environment will ensure that if car A is not present and the traffic light at intersection A is red, then car A will either become present or the emergency situation will end, or the traffic light at intersection A will turn green. Similar assumptions are made for car B. The environment also ensures that when the emergency situation is true, if car A is present and the traffic light at intersection A is green, then either the emergency situation will end, car A will move, or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red. Similar assumptions are made for car B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During emergency situations, the system will turn both traffic lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it will do so in a way that ensures the traffic light will eventually turn green when a car is present. The system will also ensure that if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, it will eventually turn green or the car will move.
