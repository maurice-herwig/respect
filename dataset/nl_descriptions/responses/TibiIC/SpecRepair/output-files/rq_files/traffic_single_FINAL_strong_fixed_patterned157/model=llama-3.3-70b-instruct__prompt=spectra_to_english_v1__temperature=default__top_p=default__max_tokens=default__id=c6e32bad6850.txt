The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police interventions do not occur frequently, and when a car is present and the light is red, the car will remain present in the next state, unless there is a police intervention. Additionally, when a car is present and the light is green, the car will move and no longer be present in the next state.

The system must guarantee two main behaviors: the green light will be on frequently when a car is present, and there will be frequent periods where no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the car's presence and the green light's state, updating its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the green light to turn on. Once the green light is on, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it periodically returns to its initial state, indicating that it has responded to the car's presence. 

Overall, the TrafficE2 system is designed to manage traffic flow by responding to the presence of cars and ensuring that the green light is on frequently, while also accounting for the environment's behavior and constraints.
