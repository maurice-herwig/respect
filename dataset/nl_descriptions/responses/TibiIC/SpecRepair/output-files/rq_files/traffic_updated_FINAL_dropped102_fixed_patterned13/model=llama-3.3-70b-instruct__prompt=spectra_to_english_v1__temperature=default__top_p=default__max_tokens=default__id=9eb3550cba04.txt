The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: when there is an emergency, the next state will have a car on road A (cara) if the current state does not have one, unless the emergency has passed. Similarly, when there is an emergency, the next state will have a car on road B (carb) if the current state does not have one, unless the emergency has passed, road B's light is green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when there is an emergency, if there is a car on road A, it will move (cara will become false) in the next state unless road A's light is not green. The same assumption applies to road B: if there is a car on road B during an emergency, it will move (carb will become false) in the next state unless the emergency has passed, there is no car on road B, or road B's light is not green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors: during non-emergency situations, road B's light will not be green. When a car is present on road A and road B's light is not green, road A's light will be green. When a car is present on road B and road B's light is green, the system will ensure that this condition is met. During non-emergency situations, both roads' lights will not be red at the same time.

The system also ensures that whenever a car appears on a road, the corresponding light will eventually turn green. This is achieved through a pattern of responses: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to cars on the roads. If a car appears on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to the initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system will always respond to cars on the roads by turning the corresponding light green.
