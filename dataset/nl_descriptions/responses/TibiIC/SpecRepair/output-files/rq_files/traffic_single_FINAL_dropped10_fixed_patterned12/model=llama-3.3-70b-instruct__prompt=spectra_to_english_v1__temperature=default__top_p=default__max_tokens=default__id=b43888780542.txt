The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or become present only when an emergency occurs or the traffic light is not green. The environment is also expected to ensure that when the police are present, the car is either absent or will become absent at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly over time. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously monitors the environment and updates its state accordingly. When a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the traffic light remains green while a car is present, the system stays in its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it can respond to cars in a timely manner.
