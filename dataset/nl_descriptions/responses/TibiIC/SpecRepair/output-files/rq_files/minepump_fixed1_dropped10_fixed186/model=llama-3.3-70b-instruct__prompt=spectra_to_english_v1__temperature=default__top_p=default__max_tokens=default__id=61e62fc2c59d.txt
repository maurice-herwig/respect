The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever the water is high, the pump is not operating. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system ensures that the pump operates based on the presence or absence of methane, while respecting the environmental constraint related to high water levels.
