The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that the emergency signal is usually false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment ensures that if a car is present on road A, it will either remain present, the emergency signal will still be true, or the light for road A will be green, and the car will move when the light is green. Similar assumptions are made for road B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green, and similarly for road B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light is turned green whenever a car is present, as long as the emergency signal is not true. The system also ensures that if a car is present and the light is not green, it will eventually turn the light green.
