The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency mode. The environment controls the presence of cars on approaches A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the emergency mode, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights for approaches A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency mode is active, it will not allow a car to approach from direction A unless the traffic light for A is green, or the emergency mode remains active, or the car is already approaching. Similarly, for direction B, the environment will not allow a car to approach unless the traffic light for B is green, or the emergency mode remains active, or the car is not approaching. Additionally, when the emergency mode is active, the environment will ensure that if a car is approaching from direction A, it will stop approaching when the traffic light for A turns red, unless the emergency mode remains active. The same applies to direction B, with the added condition that the car must not be approaching when the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that when a car approaches from direction A, the traffic light for A will eventually turn green, and similarly for direction B. This guarantee is met by the system ensuring that whenever a car is approaching, the traffic light will turn green, and once green, it will stay green until the car stops approaching or the traffic light turns red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to approaching cars. The system's response to approaching cars is governed by a safety requirement that ensures the traffic light is green when a car is approaching, and a liveness requirement that ensures the traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is approaching. The system's state is updated based on the presence of cars and the current state of the traffic lights, ensuring that the traffic lights turn green when cars are approaching and turn red when cars stop approaching.
