The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the traffic light is red (i.e., neither greena nor greenb is true), car A will not be present if there is no emergency, and car B will not be present if there is an emergency or the traffic light for road B is green and car B is not already present. Additionally, when the traffic light for road A is green, car A will move (i.e., its presence will change) if there is an emergency, and when the traffic light for road B is green, car B will move if there is an emergency.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B will not both be red at the same time, unless there is an emergency. If there is a car on road A and the traffic light for road B is red, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. If there is a car on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, the system will keep the traffic light for road B green. In emergency situations, the system will turn both traffic lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system will always be in one of two states: a state where it can respond to the presence of cars (S0), or a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green (S1). If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. If the traffic light turns green while the system is in the waiting state, it will move back to the responding state. The system will always eventually return to the responding state.
