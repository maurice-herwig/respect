The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the emergency variable is true, the environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: if the traffic light at intersection A is red, the environment will either keep cara false or make it true at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or the traffic light at intersection A turns green. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B. Furthermore, when the emergency variable is true and the traffic light at an intersection is green, the environment is assumed to either keep the corresponding car variable false or make it false at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. If there is a car at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, if there is a car at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. When an emergency situation occurs, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any cars. If a car is present at an intersection and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is responding to the car. Once the system is responding to a car, it will turn the corresponding traffic light green. The system will continue to respond to the car until the traffic light is green, at which point it will transition back to its initial state. This process repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system responds to cars at both intersections and turns the corresponding traffic lights green.
