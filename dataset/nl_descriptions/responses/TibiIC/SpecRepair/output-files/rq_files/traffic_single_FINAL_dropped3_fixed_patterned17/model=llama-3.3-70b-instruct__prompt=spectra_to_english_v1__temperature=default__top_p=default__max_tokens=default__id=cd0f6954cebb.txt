The TrafficE2 system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to occasionally not have police present and to not have a car moving when the traffic light is red, unless the police are present or the car is already moving. Additionally, the environment is assumed to have cars idle or not present when the traffic light is not green, unless a car is already moving.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and that there will be periods where no car is present. 

The system also ensures that the traffic light operates according to a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system remains in this initial state as long as there is no car present or the light is already green when a car arrives. 

In this pattern, the system ensures that it periodically returns to the initial state, allowing it to continuously respond to cars.
