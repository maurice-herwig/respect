The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, it will remain present in the next state. On the other hand, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. In emergency situations, the system will turn both lights red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
