The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, and when a car is stopped at a red light, it will remain stopped until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car's presence. When a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars.
