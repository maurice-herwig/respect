The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that whenever there is high water, the pump will not be operational, as the environment ensures that if there is no high water, the pump can be operational, but if the pump is operational, there is no high water.

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be operational at the next moment or there must be no high water. Secondly, if the pump is not operational at the next moment, then there must be no methane present.

In summary, the system ensures safe operation of the pump based on the environmental conditions of high water and methane presence, under the assumption that high water prevents pump operation.
