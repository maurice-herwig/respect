The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane becomes present, then either the water level is not high or the pump will be turned on at the next step, provided that the pump was not already on. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step. 

In summary, the system must ensure the pump is operated based on the presence of methane and the water level, while the environment ensures that methane presence is related to the pump's previous state and the water level. The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane levels, and the system-controlled output is the pump operation.
