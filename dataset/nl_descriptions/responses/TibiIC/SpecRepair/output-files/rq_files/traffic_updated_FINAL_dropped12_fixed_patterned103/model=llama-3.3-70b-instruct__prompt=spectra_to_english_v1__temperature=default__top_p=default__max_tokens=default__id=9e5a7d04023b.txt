The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and an emergency indicator, denoted by emergency. The system-controlled outputs are the green light indicators for cars A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency indicator will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the system's green light for car B is on, the environment will not have an emergency situation. The environment will also ensure that if car A is about to arrive, it will either not arrive or there will be an emergency situation, or car A will already be present. Similarly, if there is an emergency situation, the environment will either maintain the emergency situation or have the system's green light for car B on. The environment will also ensure that car A will move when its corresponding green light is on, and car B will move when its corresponding green light is on, unless there is an emergency situation.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will not have both green lights off when there is no emergency situation. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on its green light. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on its green light. When there is an emergency situation, the system will turn off both green lights. The system will also ensure that when a car is present, its corresponding green light will be turned on in response to the car's presence, following a specific pattern: initially, the system will be in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence; if the car is present and its green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on; once the green light is turned on, the system will transition back to the initial state; this pattern will repeat indefinitely, with the system always eventually responding to the car's presence by turning on its green light.
