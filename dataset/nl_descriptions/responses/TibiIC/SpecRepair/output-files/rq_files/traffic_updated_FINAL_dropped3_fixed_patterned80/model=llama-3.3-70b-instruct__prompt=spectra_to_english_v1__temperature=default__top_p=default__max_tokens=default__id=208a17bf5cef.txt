The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is present, if the light for road A is red, then there is no car on road A; when an emergency is present, the light for road B is always green; when an emergency is present or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when an emergency is present or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car; if a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green; once the light turns green, the system returns to the initial state, ready to respond to the next car; this pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always eventually responds to the presence of a car on either road.
