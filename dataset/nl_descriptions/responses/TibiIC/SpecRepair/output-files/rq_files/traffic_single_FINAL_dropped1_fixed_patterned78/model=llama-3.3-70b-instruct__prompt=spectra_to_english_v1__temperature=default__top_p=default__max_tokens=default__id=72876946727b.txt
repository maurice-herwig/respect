The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions frequently, and when a car is present, the traffic light should be green or the car should not be moving. Additionally, when a car is about to move, it should either be under police control, not moving, or the traffic light should not be green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a state machine that ensures the system reacts appropriately to the car's presence and the traffic light's state. Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the car's presence and the traffic light's state, updating its state accordingly.

The system's safety requirements dictate that it should always be in one of two states: either ready to respond to the car's presence or waiting for the traffic light to turn green. When the system is ready to respond and a car is present without the traffic light being green, it transitions to the waiting state. When the traffic light is green in the waiting state, the system returns to the ready state.

The system also ensures that it will infinitely often be in the ready state, allowing it to respond to the car's presence as needed. Overall, the TrafficE2 system is designed to manage traffic flow in response to environmental conditions, prioritizing safe and efficient movement of cars.
