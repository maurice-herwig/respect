The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency flag. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency flag is set, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency flag is set, the traffic light for road B must be green if there is a car on road B; when the emergency flag is set or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next step; and when the emergency flag is set or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's presence. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system eventually responds to the presence of cars on the roads by turning the corresponding traffic lights green.
