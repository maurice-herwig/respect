The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Firstly, if a car is present, it will either remain present or be absent at the next time step, unless there is an emergency, in which case the car's presence may change. Secondly, if a car is not present, it will either remain absent or be absent at the next time step, unless the traffic light is green, in which case the car's presence may change.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This is achieved through a pattern of behavior where the system starts in an initial state and transitions between two states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light's state. The system remains in the initial state if the car is not present or if the car is present and the traffic light is green. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a secondary state. The system returns to the initial state if the traffic light becomes green while in the secondary state. The system will infinitely often be in the initial state.

Initially, the system starts in the initial state. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always transition between states correctly based on the presence of the car and the traffic light's state. The system's liveness requirement ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state, where it can respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green.
