The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation frequently, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. When car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and car A is present, car B will remain present until its light turns green. When car A's light is green and car A is present, car A will move and no longer be present. The same applies to car B: when its light is green and it is present, it will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both green lights off at the same time, ensuring that at least one car can proceed. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B: if it is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. During an emergency, both green lights will be turned off.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding green light on, and once the light is on, the car will move and the light will be turned off. This pattern repeats, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars in a consistent and predictable manner.
