The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, and when a car is present at a red light, it will remain idle until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car is present at a green light, it will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are not both red at the same time. When a car is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding green light on. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's behavior is such that when a car is present, it will respond by turning on its corresponding green light, and it will do so in a way that ensures the light is turned on eventually. 

The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations, and its liveness requirements ensure that it will eventually turn on the green light for a present car. The system's next-state relationships ensure that it will update its state correctly in response to changes in the environment.
