The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is eventually always the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain idle in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is green, car A will move and no longer be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the green light for car A to green. The same applies to car B. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both green lights for car A and car B are red. 

The system's response to car presence is governed by a pattern that ensures the system eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding green light to green. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it remains in this state as long as the car is not present or its green light is already green. If the car is present and its green light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence.
