The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either not be present or not move until the emergency is over or the light turns green. Similarly, cars at intersection B are assumed to either not be present or not move until the emergency is over. Furthermore, when the light is green at an intersection and an emergency occurs, cars are assumed to move and clear the intersection.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green. Specifically, if a car is present at intersection A, the system will respond by turning the light at intersection A green, and similarly for intersection B. The system also guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car arrives at an intersection and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that cars are given the opportunity to pass through the intersection.
