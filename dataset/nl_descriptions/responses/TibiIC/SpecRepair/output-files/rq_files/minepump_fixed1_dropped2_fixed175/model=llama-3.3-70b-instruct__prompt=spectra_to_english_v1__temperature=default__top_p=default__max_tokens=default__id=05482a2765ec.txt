The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment will not turn the pump on when it is off, unless it was already off in the previous state.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system ensures that the pump is operated based on the presence of methane and the water level, under the assumption that the environment does not arbitrarily turn the pump on when it is off.
