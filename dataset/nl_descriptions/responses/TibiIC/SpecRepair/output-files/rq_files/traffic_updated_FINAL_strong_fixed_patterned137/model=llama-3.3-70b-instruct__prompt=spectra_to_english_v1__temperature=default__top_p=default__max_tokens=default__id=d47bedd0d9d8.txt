The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B when its light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning their respective lights green, but only after it has not done so previously. Once a car's light is turned green, it will remain in this state until the car is gone, at which point it will return to its initial state and be ready to respond to the car's presence again. The system will always eventually return to this initial state.
