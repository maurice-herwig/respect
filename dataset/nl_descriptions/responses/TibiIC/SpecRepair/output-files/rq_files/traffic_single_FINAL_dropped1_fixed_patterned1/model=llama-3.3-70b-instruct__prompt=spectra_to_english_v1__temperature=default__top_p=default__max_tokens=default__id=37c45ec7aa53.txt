The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (not green), there should be no car present. The environment is also expected to ensure that if a car is present and the police are not present, then either the car will move (not be present in the next state) or the traffic light will turn red.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green when a car is present. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car (state S0). The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or if a car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state (S1) where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state (S0). The system will infinitely often be in its initial state (S0), ensuring that it periodically resets and is ready to respond to the presence of a car.

In summary, the environment controls the car, emergency, and police presence, while the system controls the traffic light (green signal). The system ensures that it will periodically turn the traffic light green when a car is present, under the assumption that the environment behaves according to the specified rules.
