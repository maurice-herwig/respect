The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by two assumptions: 
- if the pump was previously on and remains on, then the water level will decrease in the next state, 
- and if the pump is off, then either there is no high water or there is no methane present.

Given these environmental constraints, the system must satisfy two guarantees: 
- if the water level is high, then the pump must be on, or it must turn on in the next state, 
- and if methane is detected, then the pump must turn off in the next state.

In summary, the system must operate the pump based on the environmental conditions of water level and methane presence, while the environment must behave in a way that allows the system to manage the pump effectively. The pump's operation is determined by the current and future states of the water level and methane detection, with the goal of safely managing the water level and avoiding operation in the presence of methane.
