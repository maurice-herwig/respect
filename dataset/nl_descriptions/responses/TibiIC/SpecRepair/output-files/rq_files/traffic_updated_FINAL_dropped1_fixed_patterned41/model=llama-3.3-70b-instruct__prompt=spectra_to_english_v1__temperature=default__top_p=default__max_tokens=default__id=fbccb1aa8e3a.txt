The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will not be present in the next state unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency situation persists. Similarly, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection B will not be present in the next state unless the traffic light at intersection B is green or the emergency situation persists. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A will move and not be present in the next state unless the emergency situation persists or the traffic light at intersection A turns red. The same applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. The same applies to intersection B. Furthermore, when an emergency situation is present, both traffic lights will turn red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: a state where it can respond to the presence of cars and turn the corresponding traffic light green, or a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. The system will transition between these states based on the presence of cars and the current state of the traffic lights. The system will always eventually return to the state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
