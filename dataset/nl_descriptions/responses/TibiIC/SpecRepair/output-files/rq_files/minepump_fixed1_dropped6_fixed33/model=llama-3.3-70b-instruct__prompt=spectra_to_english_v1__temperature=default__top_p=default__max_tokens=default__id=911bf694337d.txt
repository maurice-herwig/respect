The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

It is assumed that the environment will not introduce methane when the pump is already on. In other words, whenever methane becomes present, it must be the case that the pump was off in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, or there must not be high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

These guarantees ensure that the pump operates appropriately in response to the environmental conditions of methane presence and water level, while respecting the constraints imposed by the environment's behavior.
