The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that there will be times when police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car should not be moving, unless there is an emergency. When the traffic light is green, the car should move, unless the police or an emergency situation intervenes, in which case the car should not move. Furthermore, if there is no emergency, the car should not move if the police are present.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and that there will be times when no car is present. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior is characterized by a pattern of responses to the car's presence. When a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will remain so as long as the car is present, after which it may turn red again. The system will repeatedly return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
