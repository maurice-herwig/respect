The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have the emergency signal on indefinitely, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Additionally, when a car's light is green, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When the emergency signal is on, both lights will be red.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars.
