The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, a car will not start moving unless the light turns green or the car remains stationary. The environment is also assumed to behave such that when the police are present, or there is no car, or the car will not move, or the light is not green, the car will not move when the light is green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then enters a loop where it waits for a car to arrive and turns green when the car is present. If a car arrives and the light is not green, the system will turn the light green when possible. The system remains in this loop, turning the light green when a car is present and turning it off when the car is no longer present or the light has been green. The system will always eventually return to its initial waiting state. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
