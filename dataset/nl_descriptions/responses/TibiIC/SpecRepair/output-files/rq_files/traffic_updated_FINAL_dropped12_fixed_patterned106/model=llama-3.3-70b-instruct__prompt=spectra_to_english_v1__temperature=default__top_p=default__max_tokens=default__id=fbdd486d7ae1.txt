The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have frequent emergency situations, as emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when car A is about to appear, it will either not appear or there will be an emergency situation. If there is an emergency situation, then either the emergency situation will continue or the green light for car B will be on. When car A is present, it will move when the light is green. Similarly, when car B is present, it will move when the light is green, unless there is an emergency situation.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency situation, the green light for car B will be off. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. When car B is present, the system will eventually turn on either the green light for car A or the green light for car B. During an emergency situation, both green lights will be off. 

The system also ensures that the green lights for car A and car B are not both off at the same time when there is no emergency situation. The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to appear. If a car appears and the corresponding green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to turn on. Once the green light turns on, the system will transition back to the initial state. This process will repeat indefinitely, ensuring that the green light will eventually turn on when a car is present.
