The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and there should be periods where the police are not present. When the traffic light is not green, the car should not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, when the car is moving and the traffic light turns green, the car should continue moving.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present, it remains in this state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a next state, where it will turn the traffic light green, allowing the car to pass. Once the traffic light is green, the system will return to its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in this initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

Initially, the system is in this initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always follow the rules of transitioning between states based on the presence of the car and the state of the traffic light. The system's liveness requirement ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state, allowing it to respond to the car's presence. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
