The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car on road A, and it will either not introduce a car on road B, or the traffic light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment will ensure that if there is a car on road A, it will eventually move, unless the traffic light for road A is red. Similarly, if there is a car on road B, it will eventually move, unless the traffic light for road B is red or an emergency is in effect.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the traffic light for road B will be red. The system will also ensure that if there is a car on road A and the traffic light for road B is red, the traffic light for road A will eventually turn green. If there is a car on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, the system will maintain this state. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always respond correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations, and its liveness requirements ensure that it will eventually turn the traffic lights green when necessary. Specifically, when a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green, unless an emergency occurs or the car moves.
