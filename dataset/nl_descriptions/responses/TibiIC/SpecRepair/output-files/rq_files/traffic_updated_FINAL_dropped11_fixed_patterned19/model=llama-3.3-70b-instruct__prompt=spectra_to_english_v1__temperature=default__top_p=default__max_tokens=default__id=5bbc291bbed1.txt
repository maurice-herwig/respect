The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights at an intersection. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars at the intersection (cara and carb) and whether there is an emergency (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergencies do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when there is no emergency. Additionally, when the emergency light is on, car A will not move until the light turns green or the emergency ends, unless car A is already moving. Similarly, car B will not move when the emergency light is on unless the light turns green or the emergency ends. Furthermore, when the emergency light is on and the traffic light is green for car A or B, the corresponding car will move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green for car A. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green for car B. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
