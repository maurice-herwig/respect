The TrafficE2 system controls traffic flow based on environmental inputs. The environment controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence, while the system controls the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The system assumes that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be periods when the police are not present. Additionally, the system assumes that a car will not start moving when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The system also assumes that when the police are not present, a car will not move unless the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green periodically, allowing cars to pass. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be periods when there are no cars present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and responds accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of another car. The system ensures that it periodically returns to this initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.
