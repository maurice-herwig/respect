The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency condition. The environment controls the presence of cars approaching from direction A (cara) and direction B (carb), as well as the emergency condition (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for directions A (greena) and B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency condition is true, it will not allow a car to approach from direction A unless the traffic light for direction A is green, or the emergency condition persists, or the car is already approaching. Similarly, for direction B, the environment will not allow a car to approach unless the traffic light for direction B is green, or the emergency condition persists, or the car is not approaching. Additionally, when the emergency condition is true, the environment will ensure that a car approaching from direction A will move when the traffic light for direction A is green, unless the emergency condition persists or the car is not approaching. The same applies to direction B.

The system guarantees that when a car approaches from direction A, the traffic light for direction A will eventually turn green, and similarly for direction B. This guarantee is fulfilled through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to approach. If a car approaches and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to its initial state, indicating that the traffic light has turned green in response to a car approaching.

Initially, the system is in its waiting state. The system ensures that it will always satisfy the safety condition that it will either remain in its waiting state if a car is not approaching or the traffic light is green, or transition to the waiting-for-green state if a car is approaching and the traffic light is not green. The system also ensures that it will infinitely often return to its waiting state, indicating that the traffic light has turned green in response to a car approaching.
