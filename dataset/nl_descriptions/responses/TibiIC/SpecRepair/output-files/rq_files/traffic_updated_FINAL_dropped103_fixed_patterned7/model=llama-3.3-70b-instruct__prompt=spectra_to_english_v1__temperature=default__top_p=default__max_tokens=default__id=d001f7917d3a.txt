The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, represented by the variable emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will not move until the emergency situation has passed or the traffic light turns green, and cars at intersection B will not move until the emergency situation has passed or the traffic light turns green. Additionally, when the traffic light is green at intersection A, cars will move, and when the traffic light is green at intersection B, cars will move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light will eventually turn green, and when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light will eventually turn green. During an emergency situation, both traffic lights will be red.

The system also responds to the presence of cars in a specific way. When a car is present at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the traffic light green, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions. This response is guaranteed to happen in a way that ensures the traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and will remain green until the car is no longer present or the traffic light needs to turn red due to an emergency situation or the presence of a car at the other intersection. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present or the traffic light is green, and it transitions to a different state when a car is present and the traffic light is not green. The system will periodically return to the initial state, ensuring that it is always ready to respond to the presence of cars.
