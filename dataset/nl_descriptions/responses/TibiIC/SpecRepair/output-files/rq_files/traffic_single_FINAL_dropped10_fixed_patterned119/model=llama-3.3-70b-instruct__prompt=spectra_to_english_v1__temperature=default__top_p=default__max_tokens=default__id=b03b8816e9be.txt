The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when a car is present and the light is not green, the car will either remain present, an emergency will occur, or the car will leave. The environment is also assumed to behave such that when the police are present, the car will either not be present or will leave.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light turns green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then enters a loop where it remains in this state as long as a car is not present or is present and the light is green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
