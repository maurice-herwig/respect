The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Furthermore, when car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and its presence will be removed. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

In general, the system responds to the presence of cars by turning their corresponding lights green, while ensuring that both lights are not red simultaneously and taking into account emergency situations. The system's behavior is such that it prioritizes safety and responsiveness to the environment's conditions.
