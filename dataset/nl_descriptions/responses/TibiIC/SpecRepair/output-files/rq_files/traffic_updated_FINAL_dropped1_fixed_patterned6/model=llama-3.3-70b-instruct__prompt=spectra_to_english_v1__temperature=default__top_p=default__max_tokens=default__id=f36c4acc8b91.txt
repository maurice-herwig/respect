The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the occurrence of emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the emergency light is on, the environment ensures that if a car is approaching on road A, it will either stop or the emergency will end, or the light for road A will turn green. Similarly, for road B, if a car is approaching, it will either stop or the emergency will end, or the light for road B will turn green. The environment also ensures that when the emergency light is on and the light for road A is green, any approaching car on road A will either not be present or will move, or the emergency will end, or the light for road A will turn red. The same condition applies to road B.

The system guarantees that the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. The same guarantee applies to road B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs, the system ensures that either the emergency ends or both lights turn red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. The system will repeatedly respond to the presence of cars on the roads, turning the corresponding lights green when necessary.
