The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and there should be periods where the police are not present. Additionally, the environment should ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The environment should also ensure that when the traffic light is green, a car is either present or the police are present, or the traffic light remains red.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present, the system remains in this state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a next state, where it remains until the traffic light turns green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the initial state, indicating that it will periodically respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
