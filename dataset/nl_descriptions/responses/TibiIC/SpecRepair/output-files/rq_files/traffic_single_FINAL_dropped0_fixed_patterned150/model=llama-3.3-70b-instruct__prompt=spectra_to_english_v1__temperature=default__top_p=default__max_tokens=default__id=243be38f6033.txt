The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not always be present, and there will be times when they are not intervening. Additionally, when the traffic light is red (not green), the car will not start moving unless there is an emergency, the light turns green, or the car is already moving. The environment is also expected to ensure that when the police are not present, the car will either not be moving or will stop moving when the light is not green.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will turn green periodically, and when a car is present, the system will ensure that the light turns green. Furthermore, the system guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system then enters a loop where it either remains in this state, waiting for a car to arrive when the light is green, or transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green when a car is present. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it will periodically return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.
