The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by the assumption that if the water level is not high in the next state, then it will remain not high in the subsequent state, unless the pump is turned off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane levels, while the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. The system's behavior is defined solely by the relationships between the methane level and the pump's operation, as specified by the guarantees.
