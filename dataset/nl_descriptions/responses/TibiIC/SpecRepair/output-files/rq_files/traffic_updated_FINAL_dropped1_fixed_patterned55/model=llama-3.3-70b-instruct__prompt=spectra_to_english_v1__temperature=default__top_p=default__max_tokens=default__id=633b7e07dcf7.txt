The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, and when an emergency situation occurs, cars at intersection A will either not be present or will not move until the emergency situation ends or the traffic light at intersection A turns green. Similarly, when an emergency situation occurs, cars at intersection B will either not be present or will not move until the traffic light at intersection B turns green. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A will move and will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency situation ends or the traffic light at intersection A turns red. A similar assumption applies to cars at intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the traffic light at intersection B will eventually turn green. During an emergency situation, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. This process repeats as long as cars are present and the corresponding traffic lights are not green.
