The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when the system is in an emergency state, the environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: if car A is not present when the light is red, it will not become present at the next time step unless the emergency state ends or the light turns green; similarly, if car B is not present when the light is red, it will not become present at the next time step unless the emergency state ends or the light turns green. Furthermore, when the system is in an emergency state and the light is green, the environment is assumed to ensure that car A and car B move, meaning that they will not be present at the next time step unless the emergency state ends or the light turns red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green; similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car arrives at an intersection, the system will turn the corresponding light green, but only if the other light is not already green. If a car is present at an intersection and the corresponding light is not green, the system will turn the light green at some point in the future. The system will always return to its initial state after responding to a car's presence.
