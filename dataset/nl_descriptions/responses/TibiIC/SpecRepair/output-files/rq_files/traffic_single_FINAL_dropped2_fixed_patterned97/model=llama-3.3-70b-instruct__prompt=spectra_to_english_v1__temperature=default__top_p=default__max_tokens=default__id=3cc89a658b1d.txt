The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: when the police are present, the car will either be present, an emergency will occur, or the car will not be present at the next time step. Additionally, when the car is not present, it will either remain absent or the traffic light will not be green at the next time step.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This is achieved through a state machine that tracks the system's response to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is attempting to respond to the car. In this state, the system will turn the traffic light green, allowing it to transition back to the initial state. The system will infinitely often return to the initial state, ensuring that it regularly responds to the presence of a car.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of regularly turning the traffic light green when a car is present ensures safe and efficient traffic flow.
