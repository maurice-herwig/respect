The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment provides inputs indicating the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. When an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road A at the next time step if the light is red, unless the emergency persists or the light turns green. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the environment will not allow a car to be present on road B at the next time step if the light is red, unless the emergency persists, the light turns green, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment will ensure that if the light is green for road A, a car will not be present on road A at the next time step, unless the emergency persists or the light turns red. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if an emergency occurs, the light will not be green for road B.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights will never be green for both roads A and B at the same time. If a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light green for road A. Similarly, if a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light green for road B. However, if an emergency occurs, the system will ensure that the lights are always red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system will respond to the presence of a car on a road by eventually turning the corresponding light green. The system will also ensure that if a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, it will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green, and then back to the initial state once the light turns green. The system will infinitely often return to the initial state, indicating that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
