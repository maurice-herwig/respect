The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. In response, the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently on, then either the high water condition will not be present in the next state or the pump was off in the previous state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly controlled by the presence or absence of methane, with the additional constraint that the pump's operation influences the expected behavior of the high water condition in the environment.
