The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on, and when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. If an emergency situation occurs, both green lights will be turned off.

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding green light on when the car is present. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state as long as the car is not present or its green light is on. If the car is present and its green light is off, the system will transition to a state where it is responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state until the green light is turned on. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
