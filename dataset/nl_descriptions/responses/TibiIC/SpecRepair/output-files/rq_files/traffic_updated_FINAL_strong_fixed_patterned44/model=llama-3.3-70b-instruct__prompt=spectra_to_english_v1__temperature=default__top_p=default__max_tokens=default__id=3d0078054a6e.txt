The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will move the car, making it absent in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. If an emergency occurs, the system ensures that both lights turn red. The system also responds to the presence of cars A and B by eventually turning their respective lights green when they are present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system maintains a safety condition where it either responds to the presence of a car by turning its light green or remains in a state waiting for the car's light to turn green. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. 

In the context of this system, "responding" to a car means turning its corresponding light green when the car is present, which the system does in a way that ensures the light will eventually turn green if the car remains present.
