The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. When a car is present and the corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During an emergency, both lights will be red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: waiting for a car to trigger a response or waiting for the response to be fulfilled. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to the state where it waits for the response to be fulfilled. Once the response is fulfilled, i.e., the car's light is turned green, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will infinitely often be in the waiting state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
