The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency status. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and the emergency status (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency status is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when the emergency status is active, if the traffic light for road B is red, then the emergency status should still be active; when the emergency status is not active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency status is not active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green; once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern repeats infinitely, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
