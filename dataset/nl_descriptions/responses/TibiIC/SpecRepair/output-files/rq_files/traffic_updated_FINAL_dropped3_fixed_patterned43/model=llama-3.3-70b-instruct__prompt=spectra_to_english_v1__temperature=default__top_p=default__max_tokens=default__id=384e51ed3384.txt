The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is present, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when an emergency is present, the traffic light for road B should be green if there is no emergency; when an emergency is present or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will be red at the next time step; and when an emergency is present or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road B green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive on either road. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present on either road or the corresponding traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often return to the initial state, ensuring that it continually responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
