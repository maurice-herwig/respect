The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A and the light is red, the car will either remain idle, the emergency will persist, the light will turn green, or the car will move away. Similar assumptions are made for road B. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light is green for a road, the environment ensures that the car on that road will either move away or the emergency will persist, or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road A. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green for road B. During emergencies, the system ensures that both lights are red.

The system's response to cars on the roads is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green when a car is present. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's presence to be acknowledged. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, and this cycle repeats as necessary to ensure the car's presence is responded to. The system is designed to always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it continually monitors and responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
