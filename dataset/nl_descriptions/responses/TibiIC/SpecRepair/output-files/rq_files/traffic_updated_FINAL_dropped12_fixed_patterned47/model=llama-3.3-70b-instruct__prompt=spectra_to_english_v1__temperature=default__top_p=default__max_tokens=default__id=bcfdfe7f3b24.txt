The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, car A is idle unless there is an emergency, and car B is idle unless there is an emergency or the traffic light at intersection B is green. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green, car A moves, and car B moves unless there is an emergency or the traffic light at intersection B is not green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red when there is no emergency. When car A is present, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. When car B is present, the system eventually responds by turning either the traffic light at intersection A or intersection B green. During an emergency, both traffic lights are red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern of behavior. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If the system has not yet responded to a car's presence, it remains in a state where it is waiting for the car to arrive. The system always eventually returns to the waiting state.
