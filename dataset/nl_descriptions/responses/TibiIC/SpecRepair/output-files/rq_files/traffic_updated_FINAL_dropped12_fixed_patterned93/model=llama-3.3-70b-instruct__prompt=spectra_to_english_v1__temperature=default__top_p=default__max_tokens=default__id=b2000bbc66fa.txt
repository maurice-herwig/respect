The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A, the presence of car B, and an emergency signal. The system-controlled outputs are the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the emergency signal is not always present, and there are periods where it is false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is not present, car A is not moving if its green light is not on, and car B is not moving if its green light is not on. Furthermore, when the emergency signal is present, car B's behavior is constrained such that if it is not moving, it will continue to not move or its green light will be on. The environment also ensures that car A moves when its green light is on, and car B moves when its green light is on, unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The green lights for car A and car B are never both off when there is no emergency. When car A is present, the system eventually turns on its green light. When car B is present, the system eventually turns on either its green light or car A's green light. During an emergency, both green lights are off.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's behavior can be described by a pattern of response, where it responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning on the corresponding green light. This pattern involves the system being in one of two states: a state where it is ready to respond, and a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. The system transitions between these states based on the presence of the car and the green light. The system always eventually returns to the ready state.
