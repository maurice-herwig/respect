The TrafficE2 system is designed to control traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. When car A is present and its light is red and there is no emergency, it will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next time step. Conversely, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next time step, and the same applies to car B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present, the system will turn its corresponding light green, and once the light is green, the car will move and the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next presence of a car. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
