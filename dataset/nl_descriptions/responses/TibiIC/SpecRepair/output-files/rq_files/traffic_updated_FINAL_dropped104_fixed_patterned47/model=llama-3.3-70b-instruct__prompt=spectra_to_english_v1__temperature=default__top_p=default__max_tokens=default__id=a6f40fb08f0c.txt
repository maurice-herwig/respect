The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars approaching from two directions, A and B, and an emergency condition. The environment controls the presence of cars approaching from direction A (cara) and direction B (carb), as well as the emergency condition (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for directions A (greena) and B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency condition is true, it will not start a car approaching from direction A (cara) unless the traffic light for direction A is green (greena) or the emergency condition persists. Similarly, for direction B, the environment will not start a car approaching (carb) unless the traffic light for direction B is green (greenb), the emergency condition persists, or there is no car approaching from direction B. Additionally, when the emergency condition is true, the environment will ensure that cars approaching from directions A and B will move when their respective traffic lights are green, by not having a car approaching when the light is green unless the car is already moving or the light turns red.

The system guarantees that when a car approaches from direction A (cara is true), it will eventually turn the traffic light for direction A green (greena is true), and similarly for direction B. This guarantee is fulfilled by the system responding to the presence of cars in a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it infinitely often returns to this initial state, allowing it to continually respond to cars approaching from both directions.
