The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A present), `carb` (car B present), and `emergency` (emergency situation). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (green light for car A) and `greenb` (green light for car B).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, car A will not be present when the light is red unless there is an emergency, car B will not be present when the light is red unless there is an emergency or the light is green for car B, car A will move when the light is green, and car B will move when the light is green unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the lights will not both be red, and when car A is present, the system will eventually respond with a green light for car A. Additionally, when car B is present, the system will eventually respond with a green light for either car A or car B. The system also guarantees that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

The system has initial conditions where it starts in a state where it is not responding to any stimuli. The system then enters a loop where it responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding lights green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will transition back to its initial state.

In terms of safety requirements, the system ensures that it will always be in one of two states: either it is not responding to any stimuli, or it is waiting for a light to turn green. The system also ensures that it will infinitely often be in the state where it is not responding to any stimuli. The next-state relationships are defined such that the system will transition between these two states based on the presence of cars and the state of the lights.
