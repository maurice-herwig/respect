The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, and emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, indicated by greena and greenb.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have emergencies often, and when the traffic light is red for car A, car A should be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when the traffic light is red for car B, car B should be idle unless there is an emergency or the traffic light for car B is green. Additionally, when the traffic light is green for car A, car A should move, and when the traffic light is green for car B, car B should move unless there is an emergency.

The system guarantees certain behavior: when there is no emergency, the traffic light for car B should not be green if the traffic light for car A is not green. The system should also ensure that when car A is present, the traffic light for car A should eventually turn green. When car B is present, the traffic light for either car A or car B should eventually turn green. Furthermore, when there is an emergency, both traffic lights should be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green, and it does so in a way that ensures the traffic lights are turned green in response to the presence of cars in a timely manner. The system ensures that once it has responded to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green, it will continue to do so until the car is no longer present.
