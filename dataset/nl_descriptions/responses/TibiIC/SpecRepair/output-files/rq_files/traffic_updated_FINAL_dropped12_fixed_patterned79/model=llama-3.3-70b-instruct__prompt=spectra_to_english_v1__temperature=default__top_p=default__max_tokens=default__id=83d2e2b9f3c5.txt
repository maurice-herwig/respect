The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to not have frequent emergency situations, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the traffic light at intersection A is red, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when there is an emergency, car B is assumed to be idle unless the traffic light at intersection B is green. Cars A and B are also assumed to move when their respective traffic lights are green.

The system guarantees that when there is no emergency, the traffic light at intersection B will not be green. The system also guarantees that if car A is present, the traffic light at intersection A will eventually turn green. If car B is present, the system guarantees that either the traffic light at intersection A or intersection B will eventually turn green. Furthermore, when there is an emergency, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic lights green. The system ensures that if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, it will transition to a state where it will turn the traffic light green. Once the traffic light is green, the system will transition back to the waiting state. The system will always eventually return to the waiting state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
