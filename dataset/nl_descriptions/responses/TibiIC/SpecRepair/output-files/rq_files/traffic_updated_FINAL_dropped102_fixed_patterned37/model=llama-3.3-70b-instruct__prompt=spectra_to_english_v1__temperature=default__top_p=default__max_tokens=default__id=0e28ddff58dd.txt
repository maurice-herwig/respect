The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergencies (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for roads A (greena) and B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car on road A, and it will either not introduce a car on road B, or the light for road B will be green, or no car will be present on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that a car on road A will eventually move, and similarly, when the light for road B is green, a car on road B will eventually move.

The system guarantees the following behavior: during normal operation (i.e., no emergency), the light for road B will be red. When a car is present on road A and the light for road B is red, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. When a car is present on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will continue to keep the light for road B green. During an emergency, both lights will be red.

In terms of specific rules, the system operates as follows: initially, it is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system will always eventually return to this initial state, ensuring that it continually responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
