The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red and the police are present, if a car is waiting, it will remain waiting in the next state. Furthermore, when the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move in the next state.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Additionally, the system ensures that there will always be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a car is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars.
