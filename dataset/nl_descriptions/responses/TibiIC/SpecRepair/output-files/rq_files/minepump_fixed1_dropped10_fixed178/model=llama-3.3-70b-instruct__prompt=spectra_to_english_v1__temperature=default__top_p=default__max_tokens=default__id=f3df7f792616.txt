The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the detection of these conditions, specifically whether there is high water and whether methane is present. The system, in response, controls a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that whenever methane is not present, the pump should not be activated. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be activated at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not detected, then the pump must not be activated at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly tied to the detection of methane, with the pump being activated when methane is present and deactivated when methane is not present, under the assumption that the environment does not activate the pump unnecessarily when methane is not detected.
