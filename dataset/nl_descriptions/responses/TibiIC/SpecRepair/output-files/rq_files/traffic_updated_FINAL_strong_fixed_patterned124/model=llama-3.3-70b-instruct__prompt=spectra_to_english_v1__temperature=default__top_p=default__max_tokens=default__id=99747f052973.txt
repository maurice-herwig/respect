The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state, but only if car A is present and moving when car B's light is green.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present, its corresponding green light will eventually be turned on. The system also ensures that when an emergency occurs, both green lights will be turned off. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or a present car's corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn on the car's green light. Once the green light is turned on, the system returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of other cars. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
