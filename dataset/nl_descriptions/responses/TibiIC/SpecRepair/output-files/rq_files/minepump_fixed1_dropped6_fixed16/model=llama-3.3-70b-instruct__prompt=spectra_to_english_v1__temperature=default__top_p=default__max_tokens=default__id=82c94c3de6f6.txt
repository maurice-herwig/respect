The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are high water and methane, which indicate whether the water level is high and whether methane is present, respectively. The system-controlled output is the pump, which can be either on or off.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that the pump is not initially on and cannot turn on unless it was previously off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must turn on at the next step, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must turn off at the next step. 

These requirements ensure that the pump operates in response to the presence or absence of methane, while also considering the water level.
