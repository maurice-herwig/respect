The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave as follows: when there is an emergency, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there are no cars on road A; when there is an emergency, the traffic light for road B is either green or there is still an emergency; when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next step; when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on each road is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green when a car is present. This pattern has an initial state where the system is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. The system will infinitely often return to this initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars on each road.
