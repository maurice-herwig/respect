The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its green light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its green light is red while car A's green light is green, car B will remain idle. However, when a car's green light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed in the next state.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both green lights off. 

In general, the system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding green light on, unless an emergency occurs. The system ensures that if a car is present, its green light will be turned on, and once the car moves, its presence will be removed.
