The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that emergencies do not occur frequently, meaning that there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red, the environment ensures that if an emergency is present, then either a car will be present on road A at the next time step, or an emergency will still be present, or the traffic light for road A will be green. Similar assumptions are made for road B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency is present and the traffic light is green for a road, then either the emergency will still be present, or there will be no car on that road, or there will be no car on that road at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light for road B green. During emergencies, the system ensures that if the emergency is present, then either the traffic lights for both roads will be red, or the emergency will not be present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of cars on the roads by turning the corresponding traffic lights green.
