The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the levels of high water and methane, while the system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the water level is not high at the next step, then it will remain not high at the subsequent step, unless the pump is turned off.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned on at the next step, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if methane is not detected, the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and high water, with specific rules governing its next state, ensuring safe operation under the given environmental conditions. The high water level and methane presence are the environment-controlled inputs, while the pump status is the system-controlled output.
