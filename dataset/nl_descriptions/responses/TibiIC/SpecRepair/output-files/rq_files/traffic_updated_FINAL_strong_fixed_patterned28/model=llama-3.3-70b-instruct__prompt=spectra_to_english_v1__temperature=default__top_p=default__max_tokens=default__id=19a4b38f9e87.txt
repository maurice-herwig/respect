The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until its light turns green. However, when a car's light is green and there is no emergency, the car will move and its presence will be removed at the next time step. If a car's light is green and the emergency signal is present, the car's behavior is not specified.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly for car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green, but only after the car has been present for some time if its light was not already green. Once a car's light is green, it will remain green until the car moves or an emergency occurs. If a car's light is not green when the car is present, the system will keep track of this condition and will turn the light green when possible. The system will always eventually return to a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
