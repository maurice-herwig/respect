The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights based on input from the environment. The environment provides inputs `cara` and `carb`, indicating the presence of cars A and B, respectively, as well as an `emergency` signal. The system controls the traffic lights by setting `greena` and `greenb` to indicate when cars A and B can proceed.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, car A will not be present unless the traffic light for car A is green, or the emergency signal remains active, or car A will become present at the next time step. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, car B will not be present unless the traffic light for car B is green, or the emergency signal remains active, or car B will become present at the next time step, or car B is currently not present. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, if car A is present, it will move and not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency signal remains active, or the traffic light for car A is not green. The same assumption applies to car B.

The system guarantees that when car A is present, the traffic light for car A will eventually become green, and when car B is present, the traffic light for car B will eventually become green. This guarantee is fulfilled through a pattern of response, where the system initially waits for a car to be present. If a car is present and its corresponding traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it waits for the traffic light to become green. Once the traffic light becomes green, the system returns to its initial state, repeating the process indefinitely. The system ensures that it will always eventually return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars.
