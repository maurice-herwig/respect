The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and no longer be present, unless an emergency occurs for car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on, and similarly for car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will always respond to the presence of cars by eventually turning on the corresponding green light, and it will do so in a way that ensures the green lights are never both off at the same time. The system will repeatedly return to this ready state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars at any time.
