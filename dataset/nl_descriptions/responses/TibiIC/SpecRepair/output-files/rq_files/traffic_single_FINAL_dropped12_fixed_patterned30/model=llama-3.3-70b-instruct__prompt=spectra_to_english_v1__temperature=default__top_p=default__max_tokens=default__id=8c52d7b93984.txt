The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when there is no emergency, if a car is present and the light is not green, then the car will either not be present in the next state or the light will be green in the next state. It is also assumed that if the police are present or there is no car, then either the car will not be present in the next state or the light will not be green in the next state.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light is green whenever a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. The system also guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light is green, the system will transition back to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it is ready to respond to the presence of a car.

In terms of environment-controlled inputs, the system receives information about the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
