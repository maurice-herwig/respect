The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane is present, then either the water level will not be high in the next state or the pump was not operating in the previous state. 

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be activated in the next state or the water level must not be high. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state. 

In summary, the environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, and the system-controlled output is the operation of the pump. The system's behavior is constrained by the guarantees, which ensure the pump operates safely in response to the environmental conditions, under the assumption that the environment behaves in a specific way regarding methane presence and water level.
