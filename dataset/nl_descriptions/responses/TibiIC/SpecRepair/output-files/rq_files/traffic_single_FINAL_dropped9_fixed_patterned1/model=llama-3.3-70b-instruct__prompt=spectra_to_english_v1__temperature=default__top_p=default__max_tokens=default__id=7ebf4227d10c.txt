The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and when the traffic light is green, the car should either move or not be present in the next state. Additionally, when the police are present, the car should either not be present, move in the next state, or the traffic light should not be green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of its response to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the car's presence.
