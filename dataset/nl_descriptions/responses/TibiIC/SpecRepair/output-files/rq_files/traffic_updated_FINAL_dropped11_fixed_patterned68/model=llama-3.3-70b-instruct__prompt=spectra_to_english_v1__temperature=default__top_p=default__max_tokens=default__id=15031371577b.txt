The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and the occurrence of emergencies, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: emergencies do not occur frequently, and when an emergency is in effect, cars on road A do not start moving unless the light is green or the emergency ends, and similarly for road B. Additionally, when the emergency is in effect and the light is green for a road, cars on that road move and do not remain stationary unless the emergency ends or the light turns red.

The system guarantees several behaviors: it ensures that both traffic lights are never red at the same time, meaning at least one road always has a green light. When a car is present on road A, the system eventually responds by turning the light for road A green, and similarly for road B. During an emergency, if the lights are not both red, then the emergency must have ended. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is committed to turning the light green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system periodically responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding lights green.
