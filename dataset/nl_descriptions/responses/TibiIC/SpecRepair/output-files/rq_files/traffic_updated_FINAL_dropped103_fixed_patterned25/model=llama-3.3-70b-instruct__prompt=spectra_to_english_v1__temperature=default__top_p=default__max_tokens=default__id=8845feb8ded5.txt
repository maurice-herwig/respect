The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as the occurrence of emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for cars at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when `emergency` is false. When the light is red, cars at intersection A are idle, meaning that if `emergency` is true, then either `cara` will be true at the next time step, `emergency` remains true, `greena` is true, or `cara` is false. Similar assumptions apply to cars at intersection B. Additionally, when the light is green and an emergency occurs, cars at both intersections will move, meaning that if `emergency` is true, then either `emergency` remains true, the car has moved (i.e., `cara` or `carb` is false), or the light will turn red at the next time step.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights are designed such that both lights are never red at the same time, meaning that if `greena` is false, then `greenb` must be false. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green, and similarly for intersection B. During an emergency, the system ensures that the traffic lights are red, meaning that if `emergency` is true, then both `greena` and `greenb` must be false.

The system's behavior is also constrained by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of cars. The system's state evolves over time, with the presence of cars and the system's response to those cars determining the next state. The system is designed to eventually respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green.
