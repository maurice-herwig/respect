The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The environment controls the detection of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane is detected in the next state, then either the water is not high in the next state or the pump is not operating. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be activated in the next state. Secondly, if methane is not detected, then the pump must be deactivated in the next state. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly dependent on the detection of methane, with the environment's behavior constrained to ensure safe operation when methane is detected. The pump's state is updated based on the presence or absence of methane, ensuring a direct response to changes in the environment's conditions.
