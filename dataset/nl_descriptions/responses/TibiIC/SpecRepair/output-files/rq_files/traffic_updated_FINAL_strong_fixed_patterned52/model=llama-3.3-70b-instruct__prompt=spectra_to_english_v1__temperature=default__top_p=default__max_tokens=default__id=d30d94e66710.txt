The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the light for car A is red while the light for car B is green, car A will remain idle until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle. When car A's light is green and car A is present, car A will move and then be gone. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A to green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of patterns of response to stimuli. Specifically, when a car is present, the system will respond by turning its corresponding light green, but only after any previous response to the car's presence has been completed. This response pattern ensures that the system reacts appropriately to the presence of cars, while also handling emergency situations correctly.
