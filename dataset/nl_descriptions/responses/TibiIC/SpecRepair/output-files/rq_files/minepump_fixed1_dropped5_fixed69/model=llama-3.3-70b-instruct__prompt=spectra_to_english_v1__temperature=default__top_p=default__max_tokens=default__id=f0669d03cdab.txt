The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the activation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is not activated, then the presence of high water will not persist in the next state, unless the pump remains deactivated.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump is activated in the next state. Secondly, if no methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump is deactivated in the next state. 

These requirements dictate the system's behavior in response to the environment's conditions, specifically the presence or absence of methane, while also considering the environment's assumed behavior regarding high water and pump activation.
