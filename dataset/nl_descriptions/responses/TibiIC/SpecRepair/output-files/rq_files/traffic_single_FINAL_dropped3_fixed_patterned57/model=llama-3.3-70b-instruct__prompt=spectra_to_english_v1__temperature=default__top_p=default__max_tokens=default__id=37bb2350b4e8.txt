The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the green traffic light signal.

Additionally, when the police are present, the car will either be moving or the traffic light will be green, or the car will be idle. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the car will not move.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light turns green when a car is present, and this will happen infinitely often. The system also guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green infinitely often.
