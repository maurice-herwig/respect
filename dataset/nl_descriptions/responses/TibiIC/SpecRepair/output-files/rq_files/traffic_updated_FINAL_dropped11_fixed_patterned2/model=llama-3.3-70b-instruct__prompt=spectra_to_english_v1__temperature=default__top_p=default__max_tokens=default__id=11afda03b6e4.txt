The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will not move until the emergency situation is resolved, the traffic light at intersection A turns green, or the car is no longer present. Similarly, cars at intersection B will not move until the emergency situation is resolved or the traffic light at intersection B turns green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency situation, the system will ensure that both traffic lights are red.

The system's behavior is also constrained by the following rules: when an emergency situation is present, cars at intersection A will move away when the traffic light at intersection A is green, and cars at intersection B will move away when the traffic light at intersection B is green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system will respond to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it will return to its initial state once the car has passed. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars.
