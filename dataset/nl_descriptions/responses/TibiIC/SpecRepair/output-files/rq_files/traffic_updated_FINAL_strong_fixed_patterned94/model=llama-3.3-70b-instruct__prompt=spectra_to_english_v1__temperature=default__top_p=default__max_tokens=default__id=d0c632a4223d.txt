The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle until its green light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain idle until its green light turns green. Furthermore, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding green light is green, the car will move and its presence signal will be reset.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both green lights red. The system also ensures that if a car is present, its green light will be turned green in response to its presence, and this response will happen repeatedly over time.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will repeatedly turn the green light green in response to a car's presence, as long as the car remains present.
