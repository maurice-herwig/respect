The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when the emergency light is on, cars at intersection A will either not be present or will not move to the next state unless the traffic light at intersection A is green or the emergency light is still on at the next state. Similarly, when the emergency light is on, cars at intersection B will either not be present or will not move to the next state unless the traffic light at intersection B is green or the car is not present at the next state. Furthermore, when the emergency light is on and the traffic light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A will either not be present or will move to the next state where they are not present, unless the emergency light is still on or the traffic light at intersection A is not green. The same applies to intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. The same applies to intersection B. Additionally, when an emergency situation is not present, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is responding to the car. The system remains in this state until the traffic light is green, at which point it returns to the initial state. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that the system always responds to the presence of cars.
