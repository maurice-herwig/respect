The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as indicated by the fact that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the emergency light is on, car A will not move if the light is red, and will move if the light is green. Similarly, car B will not move if the light is red, and will move if the light is green. 

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights are designed such that both lights are never red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car A. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light green for car B. When an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is defined by a pattern, where the system will turn the light green for a car if the car is present, and will keep the light green until the car is gone. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
