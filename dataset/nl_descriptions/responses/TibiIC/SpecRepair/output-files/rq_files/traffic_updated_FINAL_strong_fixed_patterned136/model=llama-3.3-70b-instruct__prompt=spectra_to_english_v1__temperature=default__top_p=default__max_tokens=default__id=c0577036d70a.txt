The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency situation infinitely often, and when a car is present and its light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car's light is green and the car is present, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will be red.

The system's response to a car's presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the car's light green when the car is present. This pattern works by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the car's light is currently green or not. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will return to its initial state, ensuring that the car's light is turned green whenever the car is present.
