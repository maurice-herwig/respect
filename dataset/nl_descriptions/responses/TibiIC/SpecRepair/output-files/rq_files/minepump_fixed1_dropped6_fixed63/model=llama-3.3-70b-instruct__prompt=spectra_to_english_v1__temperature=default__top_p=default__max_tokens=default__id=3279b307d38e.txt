The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment provides input on whether there is high water and methane. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that the pump remains off initially and stays off until it is turned on.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if there is no methane, the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the presence of methane and the water level, with specific rules for turning the pump on or off in response to these environmental conditions.
