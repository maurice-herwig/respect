The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, as there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment should ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The environment should also ensure that when the traffic light is green, a car will move, unless the police are present or the car is not present.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is characterized by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, and as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present, it remains in this state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a next state, where it will remain until the traffic light turns green, at which point it returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in this initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car. 

The system and environment variables are: environment-controlled inputs - car presence, emergency situations, and police presence; system-controlled output - traffic light green signal.
