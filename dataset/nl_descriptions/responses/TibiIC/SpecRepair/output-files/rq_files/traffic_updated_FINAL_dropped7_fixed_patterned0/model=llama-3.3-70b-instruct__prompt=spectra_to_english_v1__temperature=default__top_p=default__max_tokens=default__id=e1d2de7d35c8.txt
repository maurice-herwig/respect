The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The system must ensure that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system must eventually turn the traffic light for road A to green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system must eventually turn the traffic light for road B to green.

However, during an emergency, the system must keep the traffic lights red. The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways during an emergency: if a car is not present on road A, it will not appear on road A until the emergency is over or the traffic light for road A turns green. Similarly, if a car is not present on road B, it will not appear on road B until the emergency is over, the traffic light for road B turns green, or the car on road B moves away. Additionally, if a car is present on road A or road B during an emergency, it will move away when the traffic light turns green.

In normal situations, the system responds to the presence of cars on each road by turning the corresponding traffic light to green. This response is governed by a pattern that ensures the system eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light to green, as long as the car remains present. The system repeatedly checks for the presence of cars and updates the traffic lights accordingly.
