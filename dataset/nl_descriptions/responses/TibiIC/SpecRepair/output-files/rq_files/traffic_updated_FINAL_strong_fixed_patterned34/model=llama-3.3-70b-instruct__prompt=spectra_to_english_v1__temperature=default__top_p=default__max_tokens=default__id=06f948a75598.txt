The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it is assumed that car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, car B is assumed to remain present in the next state. If car A is present and its light is green, it is assumed that car A will move and not be present in the next state. Car B is assumed to move and not be present in the next state when it is present, its light is green, and car A's light is also green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. In emergency situations, the system ensures that both car A's and car B's lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: if a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the car's light turns green, the system will transition back to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of cars again. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of cars by turning their lights green.
