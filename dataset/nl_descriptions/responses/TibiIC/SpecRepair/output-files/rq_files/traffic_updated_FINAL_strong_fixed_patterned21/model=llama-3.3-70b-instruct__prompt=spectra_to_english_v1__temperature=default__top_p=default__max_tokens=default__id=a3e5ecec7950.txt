The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle until its green light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain idle until its green light turns green. Furthermore, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding green light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next state.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both green lights red. 

In the normal operation of the traffic lights, the system responds to the presence of car A or car B by eventually turning their corresponding green lights green, but only after any previous response has been completed. This response pattern ensures that the system alternates between waiting for a car to be present and responding to that presence by turning the corresponding green light green.
