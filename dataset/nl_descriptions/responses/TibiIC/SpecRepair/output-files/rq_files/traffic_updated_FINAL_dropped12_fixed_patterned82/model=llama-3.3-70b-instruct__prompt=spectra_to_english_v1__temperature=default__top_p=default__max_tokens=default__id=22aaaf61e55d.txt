The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, and when the lights are red, car A is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency. Additionally, when the lights are red, car B is assumed to be idle unless there is an emergency or the light for car B is about to turn green. The environment is also assumed to have cars A and B move when their respective lights are green.

The system guarantees that when there is no emergency, the lights for cars A and B are not both red. The system also guarantees that when car A is present, the light for car A will eventually turn green. When car B is present, the system guarantees that either the light for car A or car B will eventually turn green. Furthermore, the system guarantees that when there is an emergency, both lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system ensures that when a car is present, the corresponding light will turn green, and when the light is green, the car will move. The system repeatedly responds to the presence of cars, turning the corresponding lights green and then returning to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car.
