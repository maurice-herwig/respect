The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever the water is high, the pump is off. 

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In this system, the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions, and the system-controlled output is the state of the pump.
