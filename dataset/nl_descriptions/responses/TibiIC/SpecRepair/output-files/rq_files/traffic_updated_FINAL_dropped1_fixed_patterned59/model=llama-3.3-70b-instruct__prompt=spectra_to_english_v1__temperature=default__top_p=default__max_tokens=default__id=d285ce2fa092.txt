The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, as there are infinitely many times when no emergency is present. Additionally, when the emergency light is on, cars at intersection A will either move next or the light will turn green, or the emergency light will still be on. Similarly, when the emergency light is on, cars at intersection B will either move next or the light will turn green, or there will be no car at intersection B. Furthermore, when the emergency light is on and the light at intersection A is green, cars at intersection A will either not be present or will move next, or the light will turn red. A similar assumption is made for intersection B.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at intersection A green. A similar guarantee is made for intersection B. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of initial conditions, safety requirements, and liveness requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's safety requirements ensure that it always responds correctly to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The liveness requirements ensure that the system will eventually respond to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green. The system's next-state behavior is determined by its current state and the presence of cars and emergency situations.
