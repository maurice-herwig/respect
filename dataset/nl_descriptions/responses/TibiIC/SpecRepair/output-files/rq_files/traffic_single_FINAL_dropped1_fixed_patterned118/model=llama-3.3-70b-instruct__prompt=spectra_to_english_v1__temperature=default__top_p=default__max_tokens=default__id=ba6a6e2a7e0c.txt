The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The environment controls the inputs: whether a car is present, whether it's an emergency, and whether the police are present. The system controls the output: whether the traffic light is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. The police will not be present indefinitely; there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, if a car is not present, the traffic light will be green. If a car is about to disappear when the light is not green, then either the police are present, the car is still present at the next moment, or the light will turn green.

The system guarantees that if a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green. This guarantee is ensured through a pattern of behavior where the system responds to the presence of a car by turning the light green. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then follows a specific pattern: if a car is present and the light is not green, it moves to a state where it awaits the light turning green; once the light turns green, it returns to the initial state, ready to respond to the car's presence again. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in the state where it is ready to respond to the car, thus ensuring the traffic light turns green when a car is present.
