The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, and the occurrence of emergencies, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that emergencies will eventually stop occurring. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars on road A will not move until the emergency stops, the light turns green, or the car is no longer present. Similarly, cars on road B will not move during an emergency until it stops or the light turns green.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light green for road A. The same applies to road B: when a car is present, the system will eventually turn the light green. During an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

When an emergency occurs, cars on both roads are expected to stop moving if their respective lights are green, or they will stop moving in the next time step. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that if a car is present on a road and the light is not green, the system will move to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will stay in this state until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red again. The system will always eventually return to a state where it can turn the light green in response to a car's presence.
