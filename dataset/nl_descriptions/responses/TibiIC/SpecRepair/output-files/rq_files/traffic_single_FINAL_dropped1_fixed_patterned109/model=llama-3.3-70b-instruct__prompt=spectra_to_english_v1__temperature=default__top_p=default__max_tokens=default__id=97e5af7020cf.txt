The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police intervention. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. It is expected that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, it is assumed that there is no car. The environment is also assumed to behave such that if the police are present, either the car will not be present in the next state or the traffic light will not be green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This is achieved through a pattern of behavior where the system starts in an initial state and transitions between two states based on the presence of the car and the traffic light being green. The system ensures that if a car is present, it will either keep the traffic light green or transition to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will keep it green as long as the car is present. If the car is no longer present, the system may turn the light off. The system will always return to its initial state when the traffic light is green and the car is present, ensuring that the traffic light is green often enough to respond to the presence of cars. 

The initial condition of the system is that it starts in its initial state. The system's safety requirements ensure that it will always transition between states correctly based on the presence of the car and the traffic light being green. The system also has a liveness requirement, ensuring that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, which means the traffic light will be green often enough to respond to cars.
