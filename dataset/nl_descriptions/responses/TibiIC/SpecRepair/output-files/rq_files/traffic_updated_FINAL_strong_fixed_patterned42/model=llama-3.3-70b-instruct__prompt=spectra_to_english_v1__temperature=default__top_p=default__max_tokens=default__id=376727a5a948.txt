The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency situation often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. When car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain idle and not move until its light turns green. When car A's light is green and car A is present, it will move and its presence will be removed. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will never both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. When an emergency situation occurs, both lights will turn red. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state as long as the car is not present or its light is already green. If the car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will respond to the car's presence in a timely manner.
