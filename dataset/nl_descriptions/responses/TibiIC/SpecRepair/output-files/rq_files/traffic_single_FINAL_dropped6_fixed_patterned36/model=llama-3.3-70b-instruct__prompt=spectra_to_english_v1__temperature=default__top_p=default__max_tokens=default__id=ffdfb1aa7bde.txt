The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the police are not present, the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will not move forward when the light is green, or the car will move forward and then stop.

The system guarantees certain behaviors. The traffic light will turn green often when a car is present. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when there are no cars present.

The system's behavior is also constrained by specific rules. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of a car by turning the light green, but only if the car is present and the light is not already green. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
