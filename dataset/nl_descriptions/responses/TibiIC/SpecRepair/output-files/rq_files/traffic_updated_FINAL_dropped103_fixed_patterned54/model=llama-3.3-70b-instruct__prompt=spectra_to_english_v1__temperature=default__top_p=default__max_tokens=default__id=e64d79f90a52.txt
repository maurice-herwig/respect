The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either move or the emergency will end or the traffic light for road A will turn green before the next step. Similarly, for road B, if a car is present during an emergency, it will either move or the emergency will end before the next step. The environment also ensures that cars on both roads will move when their respective traffic lights are green during an emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. The same applies to road B: when a car is present, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. During emergencies, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road, the system will turn the corresponding traffic light green, and once the light is green, it will stay green until the car is gone or the system decides to change the light. This pattern ensures that the system responds appropriately to the presence of cars on both roads.
