The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. However, the specification provided only considers the presence of methane. The system controls a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that when the pump is not operating, it will remain not operating or was not operating in the previous state, as long as the pump is not operating.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity. Secondly, if methane is not present, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity.

Note that the specification does not explicitly mention the initial state of the pump or how the presence of high water affects the system's behavior. The system's operation is solely defined in relation to the presence or absence of methane.
