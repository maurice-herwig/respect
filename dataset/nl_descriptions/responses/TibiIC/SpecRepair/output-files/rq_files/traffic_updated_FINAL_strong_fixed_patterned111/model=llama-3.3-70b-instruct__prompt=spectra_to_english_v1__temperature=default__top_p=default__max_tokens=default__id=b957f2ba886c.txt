The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, it will also remain present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually respond by turning its light green. The same applies to car B. If an emergency occurs, the system will ensure that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars follows a specific pattern: when a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next presence of a car. This pattern ensures that the system always eventually responds to the presence of cars by turning their lights green, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions.
