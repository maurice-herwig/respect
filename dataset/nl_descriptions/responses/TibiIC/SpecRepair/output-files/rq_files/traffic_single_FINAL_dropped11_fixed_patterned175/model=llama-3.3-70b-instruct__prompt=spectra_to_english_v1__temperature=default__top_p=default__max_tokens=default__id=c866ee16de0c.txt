The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Firstly, it is assumed that the police presence will not be ongoing indefinitely; there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that if a car is present, it will either be an emergency situation, the traffic light will be green, or the car will not be present at the next time step. Furthermore, if a car was present previously, the environment will ensure that either the police are present, the car is no longer present, or the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light will be green at some point, as long as the environment behaves according to the assumptions.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will periodically return to its initial state, indicating that it has responded to the car's presence.

The system's behavior is designed to ensure that it responds to the presence of a car in a timely manner, while also taking into account the environment's assumptions about police presence, emergency situations, and car movement.
