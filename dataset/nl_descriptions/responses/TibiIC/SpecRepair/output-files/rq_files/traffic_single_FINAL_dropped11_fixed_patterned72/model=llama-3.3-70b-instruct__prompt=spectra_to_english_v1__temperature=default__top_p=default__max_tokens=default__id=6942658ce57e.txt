The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions frequently, and when a car is present, it should either be an emergency, the traffic light should be green, or the car should not be moving towards the traffic light. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the environment should ensure that if the traffic light was previously not green, then either there is a police intervention, no car is present, or the traffic light remains not green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state. In this state, if a car is present, the system will turn the traffic light green. If the system detects a car but the traffic light is not green, it transitions to a next state where it awaits the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often be in its initial state, indicating that it periodically responds to the car's presence by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee to periodically turn the traffic light green when a car is present ensures a safe and responsive interaction between the environment and the system.
