The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, it will not activate the car presence signal on road A unless the traffic light on road A is green or the emergency signal remains active. Similarly, for road B, the environment will not activate the car presence signal unless the traffic light on road B is green, the emergency signal remains active, or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, the environment will ensure that if there is a car on road A, it will either move or the traffic light on road A will turn red, and similarly for road B, the car will move or the traffic light will turn red.

The system guarantees that whenever there is a car on road A, it will eventually turn the traffic light on road A green, and similarly for road B. The system achieves this by maintaining an internal state that tracks whether the traffic light should be green in response to the car presence signal. Initially, the system is in a state where it does not require the traffic light to be green. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is already green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it requires the traffic light to be green. Once the traffic light is green, the system returns to its initial state. The system ensures that it periodically returns to its initial state, allowing it to respond to changing car presence signals.
