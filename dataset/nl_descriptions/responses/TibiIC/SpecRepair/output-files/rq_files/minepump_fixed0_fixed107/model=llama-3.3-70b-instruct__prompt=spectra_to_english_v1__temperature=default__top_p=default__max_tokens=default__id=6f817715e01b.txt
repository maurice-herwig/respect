The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment's behavior is restricted by two assumptions: 
- If the pump was previously on and is currently on, then the water level will be low in the next state.
- If the pump is off, then either the water level is low or there is no methane present.

The system must satisfy two guarantees: 
- If the water level is high, then the pump must be on, or it must turn on in the next state.
- If methane is present, then the pump must turn off in the next state.

These requirements ensure the system's behavior in response to the environment's conditions, specifically regarding the pump's operation in relation to water levels and methane presence.
