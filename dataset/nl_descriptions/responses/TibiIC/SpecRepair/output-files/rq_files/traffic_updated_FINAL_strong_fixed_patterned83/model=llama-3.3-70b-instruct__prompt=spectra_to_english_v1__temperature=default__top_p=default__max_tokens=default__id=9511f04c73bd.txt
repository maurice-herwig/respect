The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that the emergency signal is not constantly active, and it is expected to be inactive infinitely often. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next time step. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next time step. If car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next time step. Car B will move and not be present in the next time step if it is present, its light is green, and car A's light is also green.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency signal is active, the system will turn off both green lights.

The system's behavior can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars A and B. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it will turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system will remain in this state until the car moves. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to the presence of cars A and B.
