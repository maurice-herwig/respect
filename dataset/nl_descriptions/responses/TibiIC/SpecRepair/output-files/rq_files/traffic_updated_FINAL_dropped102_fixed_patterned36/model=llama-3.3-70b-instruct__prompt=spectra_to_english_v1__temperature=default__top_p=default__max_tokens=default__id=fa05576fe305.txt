The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state will have a car on road A (cara) or the emergency will persist, or the light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state will have a car on road B (carb) or the emergency will persist, or the light for road B will be green, or there will be no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and there is a car on road A, the car will move (cara will become false) unless the light for road A is not green. The same assumption applies to road B: when an emergency occurs and there is a car on road B, the car will move (carb will become false) unless the emergency persists, or there is no car on road B, or the light for road B is not green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the light for road B will not be green. The system also guarantees that if there is a car on road A and the light for road B is not green, then the light for road A will be green. Similarly, if there is a car on road B and the light for road B is green, then the system will ensure that the light for road B remains green. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both lights will not be red at the same time.

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars is as follows: if a car is present on a road and the corresponding light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. The system will keep the light green as long as the car is present, and it will turn the light red when the car is no longer present. This response pattern applies to both roads A and B.
