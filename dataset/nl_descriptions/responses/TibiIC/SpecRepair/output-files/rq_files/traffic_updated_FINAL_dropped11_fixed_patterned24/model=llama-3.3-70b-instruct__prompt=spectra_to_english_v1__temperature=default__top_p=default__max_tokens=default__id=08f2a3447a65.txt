The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment is assumed to not have a car on road A or B unless the corresponding light is green or the emergency signal remains true in the next state. The environment is also assumed to have cars move when the corresponding light is green and the emergency signal is true.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time. When a car is present on road A or B, the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding light green, and it will continue to do so as long as the car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green, and it will stay in this state until the light turns green. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars.
