The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be periods when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red and there is no emergency, if a car is present, it will remain present until the traffic light turns green. On the other hand, when the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and there will be periods when no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and responds accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is green, the system remains in its current state. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars.
