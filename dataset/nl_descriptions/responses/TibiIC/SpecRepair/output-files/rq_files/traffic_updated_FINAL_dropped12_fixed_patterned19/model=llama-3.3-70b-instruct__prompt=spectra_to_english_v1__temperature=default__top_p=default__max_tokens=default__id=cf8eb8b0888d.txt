The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights at an intersection with two roads, A and B. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that the emergency signal is usually false. Additionally, when the emergency signal is false, the environment ensures that if a car is present on road A, it will not become present when the light is red, unless an emergency occurs. The environment also ensures that if a car is present on road B, it will not become present when the light is red, unless an emergency occurs or the light for road B is green. Furthermore, the environment guarantees that cars on both roads will move when their respective lights are green.

The system guarantees that the lights for roads A and B are not both red when there is no emergency. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. When a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn either the light for road A or road B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as there are no cars present or the corresponding light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the corresponding light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will infinitely often be in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars.
