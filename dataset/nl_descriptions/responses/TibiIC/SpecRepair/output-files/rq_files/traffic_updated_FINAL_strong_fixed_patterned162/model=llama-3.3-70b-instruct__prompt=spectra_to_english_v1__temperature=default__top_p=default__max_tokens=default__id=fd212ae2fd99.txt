The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency conditions often, meaning that it is eventually always the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present and the corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle if the other light was previously red. When a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. If a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning its corresponding light green. When an emergency condition is present, the system will ensure that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: a state where it can respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green, or a state where it is waiting for the car's light to turn green. The system will transition between these states based on the presence of cars and the state of their corresponding lights. The system will always eventually return to the state where it can respond to the presence of a car.
