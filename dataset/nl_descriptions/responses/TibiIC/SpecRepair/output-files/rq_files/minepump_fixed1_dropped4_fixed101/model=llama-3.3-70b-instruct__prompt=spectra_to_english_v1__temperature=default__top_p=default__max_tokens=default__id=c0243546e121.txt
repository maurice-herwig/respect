The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment determines the levels of water and methane, which are the inputs to the system. The system's output is the state of the pump, which can be either on or off.

It is assumed that when the water level is high, the pump will be off, and when methane is present, the pump will also be off. 

The system guarantees that if methane is detected, then either the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity or the water level is not high. Additionally, the system ensures that methane is never present.

In summary, the system must operate under the assumption that high water and methane presence have specific implications on the pump's state, and it must guarantee certain conditions regarding methane detection and pump operation, while ensuring the absence of methane.
