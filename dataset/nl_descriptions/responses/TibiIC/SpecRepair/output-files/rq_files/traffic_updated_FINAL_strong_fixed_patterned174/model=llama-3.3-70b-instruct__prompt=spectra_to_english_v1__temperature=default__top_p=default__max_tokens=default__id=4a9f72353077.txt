The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present until its light turns green. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, and car A's light was previously red, car B will remain present until its light turns green. On the other hand, when car A or car B is present and its corresponding light is green, it will move and no longer be present at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: ready to respond or waiting for a car's light to turn green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. If a car's light is green, the system will move back to the ready state. The system will infinitely often be in the ready state.
