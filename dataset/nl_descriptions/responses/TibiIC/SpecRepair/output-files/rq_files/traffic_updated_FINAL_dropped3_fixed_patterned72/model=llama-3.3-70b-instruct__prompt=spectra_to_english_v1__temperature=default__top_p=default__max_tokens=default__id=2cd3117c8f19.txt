The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as the emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; similarly, when the emergency signal is active, if the light for road B is red, then the emergency signal must remain active until the light for road B turns green. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light will turn red at the next step; likewise, when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. The system responds to the presence of cars on each road according to a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the car's presence; if a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it must turn the light green; once the light is green, the system returns to its initial state, where it can respond to the car's presence again. This pattern ensures that the system periodically returns to a state where it can respond to the presence of cars on each road.
