The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergencies (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain on the road or move forward at the next time step, unless the traffic light for road A is green. Similar assumptions are made for road B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for a road is red, the car on that road will either remain idle or move forward at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that if the emergency is not over, both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the traffic light will be turned green eventually after a car is detected. The system also ensures that once the traffic light is turned green, it will remain green until the car is no longer present, at which point it may turn red again.
