The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red and the police are present, if a car is at the intersection, it will remain there until the light changes. On the other hand, when the traffic light is green and a car is at the intersection, the car will move and no longer be at the intersection.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present at the intersection, although this may not happen immediately. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will always be times when no car is present at the intersection.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and responds accordingly. If a car is present and the light is green, the system remains in its current state. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. If the light turns green while the system is waiting, it immediately returns to its initial state. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light that responds to the presence of cars, while considering the presence of police and emergency situations, and ensures that cars move through the intersection in a safe and orderly manner.
