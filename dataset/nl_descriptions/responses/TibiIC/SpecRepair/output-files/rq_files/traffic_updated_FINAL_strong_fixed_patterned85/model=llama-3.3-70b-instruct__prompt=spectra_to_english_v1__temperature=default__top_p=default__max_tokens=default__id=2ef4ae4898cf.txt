The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain present until its light turns green. Conversely, when a car's light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed at the next time step. However, car B will only move when its light is green and car A is also present.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time. When a car is present, its corresponding traffic light will eventually turn green. Specifically, if car A is present, the system will ensure that the greenA light will turn green, and similarly for car B and the greenB light. In emergency situations, both traffic lights will turn red. 

The system's behavior is also governed by initial conditions, where initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to car presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to a car's presence by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states, ensuring that the system will always eventually respond to a car's presence.
