The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency conditions. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally expected that there will be no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present and the corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Furthermore, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and its presence will no longer be detected.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. When an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of a car is to eventually turn the corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light will be turned green whenever the car is present, and the system will return to its initial state after responding. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it can continue to respond to the presence of cars.
