The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that it is always the case that eventually, there will be a point in time where an emergency situation is not present. Additionally, when the system is in a state where the traffic light at intersection A is red, the environment will ensure that if an emergency situation is present, then either the next state will have a car present at intersection A, or an emergency situation will still be present, or the traffic light at intersection A will be green. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B.

The environment also ensures that when an emergency situation is present and the traffic light at an intersection is green, the environment will ensure that either the emergency situation is still present, or there is no car present at the intersection, or the next state will have no car present at the intersection, or the traffic light at the intersection will be red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually respond by turning the traffic light at intersection B green.

In emergency situations, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will be red. The system also follows a specific pattern of response to the presence of cars at intersections, where it will initially be in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car, and will transition to a state where it is responding to the presence of a car when the car is present and the traffic light is not green, and will transition back to the initial state when the traffic light is green. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of a car at an intersection.
