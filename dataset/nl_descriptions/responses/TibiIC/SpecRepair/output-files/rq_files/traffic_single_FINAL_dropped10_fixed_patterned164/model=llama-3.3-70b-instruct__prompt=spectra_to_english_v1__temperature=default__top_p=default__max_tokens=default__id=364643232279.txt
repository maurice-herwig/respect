The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, and when the traffic light is red, the car should not start moving unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, when the police are present, the car should not move, or if it is moving, it should stop at the next opportunity.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and there will be periods where no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it periodically returns to this initial state, allowing it to consistently respond to cars.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
