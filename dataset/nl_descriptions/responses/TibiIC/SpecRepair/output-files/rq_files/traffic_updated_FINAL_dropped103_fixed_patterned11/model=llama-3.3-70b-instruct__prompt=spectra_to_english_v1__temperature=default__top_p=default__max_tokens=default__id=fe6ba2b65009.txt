The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an `emergency` signal. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greena` and `greenb` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that the emergency signal will be false most of the time. Additionally, when the emergency signal is true, the environment will not have a car on road A waiting to proceed if the light is red, unless the emergency condition persists or the light turns green. Similar assumptions are made for road B. The environment is also assumed to have cars move on roads A and B when their respective lights are green during an emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B will not both be red at the same time. When there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. During an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars on roads A and B follows a pattern where it will turn the corresponding light green when a car is present, and it will keep the light green as long as the car is present or until it is safe to turn the light red again. This pattern ensures that the system will always eventually respond to the presence of cars on both roads.
