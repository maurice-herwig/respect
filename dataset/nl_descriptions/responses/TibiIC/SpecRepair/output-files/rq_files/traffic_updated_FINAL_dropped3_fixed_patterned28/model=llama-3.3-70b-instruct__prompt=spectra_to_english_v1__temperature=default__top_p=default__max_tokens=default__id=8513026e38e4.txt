The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is present, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; when an emergency is present, the traffic light for road B should be green if there is no emergency or if the traffic light for road B is already green; when an emergency is present or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when an emergency is present or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. The system responds to the presence of cars on each road by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the traffic light is turned green whenever a car is present, with the response being repeated infinitely often. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is governed by a set of safety rules that ensure the correct sequence of states, including the initial state, the state where the traffic light is turned green, and the state where the traffic light remains green or turns red. The system will infinitely often return to the initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
