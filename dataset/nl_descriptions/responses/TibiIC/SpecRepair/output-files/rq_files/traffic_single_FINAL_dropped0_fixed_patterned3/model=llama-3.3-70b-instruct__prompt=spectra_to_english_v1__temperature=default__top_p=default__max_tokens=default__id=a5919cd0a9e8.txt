The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, the environment is assumed to ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The environment is also expected to ensure that when the police are not present, a car will either not be moving or will stop moving when the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees two main behaviors: the traffic light will turn green often when a car is present, and there will be times when no car is present. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and responds accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will remain so as long as the car is present, after which it may turn red again. The system will repeatedly turn the traffic light green in response to the presence of a car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
