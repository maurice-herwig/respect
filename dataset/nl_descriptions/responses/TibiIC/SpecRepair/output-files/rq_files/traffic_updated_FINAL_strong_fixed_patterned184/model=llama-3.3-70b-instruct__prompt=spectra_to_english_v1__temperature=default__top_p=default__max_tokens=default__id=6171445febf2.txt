The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations, while the system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain idle in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is governed by a pattern that ensures the system responds to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding green light on. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it remains in this state as long as the car is not present or its green light is already on. If the car is present and its green light is off, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars A and B.
