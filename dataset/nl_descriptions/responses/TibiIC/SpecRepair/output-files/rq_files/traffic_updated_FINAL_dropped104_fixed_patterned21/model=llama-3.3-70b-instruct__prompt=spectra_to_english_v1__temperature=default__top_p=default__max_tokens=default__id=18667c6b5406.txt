The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as an emergency signal. The system, in turn, controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of car A, the presence of car B, and the emergency signal. The system-controlled outputs are the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, car A will not be present in the next state unless the green light for car A is active, the emergency signal remains active, or car A is already present. Similarly, when the emergency signal is active, car B will not be present in the next state unless the green light for car B is active, the emergency signal remains active, car B is already present, or car B is not present. Additionally, when the emergency signal is active, car A will move (i.e., not be present in the next state) unless the emergency signal remains active, car A is not present, the green light for car A is not active, or car A will not be present in the next state. Car B is also expected to move when the emergency signal is active, unless car B is not present, the green light for car B is not active, or car B will not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees that when car A is present, the green light for car A will be active, and when car B is present, the green light for car B will be active. This guarantee is met by the system responding to the presence of cars A and B in a specific pattern. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then enters a cycle where it responds to the presence of cars by activating the corresponding green lights. If a car is present and its green light is not active, the system transitions to a state where it will activate the green light in the next state. If the green light is active when a car is present, the system remains in the ready state. The system will infinitely often return to the ready state, ensuring that it can respond to the presence of cars.
