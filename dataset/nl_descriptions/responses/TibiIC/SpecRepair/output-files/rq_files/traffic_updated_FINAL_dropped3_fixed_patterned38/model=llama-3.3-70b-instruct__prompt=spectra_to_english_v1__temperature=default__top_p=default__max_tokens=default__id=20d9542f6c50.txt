The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, as well as the occurrence of an emergency, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the traffic light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when there is an emergency, the traffic light at intersection B is either green or there is still an emergency; when there is an emergency or no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will move or the traffic light at intersection A will turn red at the next time step; and when there is an emergency or no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green; and when there is a car at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that the system eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
