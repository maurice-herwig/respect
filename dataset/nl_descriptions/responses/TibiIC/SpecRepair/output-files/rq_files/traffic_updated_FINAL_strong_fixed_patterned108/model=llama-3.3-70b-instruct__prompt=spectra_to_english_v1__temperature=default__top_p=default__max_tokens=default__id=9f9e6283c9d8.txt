The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergency situations frequently, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car's light is green and the car is present, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. During emergency situations, both lights will be red.

The system responds to the presence of cars A and B according to a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it remembers that the car is waiting. The system will remain in this state until the car's light is turned green, at which point it will transition back to the initial state. This pattern ensures that the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its light green. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that it can continue to respond to the presence of cars.
