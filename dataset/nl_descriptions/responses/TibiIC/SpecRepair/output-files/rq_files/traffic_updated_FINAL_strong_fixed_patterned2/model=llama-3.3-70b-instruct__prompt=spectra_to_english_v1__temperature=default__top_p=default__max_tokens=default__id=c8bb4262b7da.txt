The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the emergency signal is not constantly active, meaning there are infinitely many times when the emergency signal is false. When car A is present and the light for car A is red while the light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the light for car B is red while the light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B: when car B is present and its light is green, car B will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car B green. If the emergency signal is active, both lights for car A and car B will be red.

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning its corresponding light green. This pattern starts in an initial state where the system is not waiting for a response, and it transitions between states based on the presence of the car and the state of its light. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that it responds to the presence of cars A and B in a timely manner.
