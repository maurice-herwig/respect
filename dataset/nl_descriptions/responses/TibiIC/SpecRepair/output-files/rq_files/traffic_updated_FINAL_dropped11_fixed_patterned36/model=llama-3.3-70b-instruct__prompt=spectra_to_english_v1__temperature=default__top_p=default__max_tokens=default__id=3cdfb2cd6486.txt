The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, represented by `cara` and `carb`, as well as emergency situations, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights at intersections A and B, represented by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cars at intersection A are assumed to either remain idle or become present at the next time step if the light is red or the emergency persists, and cars at intersection B are assumed to either remain idle or become present at the next time step if the emergency persists. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the light is green, cars at both intersections A and B are assumed to move, meaning they will either not be present at the next time step or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green, and similarly, when a car is present at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system then continuously monitors the presence of cars and emergencies, updating the traffic lights accordingly to ensure safe and responsive traffic control. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that if a car is present, the corresponding light will eventually turn green, and if the light is green, the car will eventually move, allowing for continuous traffic flow.
