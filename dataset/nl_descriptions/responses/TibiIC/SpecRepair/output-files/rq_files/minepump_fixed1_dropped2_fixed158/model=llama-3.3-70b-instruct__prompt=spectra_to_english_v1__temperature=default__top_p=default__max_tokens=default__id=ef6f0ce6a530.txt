The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

It is assumed that if the water was high in the previous state, then the pump must have been off in the previous state, or if the pump was off in the previous state, it remains consistent with the environment's behavior.

The system guarantees two conditions. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump will be turned on in the next state or there is no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump will be turned off in the next state. 

These guarantees ensure the system responds appropriately to the environmental conditions of methane presence and water level, while the assumption constrains the environment's past behavior regarding water level and pump state.
