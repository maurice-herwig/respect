The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present, its light is red, and there is no emergency, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. Conversely, when car A or car B is present and its respective light is green, the environment will ensure that the car is not present in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. Furthermore, whenever there is an emergency, the system will ensure that both lights are red.

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of its response to the presence of cars A and B. The system will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding light green, but only if the other car's light is not already green. This response is modeled as a pattern of behavior where the system initially waits for a car to be present, then responds by turning the light green, and eventually returns to waiting for the car to be present again. This pattern of waiting and responding ensures that the system always eventually responds to the presence of a car by turning its light green.
