The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersections A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green at intersections A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that emergency situations will eventually become false. Additionally, when the emergency flag is true, the environment is assumed to behave in specific ways: if the light is red at intersection A, the next state will either have a car present at A, the emergency flag will still be true, the light at A will be green, or there will be no car at A. Similar assumptions are made for intersection B. Furthermore, when the emergency flag is true, the environment is assumed to have cars move when the light is green at both intersections A and B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time. If there is a car present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light at A green. A similar guarantee is made for intersection B. When an emergency situation occurs, the system will ensure that both lights are red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the light green when a car is present. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states, one where it is ready to respond to a car and one where it is waiting for the car to be served. The system will always eventually return to the ready state.
