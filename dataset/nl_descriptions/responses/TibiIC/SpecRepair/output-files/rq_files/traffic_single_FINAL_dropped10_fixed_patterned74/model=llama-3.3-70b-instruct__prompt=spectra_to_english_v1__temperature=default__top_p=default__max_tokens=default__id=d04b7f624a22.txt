The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car will either not be present, be idle, or an emergency situation will occur, until the traffic light turns green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when the police are present, or the car is not present, the car will either not move or will move to a stopped state.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as a car is not present or is present and the traffic light is green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system will repeatedly return to this initial state, ensuring that the traffic light turns green when a car is present.
