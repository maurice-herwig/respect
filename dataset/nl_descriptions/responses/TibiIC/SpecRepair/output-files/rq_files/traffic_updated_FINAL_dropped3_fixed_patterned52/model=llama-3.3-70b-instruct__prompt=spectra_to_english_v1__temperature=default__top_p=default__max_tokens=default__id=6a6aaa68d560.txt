The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and emergency conditions (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave as follows: when there is an emergency, if the light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A. Additionally, when there is an emergency, the environment will either maintain the emergency state or have the light for road B be green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when there is an emergency or no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the light for road A will be red at the next time step. Similarly, when there is an emergency or no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on each road can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car's presence. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely often, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars on each road.
