The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the inputs: `cara` (car A is present), `carb` (car B is present), and `emergency` (an emergency situation is occurring). The system controls the outputs: `greena` (the traffic light for car A is green) and `greenb` (the traffic light for car B is green).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency situation occurs, car A will not be present at the next time step if it is not already present, and car B will either not be present at the next time step, or it will be present and the traffic light for car B will be green, or car B will not be present. Additionally, when an emergency situation occurs, if car A is present, it will not be present at the next time step if the traffic light for car A is not green. Similarly, if car B is present when an emergency situation occurs, it will not be present at the next time step if the traffic light for car B is not green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the traffic light for car B will not be green. If car A is present and the traffic light for car B is not green, the traffic light for car A will be green. If car B is present and the traffic light for car B is green, the system will ensure that this condition is met. When there is no emergency, both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

The system also ensures that the presence of cars and the state of the traffic lights are related in the following way: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of cars. If a car is present and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to a state where it is responding to the presence of the car. If the corresponding traffic light becomes green while the system is in this responding state, the system will move back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state.
