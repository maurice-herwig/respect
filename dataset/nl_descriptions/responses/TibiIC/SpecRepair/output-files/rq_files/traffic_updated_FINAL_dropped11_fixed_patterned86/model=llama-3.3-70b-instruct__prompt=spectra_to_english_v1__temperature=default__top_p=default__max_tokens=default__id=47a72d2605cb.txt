The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and emergency situations, denoted by emergency. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for cars A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that car A is idle when the light is red, unless the emergency persists or the light turns green. Similarly, the environment ensures that car B is idle when the light is red, unless the emergency persists or the light turns green. The environment also ensures that car A moves when the light is green, unless an emergency occurs or the light turns red. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system responds by turning the light green for car A. Similarly, when car B is present, the system responds by turning the light green for car B. The system also guarantees that when an emergency occurs, both lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that it will eventually turn the corresponding light green when a car is present, and it will not turn the light green when no car is present. The system will repeatedly return to this initial state, ensuring that it can continue to respond to the presence of cars. 

In the event of an emergency, the system will turn both lights red. The system's behavior is designed to ensure safe and responsive traffic control, taking into account the presence of cars and emergency situations.
