The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, with the environment providing inputs and the system responding with outputs. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green and a car is present, the car will remain present until the traffic light turns green. When the traffic light is green, a car is present, and there are no police, the car will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of cars.
