The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, an emergency, and police. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: the police will not be present indefinitely, and whenever the police arrive, the car will either already be present or will arrive in the next moment, unless the car is already absent. Additionally, the car will move when the traffic light is green, unless the police are present or the car is already not moving, in which case it may remain still even when the light turns green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern where the system starts in an initial state, waiting for a car. If a car is present and the light is green, or if no car is present, the system remains in this waiting state. If a car is present but the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to the waiting state. The system will infinitely often be in this waiting state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car by turning the traffic light green. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, an emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. The system's guarantee of periodically turning the traffic light green in response to a car's presence ensures a safe and responsive interaction between the environment and the system.
