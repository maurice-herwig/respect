The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions. The system-controlled output is the state of the pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the water is not high and the pump was previously off, then if the water becomes not high, it will remain not high.

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next step, or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system's behavior is constrained by the environment's conditions, and it must respond accordingly to ensure safe operation. The pump's state is determined based on the presence of methane and high water, with specific rules governing its next state.
