The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the levels of water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever methane is present, the pump was not operating in the previous state. 

The system must satisfy two guarantees. Firstly, if methane is present, then either the pump must be turned on at the next opportunity, or there must be no high water. Secondly, if there is no methane, then the pump must be turned off at the next opportunity. 

In summary, the system's pump operation is dependent on the environment's methane and water levels, with specific rules governing when the pump should be turned on or off based on these conditions. The environment's behavior is restricted such that the presence of methane implies the pump was previously off, and the system must respond accordingly to ensure safe operation.
