The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B. When an emergency occurs, both lights will be red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system responds to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light is turned green only when the car is present, and the system returns to its initial state once the light has been turned green. The system always eventually returns to this initial state.
