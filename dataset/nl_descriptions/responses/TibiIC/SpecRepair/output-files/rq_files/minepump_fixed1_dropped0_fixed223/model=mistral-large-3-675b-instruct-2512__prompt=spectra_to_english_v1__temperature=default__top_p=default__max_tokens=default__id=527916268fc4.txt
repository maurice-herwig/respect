### **Minepump System Requirements**

This system controls a pump in response to environmental conditions, specifically the presence of high water and methane gas.

**Inputs (controlled by the environment):**
- `highwater`: A signal indicating whether the water level is dangerously high.
- `methane`: A signal indicating whether methane gas is detected.

**Output (controlled by the system):**
- `pump`: A signal determining whether the pump is active.

**Assumptions on the environment:**
- Whenever the water level is high (`highwater = true`), the environment ensures that either the pump will be turned off in the next state (`next(pump = false)`) or the water level will drop (`next(highwater = false)`).

**System guarantees:**
1. **Safety guarantee (water and methane handling):**
   - At all times, if methane is detected (`methane = true`), then in the next state, either the pump must be on (`next(pump = true)`) or the water level must no longer be high (`highwater = false`).

2. **Safety guarantee (methane response):**
   - At all times, if methane is not detected (`methane = false`), then in the next state, the pump must be off (`next(pump = false)`).
