The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that whenever methane is present, the pump is initially off.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump will be turned on at the next step. Secondly, if no methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump will be turned off at the next step.

In summary, the system's pump operation is directly controlled based on the presence or absence of methane in the environment, with the goal of turning the pump on when methane is detected and turning it off when methane is not present.
