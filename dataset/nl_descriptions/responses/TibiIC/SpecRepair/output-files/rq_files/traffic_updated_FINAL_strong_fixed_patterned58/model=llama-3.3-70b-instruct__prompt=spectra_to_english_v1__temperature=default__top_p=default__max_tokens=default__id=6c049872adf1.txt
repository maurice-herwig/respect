The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted as greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have emergencies often, and when a car is stopped at a red light, it will remain stopped until the light turns green. Specifically, if car A is present and its light is red while car B's light is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, if car B is present and its light is red while car A's light is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car's light turns green, the car will move and no longer be present in the next state.

The system guarantees certain behavior: it will never have both lights red at the same time, and when a car is present, its corresponding light will eventually turn green. If an emergency occurs, both lights will turn red. The system also ensures that when a car is present, its light will turn green in response to its presence, and this response will happen repeatedly over time.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to a car's presence is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the car's light green when the car is present, and will return to a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence again. This pattern is repeated infinitely often, ensuring that the system continually responds to the presence of cars.
