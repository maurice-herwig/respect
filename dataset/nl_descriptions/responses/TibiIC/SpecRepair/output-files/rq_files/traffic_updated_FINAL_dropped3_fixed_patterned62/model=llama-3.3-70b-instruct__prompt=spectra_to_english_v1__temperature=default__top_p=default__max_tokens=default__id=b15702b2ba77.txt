The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the state of the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when there is an emergency, if the light at intersection A is red, then there is no car at intersection A; when there is an emergency, the light at intersection B is either green or there is still an emergency; when there is an emergency or no car at intersection A, the car at intersection A will either move or the light will turn red at the next step; and when there is an emergency or no car at intersection B, the car at intersection B will move at the next step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car at intersection A, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection A green; and when there is a car at intersection B, the system will eventually turn the light at intersection B green.

More specifically, the system responds to the presence of cars by transitioning through a series of states. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to its initial state, ensuring that it continues to respond to the presence of cars.
