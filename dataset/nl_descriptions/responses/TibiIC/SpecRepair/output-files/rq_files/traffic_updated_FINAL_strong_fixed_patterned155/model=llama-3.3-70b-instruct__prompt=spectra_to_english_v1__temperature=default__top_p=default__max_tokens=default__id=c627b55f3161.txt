The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by `carA` and `carB`, and the occurrence of emergencies, represented by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights, with `greenA` and `greenB` indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when a car is present on road A and the light is red, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the light is red, and there is no emergency, the car will remain present until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present on either road and the corresponding light is green, the car will move and no longer be present at the next time step.

The system guarantees that the lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to cars is governed by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually turn the corresponding light green when a car is present, and will return to a state of readiness after doing so. This pattern also ensures that the system will not turn the light green when there is no car present, unless it is in the process of responding to a previous car. The system will always eventually return to the ready state.
