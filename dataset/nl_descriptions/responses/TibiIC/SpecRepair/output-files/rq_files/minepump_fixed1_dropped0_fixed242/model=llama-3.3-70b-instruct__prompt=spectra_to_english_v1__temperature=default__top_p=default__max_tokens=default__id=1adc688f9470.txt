The Minepump system operates in an environment where water levels and methane presence are monitored. The environment controls the detection of high water levels and methane presence, while the system controls the activation of a pump. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if methane becomes present, and the pump was not previously active, then the water level will not be high when the methane appears.

The system must satisfy two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, the pump must be activated at the next opportunity, unless the water level is not high. Secondly, if methane is not detected, the pump must not be active at the next opportunity.

In summary, the system's pump is controlled based on the environment's methane detection and water level, with specific rules governing when the pump should be activated or deactivated in response to these environmental conditions.
