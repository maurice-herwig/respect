The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have police presence indefinitely, and there will be periods where police are not present. Additionally, the environment will ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. If there is an emergency, the environment will either have police presence or ensure the car does not move when the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is governed by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of its response to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically resets and is ready to respond to the presence of a car. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
