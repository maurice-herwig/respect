The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as an emergency indicator. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency is indicated, if the light for road A is red, then there should be no car on road A; similarly, when an emergency is indicated, if the light for road B is red, then an emergency should still be indicated. Additionally, when an emergency is not indicated or there is no car on road A, or the car on road A will move next, or the light for road A is red, then the car on road A should move; a similar assumption applies to road B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A green; similarly, when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the light for road B green.

The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system moves to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state, where it can respond to the presence of another car. This pattern ensures that the system periodically responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
