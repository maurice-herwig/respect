The TrafficE2 system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is assumed to occasionally not have police present and to not have a car moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency. Additionally, the environment is assumed to either have police present, not have a car, have the car move, or not have the green light when a car is present.

The system guarantees that it will often have the green light on when a car is present, as long as the environment satisfies the assumptions. The system also guarantees that there will often be no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system's response to the car's presence is as follows: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it remembers that a car was present without the green light. Once the green light is on, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system remains in this initial state as long as there is no car or the green light is on when a car is present. The system will infinitely often return to this initial state. 

The traffic light will be updated based on the system's state and the presence of the car, ensuring that the green light is on when a car is present, as required by the system's guarantees.
