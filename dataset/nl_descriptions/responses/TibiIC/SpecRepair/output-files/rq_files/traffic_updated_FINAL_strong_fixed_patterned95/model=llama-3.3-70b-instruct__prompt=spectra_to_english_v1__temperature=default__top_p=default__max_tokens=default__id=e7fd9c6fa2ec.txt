The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have frequent emergency situations, meaning that it is generally the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain present in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is green, car A will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B. In emergency situations, the system will turn off both green lights. 

The system's response to the presence of cars A and B can be described as follows: initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the presence of a car. If a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning on the corresponding green light. If the system is not responding to a car's presence and the car is present, it will move to a state where it is waiting for the car's presence to be acknowledged. Once the system acknowledges the car's presence by turning on the green light, it will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.
