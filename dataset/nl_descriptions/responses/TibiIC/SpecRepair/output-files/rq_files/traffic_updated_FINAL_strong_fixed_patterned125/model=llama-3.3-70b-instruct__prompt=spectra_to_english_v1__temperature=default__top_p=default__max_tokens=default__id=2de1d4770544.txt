The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, it will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, it will also remain present in the next state. However, when car A is present and its light is green, it will move and not be present in the next state. Car B moves and is not present in the next state when it is present, its light is green, and car A is also present.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. The same applies to car B: when it is present, the system will eventually turn its light green. If an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system remains in this state as long as the conditions for turning the lights green are met. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is turned green, the system returns to its initial state. The system always eventually returns to this initial state, ensuring that it can continuously respond to the presence of cars.
