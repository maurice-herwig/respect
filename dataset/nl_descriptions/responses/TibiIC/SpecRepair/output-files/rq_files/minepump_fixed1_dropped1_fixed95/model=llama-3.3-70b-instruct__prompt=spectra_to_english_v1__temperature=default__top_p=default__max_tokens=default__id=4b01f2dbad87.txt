The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the detection of high water and methane, while the system controls the operation of a pump.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump is currently on, then the water level will not be high in the next state, unless the pump was previously off.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if methane is detected, then the pump must be turned on in the next state. Secondly, if no methane is detected, then the pump must be turned off in the next state.

In summary, the system's pump operation is dependent on the detection of methane, and the environment's behavior regarding the water level is constrained when the pump is on.
