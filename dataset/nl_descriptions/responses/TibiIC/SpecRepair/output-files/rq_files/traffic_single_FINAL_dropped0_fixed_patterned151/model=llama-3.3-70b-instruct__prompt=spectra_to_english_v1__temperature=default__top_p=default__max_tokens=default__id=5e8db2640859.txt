The TrafficE2 system controls a traffic light, interacting with an environment that includes cars, emergency situations, and police presence. The environment controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police, while the system controls the traffic light's green status.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have police presence indefinitely, and when a car is present, it will either be an emergency situation, the traffic light will be green, or the car will not be present in the next moment. Additionally, when the traffic light is green, the car will move, unless there is an emergency situation or the traffic light turns red. If there is no emergency situation, the car will not move when the traffic light is not green.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, and that there will be periods where no car is present. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to a car's presence. The system's behavior is constrained by the following rules: if a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green; if the traffic light is green when a car is present, the system will remain in its current state; if the traffic light turns green while the system is waiting, it will transition back to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.
