The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (cara) and intersection B (carb), as well as the occurrence of emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (greena) and intersection B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, emergency situations do not occur frequently, meaning that there are infinitely many times when an emergency is not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is red at intersection A, car A will not be present or will become present at the next time step, unless an emergency is occurring or the light is green. Similarly, when the traffic light is red at intersection B, car B will not be present or will become present at the next time step, unless the light is green or car B is not present. Furthermore, when an emergency is occurring and the light is green at intersection A, car A will either not be present, become absent at the next time step, or the light will turn red. The same applies to intersection B: when an emergency is occurring and the light is green, car B will either not be present, become absent at the next time step, or the light will turn red.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic lights at intersections A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present at intersection A, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green at intersection A. The same applies to intersection B: when a car is present, the system will eventually respond by turning the light green. Additionally, when an emergency is occurring, both traffic lights will be red.

The system's behavior is also governed by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green, and it does so in a way that ensures the light is turned green whenever a car is present. The system remains in this state until the car is no longer present or the light is turned red, at which point it returns to its initial state. This process repeats infinitely, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
