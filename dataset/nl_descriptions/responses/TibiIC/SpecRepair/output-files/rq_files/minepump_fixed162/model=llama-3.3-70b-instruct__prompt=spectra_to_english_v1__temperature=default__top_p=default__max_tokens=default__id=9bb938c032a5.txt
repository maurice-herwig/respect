The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The system controls a pump. 

The environment controls the high water and methane conditions. The system controls the pump operation.

It is assumed that the environment behaves in such a way that if the pump is on in the current and previous states, then the high water condition will not be present in the next state.

The system must guarantee two conditions. Firstly, if the high water condition is present and there is no methane, then the pump must be on in the next state. Secondly, if methane is present, then the pump must be off in the next state. 

There are no specified initial conditions or liveness requirements for the system. The system's behavior is defined solely by the safety requirements stated above, which dictate the next-state relationships based on the current state of the environment and the system.
