The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (`cara`), the presence of cars on road B (`carb`), and whether an emergency is occurring (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (`greena`) and road B (`greenb`).

The system assumes that when an emergency is occurring, cars on road A will not be present in the next state if they are not already present. Similarly, when an emergency is occurring, cars on road B will not be present in the next state if they are not already present, unless the traffic light for road B is green or no car is present on road B. The system also assumes that when an emergency is occurring and the traffic light for road A is green, cars on road A will not be present in the next state if they are present. Likewise, when an emergency is occurring and the traffic light for road B is green, cars on road B will not be present in the next state if they are present, unless an emergency is still occurring.

The system guarantees that when there is no emergency, the traffic light for road B will not be green. The system also guarantees that if a car is present on road A and the traffic light for road B is not green, then the traffic light for road A will be green. If a car is present on road B and the traffic light for road B is green, then this condition is always satisfied. When there is no emergency, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will not be red at the same time.

Initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of cars. The system will always be in one of two states: a state where it can respond to the presence of cars and grant the corresponding traffic light, or a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to be granted. If a car is present on a road and the corresponding traffic light is not green, the system will move to the waiting state. If the traffic light is granted, the system will return to the responsive state. The system will infinitely often be in the responsive state.
