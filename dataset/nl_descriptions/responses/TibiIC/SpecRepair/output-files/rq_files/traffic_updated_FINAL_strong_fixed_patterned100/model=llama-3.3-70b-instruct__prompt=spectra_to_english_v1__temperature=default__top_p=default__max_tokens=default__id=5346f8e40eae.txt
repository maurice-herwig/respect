The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by carA and carB, and the emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: it will not have an emergency signal indefinitely often, and when a car is present and its light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car's light is green and the car is present, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for cars A and B will never both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its light green, and similarly for car B. If an emergency signal is present, both traffic lights will be red. 

The system responds to the presence of cars A and B according to a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car by turning its light green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it must turn the light green. Once the light is green, the system returns to the initial state. The system will infinitely often be in the state where it can respond to the presence of a car.
