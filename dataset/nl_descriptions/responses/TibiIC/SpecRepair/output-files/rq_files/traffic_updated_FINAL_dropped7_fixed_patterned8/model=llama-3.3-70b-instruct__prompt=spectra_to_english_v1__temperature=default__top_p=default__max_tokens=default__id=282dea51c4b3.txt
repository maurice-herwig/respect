The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, and the occurrence of emergencies. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state of cara will be true, or the emergency will continue, or the traffic light for road A will be green. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state of carb will be true, or the emergency will continue, or the traffic light for road B will be green, or carb will be false. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, cara will become false, or the emergency will continue, or the traffic light for road A will not be green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that when an emergency occurs, the traffic light for road B will not be green.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. However, during an emergency, the system will not turn the traffic lights green.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive on either road. The system then responds to the presence of cars on the roads by turning the corresponding traffic lights green. If a car is present on a road and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will return to its initial state. The system will infinitely often return to its initial state, ensuring that it continuously responds to the presence of cars on the roads.
