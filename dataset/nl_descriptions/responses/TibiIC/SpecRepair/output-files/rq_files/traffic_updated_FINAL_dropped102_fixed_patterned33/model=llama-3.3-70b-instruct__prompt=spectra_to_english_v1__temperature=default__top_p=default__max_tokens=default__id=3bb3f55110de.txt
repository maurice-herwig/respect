The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the traffic lights, with greena and greenb indicating whether the lights are green for roads A and B, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not immediately introduce a car on road A, and it will either not introduce a car on road B, keep the emergency state, or have the light for road B be green or have no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will eventually remove the car from road A. Similarly, when an emergency occurs and the light for road B is green, the environment will either keep the emergency state, have no car on road B, or eventually remove the car from road B.

The system guarantees certain behaviors: during non-emergency situations, it will not have both lights red, and if there is a car on road A and the light for road B is red, it will eventually turn the light for road A green. If there is a car on road B and the light for road B is green, the system will keep the light for road B green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

The system responds to the presence of cars on the roads according to a specific pattern: initially, it is in a state where it can respond to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the corresponding light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system will return to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern repeats indefinitely, ensuring that the system always eventually responds to the presence of a car on either road.
