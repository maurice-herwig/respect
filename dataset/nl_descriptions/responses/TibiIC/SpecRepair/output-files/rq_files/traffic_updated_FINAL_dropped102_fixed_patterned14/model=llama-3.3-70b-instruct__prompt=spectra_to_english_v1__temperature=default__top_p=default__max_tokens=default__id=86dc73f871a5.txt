The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to environmental inputs. The environment controls three boolean inputs: `cara` (indicating the presence of a car at intersection A), `carb` (indicating the presence of a car at intersection B), and `emergency` (indicating an emergency situation). The system controls two boolean outputs: `greena` (the state of the traffic light at intersection A) and `greenb` (the state of the traffic light at intersection B).

The system assumes that when the traffic light is red (i.e., not green), cars will not be present at the intersection. Specifically, when the emergency flag is false, if the traffic light at intersection A is not green, the next state will have no car at intersection A. When the emergency flag is true, if the traffic light at intersection B is green or there is no car at intersection B, the next state will have no car at intersection B. Additionally, the system assumes that when the traffic light is green, cars will move. Specifically, when the emergency flag is false, if there is a car at intersection A and the traffic light at intersection A is green, the next state will have no car at intersection A. When the emergency flag is true, if there is a car at intersection B and the traffic light at intersection B is green, the next state will have no car at intersection B.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will not both be red at the same time, unless an emergency situation is occurring. If a car is present at intersection A and the traffic light at intersection B is not green, the system will eventually turn the traffic light at intersection A green. If a car is present at intersection B and the traffic light at intersection B is green, the system will keep the traffic light at intersection B green. In an emergency situation, the system will turn both traffic lights red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives at an intersection, the system will eventually turn the corresponding traffic light green. The system will continue to alternate between waiting for a car to arrive and turning the traffic light green, ensuring that the traffic light is turned green whenever a car is present at the intersection.
