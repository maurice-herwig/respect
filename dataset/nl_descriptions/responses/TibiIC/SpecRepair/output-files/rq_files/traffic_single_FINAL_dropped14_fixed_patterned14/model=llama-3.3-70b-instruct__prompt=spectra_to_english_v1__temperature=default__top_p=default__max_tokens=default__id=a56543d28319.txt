The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police interventions often, meaning that there will be times when police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car should not be moving unless there is an emergency or the light is about to turn green. Furthermore, when the emergency flag is not set, the car should not move when the light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by turning the light green. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of its response to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically resets and is ready to respond to the car's presence. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.
