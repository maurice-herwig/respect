The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the occurrence of emergencies.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights will never both be red at the same time, ensuring that at least one road always has a green light. Additionally, when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the light for road A to green, and similarly for road B.

In the event of an emergency, the system ensures that both lights will be red. The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways during emergencies: if road A's light is red during an emergency, the car on road A will not move until either the emergency ends, the light turns green, or the car is no longer present; similarly for road B. Conversely, if road A's light is green during an emergency, the car on road A will move and will not be present at the next time step, unless the emergency ends or the light turns red; the same assumption applies to road B.

This pattern involves the system tracking its state and responding to the presence or absence of cars on the roads. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to a car's presence. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the light is already green when a car is present. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is responding to the car's presence. The system will turn the light green when it is in this responding state and will then return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
