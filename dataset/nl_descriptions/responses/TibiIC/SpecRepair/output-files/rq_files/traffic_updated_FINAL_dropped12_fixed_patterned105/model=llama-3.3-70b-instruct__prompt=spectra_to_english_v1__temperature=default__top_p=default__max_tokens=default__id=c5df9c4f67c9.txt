The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars at intersection A (`cara`) and intersection B (`carb`), as well as emergency situations (`emergency`). The system controls the traffic lights at intersection A (`greena`) and intersection B (`greenb`).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is always eventually the case that there is no emergency. Additionally, when the traffic light at intersection A is red, the environment ensures that there is no car at intersection A unless there is an emergency. Similarly, when there is an emergency, the environment ensures that either the emergency persists or the traffic light at intersection B is green. The environment also ensures that cars at intersection A move when the traffic light is green, and cars at intersection B move when the traffic light is green and there is no emergency.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights at both intersections are not both red when there is no emergency. When there is a car at intersection A, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection A green. Similarly, when there is a car at intersection B, the system eventually responds by turning the traffic light at intersection B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red. 

The system's response to cars at the intersections follows a specific pattern: initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. When a car arrives, the system responds by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light is green, the system returns to its initial state, waiting for the next car to arrive. This pattern ensures that the system always eventually responds to cars at the intersections by turning the corresponding traffic light green.
