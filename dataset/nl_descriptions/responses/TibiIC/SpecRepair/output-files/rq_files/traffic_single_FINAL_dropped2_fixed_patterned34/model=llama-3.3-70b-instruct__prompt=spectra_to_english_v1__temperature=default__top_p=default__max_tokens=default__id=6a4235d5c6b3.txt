The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, when the police are not present, the car will be present in the next state if it is not already present, unless there is an emergency or the car is already absent. Additionally, if a car is present, it will either remain present or move in the next state, unless the traffic light is green.

The system guarantees that it will often respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This response is defined by a pattern that describes the system's behavior in terms of its response to the car's presence. Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to the car. The system remains in this state as long as the car is not present or the traffic light is green when the car is present. If the car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state. The system will infinitely often be in its initial state, ensuring that it periodically responds to the presence of a car.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
