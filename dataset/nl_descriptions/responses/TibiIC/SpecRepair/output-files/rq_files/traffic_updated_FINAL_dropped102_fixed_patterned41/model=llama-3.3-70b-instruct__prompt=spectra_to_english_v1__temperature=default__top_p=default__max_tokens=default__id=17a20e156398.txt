The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to the presence of cars and emergency situations. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, and an emergency signal, denoted by `emergency`. The system-controlled outputs are the green light indicators for roads A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, the next state of car A will be true, meaning it will be present. Similarly, when an emergency occurs, the next state of car B will be true if it is not already present and the green light for road B is not on, or if car B is not present. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, car A will move away when the green light for road A is off, and car B will move away when the green light for road B is off.

The system guarantees the following behavior: when there is no emergency, the green light for road B will be off. The system will also respond to the presence of cars by turning on the corresponding green lights: when car A is present and the green light for road B is off, the green light for road A will be on, and when car B is present and the green light for road B is on, the green light for road B will remain on. Furthermore, when there is no emergency, both green lights will not be off at the same time, ensuring that at least one road has a green light.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the presence of a car by turning on the corresponding green light. This pattern involves the system transitioning between two states: one where the system is ready to respond, and one where the system is waiting for the corresponding green light to be turned on. The system will always eventually return to the ready state, ensuring that it will continue to respond to the presence of cars.
