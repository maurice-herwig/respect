The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency, and police. The system controls the traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when the traffic light is not green, the car should not start moving, and if the car is already moving, it should continue to move or the traffic light should turn green. The environment is also assumed to behave such that if the police are present, or there is no car, or the car will not move next, then the traffic light should not be green.

Given these assumptions, the system guarantees two things: first, the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present; second, there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the car's presence. The system then continuously checks the car's presence and the traffic light's state, updating its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it waits for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. If the traffic light remains green when a car is present, the system stays in its initial state. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency, and police, while the system-controlled output is the traffic light's green signal. The system ensures that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present and that there will be times when no car is present, under the given assumptions about the environment's behavior.
