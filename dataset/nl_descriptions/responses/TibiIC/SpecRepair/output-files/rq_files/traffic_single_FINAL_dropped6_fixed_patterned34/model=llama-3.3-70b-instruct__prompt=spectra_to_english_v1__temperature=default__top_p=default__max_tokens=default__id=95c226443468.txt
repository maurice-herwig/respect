The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, when the police are not present, the car will not start moving when the light is red, unless there is an emergency or the light turns green. It is also assumed that when the police are present, the car will either not be present or will not move when the light is green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, and this will happen repeatedly over time. Furthermore, the system ensures that there will be times when no car is present.

The system starts in an initial state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present and the light is not green, the system will eventually turn the light green. Once the light is green, it will stay green as long as the car is present. If the car is no longer present, the system will turn the light red. This pattern of behavior will repeat over time, ensuring that the system responds to the presence of cars in a timely manner.
