The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways: it should not have police presence indefinitely, allowing for periods without police. Additionally, when there is an emergency, the car should either be present and the light is green, or the car remains present in the next state, or the emergency condition persists. The car should also not remain present indefinitely, moving when the light is green.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the light green when a car is present. This response is characterized by a pattern where the system initially waits for a car (state S0). If a car is present and the light is not green, the system transitions to a state (S1) where it awaits the light turning green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial waiting state (S0). The system ensures that it periodically returns to this waiting state (S0), indicating that the traffic light will turn green infinitely often when a car is present. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
