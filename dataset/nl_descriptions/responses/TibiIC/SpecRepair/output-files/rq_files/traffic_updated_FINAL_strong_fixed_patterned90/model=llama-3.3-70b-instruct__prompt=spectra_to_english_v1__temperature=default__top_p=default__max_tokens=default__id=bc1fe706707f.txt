The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is green, car A will remain idle and not move. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is green, car B will remain idle and not move. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is green, car A will move and no longer be present. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. The same applies to car B. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. 

In terms of initial conditions, the system starts in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars A and B. The system's behavior can be described as follows: when a car is present, the system will eventually turn its corresponding green light on, and when the green light is on, the car will move and no longer be present. The system will always ensure that at least one of the green lights is on, unless there is an emergency, in which case both lights will be turned off.
