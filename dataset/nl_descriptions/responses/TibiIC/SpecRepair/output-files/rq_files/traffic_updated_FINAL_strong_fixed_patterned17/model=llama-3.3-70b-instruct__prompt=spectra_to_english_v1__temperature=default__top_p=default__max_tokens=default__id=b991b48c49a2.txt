The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: it will not have an emergency signal indefinitely, and when a car is present and its corresponding light is red while the other car's light is green, the car will remain present in the next state. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and not be present in the next state.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the lights for car A and car B will not both be red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on, and similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on. If an emergency signal is present, both green lights will be turned off.

The system's response to the presence of a car is defined by a pattern that ensures the system will eventually respond to the car's presence by turning its corresponding green light on. This pattern has an initial state where the system is not responding to the car's presence, and it will remain in this state as long as the car is not present or its green light is already on. If the car is present and its green light is not on, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the green light to be turned on. Once the green light is turned on, the system will return to its initial state. The system will always eventually return to its initial state.
