The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on these roads and an emergency signal. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, represented by the variables cara and carb, as well as an emergency signal. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, represented by the variables greena and greenb.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when the emergency signal is active, if the traffic light for road A is red, then there must be no car on road A; when the emergency signal is active, the traffic light for road B must be green if there is a car on road B; when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road A, the car on road A will move or the traffic light for road A will turn red at the next time step; and when the emergency signal is active or there is no car on road B, the car on road B will move at the next time step.

The system guarantees the following behavior: the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time; when there is a car on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green; and when there is a car on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. The system responds to the presence of cars on the roads by turning the corresponding traffic lights green, and it does so in a way that ensures the traffic lights are never both red simultaneously. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads.
