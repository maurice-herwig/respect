The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights in response to cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, denoted by `cara` and `carb`, respectively, as well as emergency situations, denoted by `emergency`. The system controls the traffic lights for cars A and B, denoted by `greena` and `greenb`, respectively.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, car A is assumed to either not be present or not move until the emergency is over or the traffic light turns green. Similarly, car B is assumed to either not be present or not move until the emergency is over or the traffic light turns green. Furthermore, when an emergency occurs and the traffic light is green, car A is assumed to move, and car B is also assumed to move.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for cars A and B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system guarantees that the traffic light for car A will eventually turn green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system guarantees that the traffic light for car B will eventually turn green. In emergency situations, the system guarantees that both traffic lights will be red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then responds to the presence of cars by turning the corresponding traffic light green. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system will transition to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system will transition back to the initial state. The system will always eventually return to the initial state, ensuring that it periodically checks for the presence of cars and responds accordingly.
