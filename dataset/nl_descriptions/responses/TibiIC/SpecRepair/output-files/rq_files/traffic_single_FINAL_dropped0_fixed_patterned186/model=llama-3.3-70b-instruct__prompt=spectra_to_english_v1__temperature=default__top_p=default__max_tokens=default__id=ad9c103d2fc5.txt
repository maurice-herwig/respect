The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, the environment is expected to ensure that a car is not present when the traffic light is red, unless there is an emergency or the car was already present. The environment is also expected to ensure that when the traffic light was previously green, the car will either not be present or will not remain present when the light is no longer green.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The traffic light will turn green when a car is present, although this may not happen immediately. Additionally, the system ensures that there will be times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of a car. The system remains in this state as long as there is no car present or the traffic light is green when a car is present. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system will repeatedly return to this initial state, ensuring that it can respond to cars as needed.
