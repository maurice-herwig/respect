The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on road A (cara) and road B (carb), as well as emergency situations (emergency). The system controls the traffic lights for road A (greena) and road B (greenb).

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when an emergency occurs, the environment ensures that if a car is on road A, it will either remain on the road or move forward at the next opportunity, unless the traffic light for road A is green. Similar assumptions are made for road B. The environment also ensures that when an emergency occurs and the traffic light for a road is red, the car on that road will either remain idle or move forward at the next opportunity, unless there is no car on the road.

The system guarantees that the traffic lights for roads A and B are never both red at the same time. When a car is present on road A, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road A green. Similarly, when a car is present on road B, the system will eventually turn the traffic light for road B green. During an emergency, the system ensures that both traffic lights are red.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars on the roads. The system's response to the presence of cars on the roads follows a specific pattern: when a car is present on a road and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. This pattern ensures that the system responds to the presence of cars on the roads in a timely manner.
