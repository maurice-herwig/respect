The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the car will not start moving when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. Additionally, when the police are present, the car will either not be moving or will stop moving if it was previously moving.

The system guarantees that it will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning the traffic light green. This means that whenever a car is present, the system will ensure that the traffic light turns green, but it may do so after some delay.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then enters a loop where it either remains in this waiting state if there is no car or if the car is present and the traffic light is green, or it transitions to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green if a car is present and the light is not green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its waiting state. The system ensures that it will infinitely often return to this waiting state.

In summary, the TrafficE2 system is designed to control a traffic light in response to the presence of a car, while taking into account emergency situations and police presence. The system ensures that the traffic light will turn green whenever a car is present, while the environment ensures that the car will behave safely and respectfully of the traffic light and police presence.
