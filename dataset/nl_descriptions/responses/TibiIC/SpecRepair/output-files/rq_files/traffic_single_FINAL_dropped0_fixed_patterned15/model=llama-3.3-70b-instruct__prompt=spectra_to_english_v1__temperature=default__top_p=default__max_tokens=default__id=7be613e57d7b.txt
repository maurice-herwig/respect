The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency situations, and police interventions. The system controls the state of a traffic light, specifically whether it is green or not.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. It is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when they are not present. Additionally, the environment is expected to ensure that a car is idle when the traffic light is not green, unless there is an emergency or the car is already moving. The car is also expected to move when the traffic light is green, unless it was already moving or the traffic light turns red.

The system guarantees two main behaviors. Firstly, it ensures that the traffic light will turn green often, and when a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green. Secondly, the system guarantees that there will be times when there is no car present.

The system's behavior is also governed by initial conditions and safety requirements. Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously checks for the presence of a car and adjusts its state accordingly. If a car is present and the traffic light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the traffic light to turn green. Once the traffic light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, ready to respond to the next car. The system ensures that it will eventually return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars in a timely manner.
