The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to the presence of these cars and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergency situations frequently, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when car A is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car A present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its light is red, the environment will keep car B present in the next state. When car A is present, its light is green, and there is no emergency, the environment will make car A absent in the next state. When car B is present and its light is green, the environment will make car B absent in the next state.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are never both red at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually make its light green. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually make its light green. If an emergency situation occurs, the system will turn off both green lights.

The system's behavior can be understood in terms of patterns of response. When a car is present, the system will respond by turning its corresponding light green, but only after the car has been present for some time. This response pattern ensures that the system reacts to the presence of cars in a way that is consistent with the guarantees mentioned above. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is not responding to any car. The system remains in this state as long as no car is present or if a car is present and its corresponding light is green. If a car is present and its light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is responding to the car. The system returns to the initial state once the car's light is turned green. This pattern of response ensures that the system's guarantees are met.
