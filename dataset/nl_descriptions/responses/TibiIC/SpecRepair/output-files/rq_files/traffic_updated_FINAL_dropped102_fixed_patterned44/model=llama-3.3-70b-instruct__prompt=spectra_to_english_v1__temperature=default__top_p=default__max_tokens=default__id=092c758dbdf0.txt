The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two roads, A and B, in response to the presence of cars on each road and emergency situations. The environment controls the presence of cars on roads A and B, denoted by cara and carb, respectively, as well as the emergency status. The system controls the traffic lights for roads A and B, denoted by greena and greenb, respectively.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: when an emergency occurs, it will not have a car on road A at the next time step if there is no car on road A currently, and similarly for road B, unless the light for road B is green or there is no car on road B. Additionally, when an emergency occurs and the light for road A is green, the environment will ensure that there is no car on road A at the next time step, unless the light for road A is not green. The same assumption applies to road B.

The system guarantees the following behavior: it will never have both lights red when there is no emergency, and if there is a car on road A, it will eventually turn the light for road A green when the light for road B is red. Similarly, if there is a car on road B, it will eventually turn the light for road B green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

The system's behavior is also governed by a response pattern, which ensures that when a certain condition is met (e.g., a car is present on a road), the system will respond by turning the corresponding light green. This response pattern has an initial state and evolves over time based on the presence of cars and the current state of the lights. The pattern ensures that the system will periodically return to its initial state, indicating that the response has been made.
