The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and an emergency signal from the environment. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and the emergency signal. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that the emergency signal is not constantly active, and it is expected to be inactive at some point in the future. Additionally, when car A is present and the green light for car A is red while the green light for car B is on, car A will remain idle in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and the green light for car B is red while the green light for car A is on, car B will remain idle in the next state. Furthermore, when car A is present and its green light is on, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B.

The system guarantees that the green lights for car A and car B are never both off at the same time. When car A is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car A. Similarly, when car B is present, the system will eventually turn on the green light for car B. If an emergency signal is active, the system will turn off both green lights. The system also ensures that if a car is present, its corresponding green light will be turned on in response to its presence, and this response will happen repeatedly over time. 

Initially, the system is in a state where it is ready to respond to the presence of cars. The system's response to the presence of cars is such that it will repeatedly turn on the green light for a car when it is present, and turn it off when the car is not present, while ensuring that the green lights for both cars are never both off at the same time.
