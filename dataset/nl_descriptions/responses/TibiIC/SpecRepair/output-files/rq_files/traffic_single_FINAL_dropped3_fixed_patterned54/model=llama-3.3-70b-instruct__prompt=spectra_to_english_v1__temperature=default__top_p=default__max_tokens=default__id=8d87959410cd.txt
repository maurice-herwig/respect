The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, specifically whether it is green.

The environment is assumed to behave in certain ways. Specifically, it is assumed that the police will not be present indefinitely, and there will be times when the police are not present. Additionally, when there is no emergency, if the car is not present and the light is not green, then the car will either not appear or the light will turn green. The environment is also assumed to ensure that if a car is not present, then either the car will not appear or the light will not be green, or the car will remain absent.

The system guarantees certain behavior. The system will ensure that the traffic light turns green whenever a car is present, infinitely often. Additionally, the system guarantees that there will be times when no car is present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is prepared to respond to the presence of a car. The system then continuously monitors the presence of the car and updates its state accordingly. If a car is present and the light is not green, the system moves to a state where it is waiting for the light to turn green. Once the light turns green, the system returns to its initial state, prepared to respond to the next car. If a car is not present, the system remains in its initial state. The system ensures that it will eventually return to its initial state, allowing it to respond to cars infinitely often.
