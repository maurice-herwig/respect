The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic flow based on input from the environment. The environment controls three inputs: `carA` and `carB`, which indicate the presence of cars at intersections A and B, respectively, and `emergency`, which signals an emergency situation. The system controls two outputs: `greenA` and `greenB`, which represent the traffic lights at intersections A and B.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following way: cars will always be present at both intersections A and B at some point in time, and emergency situations will always occur at some point in time, but not always. Additionally, when a car is present at an intersection and the traffic light is not green, the car will still be present at the next time step. Conversely, when a car is present at an intersection and the traffic light is green, the car will not be present at the next time step.

The system must satisfy the following guarantees: the traffic lights at intersections A and B must not both be green at the same time. Additionally, the traffic light at intersection A will be green at some point in time, and the traffic light at intersection B will also be green at some point in time. In the event of an emergency, both traffic lights must be red.

Overall, the system must manage the traffic flow to ensure safe and efficient passage of cars through the intersections, while also responding to emergency situations.
