

Specification name: TrafficE2

Environment variables:
- carA: boolean
- carB: boolean
- emergency: boolean

System variables:
- greenA: boolean
- greenB: boolean

**System Overview**
This specification describes a traffic light controller for two intersections, A and B. The system interacts with an environment that signals the presence of cars and emergency conditions. The system controls the green light signals for each intersection.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment controls the variables `carA`, `carB`, and `emergency`. The following behaviors are assumed:
- **Car Arrivals:** Cars arrive at intersection A infinitely often, and cars arrive at intersection B infinitely often.
- **Emergency Signal:** The emergency signal is not permanently active; it becomes inactive infinitely often.
- **Car Presence Dynamics:**
  - If a car is present at intersection A (`carA` is true) and light A is not green, the car remains present in the next step.
  - If a car is present at intersection A (`carA` is true) and light A is green, the car clears in the next step.
  - If a car is present at intersection B (`carB` is true) and light B is not green, the car remains present in the next step.
  - If a car is present at intersection B (`carB` is true) and light B is green, the car clears in the next step.

**System Guarantees**
The system controls the variables `greenA` and `greenB`. The system must satisfy the following requirements:
- **Safety:** The lights for intersection A and intersection B must never be green simultaneously.
- **Emergency Response:** If the emergency signal is active (`emergency` is true), both light A and light B must be not green.
- **Liveness:** Light A must turn green infinitely often.
- **Liveness:** Light B must turn green infinitely often.
