The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls two conditions: high water and methane presence. The system controls a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane, and the system guarantees that the pump is off.

The system must ensure that whenever high water is present, the pump will be turned on at the next step. Conversely, if methane is detected, the pump must be turned off at the next step.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously on and is still on, then at the next step, the high water condition will be resolved. Additionally, the environment is assumed to never have both high water and methane present simultaneously.

These requirements define the expected behavior of the Minepump system in response to the environment's conditions, ensuring safe and proper operation of the pump.
