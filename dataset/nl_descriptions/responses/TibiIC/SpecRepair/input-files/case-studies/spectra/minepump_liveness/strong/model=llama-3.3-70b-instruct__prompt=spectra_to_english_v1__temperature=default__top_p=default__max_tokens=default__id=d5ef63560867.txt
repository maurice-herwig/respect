The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls these conditions, while the system controls the operation of a pump and a flag indicator.

Initially, the environment is assumed to be in a safe state, with no high water and no methane present. In this initial state, the system guarantees that the pump is not operating.

The system ensures that if high water is detected, the pump will be activated in the next state. Conversely, if methane is detected, the pump will be deactivated in the next state.

The environment is assumed to behave in a way that if the pump was previously operating and continues to operate, the high water condition will be resolved in the next state. Additionally, the environment is assumed to never have both high water and methane present simultaneously.

The system guarantees that the flag indicator will eventually be deactivated. However, if high water is detected, the flag indicator will be activated.

The environment is assumed to behave such that if high water is present and the pump is not operating (either previously or currently), the high water condition will persist in the next state. Furthermore, the environment is assumed to eventually reach a state where methane is absent and remains absent in the next state.

The system-controlled outputs are the pump operation and the flag indicator, while the environment-controlled inputs are the high water and methane conditions.
