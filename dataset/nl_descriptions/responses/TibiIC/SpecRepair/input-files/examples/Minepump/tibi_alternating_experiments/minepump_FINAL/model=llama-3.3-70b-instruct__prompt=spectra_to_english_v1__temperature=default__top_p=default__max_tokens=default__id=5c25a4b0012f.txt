The Minepump system operates in an environment where two conditions are monitored: the presence of high water and the presence of methane. The environment controls the high water and methane conditions, while the system controls the pump operation.

Initially, the environment is assumed to be in a safe state, with no high water and no methane present. Correspondingly, the system is required to be in a state where the pump is not operating.

The system must ensure that whenever high water is present and there is no methane, the pump will be activated in the next state. On the other hand, if methane is detected, the system must ensure that the pump is not operating in the next state.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump has been operating and continues to operate, the high water condition will be resolved in the next state.

In summary, the system's behavior is defined by the following rules: 
- The pump is initially off.
- The pump turns on when high water is present and methane is not.
- The pump turns off when methane is present.
- The environment ensures that continuous pump operation will lead to the resolution of the high water condition.
