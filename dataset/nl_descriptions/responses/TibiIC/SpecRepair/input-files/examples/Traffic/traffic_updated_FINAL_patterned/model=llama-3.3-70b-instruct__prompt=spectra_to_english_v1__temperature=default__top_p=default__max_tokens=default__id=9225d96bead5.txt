The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of car A, car B, and emergency situations. The system controls the green lights for car A and car B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when a car is present and the corresponding light is red, and there is no emergency, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, and there is no emergency, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees that the lights for car A and car B are not both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car A green. Similarly, if car B is present, the system will eventually turn the light for car B green. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn both lights red.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system responds to the presence of car A and car B by turning their corresponding lights green, but only when the other light is not already green. The system ensures that if a car is present and its light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed, allowing the system to turn the light red and potentially turn the other light green.
