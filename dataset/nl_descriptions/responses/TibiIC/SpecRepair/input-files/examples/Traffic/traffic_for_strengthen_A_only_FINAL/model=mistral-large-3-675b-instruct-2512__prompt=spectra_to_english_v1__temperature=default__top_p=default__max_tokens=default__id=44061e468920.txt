### **Requirements for Traffic Light System (TrafficE2)**

This system controls a traffic light for direction **A**, interacting with its environment, which includes the presence of cars, emergency vehicles, and police activity.

#### **Inputs (controlled by the environment):**
- `carA`: Indicates whether a car is waiting at direction **A** (true if present, false otherwise).
- `emergency`: Indicates whether an emergency vehicle is present (true if active, false otherwise).
- `police`: Indicates whether police activity is interfering with normal traffic rules (true if active, false otherwise).

#### **Outputs (controlled by the system):**
- `greenA`: Determines whether the traffic light for direction **A** is green (true if green, false otherwise).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment:**
1. **Initial Conditions:** At startup, no car is present at direction **A**, and no emergency is ongoing.
2. **Cars Arrival:** Cars will arrive infinitely often at direction **A** (the environment will keep setting `carA` to true repeatedly over time).
3. **Police Activity:** Police interference will not occur infinitely often (the environment will eventually stop setting `police` to true).
4. **Car Behavior When Light is Red:** If a car is waiting at direction **A**, the light is red, and no emergency is present, the car will remain until the light turns green.
5. **Car Behavior When Light is Green:** If a car is waiting at direction **A**, the light is green, and no police interference is present, the car will proceed (i.e., `carA` will become false in the next step).

#### **Guarantees from the System:**
1. **Initial Condition:** At startup, the light for direction **A** is not green (i.e., it is red).
2. **Green Light Occurrence:** The light for direction **A** will turn green infinitely often over time.
3. **No Car Present Eventually:** If no car is waiting at direction **A**, this condition will occur infinitely often (i.e., the system ensures that the road eventually clears).

The system must ensure these guarantees whenever the environment behaves according to the specified assumptions.
