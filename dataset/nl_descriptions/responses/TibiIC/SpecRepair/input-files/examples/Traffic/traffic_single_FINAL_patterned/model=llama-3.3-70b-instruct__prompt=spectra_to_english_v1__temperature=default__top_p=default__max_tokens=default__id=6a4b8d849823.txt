The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system controls the state of a traffic light, indicated by the green signal.

The environment is assumed to not have police presence often, meaning that there will be infinitely many times when there is no police. Additionally, when the traffic light is red and there is no emergency, if a car is present, it will remain present until the traffic light turns green. Conversely, when the traffic light is green and there is no police presence, if a car is present, it will move and no longer be present.

The system guarantees that the traffic light will turn green often in response to the presence of a car, following a specific pattern. This pattern ensures that when a car is present, the traffic light will eventually turn green, and when the traffic light is green, the car will move. The system also guarantees that there will be infinitely many times when there is no car present.

Initially, the system starts in a state where it is waiting for a car to arrive. The system then follows a set of rules to ensure that it responds correctly to the presence of a car. If a car is present and the traffic light is red, the system will wait until the car moves or the traffic light turns green. If the traffic light is green and a car is present, the car will move and the system will return to its initial state. If the traffic light is green and no car is present, the system will remain in its initial state.

The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of a car, emergency conditions, and police presence. The system-controlled output is the state of the traffic light, indicated by the green signal.
