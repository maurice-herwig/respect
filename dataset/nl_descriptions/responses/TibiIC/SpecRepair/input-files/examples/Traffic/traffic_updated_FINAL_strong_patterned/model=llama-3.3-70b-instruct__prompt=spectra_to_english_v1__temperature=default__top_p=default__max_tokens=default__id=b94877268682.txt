The TrafficE2 system controls traffic lights for two cars, A and B, in response to their presence and emergency conditions. The environment controls the presence of cars A and B, as well as emergency signals. The system controls the green lights for cars A and B.

The environment is assumed to not have emergencies often, meaning that it is generally not in an emergency state. Additionally, when a car is present and its corresponding light is red, the car will remain idle until the light turns green. Conversely, when a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

The system guarantees that the lights for cars A and B are not both red at the same time. If car A is present, the system will eventually turn its green light on, and similarly for car B. When an emergency occurs, the system will turn off both green lights. The system also ensures that if a car is present and its corresponding light is green, the car will move and its presence will be removed.

Initially, the system is in a state where it is waiting for a car to be present. The system will respond to the presence of a car by eventually turning its corresponding green light on, while ensuring that the other car's light is not green at the same time. This response will happen infinitely often, as the system will always return to its initial state after responding to a car's presence.
