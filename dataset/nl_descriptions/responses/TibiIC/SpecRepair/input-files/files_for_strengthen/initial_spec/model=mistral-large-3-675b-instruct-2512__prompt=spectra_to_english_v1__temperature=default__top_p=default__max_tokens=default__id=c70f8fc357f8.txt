### **Requirements for Traffic Light System (Intersection for Lane A)**

#### **Inputs (Environment-Controlled)**
The environment provides the following signals:
- `carA`: Indicates whether a vehicle is present in lane A (true if present, false otherwise).
- `emergency`: Indicates whether an emergency vehicle is present (true if present, false otherwise).
- `police`: Indicates whether a police vehicle is present (true if present, false otherwise).

#### **Outputs (System-Controlled)**
The system controls:
- `greenA`: Indicates whether the traffic light for lane A is green (true if green, false otherwise).

---

#### **Initial Conditions**
- **Environment:** Initially, there are no vehicles in lane A (`carA` is false) and no emergency vehicles (`emergency` is false).
- **System:** The traffic light for lane A starts in a non-green state (`greenA` is false).

---

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
1. **Vehicles in Lane A Appear Infinitely Often:** Over time, vehicles will repeatedly appear in lane A (`carA` becomes true infinitely often).
2. **Police Vehicles Are Rare:** Police vehicles (`police`) will be absent infinitely often (they do not appear continuously).
3. **Vehicle Behavior When Light Is Not Green:**
   - If a vehicle is in lane A (`carA` is true) and the light is not green (`greenA` is false), the vehicle remains in the lane in the next time step (`carA` stays true).
4. **Vehicle Behavior When Light Is Green:**
   - If a vehicle is in lane A (`carA` is true) and the light is green (`greenA` is true), the vehicle leaves the lane in the next time step (`carA` becomes false).

---

#### **Guarantees Provided by the System**
1. **Traffic Light for Lane A Turns Green Infinitely Often:** The system ensures that the light for lane A (`greenA`) will transition to green infinitely often, allowing vehicles to pass.
2. **Lane A Remains Empty Infinitely Often:** The system ensures that lane A (`carA`) will repeatedly become empty (no vehicle present) infinitely often.

The system does not explicitly define additional safety or fairness constraints beyond these guarantees. Its behavior is constrained by the assumptions on how vehicles respond to the traffic light state.
