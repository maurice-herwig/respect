The TrafficE2 system is a reactive system that controls traffic flow based on input from the environment. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, emergency situations, and police presence, represented by the variables carA, carB, emergency, and police. The system-controlled outputs are the green light indicators for cars A and B, represented by the variables greenA and greenB.

The environment is assumed to initially have no cars A or B present and no emergency situation. Additionally, the environment is expected to detect cars A and B infinitely often. 

The system guarantees that initially, neither green light A nor green light B is on. The system also ensures that when the green light is on for car A, the green light for car B is off, and vice versa. Furthermore, the system guarantees that the green lights for both cars A and B will be turned on infinitely often.

The system also guarantees that there will be periods where car A is not present infinitely often. 

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that when car A is present and its green light is off, it will remain present in the next state. Similarly, when car B is present and its green light is off, it will remain present in the next state. On the other hand, when car A is present and its green light is on, it will move and not be present in the next state. The same applies to car B. 

In summary, the TrafficE2 system controls the green lights for cars A and B based on their presence and ensures safe and alternating access to the traffic flow, while also considering the environment's behavior and assumptions about the detection of cars and emergency situations.
