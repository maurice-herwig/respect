### **Traffic Light Control System – Requirements Description**

The system controls a traffic intersection with two directions, **A** and **B**, each governed by a traffic light. The environment provides inputs indicating whether a vehicle is present in direction **A** (`carA`), direction **B** (`carB`), or if an **emergency** vehicle is approaching.

- **Environment-controlled inputs:**
  - `carA` – indicates the presence of a vehicle waiting in direction A.
  - `carB` – indicates the presence of a vehicle waiting in direction B.
  - `emergency` – indicates an emergency vehicle is approaching the intersection.

- **System-controlled outputs:**
  - `greenA` – indicates that the traffic light for direction A is green.
  - `greenB` – indicates that the traffic light for direction B is green.

#### **Assumptions on the Environment:**
The environment ensures that:
- Vehicles (or detection of vehicles) appear in direction **A** infinitely often over time.
- Vehicles (or detection of vehicles) appear in direction **B** infinitely often over time.

#### **System Guarantees (Requirements):**
The system must ensure the following at all times:

1. **Safety:**
   - The lights for directions **A** and **B** must never both be green simultaneously (`greenA` and `greenB` cannot be true at the same time).
   - If an **emergency** is detected, both directions **A** and **B** must immediately have their lights set to **not green** (ensuring the intersection is clear for the emergency vehicle).

2. **Liveness:**
   - Over time, the light for direction **A** must become green infinitely often, *unless* an emergency is present.
   - Over time, the light for direction **B** must become green infinitely often, *unless* an emergency is present.
