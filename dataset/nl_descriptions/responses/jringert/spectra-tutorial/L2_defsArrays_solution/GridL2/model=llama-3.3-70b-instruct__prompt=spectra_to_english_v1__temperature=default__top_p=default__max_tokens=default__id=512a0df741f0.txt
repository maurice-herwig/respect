The system, GridL2, describes the interaction between the environment and a robotic system with a robot and a drone. The environment controls the initial and subsequent positions of the robot, represented by the coordinates `rob[0]` and `rob[1]`, both ranging from 0 to 20. The system controls the movements of the robot and the drone, with possible movements being up, down, left, right, or staying in place.

The environment is assumed to start the robot at position (0,0). Additionally, whenever the robot moves up, the environment ensures that the robot's vertical position decreases by 1 in the next step.

The system guarantees that the movements of the robot and the drone are always inverse to each other. The system also ensures that the robot does not attempt to move outside the boundaries of a 5x5 grid, where the robot's position is (0,0) at the bottom-left corner and (4,4) at the top-right corner. Specifically, if the robot is at the left edge, it cannot move left; if it is at the right edge, it cannot move right; if it is at the bottom edge, it cannot move up; and if it is at the top edge, it cannot move down.

Furthermore, if the robot chooses to stay in place, its position remains unchanged. The system maintains the integrity of the robot's movement by ensuring that if it stays, its coordinates do not change, thus maintaining its current position.
