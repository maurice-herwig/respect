The TrafficE2 system controls traffic flow for two cars, A and B. The environment determines the presence of car A and car B, while the system controls the green lights for each car. 

The environment is assumed to always have cars A and B present at some point, with each car appearing infinitely often. 

The system guarantees that the green lights for cars A and B are never both on at the same time. Additionally, the system ensures that the green light for car A and the green light for car B each turn on infinitely often. 

In this system, there are no specified initial conditions or next-state relationships, only ongoing safety and liveness requirements. The environment-controlled inputs are the presence of cars A and B, and the system-controlled outputs are the green lights for cars A and B.
