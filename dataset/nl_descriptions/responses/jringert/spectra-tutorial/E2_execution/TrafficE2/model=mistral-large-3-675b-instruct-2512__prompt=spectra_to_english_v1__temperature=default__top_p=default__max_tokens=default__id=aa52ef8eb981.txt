The **TrafficE2** system manages traffic signals at an intersection for two directions, **A** and **B**. The behavior is as follows:

### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment** controls two inputs:
  - `carA`, which indicates whether a car is present in direction **A**.
  - `carB`, which indicates whether a car is present in direction **B**.
- The **system** controls two outputs:
  - `greenA`, which indicates whether the traffic signal for direction **A** is green.
  - `greenB`, which indicates whether the traffic signal for direction **B** is green.

### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must ensure that:
- Cars appear in direction **A** infinitely often.
- Cars appear in direction **B** infinitely often.

### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure the following under all conditions:

1. **Safety Requirement:**
   - At no time are both signals (`greenA` and `greenB`) green simultaneously—this prevents collisions.

2. **Liveness Requirements:**
   - The signal for direction **A** (`greenA`) must turn green infinitely often.
   - The signal for direction **B** (`greenB`) must turn green infinitely often.

The system ensures that whenever cars appear in a direction (as guaranteed by the environment), that direction’s signal will eventually become green, while always maintaining safe operation.
