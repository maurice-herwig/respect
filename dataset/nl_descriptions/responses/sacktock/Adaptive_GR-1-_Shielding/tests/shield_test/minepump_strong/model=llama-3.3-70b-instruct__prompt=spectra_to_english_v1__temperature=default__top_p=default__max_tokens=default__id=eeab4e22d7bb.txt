The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system, in turn, controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system guarantees that the pump is not operating.

The system ensures that if high water is present, the pump will be turned on at the next opportunity. Conversely, if methane is detected, the pump will be turned off at the next opportunity.

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that if the pump was previously operating and is still operating, the high water condition will be resolved at the next opportunity. Additionally, the environment is assumed to never have both high water and methane present simultaneously.

These assumptions and guarantees define the expected behavior of the Minepump system in response to the environment's conditions, ensuring safe and proper operation of the pump.
