The Minepump system interacts with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system guarantees that the pump is not operating.

The environment is expected to behave in a way that, if the pump was previously operating and is still operating, then the next state will have no high water.

Whenever high water is present and there is no methane, the system guarantees that the pump will be operating in the next state. However, if methane is present, the system guarantees that the pump will not be operating in the next state.

The system's environment-controlled inputs are the presence of high water and methane, and its system-controlled output is the operation of the pump.
