### **Minepump System Requirements**

The **Minepump** system manages a pump in response to water levels and methane presence in an environment, ensuring safe and correct operation.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The **environment** provides two inputs:
  - `highwater`: Indicates whether the water level is critically high.
  - `methane`: Indicates whether dangerous methane gas is detected.
- The **system** controls one output:
  - `pump`: Determines whether the pump is on (`true`) or off (`false`).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
1. **Initial condition**: At startup, both `highwater` and `methane` are `false` (no high water, no methane).
2. **Continuing behavior**: If the pump was on in the previous step and remains on, the environment ensures that `highwater` becomes `false` in the next step (i.e., the pump will eventually reduce the water level if left running).

#### **System Guarantees**
1. **Initial condition**: At startup, the pump is off (`pump` is `false`).
2. **Safety requirements (must always hold):**
   - If `highwater` is `true` and `methane` is `false` in a given step, the pump must turn on in the next step (ensuring water is removed when safe).
   - If `methane` is `true` in any step, the pump must turn off in the next step (preventing ignition risk).
3. *(No explicit liveness requirements are specified beyond the above safety rules.)*
