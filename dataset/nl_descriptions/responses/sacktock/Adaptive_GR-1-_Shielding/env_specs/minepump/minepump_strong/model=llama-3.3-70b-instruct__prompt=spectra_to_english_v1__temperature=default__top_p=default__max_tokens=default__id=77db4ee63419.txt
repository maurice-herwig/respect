The Minepump system is designed to interact with its environment, which controls the presence of high water and methane. The system controls the operation of a pump. 

Initially, the environment is assumed to have no high water and no methane present, and the system is required to have the pump off.

The environment is expected to behave in a way that when the pump is on and remains on, the next state will not have high water, and at any point, either there is no high water or there is no methane.

The system guarantees that if high water is present, it will turn on the pump in the next state, and if methane is detected, it will turn off the pump in the next state. 

In summary, the environment controls the conditions of high water and methane, and the system controls the pump. The system's behavior is to turn on the pump when high water is present and turn it off when methane is detected, under the assumptions that the environment will not indefinitely maintain both high water and methane, and that the pump's operation affects the presence of high water.
