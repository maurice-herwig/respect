The system is a robot that operates within a defined area, interacting with its environment. The robot's position is determined by its x and y coordinates, represented by `robotX` and `robotY`, which are controlled by the system.

The environment provides no direct inputs to the system in this specification. However, the robot's behavior is influenced by the presence of static obstacles and target locations.

The system assumes that the environment will not directly interfere with the robot's movement or position.

The system guarantees the following behavior:

- The robot will start at a specific location, where `robotX` is 7 and `robotY` is 0.
- The robot will move in a way that its x and y coordinates change by at most 1 unit at a time.
- The robot will avoid static obstacles by ensuring that its x coordinate is not equal to the x coordinate of the static obstacle (2) unless its y coordinate is not within the range of the static obstacles' y coordinates (2, 3, 4, or 5).
- The robot will infinitely often reach each of the three target locations: (0,0), (7,0), and (7,7).

The system's behavior is constrained by these requirements, ensuring the robot moves legally, avoids obstacles, and reaches all target locations.
