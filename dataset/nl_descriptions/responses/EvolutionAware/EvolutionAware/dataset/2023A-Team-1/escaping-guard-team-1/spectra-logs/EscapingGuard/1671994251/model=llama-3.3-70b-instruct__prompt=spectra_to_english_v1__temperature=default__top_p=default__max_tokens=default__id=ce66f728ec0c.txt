The system, referred to as EscapingGuard, interacts with its environment through the movement of a robot within a defined space. The robot's position is determined by its x and y coordinates, denoted as robotX and robotY, respectively, both of which are controlled by the system and are restricted to a range of 0 to D, where D is a constant equal to 7.

The environment's behavior is not explicitly restricted by any assumptions in this specification. 

The system guarantees that the robot moves in a way that its x and y coordinates change by at most 1 unit at any step. This means that from one moment to the next, the robot can either stay in the same position, move one unit to the left, right, up, or down, but it cannot move more than one unit in any direction. This constraint applies to both the x and y coordinates of the robot, ensuring that the robot's movement is restricted to adjacent positions in the defined space.
