The system is a robot that operates within a 2D space with dimensions 0 to 7. The robot's position is controlled by the system through the variables `robotX` and `robotY`. 

The environment does not have explicitly declared inputs in this specification. 

The system is expected to operate under certain assumptions about the environment, although none are explicitly stated in this specification.

The system guarantees the following behavior: 
- The robot starts at the location (7, 0).
- The robot moves in a way that its x and y coordinates change by at most 1 unit at a time.
- The robot avoids the static obstacle located at x-coordinate 2, specifically when the y-coordinate is 2, 3, 4, or 5.
- The robot will infinitely often reach each of the three target locations: (0, 0), (7, 7), and (0, 7).

Additionally, the system has a turn counter that increments and resets modulo 3. Whenever the turn counter reaches 3, the robot's position will remain fixed, meaning its x and y coordinates will not change until the turn counter resets. 

There are no explicit liveness requirements beyond reaching the target locations, and there are no additional safety requirements beyond avoiding the static obstacles and the robot's movement constraints. The initial condition of the robot's position and the turn counter's behavior are also defined.
