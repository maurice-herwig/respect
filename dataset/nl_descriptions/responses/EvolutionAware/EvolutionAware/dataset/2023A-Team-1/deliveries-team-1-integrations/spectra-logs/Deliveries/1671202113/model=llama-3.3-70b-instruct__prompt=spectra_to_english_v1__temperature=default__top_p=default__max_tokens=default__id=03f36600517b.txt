The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between a robotic delivery system and its environment. The environment controls the elevator's vertical position, represented by `elevatorY`, and the horizontal positions of two delivery locations, `deliveriesX`. The system controls the positions of two robots, `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to move the elevator up or down by one floor, or keep it at the same floor, at each step. Additionally, the elevator is expected to visit the ground floor (`elevatorY = 0`) and the top floor (`elevatorY = 5`) infinitely often.

The system guarantees that the robots move horizontally or vertically by one unit, or remain at the same position, at each step. Initially, both robots are at the top floor (`robotY0 = 5` and `robotY1 = 5`).

When a robot moves between floors, it must do so when it is aligned with the elevator, and it must move to the elevator's position on the next step. Specifically, if `robotY0` changes, then `robotY0` must be equal to `elevatorY`, `robotX0` must be at the origin (`0`), and `robotX0` must move to the elevator's horizontal position (`elevatorX`) on the next step. The same condition applies to `robotY1`.

The system also guarantees that each robot visits the ground floor (`robotY0 = 0` and `robotY1 = 0`) and the top floor (`robotY0 = 5` and `robotY1 = 5`) infinitely often.
