The system, Deliveries, describes the interaction between an environment and a robotic system operating within a defined grid. The environment controls the elevator's vertical position, represented by `elevatorY`, and the x and y coordinates of two delivery points, `deliveriesX` and `deliveriesY`. The system controls the positions of two robots, with their initial x and y coordinates represented by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to move the elevator vertically in a manner that allows it to change its position by at most one unit at a time. Additionally, the elevator is assumed to visit the bottom and top floors (where `elevatorY` equals 0 and 5, respectively) infinitely often.

The system guarantees that the robots move horizontally and vertically by changing their positions by at most one unit at a time. Initially, both robots are positioned at a y-coordinate of 5. The system ensures that when a robot changes its vertical position, it does so by moving to the elevator's current position, and then moves horizontally to the elevator's x-position (which is fixed at 0) in the next step.

The system also guarantees that each robot visits the bottom and top floors infinitely often, as indicated by their y-coordinates (`robotY0` and `robotY1`) being equal to 0 and 5 infinitely often.
