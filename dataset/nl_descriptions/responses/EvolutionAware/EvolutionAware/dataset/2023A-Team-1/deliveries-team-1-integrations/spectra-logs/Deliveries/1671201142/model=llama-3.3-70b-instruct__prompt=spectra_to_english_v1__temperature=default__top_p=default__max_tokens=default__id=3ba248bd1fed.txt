The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of two robots within a defined space. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator, represented by `elevatorY`, while the system controls the positions of two robots, `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, which represent their horizontal and vertical coordinates.

The environment is assumed to move the elevator in a way that its vertical position changes by at most one unit at a time, allowing it to move up, down, or stay at the same position.

The system guarantees that initially, both robots are positioned at a vertical coordinate of 5. At all times, the system ensures that the horizontal and vertical positions of both robots change by at most one unit, allowing them to move horizontally or vertically by one unit or remain stationary.

Furthermore, the system guarantees that when a robot is not at the elevator's horizontal position (which is fixed at 0), it moves vertically to the next floor. If a robot is moving vertically, its horizontal position will be updated to match the elevator's position when it reaches the next floor. This rule applies to both robots, ensuring they move between floors in conjunction with the elevator's movement.
