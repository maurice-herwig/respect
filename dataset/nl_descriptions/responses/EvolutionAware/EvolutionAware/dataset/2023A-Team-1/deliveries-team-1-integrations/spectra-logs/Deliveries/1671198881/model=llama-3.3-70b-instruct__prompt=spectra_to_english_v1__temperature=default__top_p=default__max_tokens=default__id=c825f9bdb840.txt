The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between an environment and a robotic delivery system operating within a defined grid. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator, represented by `elevatorY`, which can range from 0 to 5. The system controls the positions of two robots, with their initial x-coordinates (`robotX0` and `robotX1`) and y-coordinates (`robotY0` and `robotY1`) being within the defined grid of size 5x5.

It is assumed that the elevator's vertical position changes by at most one unit at a time, allowing it to move up, down, or remain stationary.

The system guarantees that both robots move horizontally and vertically by at most one unit at a time, allowing them to move in any of the eight possible directions (including remaining stationary). Initially, both robots are positioned at y-coordinate 5.

When a robot is not at the origin (x-coordinate 0), it must move vertically to the next floor, implying that the robot's vertical movement is synchronized with the elevator's movement when the robot is not at the starting position. This ensures that the robots move between floors in conjunction with the elevator's movement, but only when they are not at the origin. 

At all times, the system ensures that the robots' movements adhere to these constraints, providing a safe and controlled operation within the defined environment.
