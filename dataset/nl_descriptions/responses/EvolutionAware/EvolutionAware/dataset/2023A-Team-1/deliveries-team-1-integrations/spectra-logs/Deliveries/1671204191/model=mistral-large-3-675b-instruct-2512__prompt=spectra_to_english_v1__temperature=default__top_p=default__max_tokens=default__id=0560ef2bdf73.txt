### **Requirements Description for the Deliveries System**

The system models a reactive environment where two robots move within a grid to deliver packages. The grid is bounded by dimensions **5 × 5**, with coordinates ranging from **0 to 5** along both the X and Y axes.

#### **Environment-Controlled Inputs**
The environment provides:
- The vertical position (`elevatorY`) of an elevator (though this is a mock variable and does not influence system behavior in this specification).
- The target delivery coordinates for two packages, given as two X-axis positions (`deliveriesX`) and two Y-axis positions (`deliveriesY`).

#### **System-Controlled Outputs**
The system controls the positions of two robots:
- `robotX0` and `robotY0` (coordinates of the first robot).
- `robotX1` and `robotY1` (coordinates of the second robot).

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure that:
1. **Robot 0** reaches the first delivery target infinitely often. Specifically, it must eventually (and repeatedly) be at the position `(deliveriesX[0], deliveriesY[0])`.
2. **Robot 1** reaches the second delivery target infinitely often. Specifically, it must eventually (and repeatedly) be at the position `(deliveriesX[1], deliveriesY[1])`.

There are no explicit assumptions on the environment or initial conditions in this specification. The requirements focus solely on the liveness guarantees—that both robots must keep delivering their assigned packages over time.
