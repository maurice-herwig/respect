The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to control the movement of two robots within a defined space. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator and the locations of deliveries, while the system controls the positions of the two robots, denoted as robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1, which represent their horizontal and vertical coordinates.

Initially, both robots are positioned at a vertical coordinate of 5. The system ensures that the robots move horizontally and vertically in a manner that allows them to change their position by at most one unit in either direction at any given time.

The environment is assumed to control the elevator's vertical movement in a similar constrained manner, allowing it to move up or down by at most one unit or remain stationary.

The system guarantees that when a robot changes its vertical position, it must do so by moving to the elevator's horizontal position (which is fixed at 0) before ascending or descending. This ensures that robots only change floors when they are aligned with the elevator.

The system operates within a defined grid, where the horizontal dimension (DimX) ranges from 0 to 5, and the vertical dimension (DimY) also ranges from 0 to 5. The elevator's horizontal position is fixed at 0, and its vertical position is controlled by the environment.
