The system consists of two robots operating within a defined area of dimensions 5x5. The environment provides the destinations for package deliveries, specified by two sets of coordinates (deliveriesX and deliveriesY). The system controls the positions of the two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1). 

The environment is expected to provide the delivery coordinates. The system guarantees that each robot will periodically reach its designated delivery point. Specifically, robot 0 will infinitely often be at the location specified by deliveriesX[0] and deliveriesY[0], and robot 1 will infinitely often be at the location specified by deliveriesX[1] and deliveriesY[1].

Additionally, the system ensures that both robots are initially positioned at the right corner of the ground floor, with robot 0 at coordinates (5, 5) and robot 1 also at coordinates (5, 5). 

Note that an elevator position (elevatorY) is provided by the environment, but its influence on the system's behavior is not specified in this description. The system's behavior is determined solely by the delivery coordinates and the initial positions of the robots.
