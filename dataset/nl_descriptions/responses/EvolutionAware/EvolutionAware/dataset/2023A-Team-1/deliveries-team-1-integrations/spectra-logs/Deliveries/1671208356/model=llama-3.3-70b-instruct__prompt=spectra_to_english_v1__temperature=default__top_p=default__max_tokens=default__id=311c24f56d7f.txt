The system, called Deliveries, involves an ongoing interaction between the environment and two robots. The environment controls the locations of two deliveries, specified by their x and y coordinates (deliveriesX and deliveriesY), as well as a mock elevator position (elevatorY). The system controls the positions of two robots, robot0 and robot1, with their respective x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1).

The environment is assumed to always place the deliveries at locations that are not on the ground floor, meaning their y-coordinates (deliveriesY) are never equal to 5.

The system guarantees that each robot will deliver its corresponding package by infinitely often visiting the location of its assigned delivery. Specifically, robot0 will infinitely often be at the location specified by deliveriesX[0] and deliveriesY[0], and robot1 will infinitely often be at the location specified by deliveriesX[1] and deliveriesY[1].

Additionally, the system guarantees that each robot will infinitely often return to the right corner of the ground floor, which is located at (5, 5).

The system also maintains two counters, packagesDeliveredByRobot0 and packagesDeliveredByRobot1, to track the number of packages delivered by each robot. These counters are incremented whenever a robot is at the location of its assigned delivery and are reset whenever the robot returns to the right corner of the ground floor. The counters can hold values from 0 to 3 and do not overflow.
