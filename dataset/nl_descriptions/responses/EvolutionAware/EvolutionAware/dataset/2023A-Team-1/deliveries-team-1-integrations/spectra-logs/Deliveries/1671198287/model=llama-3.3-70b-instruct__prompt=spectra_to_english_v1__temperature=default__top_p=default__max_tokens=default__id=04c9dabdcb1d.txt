The system controls two robots, each with coordinates (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1), within a grid of size 5x5. Initially, both robots are positioned at y-coordinate 5. 

The system guarantees that the robots move in a way that their x and y coordinates change by at most 1 unit at a time. This means that from one time step to the next, each robot's x and y coordinates can either increase by 1, decrease by 1, or remain the same. This movement rule applies to both robots at all times. 

There are no specified assumptions about the environment's behavior, and there are no environment-controlled inputs. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of the movement of the two robots.
