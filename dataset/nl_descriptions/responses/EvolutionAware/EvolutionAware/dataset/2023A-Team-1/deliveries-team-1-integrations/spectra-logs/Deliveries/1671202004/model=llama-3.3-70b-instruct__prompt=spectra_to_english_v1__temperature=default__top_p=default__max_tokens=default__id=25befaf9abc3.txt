The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of two robots within a defined space. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator and the horizontal and vertical positions of two delivery points. The system controls the horizontal and vertical positions of the two robots.

Initially, both robots are positioned at a vertical level of 5 units. The system ensures that the robots move horizontally and vertically in a distance of either 0, 1, or -1 units at each step. The environment is assumed to move the elevator vertically in a similar manner, with a change in its position of either 0, 1, or -1 units at each step.

The system guarantees that when a robot changes its vertical position, it must be aligned with the elevator and positioned at the leftmost horizontal position, and it will then move to the elevator's horizontal position in the next step. This rule applies to both robots. 

The horizontal and vertical positions of the robots and the elevator are constrained within predefined ranges, with the horizontal positions ranging from 0 to 5 units and the vertical positions ranging from 0 to 5 units. The elevator's horizontal position is fixed at 0 units.
