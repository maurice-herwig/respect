The system, called Deliveries, involves an interaction between the environment and two robots. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator and the horizontal and vertical positions of two delivery points. The system controls the positions of two robots, with initial vertical positions at 5 units.

Initially, both robots are positioned at a vertical level of 5 units. The robots move horizontally and vertically in a way that their next positions are either one unit to the left, to the right, or remain the same. The elevator also moves vertically, with its next position being either one unit up, down, or remaining the same.

The system guarantees that the robots will move in the specified manner. Additionally, when a robot changes its vertical position, it must be at the same vertical level as the elevator and at the leftmost horizontal position, and it will then move to the elevator's horizontal position in the next step. This ensures that the robots can only move between floors when they are aligned with the elevator.
