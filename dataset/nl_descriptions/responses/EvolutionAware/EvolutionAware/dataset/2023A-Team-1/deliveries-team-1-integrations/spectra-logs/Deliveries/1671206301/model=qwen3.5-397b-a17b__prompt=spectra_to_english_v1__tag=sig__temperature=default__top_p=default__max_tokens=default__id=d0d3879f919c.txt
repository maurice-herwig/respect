

Specification name: Deliveries

Named domains:
- DimX: Int(0..DX)
- DimY: Int(0..DY)
- Coord: Int(0..DY)

Environment variables:
- elevatorY: DimY
- deliveriesX: DimX [2]
- deliveriesY: DimY [2]

System variables:
- robotX0: DimX
- robotY0: DimY
- robotX1: DimX
- robotY1: DimY

**System Overview**
The system controls two robots, Robot 0 and Robot 1, operating within a grid where coordinates range from 0 to 5 in both X and Y dimensions. The environment provides target delivery coordinates for two packages. The system must manage the movement of both robots to satisfy delivery and return requirements while maintaining delivery counters.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment guarantees that the X-coordinate for both delivery locations is never 5. Specifically, `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesX[1]` will always differ from 5.

**System Guarantees**

*Initial Conditions*
- The delivery counter for Robot 0 (`packagesDeliveredByRobot0`) initializes to 0.
- The delivery counter for Robot 1 (`packagesDeliveredByRobot1`) initializes to 0.

*Liveness Requirements*
- Robot 0 must infinitely often reach the target delivery coordinates specified by `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesY[0]`.
- Robot 1 must infinitely often reach the target delivery coordinates specified by `deliveriesX[1]` and `deliveriesY[1]`.
- Robot 0 must infinitely often reach the coordinate (5, 5).
- Robot 1 must infinitely often reach the coordinate (5, 5).

*Counter Update Rules*
- The counter `packagesDeliveredByRobot0` increments when Robot 0 is located at its delivery coordinates (`deliveriesX[0]`, `deliveriesY[0]`). It resets to 0 when Robot 0 is located at coordinate (5, 5). If the counter reaches its maximum capacity of 4, it retains that value.
- The counter `packagesDeliveredByRobot1` increments when Robot 1 is located at its delivery coordinates (`deliveriesX[1]`, `deliveriesY[1]`). It resets to 0 when Robot 1 is located at coordinate (5, 5). If the counter reaches its maximum capacity of 4, it retains that value.

*Safety Requirements*
- The system must ensure that the value of `packagesDeliveredByRobot0` remains strictly less than 4 at all times.
