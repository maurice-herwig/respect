The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the delivery of packages by two robots. The environment controls the locations of the deliveries, represented by `deliveriesX` and `deliveriesY`, which are pairs of coordinates. The system controls the positions of the two robots, `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, which are also coordinates.

The environment is assumed to never place deliveries at the right corner of the ground floor, as specified by the condition that `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesX[1]` are never equal to 5.

The system guarantees that each robot will deliver packages at their respective delivery locations. Specifically, `robot0` will deliver packages at `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesY[0]`, and `robot1` will deliver packages at `deliveriesX[1]` and `deliveriesY[1]`. Additionally, both robots will always be at the right corner of the ground floor, with coordinates (5, 5), when not delivering packages.

The system also keeps track of the number of packages delivered by each robot using counters `packagesDeliveredByRobot0` and `packagesDeliveredByRobot1`. These counters increment when a robot delivers a package (i.e., when its coordinates match the delivery coordinates) and reset when the robot returns to the right corner of the ground floor.

The system ensures that each robot delivers fewer than 4 packages. This constraint applies to both robots, limiting their total deliveries. 

Note that there is also an `elevatorY` variable controlled by the environment, but its role in the system's behavior is not specified in the given requirements.
