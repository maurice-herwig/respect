The **CatchingIntruder** system models the behavior of two robots attempting to track and intercept an intruder in a bounded 7x7 grid (indexed from 0 to 6). The environment controls the position of the intruder, while the system controls the positions of the two robots.

### **Inputs (Environment-Controlled)**
- The **intruder's position** is defined by its coordinates *(intruderX, intruderY)*, both of which are integers ranging from 0 to 6 (inclusive). The environment determines these values, meaning the intruder's movement is externally dictated and unpredictable from the system's perspective.

### **Outputs (System-Controlled)**
- The **positions of the two robots** are represented by their coordinates:
  - Robot 0: *(robotX0, robotY0)*
  - Robot 1: *(robotX1, robotY1)*
  Each coordinate (for both robots) is an integer between 0 and 6. The system is responsible for determining and updating these positions to achieve its objectives.
