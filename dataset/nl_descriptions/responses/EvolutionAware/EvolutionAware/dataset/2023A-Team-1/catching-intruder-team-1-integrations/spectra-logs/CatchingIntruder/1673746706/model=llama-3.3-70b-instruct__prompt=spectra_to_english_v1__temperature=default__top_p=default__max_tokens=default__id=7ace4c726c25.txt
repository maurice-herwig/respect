The system, CatchingIntruder, describes an interaction between the environment and the system. The environment controls the position of an intruder, specified by its x and y coordinates, intruderX and intruderY, within a defined dimension of size D, which is 7 units. 

The system, in turn, controls the positions of a robot at two different points in time, denoted as (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1), also within the same dimension. 

No specific assumptions are made about the environment's behavior, and no guarantees are defined for the system's behavior in this specification. The initial conditions and any temporal relationships between the system's and environment's variables are not specified. 

The dimension, D, is fixed at 7 units, and all positions, whether of the intruder or the robot, are restricted to integer values between 0 and D, inclusive.
