The system consists of two robots and an intruder moving within a 7x7 grid. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by its x and y coordinates (intruderX and intruderY). The system controls the positions of the two robots, with each robot's position defined by its x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the intruder according to its own rules, without any specified restrictions. 

The system must guarantee the following behaviors: 
- Each robot must avoid a static obstacle located at y-coordinate 1, unless the robot is not in the x-coordinate range of 2 to 5.
- The two robots must not occupy the same location or pass each other, meaning they cannot have the same x and y coordinates or swap positions.
- Each robot must avoid the intruder, ensuring they do not occupy the same location or pass each other.

These guarantees must hold at all times, ensuring the robots maintain safe distances from the static obstacle, each other, and the intruder.
