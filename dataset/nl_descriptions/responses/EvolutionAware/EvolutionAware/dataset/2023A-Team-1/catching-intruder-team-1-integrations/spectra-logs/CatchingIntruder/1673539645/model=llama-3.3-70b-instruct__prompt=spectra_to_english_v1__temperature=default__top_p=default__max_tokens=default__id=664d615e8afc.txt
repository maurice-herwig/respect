The system consists of two robots and an intruder moving within a 7x7 grid. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by its x and y coordinates (intruderX and intruderY). The system controls the positions of the two robots, with each robot's position specified by its x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the intruder in a way that is not explicitly restricted by the specification. 

The system must guarantee the following behaviors:
- The first robot must avoid a static obstacle located at y-coordinate 1, unless it is outside the x-coordinate range of 2 to 5.
- The second robot must also avoid the same static obstacle under the same conditions.
- The two robots must not occupy the same location or pass each other, meaning their x and y coordinates cannot be the same or swapped with each other in the next step.
- The first robot must not occupy the same location as the intruder or pass the intruder, ensuring that their x and y coordinates are never the same or swapped with each other in the next step.
- The second robot must also respect the intruder's position in the same manner as the first robot.

These guarantees must always hold, ensuring the safe and respectful movement of the robots in relation to the intruder and the static obstacles.
