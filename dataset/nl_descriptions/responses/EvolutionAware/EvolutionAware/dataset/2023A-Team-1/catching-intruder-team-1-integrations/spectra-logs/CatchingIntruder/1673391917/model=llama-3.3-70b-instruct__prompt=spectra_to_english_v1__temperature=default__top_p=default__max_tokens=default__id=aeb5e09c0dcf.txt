The system consists of two robots and an intruder moving within a 7x7 grid. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by `intruderX` and `intruderY`, while the system controls the positions of the two robots, specified by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to move the intruder arbitrarily, with no restrictions on its movement.

The system must guarantee the following behaviors:

- Robot 0 must avoid a static obstacle located at y-coordinate 1, unless it is outside the x-coordinate range of 2 to 5. 
- Robot 1 must also avoid the same static obstacle under the same conditions.
- The two robots must not collide with each other, meaning they cannot occupy the same position at the same time.
- Robot 0 must not move over the intruder's position.
- Robot 1 must also not move over the intruder's position.

These guarantees must hold at all times, ensuring the robots' safe and collision-free movement within the grid while avoiding the static obstacle and the intruder.
