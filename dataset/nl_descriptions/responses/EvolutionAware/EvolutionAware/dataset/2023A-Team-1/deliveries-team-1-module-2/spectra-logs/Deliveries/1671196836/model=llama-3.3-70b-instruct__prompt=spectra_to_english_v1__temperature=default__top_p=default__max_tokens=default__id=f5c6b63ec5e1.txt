The system is designed to control two robots, with positions represented by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`, within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment provides the delivery locations, represented by `deliveriesX` and `deliveriesY`, which are pairs of x and y coordinates. The environment also controls the elevator's position, represented by `elevatorX` and `elevatorY`, although these are not used in the system's guarantees.

The system assumes that the environment will provide valid delivery locations within the grid. 

The system guarantees that it will infinitely often move `robotX0` and `robotY0` to match `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesY[0]`, and `robotX1` and `robotY1` to match `deliveriesX[1]` and `deliveriesY[1]`. However, it also guarantees that the robots will not remain at the delivery locations, as it must infinitely often not have `robotX0` and `robotY0` equal to `deliveriesX[0]` and `deliveriesY[0]`, and not have `robotX1` and `robotY1` equal to `deliveriesX[1]` and `deliveriesY[1]`. 

In other words, the system will periodically visit the delivery locations, but it will not stay at those locations. The system does not specify any initial conditions or next-state relationships for the robots' positions.
