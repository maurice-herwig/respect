### **Requirements for the EscapingGuard Reactive System**

The system models the behavior of a robot moving within a 7×7 grid (where grid positions are integers ranging from 0 to 7 along both axes: `robotX` and `robotY`).

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- The robot’s position (`robotX`, `robotY`) is controlled by the system and updated over time. There are no environment-controlled inputs in this specification.

#### **Initial Condition**
- At startup, the robot must begin at position `(7, 0)` (i.e., `robotX = 7` and `robotY = 0`).

#### **Safety Requirements (Always-Held Constraints)**
- The robot’s movement is restricted to adjacent cells in each step. Specifically, from its current position `(robotX, robotY)`, the next position must satisfy:
  - `robotX` changes by at most ±1 (i.e., it stays the same, or moves one step left or right).
  - `robotY` changes by at most ±1 (i.e., it stays the same, or moves one step up or down).
  - The robot cannot "teleport" or skip cells.

#### **Liveness Requirements (Infinitely Often Conditions)**
- The robot must visit the following three target positions infinitely often during its operation:
  - **T1:** `(0, 0)` (origin, bottom-left corner).
  - **T2:** `(7, 7)` (top-right corner).
  - **T3:** `(0, 7)` (top-left corner).
