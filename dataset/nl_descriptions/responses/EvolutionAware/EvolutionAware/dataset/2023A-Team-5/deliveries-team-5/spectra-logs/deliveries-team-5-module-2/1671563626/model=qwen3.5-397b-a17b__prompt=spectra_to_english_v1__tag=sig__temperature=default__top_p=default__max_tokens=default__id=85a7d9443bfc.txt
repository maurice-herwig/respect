

Specification name: Module2

Named domains:
- DimX: Int(0..DX)
- DimY: Int(0..DY)
- Coord: Int(0..DY)

Environment variables:
- elevator: DimY

System variables:
- robotX0: DimX
- robotY0: DimY
- robotX1: DimX
- robotY1: DimY

**System Overview**
The system controls two robots, Robot 0 and Robot 1, operating within a grid defined by X coordinates ranging from 0 to DX and Y coordinates ranging from 0 to DY. The environment controls an elevator that moves along the Y axis. The robots must navigate the grid while avoiding collisions and coordinating with the elevator when positioned at X=0.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment behaves according to the following constraints:
- The elevator position changes by at most 1 unit per step (it may move up, down, or stay stationary).
- The elevator visits floor 0 infinitely often.
- The elevator visits floor DY infinitely often.

**System Guarantees**
The system must satisfy the following requirements:

*Initial Conditions*
- Initially, Robot 0 is at Y coordinate DY.
- Initially, Robot 1 is at Y coordinate DY.

*Safety Requirements*
- **Collision Avoidance:** The two robots must never occupy the same position simultaneously. They must differ in either their X coordinate or their Y coordinate at all times.
- **Swap Prevention:** If both robots are on the same Y coordinate, they cannot exchange X coordinates in a single step.
- **Elevator Alignment:** Whenever a robot is at X coordinate 0, its Y coordinate must exactly match the current position of the elevator.
- **Vertical Movement Restriction:** A robot may only change its Y coordinate when its X coordinate is 0. If a robot is at any X coordinate other than 0, its Y coordinate must remain constant.

*Movement Constraints*
- **Horizontal Movement:** In each step, a robot's X coordinate may change by at most 1 unit (increment, decrement, or stay constant).
- **Manhattan Movement:** In each step, a robot must keep at least one coordinate constant. It cannot change both its X and Y coordinates simultaneously.
