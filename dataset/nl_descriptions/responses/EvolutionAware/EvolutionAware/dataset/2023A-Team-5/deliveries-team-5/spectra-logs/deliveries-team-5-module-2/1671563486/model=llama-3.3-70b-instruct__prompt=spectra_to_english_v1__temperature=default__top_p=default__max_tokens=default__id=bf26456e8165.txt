The system consists of two robots and an elevator in a grid with dimensions DX by DY. The environment controls the elevator's position, while the system controls the positions of the two robots. The environment is assumed to move the elevator up or down by at most one unit at a time and to visit both the bottom (position 0) and top (position DY) of the grid infinitely often.

The system guarantees that when a robot is at position 0 on the x-axis, its y-axis position will be updated to match the current elevator position. The system also ensures that the x-axis positions of the robots change by at most one unit at a time.

Initially, both robots are positioned at the top of the grid on the y-axis. The system guarantees that the robots will not collide by ensuring that if they are at the same y-axis position, they are not at the same x-axis position, and if they are at the same position, they will move apart.

When a robot is not at position 0 on the x-axis, its y-axis position remains unchanged. Additionally, at any given time, each robot either stays at its current x-axis position or stays at its current y-axis position. This means that a robot can only move in one direction (either x or y) at a time.

The environment-controlled input is the elevator's position, and the system-controlled outputs are the positions of the two robots. The system's behavior is designed to prevent collisions between the robots while ensuring they can move in response to the elevator's position.
