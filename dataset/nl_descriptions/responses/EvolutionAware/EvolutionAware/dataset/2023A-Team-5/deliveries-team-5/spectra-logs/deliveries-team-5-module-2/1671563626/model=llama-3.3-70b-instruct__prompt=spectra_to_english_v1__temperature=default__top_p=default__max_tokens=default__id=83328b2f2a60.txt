The system consists of two robots and an elevator in a grid with dimensions DX by DY. The environment controls the elevator's position, while the system controls the positions of the two robots. The environment is assumed to move the elevator up or down by at most one unit at a time and to visit the top and bottom floors infinitely often.

The system guarantees that when a robot is at the leftmost position (x = 0), its vertical position (y) will match the elevator's position. The robots' horizontal positions can change by at most one unit at a time. Initially, both robots are at the topmost position (y = DY).

To prevent collisions and switching places, the system ensures that if the two robots are at the same vertical position, they will not move to the same horizontal position in the next step, and if they are at the same horizontal position, they will not be at the same vertical position.

When a robot is not at the leftmost position, its vertical position remains unchanged. Additionally, at any given time, each robot either maintains its current horizontal position or its current vertical position. The system ensures that the robots operate within these constraints while responding to the elevator's movements.
