The system interacts with an environment that controls the elevator position, represented by the variable "elevator", which ranges from 0 to DY (5). The system, in turn, controls the positions of two robots, with initial x and y coordinates represented by "robotX0", "robotY0", "robotX1", and "robotY1", all within a defined grid of size DX (5) by DY (5).

The environment is assumed to behave in such a way that the elevator position changes by at most 1 unit at a time. Additionally, it is assumed that the elevator will infinitely often be at both the bottom (position 0) and the top (position DY) of its range.

The system guarantees that whenever a robot is at the leftmost position (x = 0), its y-coordinate will be updated to match the current elevator position. This applies to both robots, meaning that if either robotX0 or robotX1 is 0, then the corresponding robotY0 or robotY1 will be set to the elevator's position in the next state. 

The initial x and y coordinates of the robots are system-controlled outputs, while the elevator position is an environment-controlled input. The system's behavior ensures that the robots' y-coordinates are aligned with the elevator's position whenever the robots are at the specified x-coordinate, under the given environmental assumptions.
