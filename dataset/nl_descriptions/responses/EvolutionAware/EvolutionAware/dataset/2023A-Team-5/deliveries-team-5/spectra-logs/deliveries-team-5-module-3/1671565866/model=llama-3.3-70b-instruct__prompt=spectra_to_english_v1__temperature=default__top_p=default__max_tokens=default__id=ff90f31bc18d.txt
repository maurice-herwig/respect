The system consists of two robots operating within a defined area of dimensions DX by DY, where DX and DY are both 5 units. The environment provides target coordinates, targetX0, targetY0, targetX1, and targetY1, for the two robots to reach. The system controls the positions of the robots, represented by robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1.

The environment is assumed to always provide non-zero targetX0 and targetX1 values and targetY0 and targetY1 values that are not equal to DY. Additionally, whenever a robot reaches its target, the environment will change the target's Y-coordinate in the next step.

The system guarantees that each robot will infinitely often reach its target, meaning both robots will repeatedly arrive at their respective target coordinates. 

Two counters, packages0 and packages1, keep track of the number of times each robot reaches its target, with a maximum count of 3. The counters increment each time a robot reaches its target and reset when the robot's Y-coordinate equals DY. If a counter reaches 3, the system ensures that the corresponding robot will not be at its target.

Initially, the counters are set to 0. The system operates under the constraints that the environment provides valid target coordinates and changes them as required, and the system adjusts the robot positions accordingly to meet the guarantees of repeatedly reaching the targets and managing the counters as specified.
