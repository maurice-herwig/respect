### **Requirements Description for Module2**

This specification describes a reactive system consisting of two robots and an elevator, operating within a bounded grid. The system controls the initial positions of both robots, while the elevator's movement is determined by the environment.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- **Environment-controlled input:**
  - `elevator`: The vertical position of the elevator, ranging from `0` to `5`.
- **System-controlled outputs:**
  - `robotX0`, `robotY0`: The initial grid coordinates of the first robot, where its horizontal position (`robotX0`) ranges from `0` to `5` and its vertical position (`robotY0`) ranges from `0` to `5`.
  - `robotX1`, `robotY1`: The initial grid coordinates of the second robot, with the same bounds as the first.

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
- The elevator moves smoothly: at each step, its position changes by at most `1` (either increasing or decreasing by `1` or staying the same).
- The elevator frequently visits the top (`5`) and bottom (`0`) of its range infinitely often.

#### **System Guarantees**
- Whenever either robot is at the leftmost column (`robotX = 0`), its next vertical position must match the current elevator position.
  - Specifically:
    - If `robotX0` moves to `0` in the next step, then `robotY0` must equal the current `elevator` position in that next step.
    - If `robotX1` moves to `0` in the next step, then `robotY1` must equal the current `elevator` position in that next step.
