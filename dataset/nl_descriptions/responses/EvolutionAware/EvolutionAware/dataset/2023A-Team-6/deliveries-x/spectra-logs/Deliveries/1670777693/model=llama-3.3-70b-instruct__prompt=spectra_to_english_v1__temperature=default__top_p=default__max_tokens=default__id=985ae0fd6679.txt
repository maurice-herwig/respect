The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between the environment and a robotic delivery system operating within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the locations of packages, specified by their x and y coordinates (package0X, package0Y, package1X, package1Y). The system controls the locations of two robots, also specified by their x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to provide the locations of the packages. The system guarantees that each robot will eventually reach its corresponding package. Specifically, the system ensures that robot 0 will infinitely often be at the same location as package 0 (i.e., robotX0 = package0X and robotY0 = package0Y), and similarly, robot 1 will infinitely often be at the same location as package 1 (i.e., robotX1 = package1X and robotY1 = package1Y). 

There are no specified initial conditions or safety requirements beyond the guarantee that the robots will reach the packages. The system's behavior is solely focused on ensuring that the robots eventually arrive at the package locations provided by the environment.
