This means that every third turn, the robot does not change its position, but its movement pattern over three turns allows for a change in position in two out of the three turns. 

This movement pattern essentially dictates how the robot's position (robotX and robotY) updates over time, ensuring a specific type of movement behavior from the system in response to the turn counter's progression.
