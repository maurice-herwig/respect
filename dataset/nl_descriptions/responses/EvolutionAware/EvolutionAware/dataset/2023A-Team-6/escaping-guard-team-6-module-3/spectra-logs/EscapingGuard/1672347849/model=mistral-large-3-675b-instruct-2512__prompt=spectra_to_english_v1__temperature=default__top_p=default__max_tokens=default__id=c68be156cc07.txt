### **Requirements for the EscapingGuard System**

The system controls the position of a robot within a 7×7 grid, where both coordinates (`robotX` and `robotY`) range from 0 to 6 (inclusive). The robot's position must never coincide with any of the following fixed obstacles located at specific coordinates:

- (2, 2)
- (2, 3)
- (2, 4)
- (2, 5)

At **all times**, the robot must avoid occupying any of these forbidden positions. This safety requirement ensures that the robot does not collide with the predefined obstacles in its operational space. No other assumptions are made about the environment, and no additional guarantees or temporal behaviors (such as liveness or next-state conditions) are imposed on the system.
