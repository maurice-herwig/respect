The system, referred to as EscapingGuard, interacts with its environment and controls the position of a robot within a defined dimensional space. The robot's position is determined by its x and y coordinates, represented by the variables robotX and robotY, respectively. These coordinates are restricted to a range of values from 0 to D, where D is defined as 7.

The system guarantees that the robot will avoid touching fixed obstacles. Specifically, the robot must never be at position (2, 6) or (3, 6) simultaneously. This means that at all times, if the robot's x-coordinate is 2, its y-coordinate must not be 6, and if the robot's x-coordinate is 3, its y-coordinate must also not be 6. This guarantee ensures the robot's safe navigation around the obstacles by maintaining a safe distance from these specific points.
