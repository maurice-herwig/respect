The EscapingGuard system controls a robot's position, defined by its x and y coordinates, within a grid of size D x D, where D is 7. The system ensures the robot's movement while avoiding certain fixed obstacles. 

The environment does not have direct control over any variables in this specification. The system-controlled outputs are the robot's x and y coordinates, represented by robotX and robotY, respectively.

There are no explicit assumptions about the environment's behavior in this specification.

The system guarantee, referred to as robotDoesntTouchFixedObstacles, requires that the robot must always avoid touching two specific fixed obstacles located at (2,6) and (3,6). This means that the robot is never allowed to have its x coordinate as 2 and its y coordinate as 6 at the same time, nor can it have its x coordinate as 3 and its y coordinate as 6 simultaneously. This condition must hold true at all times.
