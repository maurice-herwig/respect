

Specification name: EscapingGuard

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- guardX: Dim
- guardY: Dim

System variables:
- robotX: Dim
- robotY: Dim

The system controls the coordinates of a robot (`robotX`, `robotY`) on a grid, while the environment controls the coordinates of a guard (`guardX`, `guardY`). The grid dimensions are defined by the domain `Dim`, representing integers from 0 to 7.

The primary safety guarantee requires that the robot never touches the guard. The guard occupies a 2x2 area defined by its top-left corner at (`guardX`, `guardY`). At all times, the robot's position must not coincide with any of the four coordinates comprising this area: (`guardX`, `guardY`), (`guardX`+1, `guardY`), (`guardX`, `guardY`+1), or (`guardX`+1, `guardY`+1).

An internal turn counter `turnNum` tracks the progression of time, cycling repeatedly through the values 0, 1, and 2, starting at 0.

The environment is subject to assumptions regarding the guard's movement based on the turn counter:
- During turns 0 and 1, the guard must remain stationary; its coordinates must not change.
- During turn 1, specific conditions regarding the robot's position relative to the guard dictate that the guard moves one step towards the robot:
  - If the robot's X coordinate is more than 1 unit greater than the guard's X coordinate, the guard's X coordinate must increase by 1.
  - If the robot's Y coordinate is more than 1 unit greater than the guard's Y coordinate, the guard's Y coordinate must increase by 1.
  - If the robot's X coordinate is less than the guard's X coordinate, the guard's X coordinate must decrease by 1.
  - If the robot's X coordinate is less than the guard's Y coordinate, the guard's Y coordinate must decrease by 1.
