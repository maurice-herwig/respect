The system is designed to interact with an environment that controls the movement of an intruder. The environment's inputs are the intruder's x and y coordinates, and a counter that tracks the number of times the intruder stays in place. The system's outputs are the x and y coordinates of two robots.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following way: the intruder starts at the bottom of the space, with its y coordinate initially set to 7. The intruder's movement is restricted such that it can only move horizontally or vertically, one step at a time, or stay in place. The intruder must stay in place for four consecutive time steps infinitely many times. When the intruder moves, it resets the stay-in-place counter, and when it stays in place, the counter increments. The intruder is also restricted from colliding with certain obstacles.

The system must ensure that the intruder's movement follows these rules: when the stay-in-place counter is not equal to 4, the intruder can move either horizontally or vertically, or stay in place, but not both. When the counter is equal to 4, the intruder must move to the bottom of the space, with its y coordinate set to 7, and it cannot stay in place.

Initially, the intruder's y coordinate is set to 7, and the stay-in-place counter is set to 0. At all times, the intruder must avoid colliding with the obstacles. The system must ensure that these rules are followed, while the environment controls the intruder's movement and the stay-in-place counter.
