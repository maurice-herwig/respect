

The **CatchingIntruder** specification describes a reactive system involving two robots and an intruder operating on a grid. The grid coordinates are integers ranging from 0 to 7.

Specification name: CatchingIntruder

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)
- four: Int(0..4)

Environment variables:
- intruderX: Dim
- intruderY: Dim
- dontMove: four

System variables:
- robotX0: Dim
- robotY0: Dim
- robotX1: Dim
- robotY1: Dim

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment controls the intruder's position and a movement counter. The following constraints restrict the environment's behavior:

*   **Initial Conditions:** At the start of the system, the intruder's Y-coordinate (`intruderY`) is 7, and the counter (`dontMove`) is 0.
*   **Obstacle Avoidance:** The intruder is never located at coordinates (2,1), (3,1), (4,1), or (5,1).
*   **Movement Rules:**
    *   Whenever the `dontMove` counter is not 4, the intruder must perform a valid grid move. This consists of either changing the X-coordinate by at most 1 while keeping Y constant, changing the Y-coordinate by at most 1 while keeping X constant, or remaining in the same position.
    *   If the intruder changes its X-coordinate while keeping its Y-coordinate constant, the `dontMove` counter resets to 0 in the next step.
    *   If the intruder remains in the exact same position (both X and Y unchanged), the `dontMove` counter increments by 1 in the next step.
*   **Reset Condition:** If the `dontMove` counter reaches 4, the intruder must move to the bottom row (`intruderY` becomes 7) in the next step. Additionally, the intruder cannot remain in the exact same position (both X and Y unchanged) during this transition.

**System Guarantees**
The system controls the coordinates of two robots (`robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, `robotY1`). No specific behavioral guarantees are defined for these variables in this specification.
