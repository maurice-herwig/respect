The system is designed to interact with an environment that controls an intruder's position. The environment provides the intruder's x and y coordinates, denoted as `intruderX` and `intruderY`. The system, in turn, controls the positions of two robots, with their initial x and y coordinates denoted as `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- The intruder starts at the bottom, with its initial y-coordinate being 7.
- The environment also controls a counter, `dontMove`, which is initially set to 0.
- When the intruder moves, the `dontMove` counter is reset to 0. If the intruder remains stationary, the counter increments by 1.
- The intruder avoids colliding with obstacles at specific coordinates (2,1), (4,1), (3,1), and (5,1).
- The intruder moves according to certain rules: when the `dontMove` counter is not equal to 4, the intruder either moves horizontally or vertically (but not both) or remains stationary.
- When the `dontMove` counter equals 4, the intruder reappears at the bottom (y-coordinate 7) and changes its position.

The system must satisfy these assumptions, but its specific behavior in response to the environment's actions is not defined in this specification. The initial conditions and constraints on the intruder's movement are the primary focus of this description.
