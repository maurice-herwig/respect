The system, CatchingIntruder, is a reactive system that interacts with an environment. The environment controls the intruder's position, represented by `intruderX` and `intruderY`, and a counter `dontMove`. The system controls the positions of two robots, represented by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to satisfy several conditions. Initially, the intruder starts at the bottom, with `intruderY` equal to 7. The `dontMove` counter is initially set to 0. The intruder must stay in place for 4 consecutive times infinitely often. When the intruder moves, it can only move one step in either the x or y direction, or stay in place. The intruder cannot collide with obstacles at positions (2,1), (4,1), (3,1), and (5,1).

When the `dontMove` counter is not equal to 4, the intruder moves according to the following rules: it can move one step in the y direction while staying in place in the x direction, or move one step in the x direction while staying in place in the y direction, or stay in place in both directions. When the `dontMove` counter is equal to 4, the intruder moves to the bottom, with `intruderY` equal to 7, and it cannot stay in place in both directions.

The system must ensure that the robots do not collide with the same obstacles as the intruder, although the specific behavior of the robots is not defined in this specification. The initial positions of the robots are not specified, and there are no guarantees defined for the system's behavior. The specification only defines the assumptions on the environment's behavior.
