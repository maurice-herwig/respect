The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of robots and an elevator within a defined grid. The grid is defined by the dimensions DX (5 units wide) and DY (5 units high), with the robots and lamps positioned within these bounds. The initial positions of the robots are defined by robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1. 

The system's behavior is constrained by the environment's assumptions, ensuring that the robots' movements are coordinated with the elevator's position, while the environment maintains the elevator's movement within specified bounds.
