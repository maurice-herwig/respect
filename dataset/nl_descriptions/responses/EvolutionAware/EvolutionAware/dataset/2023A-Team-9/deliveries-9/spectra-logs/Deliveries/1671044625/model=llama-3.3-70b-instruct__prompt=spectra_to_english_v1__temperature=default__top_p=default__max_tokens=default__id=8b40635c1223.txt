The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between an environment and two robots operating within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the positions of two lamps, lamp0 and lamp1, and an elevator, while the system controls the positions of two robots, robot0 and robot1.

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only horizontally, with its position changing by at most one unit at a time. Additionally, the environment is assumed to periodically move the elevator to both the top and bottom of the grid, as the elevator's position will infinitely often be at the minimum (0) and maximum (5) values of the vertical dimension.

The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time, changing its x-coordinate by at most one unit. When robot0 is at the left edge of the grid (x-coordinate 0), it moves vertically only when the elevator moves, and its new vertical position is the same as the elevator's new position. When robot0 is not at the left edge, it does not move vertically, maintaining its current vertical position. 

Note that the specification does not provide information about the initial positions of the robots or the lamps, nor does it specify any constraints on the movement of lamp0 and lamp1 or robot1's vertical movement. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of the constraints on the robots' horizontal movement and robot0's vertical movement in relation to the elevator.
