

Specification name: Deliveries

Named domains:
- DimX: Int(0..DX)
- DimY: Int(0..DY)
- Coord: Int(0..DY)

Environment variables:
- lamp0X: DimX
- lamp0Y: DimY
- lamp1X: DimX
- lamp1Y: DimY
- elevator: DimY

System variables:
- robotX0: DimX
- robotY0: DimY
- robotX1: DimX
- robotY1: DimY

The Deliveries specification describes a reactive system where the system controls the coordinates of two robots (robot 0 and robot 1) along X and Y axes. The environment provides the coordinates for two lamps (lamp 0 and lamp 1) and controls the position of an elevator along the Y axis.

The environment is subject to specific assumptions regarding the elevator behavior. The elevator position must adhere to a safety constraint where its value changes by at most one unit between consecutive steps; it may increase by one, decrease by one, or remain unchanged. Additionally, the environment must satisfy liveness requirements ensuring that the elevator visits the minimum position (0) infinitely often and visits the maximum position (DY) infinitely often during operation.
