The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment in a 5x5 grid, where the x-axis ranges from 0 to 5 (DX) and the y-axis ranges from 0 to 5 (DY). The environment controls the positions of two lamps (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) and an elevator (elevator), while the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only horizontally, with its x-coordinate remaining constant. Additionally, the elevator is assumed to periodically return to both the bottom (y=0) and top (y=DY) of the grid.

The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time, either staying in place, moving one step to the left, or moving one step to the right. Furthermore, when robot0 is at the left edge of the grid (x=0), it moves vertically only when the elevator moves, and its vertical position is updated to match the elevator's new position. 

Note that the vertical movement of robot1 is not specified, and the system's behavior when the robots are not at the left edge is also not constrained by the given guarantees. The initial positions of the robots and lamps are defined as robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1, lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, and lamp1Y.
