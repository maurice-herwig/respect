The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of robots and an elevator within a defined grid. The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time, changing its x-coordinate by at most one unit. Additionally, the system ensures that when robot0 is at the leftmost position (x=0), its vertical movement is synchronized with the elevator's movement, meaning its y-coordinate changes to match the elevator's next position.

The grid is defined by the dimensions DX (5 units wide) and DY (5 units high), with the robots and elevator operating within these boundaries. The initial positions of the robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1) are defined as system-controlled variables, but their specific starting values are not specified.
