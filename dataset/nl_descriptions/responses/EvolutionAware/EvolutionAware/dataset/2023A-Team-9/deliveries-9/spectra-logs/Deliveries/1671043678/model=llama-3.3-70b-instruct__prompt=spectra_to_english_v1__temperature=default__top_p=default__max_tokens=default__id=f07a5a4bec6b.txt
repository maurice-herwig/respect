The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment in a two-dimensional space with dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the positions of two lamps (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) and an elevator (elevator), while the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only vertically, either up or down by one unit, or not at all, and the elevator will be at both the top (DY) and bottom (0) of its range infinitely often.

The system guarantees that each robot moves one step at a time, either horizontally or vertically, or not at all, in a way that its x and y coordinates change by at most one unit at a time. This movement constraint applies to both robots at all times. 

Note that the initial positions of the robots are defined as robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1, but the specific values of these positions are not specified.
