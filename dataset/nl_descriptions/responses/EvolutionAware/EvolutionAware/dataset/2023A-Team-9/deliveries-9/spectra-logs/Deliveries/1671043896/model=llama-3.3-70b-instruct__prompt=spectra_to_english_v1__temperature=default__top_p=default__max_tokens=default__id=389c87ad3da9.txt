The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between the environment and two robots operating within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the positions of two lamps (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) and an elevator (elevator), while the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only vertically, either up or down by one step, and to keep the elevator at either the bottom (0) or the top (DY) of the grid infinitely often.

The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time. Additionally, the system ensures that when the first robot is at the leftmost position (robotX0 = 0), its vertical movement is synchronized with the elevator's movement, meaning it can only move up or down when the elevator moves.

The initial positions of the robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1) are system-controlled outputs, while the positions of the lamps and the elevator are environment-controlled inputs. The system's behavior is restricted by the assumptions on the environment's control of the elevator, and it must satisfy the guarantees of robot movement in response to the environment's actions.
