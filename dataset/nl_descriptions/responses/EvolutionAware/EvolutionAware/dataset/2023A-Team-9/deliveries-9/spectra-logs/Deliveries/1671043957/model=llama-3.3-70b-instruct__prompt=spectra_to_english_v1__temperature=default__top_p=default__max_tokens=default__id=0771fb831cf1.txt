The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between the environment and two robots operating within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the positions of two lamps (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) and an elevator, while the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only vertically, either up or down by one step, and to keep the elevator at either the bottom (0) or the top (DY) of the grid infinitely often.

The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time. Additionally, the system ensures that when robot0 is at the left edge of the grid (robotX0 = 0), its vertical movement is restricted to follow the elevator's position.

Initially, the robots and lamps are placed at unspecified positions within the grid. The system's safety requirements dictate that the robots' horizontal movements are always one step at a time, and the robot0's vertical movement is tied to the elevator when at the left edge. There are no explicit liveness requirements specified for the robots' movements beyond the elevator's periodic movement to the top or bottom of the grid.
