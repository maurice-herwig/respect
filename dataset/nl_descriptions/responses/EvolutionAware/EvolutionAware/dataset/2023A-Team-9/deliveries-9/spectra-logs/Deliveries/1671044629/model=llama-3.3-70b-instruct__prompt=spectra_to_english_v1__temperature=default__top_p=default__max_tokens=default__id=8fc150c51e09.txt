The system, called Deliveries, involves an interaction between the environment and two robots operating within a grid of dimensions 5x5. The environment controls the positions of two lamps (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) and an elevator, while the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to move the elevator only vertically, either up or down by one step, and to position the elevator at either the top (DY) or the bottom (0) of the grid infinitely often.

The system guarantees that each robot moves horizontally one step at a time. Additionally, the system ensures that the first robot (robot0) moves vertically only when it is positioned at the elevator (x=0), and in this case, its vertical movement follows the elevator's movement. When the robot is not at the elevator (x!=0), it cannot move vertically.

The initial positions of the robots are not specified, but their movements are constrained by the above rules. The system's behavior is defined by these safety requirements, which must always hold, and there are no specified liveness requirements beyond the elevator's periodic movement to the top or bottom of the grid.
