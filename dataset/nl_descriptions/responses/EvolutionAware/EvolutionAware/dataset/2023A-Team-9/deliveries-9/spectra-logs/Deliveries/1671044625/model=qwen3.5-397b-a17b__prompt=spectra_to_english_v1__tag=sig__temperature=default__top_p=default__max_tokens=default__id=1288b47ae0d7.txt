

Specification name: Deliveries

Named domains:
- DimX: Int(0..DX)
- DimY: Int(0..DY)
- Coord: Int(0..DY)

Environment variables:
- lamp0X: DimX
- lamp0Y: DimY
- lamp1X: DimX
- lamp1Y: DimY
- elevator: DimY

System variables:
- robotX0: DimX
- robotY0: DimY
- robotX1: DimX
- robotY1: DimY

The system controls the coordinates of two robots within a grid defined by dimensions DX and DY, while the environment controls the position of an elevator and two lamps. 

Regarding the environment, the elevator position must change by at most one unit per step, meaning it may increase by one, decrease by one, or remain unchanged. Additionally, the elevator must visit position 0 infinitely often and must visit position DY infinitely often.

Regarding the system, Robot 0's X-coordinate and Robot 1's X-coordinate must each change by at most one unit per step. Robot 0's Y-coordinate behavior depends on its current X-coordinate: if Robot 0 is at X-position 0, its next Y-coordinate must equal the elevator's next Y-coordinate; otherwise, Robot 0's Y-coordinate must remain unchanged in the next step.
