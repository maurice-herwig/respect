The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment through a set of variables. The environment controls the positions of two lamps, `lamp0` and `lamp1`, with coordinates (`lamp0X`, `lamp0Y`) and (`lamp1X`, `lamp1Y`), respectively. The system controls the positions of two robots, with initial positions (`robotX0`, `robotY0`) and (`robotX1`, `robotY1`).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- Whenever `lamp0` is at the same location as the first robot, its y-coordinate will change in the next step.
- `lamp1` will not move until it reaches the same location as the second robot.

The system must satisfy the following conditions:
- The positions of the robots and lamps are constrained within a grid of size 5x5.
- The movement of the robots and lamps is restricted to adjacent positions in the grid.

Note that the specification does not explicitly define the guarantees required from the system, nor does it describe the initial conditions, safety requirements, liveness requirements, or next-state/update rules for the system-controlled variables. The system's behavior is only constrained by the assumptions on the environment.
