The system, called Deliveries, interacts with an environment that controls the positions of two lamps, lamp0 and lamp1, in a two-dimensional space with dimensions DX by DY. The system controls the positions of two robots, robot0 and robot1, in the same space. 

The environment-controlled inputs are the x and y coordinates of lamp0 (lamp0X and lamp0Y) and lamp1 (lamp1X and lamp1Y). The system-controlled outputs are the x and y coordinates of robot0 (robotX0 and robotY0) and robot1 (robotX1 and robotY1).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following way: lamp0 will not change its position until it has reached the same location as robot0, and similarly, lamp1 will not change its position until it has reached the same location as robot1.

There are no specified guarantees required from the system, nor are there any initial conditions, safety requirements, liveness requirements, or next-state/update rules defined for the system's behavior. The specification only defines the assumptions on the environment's behavior regarding the movement of the lamps in relation to the robots' positions.
