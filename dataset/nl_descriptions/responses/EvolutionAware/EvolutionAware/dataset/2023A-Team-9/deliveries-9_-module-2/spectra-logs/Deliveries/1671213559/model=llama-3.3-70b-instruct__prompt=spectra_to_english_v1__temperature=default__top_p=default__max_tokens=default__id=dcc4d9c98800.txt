The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between a robotic delivery system and its environment, which includes lamps. The environment controls the positions of two lamps, specified by their x and y coordinates (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y), within a defined grid of size 5x5 (DX = 5, DY = 5). The system controls the positions of two robots, also specified by their x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1), within the same grid.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- When a lamp (lamp0) reaches the same location as a robot (robotX0, robotY0), it will move to a different y position.
- The second lamp (lamp1) will not change its position until it reaches the same location as the second robot (robotX1, robotY1).

The system must ensure that the robots move within the defined grid, with their x and y positions changing by at most 1 unit at a time, as defined by the moveDistX and moveDistY predicates. Additionally, the system must ensure that the robots' y positions can change by at most 1 unit at a time when moving like an elevator, as defined by the elevatorMoveDistY predicate. 

The robots and lamps are considered to be at the same location if their x and y coordinates match, as defined by the equalLocation predicate. 

Note that the specification does not define any specific initial conditions, liveness requirements, or next-state/update rules beyond the movement restrictions and assumptions on the environment.
