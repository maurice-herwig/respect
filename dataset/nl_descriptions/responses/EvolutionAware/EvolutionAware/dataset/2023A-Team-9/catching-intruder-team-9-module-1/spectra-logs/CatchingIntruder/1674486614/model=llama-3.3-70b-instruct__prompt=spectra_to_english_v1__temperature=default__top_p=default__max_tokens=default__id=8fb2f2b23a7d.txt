The system consists of two robots moving within a 7x7 grid. The environment does not control any inputs in this specification. The system-controlled outputs are the positions of the two robots, represented by (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1).

The system starts with robot 0 at position (0, 0) and robot 1 at position (7, 0). At all times, the two robots must not be at the same position.

The robots can move in one of three ways: they can change their x-coordinate while keeping their y-coordinate the same, change their y-coordinate while keeping their x-coordinate the same, or stay at the same position. This means that each robot can move horizontally or vertically, but not diagonally, and can also choose not to move at all.

If robot 0 reaches the bottom left corner of the grid (position (0, 7)), then robot 1 must stay at its current position and not move. 

Additionally, there are certain positions that the robots must avoid: robot 0 must never be at positions (2, 1), (4, 1), (3, 1), or (5, 1), and robot 1 must also never be at these positions. 

The system must ensure that these conditions are met at all times, without any external input from the environment.
