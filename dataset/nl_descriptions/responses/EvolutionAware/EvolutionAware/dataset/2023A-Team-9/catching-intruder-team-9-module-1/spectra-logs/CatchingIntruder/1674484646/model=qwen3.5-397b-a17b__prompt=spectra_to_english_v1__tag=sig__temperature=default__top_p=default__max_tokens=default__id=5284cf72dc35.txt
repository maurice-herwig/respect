

Specification name: CatchingIntruder

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- none

System variables:
- robotX0: Dim
- robotY0: Dim
- robotX1: Dim
- robotY1: Dim

The system controls the positions of two robots, Robot 0 and Robot 1, on a two-dimensional grid. The coordinates for both robots are integers ranging from 0 to 7 inclusive, where the domain bound D is defined as 7. There are no environment-controlled inputs; all variables are system-controlled outputs.

Initially, Robot 0 is positioned at coordinates (0, 0), and Robot 1 is positioned at coordinates (7, 0).

At every step, the system must ensure the following safety constraints regarding movement:
1. Each robot may either change its X coordinate by at most 1 while keeping its Y coordinate constant, change its Y coordinate by at most 1 while keeping its X coordinate constant, or remain completely stationary. Diagonal movement is not permitted.
2. The two robots must never occupy the same coordinates simultaneously.

Additionally, the movement of Robot 1 is restricted based on the position history of Robot 0. Robot 1 must remain stationary until Robot 0 has reached the position (0, 7) at least once. Once Robot 0 has reached this position, the restriction on Robot 1 is lifted, and Robot 1 may move according to the standard movement constraints described above.
