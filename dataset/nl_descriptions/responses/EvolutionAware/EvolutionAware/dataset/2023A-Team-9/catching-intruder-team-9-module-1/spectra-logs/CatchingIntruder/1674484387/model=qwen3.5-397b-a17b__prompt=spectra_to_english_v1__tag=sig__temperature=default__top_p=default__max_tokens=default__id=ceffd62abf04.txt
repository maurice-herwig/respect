

Specification name: CatchingIntruder

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- none

System variables:
- robotX0: Dim
- robotY0: Dim
- robotX1: Dim
- robotY1: Dim

The system controls the positions of two robots, Robot 0 and Robot 1, on a two-dimensional grid. The coordinate values for all position variables are integers ranging from 0 to 7.

Initially, Robot 0 is positioned at coordinates (0, 0), and Robot 1 is positioned at coordinates (7, 0).

Throughout operation, both robots must adhere to specific movement constraints. At each step, a robot may either change its horizontal coordinate (`robotX`) by at most 1 unit while keeping its vertical coordinate (`robotY`) constant, change its vertical coordinate by at most 1 unit while keeping its horizontal coordinate constant, or remain completely stationary. Diagonal movements, where both coordinates change simultaneously, are not permitted.

Additionally, a conditional constraint applies to Robot 1. Robot 1 must remain stationary at its current position until Robot 0 reaches the specific location where `robotX0` is 0 and `robotY0` is 7. Once Robot 0 reaches this location, the restriction on Robot 1 is lifted, allowing it to move according to the general movement constraints described above. Until Robot 0 reaches this location, Robot 1 is prohibited from changing its coordinates.
