### Requirements for the *CatchingIntruder* Reactive System

The system models two robots operating in a discrete 7×7 grid (where coordinates range from 0 to 7). The environment has no direct inputs, while the system controls the positions of both robots over time.

#### **Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation:
- The first robot begins at position **(0, 0)**.
- The second robot begins at position **(7, 0)**.

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must ensure the following behavior for the robots' movements:
- In each time step, a robot’s *x* or *y* coordinate may **stay the same** or **change by at most 1** (i.e., it can increment, decrement, or remain static in each dimension independently).

This defines the allowable transitions for both robots’ positions at every step of their operation.
