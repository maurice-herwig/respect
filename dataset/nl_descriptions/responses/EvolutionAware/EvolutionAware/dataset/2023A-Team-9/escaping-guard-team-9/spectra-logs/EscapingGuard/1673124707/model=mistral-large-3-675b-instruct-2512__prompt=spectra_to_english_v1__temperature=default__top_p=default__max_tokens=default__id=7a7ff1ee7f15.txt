### **Requirements Description for the EscapingGuard System**

This system models a robot moving in a 7×7 grid (with coordinates ranging from 0 to 6) while avoiding a guard. The environment controls the guard’s position, and the system controls the robot’s position.

#### **Inputs and Outputs**
- **Environment-controlled inputs (guard position):**
  - `guardX` (horizontal position, 0–6)
  - `guardY` (vertical position, 0–6)
- **System-controlled outputs (robot position):**
  - `robotX` (horizontal position, 0–6)
  - `robotY` (vertical position, 0–6)

#### **Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation:
- The robot must be at position `(7, 0)` (outside the grid, as `7` is technically out of bounds for `Dim`).
- The guard’s initial position is unrestricted.

#### **System Guarantees**
The specification currently only defines the robot’s initial position and does not specify further safety, liveness, or next-state behavior. The robot is not yet required to move, avoid the guard, or reach any particular goal beyond its starting position. Further guarantees (such as collision avoidance or movement rules) may be defined later.
