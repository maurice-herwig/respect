

Specification name: EscapingGuard

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- Obstacles: Dim[4][2]
- targets: Dim[3][2]

System variables:
- robot: Dim[2]

The system controls the `robot` position, while the environment controls the `Obstacles` and `targets` variables. All coordinate values are integers within the range defined by the `Dim` domain.

Initially, the `robot` is located at coordinates (7, 0).

Regarding safety, the system constrains the movement of the `robot` such that between any two consecutive steps, each coordinate of the `robot` must either remain unchanged, increase by exactly 1, or decrease by exactly 1.

Regarding liveness, the system guarantees that the `robot` will infinitely often reach the position (0, 0). Additionally, the `robot` must infinitely often reach the position (7, 7). Finally, the `robot` must infinitely often return to the position (7, 0).
