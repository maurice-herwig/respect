The system is an Escaping Guard that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the positions of obstacles and targets, represented by the `Obstacles` and `targets` variables, respectively. The system controls the position of a robot, represented by the `robot` variable, which has two dimensions, `robot[0]` and `robot[1]`.

The environment is assumed to provide valid positions for obstacles and targets within a defined boundary of size `D`, which is set to 7.

The system guarantees that the robot will always move, either horizontally or vertically, by changing its position by 1 unit in either direction, or by staying at the same position. This movement is defined by the `moveDist` predicate, which applies to both dimensions of the robot's position.

Initially, the robot is positioned at coordinates (7, 0).

The system also guarantees that the robot will infinitely often visit three specific target locations: (0, 0), (7, 7), and (7, 0). This means that the robot will repeatedly move to and from these target locations over time.

The system's movement is restricted to within the defined boundary, and it must navigate around the obstacles provided by the environment. However, the specific behavior of the system in response to obstacles is not defined beyond the guarantees of movement and target visits.
