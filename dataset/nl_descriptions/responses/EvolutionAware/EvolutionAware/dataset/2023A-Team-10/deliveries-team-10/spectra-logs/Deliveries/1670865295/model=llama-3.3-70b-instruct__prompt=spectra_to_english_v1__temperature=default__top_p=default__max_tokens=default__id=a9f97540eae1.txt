The system, called Deliveries, describes the interaction between the environment and two robots moving within a defined area. The environment controls the vertical position of an elevator, represented by `elevatorY`. The system controls the horizontal and vertical positions of two robots, `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to operate within the constraints defined by the system's guarantees. The system guarantees that initially, both robots are at the bottom of the area, with `robotY0` and `robotY1` set to 0. At all times, the system ensures that the two robots do not collide, meaning they are never at the same horizontal and vertical position simultaneously.

The system also guarantees that both robots move horizontally, either staying in the same position, moving one position to the left, or moving one position to the right. When a robot is aligned with the elevator's horizontal position (`elevatorX`), the system ensures that the robot's vertical position matches the elevator's vertical position (`elevatorY`). This applies to both robots.

The area in which the robots operate is defined by the dimensions `DX` (5 units wide) and `DY` (5 units tall), with the elevator's horizontal position fixed at `elevatorX` (0). The robots' movements are restricted to within these boundaries, with their vertical positions able to change based on their alignment with the elevator.
