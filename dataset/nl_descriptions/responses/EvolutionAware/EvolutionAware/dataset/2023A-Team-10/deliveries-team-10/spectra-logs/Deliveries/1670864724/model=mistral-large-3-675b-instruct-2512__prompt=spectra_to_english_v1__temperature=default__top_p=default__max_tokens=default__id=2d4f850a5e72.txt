### **Requirements Description for the Deliveries System**

This system controls the movement of two robots in a grid of size 5×5 (coordinates ranging from 0 to 5 in both the X and Y directions). The environment does not provide any inputs in this specification. The system manages the following outputs:

- The **X and Y positions** (`robotX0`, `robotY0`) of the first robot.
- The **X and Y positions** (`robotX1`, `robotY1`) of the second robot.

#### **Initial Conditions**
At the start of operation:
- The first robot must be positioned at **Y = 0**.
- The second robot must also be positioned at **Y = 0**.

#### **Safety Requirements**
At all times, the system must ensure that:
- The two robots **never occupy the same position simultaneously** (i.e., they must not collide).

#### **Movement Rules**
The system must update the robots' positions according to the following constraints:

- **Horizontal Movement (X-axis):**
  - A robot’s X position in the next step may:
    - Increase by 1 (move right).
    - Decrease by 1 (move left).
    - Remain the same (no horizontal movement).

- **Vertical Movement (Y-axis):**
  - A robot’s Y position in the next step may:
    - Increase by 1 (move upward).
    - Decrease by 1 (move downward).

No other positional updates are permitted. The specification does not impose additional constraints on how the system determines when or in which direction each robot moves.
