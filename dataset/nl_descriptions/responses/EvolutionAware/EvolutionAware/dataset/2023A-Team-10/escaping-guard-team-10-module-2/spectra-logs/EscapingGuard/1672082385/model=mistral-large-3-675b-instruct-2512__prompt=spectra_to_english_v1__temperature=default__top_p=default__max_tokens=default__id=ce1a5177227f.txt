### **Requirements Description for EscapingGuard**

This system monitors a two-dimensional grid of size **7×7** (where coordinates range from **0** to **7**) in which a moving agent, referred to as the *guard*, operates. The **environment** controls the guard’s position at all times, specifying its coordinates within the grid. The system has no direct control over the guard’s movement but must respond to it under certain conditions.

#### **Environment Assumptions**
The environment must ensure that the guard’s movement is constrained as follows:
- From any given position, the guard may **stay in the same grid cell**, **move one cell to the left**, or **move one cell to the right** in the next step. No other movements (e.g., jumps or diagonal shifts) are permitted.

#### **System Guarantees**
The specification does not yet impose any explicit safety, liveness, or response guarantees on the system. The only defined aspect of the system’s behavior is its **initial condition**, which is not specified in this fragment.

At this stage, the system’s obligations are limited to correctly observing the guard’s position and abiding by the environment’s movement constraints. Further requirements (such as reactive behavior, error handling, or temporal guarantees) may be added later.
