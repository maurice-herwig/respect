This specification describes a patrolling robot system that operates within a bounded grid. The grid spans 5 positions along the x-axis (0 to 4) and 5 positions along the y-axis (0 to 4).

**Environment-controlled inputs:**
- The environment provides a boolean array `isGoalBlocked` of size 3, where each element indicates whether a particular "goal" area is currently blocked (true) or unblocked (false).
- The environment also provides a 3x2 array `goals`, where each of the three goals is defined by a pair of coordinates (DimX, DimY) representing its position in the grid.

**System-controlled outputs:**
- The system controls the robot's position, represented by `robotX` (its x-coordinate) and `robotY` (its y-coordinate). Both coordinates are integers between 0 and 4.

**Assumptions on the environment:**
- For each of the three goals, the environment guarantees that the `isGoalBlocked` status for that goal will alternate between blocked (true) and unblocked (false) infinitely often over time. This means no goal will remain permanently blocked or permanently unblocked; instead, its status will keep changing.

No guarantees or behaviors are specified for the system in this fragment beyond its control over the robot's position. The environment's behavior regarding the goals' positions (`goals`) is unrestricted in this specification.
