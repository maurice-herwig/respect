The Patrolling system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the x and y coordinates of three goals, represented by `goalsX` and `goalsY`. The system controls the x and y coordinates of a robot, represented by `robotX` and `robotY`, as well as a boolean array `isGoalBlocked` that indicates whether each goal is blocked.

The environment is assumed to provide valid goal coordinates within the range of 0 to 5. 

The system guarantees that the first goal is never blocked, as indicated by `isGoalBlocked[0]` being always false. For the second and third goals, the system guarantees that each goal is infinitely often blocked and infinitely often not blocked, meaning that the blocked status of these goals will change over time. 

Note that the initial conditions and next-state relationships for the robot's position and the goals' coordinates are not specified. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of the blocked status of the goals.
