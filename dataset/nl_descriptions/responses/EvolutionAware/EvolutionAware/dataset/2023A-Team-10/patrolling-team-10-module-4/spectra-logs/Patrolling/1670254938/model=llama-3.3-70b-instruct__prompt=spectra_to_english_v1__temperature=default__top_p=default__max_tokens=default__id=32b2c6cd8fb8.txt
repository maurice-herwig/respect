The Patrolling system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the position of a moving obstacle in the x and y dimensions, represented by `movingObstacleX` and `movingObstacleY`, respectively, as well as a `lowBattery` signal. The system controls the position of a robot, represented by `robot`, which has two dimensions.

The environment is assumed to always keep the moving obstacle at a fixed y-position of 3.

The system guarantees that the robot will never be at the same position as the moving obstacle and will never be at certain predefined obstacle positions. 

Initially and at all times, the robot must avoid the moving obstacle and the predefined obstacles.

Whenever the `lowBattery` signal is true, the system guarantees that the robot will eventually move to the charging station, which is located at position (4,0), within a finite number of steps.

The robot is always moving, either horizontally or vertically, by changing its position by either -1, 0, or 1 in either dimension. This movement is ongoing, with the robot's position updated at each step according to these rules.
