The Patrolling system is designed to control a robot's movement within a defined area. The environment controls the position of a moving obstacle in the x-direction (`movingObstacleX`) and y-direction (`movingObstacleY`), as well as a `lowBattery` signal. The system controls the robot's position, represented by `robot[0]` and `robot[1]`.

The environment is assumed to always keep the moving obstacle at a fixed x-position of 3.

The system guarantees that the robot will never be at the same position as the moving obstacle or at certain predefined obstacle positions. 

Initially and at all times, the robot must avoid the moving obstacle and the predefined obstacles.

When the `lowBattery` signal is detected, the system guarantees that the robot will move to the charging station within a finite number of steps.

The robot's movement is restricted such that it can only move one step in either direction (up, down, left, or right) or remain stationary. This movement rule applies to both the x and y coordinates of the robot's position. 

The system ensures that the robot will always move according to this rule, either changing its position by one step or staying in the same position.
