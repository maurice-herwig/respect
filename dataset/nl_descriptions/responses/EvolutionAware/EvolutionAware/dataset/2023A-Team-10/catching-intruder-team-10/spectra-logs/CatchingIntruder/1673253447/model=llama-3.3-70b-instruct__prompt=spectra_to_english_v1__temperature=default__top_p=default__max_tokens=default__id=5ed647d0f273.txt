- At all times, both robots avoid obstacles located at positions (2, 1), (3, 1), (4, 1), and (5, 1).
- At all times, the robots do not collide with each other, meaning they never occupy the same position simultaneously.
- At all times, Robot 0 moves either horizontally or vertically by one unit, changing either its x or y coordinate.
- Once Robot 0 reaches the bottom-left corner (0, 7), Robot 1 moves either horizontally or vertically by one unit, changing either its x or y coordinate.

The system's movement is restricted to single-unit steps, either horizontally or vertically, and the robots must avoid obstacles and collisions at all times. The initial positions of the robots are predefined, and their subsequent movements are determined by the system's guarantees.
