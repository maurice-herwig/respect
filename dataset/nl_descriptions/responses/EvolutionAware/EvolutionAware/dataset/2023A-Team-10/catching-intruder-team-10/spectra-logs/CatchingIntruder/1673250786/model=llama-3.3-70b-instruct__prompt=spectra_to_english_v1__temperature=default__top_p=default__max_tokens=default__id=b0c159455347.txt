The system, CatchingIntruder, is designed to control two robots within a 7x7 grid, where the environment has no direct control over the robot positions. The system must ensure that both robots avoid certain obstacle locations and do not collide with each other.

The environment-controlled inputs are implicit, as the system must adapt to any changes in the environment. The system-controlled outputs are the positions of the two robots, represented by (robotX0, robotY0) and (robotX1, robotY1).

There are no explicit assumptions about the environment's behavior. The system must operate correctly under any environmental conditions.

The system guarantees the following behavior: 
- Both robots must always avoid the obstacle locations at (2,1), (3,1), (4,1), and (5,1).
- The two robots must never occupy the same position at the same time.
- Each robot must move either horizontally or vertically by one unit at a time, either increasing or decreasing its position by 1.

The system's movement rules dictate that each robot can only move in one direction (either x or y) at a time, while keeping the other direction's position constant. This ensures that the robots can only move in a grid-like pattern, either left/right or up/down, but not diagonally. 

There are no specified initial conditions or liveness requirements beyond the safety requirements and movement rules. The system must maintain these guarantees at all times, without any specific requirements for the robots to reach certain positions or achieve specific goals.
