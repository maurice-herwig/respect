**Requirements for the Catching Intruder System**

The system tracks two robots in a two-dimensional grid of size 7×7 (indices ranging from 0 to 6). The positions of the robots are controlled by the system and represented by the following outputs:

- `robotX0` and `robotY0`: The x- and y-coordinates (respectively) of the first robot.
- `robotX1` and `robotY1`: The x- and y-coordinates (respectively) of the second robot.

The environment does not provide any inputs to this system, nor are there assumptions imposed on the environment’s behavior.

The system guarantees that the robots' coordinates always remain within the defined grid boundaries (i.e., integers between 0 and 6, inclusive). No additional behavior, initial conditions, or temporal constraints are specified for the robots' movement or interaction.
