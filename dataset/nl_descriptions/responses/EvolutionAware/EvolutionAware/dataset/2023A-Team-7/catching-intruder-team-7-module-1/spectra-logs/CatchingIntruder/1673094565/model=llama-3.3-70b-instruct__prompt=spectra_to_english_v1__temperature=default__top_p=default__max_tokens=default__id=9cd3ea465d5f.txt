The system, called CatchingIntruder, describes the interaction between two robots and an intruder within a 7x7 grid. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by `intruderX` and `intruderY`, while the system controls the positions of two robots, with the first robot's position specified by `robotX0` and `robotY0`, and the second robot's position specified by `robotX1` and `robotY1`.

The environment is assumed to move the intruder in a way that either increments, decrements, or keeps its x and y coordinates the same, with at least one of the coordinates remaining stationary. This assumption applies at all times.

The system guarantees that both robots will move in a similar manner, with each robot's x and y coordinates either incrementing, decrementing, or remaining the same, and at least one of the coordinates remaining stationary at all times.

Initially, the first robot is positioned at (0,0), and the second robot is positioned at (7,0). The intruder is assumed to start at a position with a y-coordinate of 7. 

These initial positions and movement rules define the behavior of the system and its interaction with the environment.
