The system consists of two lamps and two robots operating within a defined grid of dimensions DX by DY, where DX and DY are both equal to 5. The environment controls the initial positions of the lamps, while the system controls the positions of the robots and the lamps once they are picked up.

The environment is assumed to always keep both lamps on floors other than the ground floor, as indicated by their y-coordinates (lamp0Y and lamp1Y) never being equal to 0.

The system guarantees that the lamps will not move until they are correctly picked up by their corresponding robots. However, the specific conditions under which the lamps are considered to be picked up and the subsequent movement rules are not defined in this specification.

Initially, the system has the robots and lamps positioned at specific coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1, lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y) within the defined grid. 

Note that the movement rules for the lamps once picked up, and any additional requirements for the robots, are not specified in this description.
