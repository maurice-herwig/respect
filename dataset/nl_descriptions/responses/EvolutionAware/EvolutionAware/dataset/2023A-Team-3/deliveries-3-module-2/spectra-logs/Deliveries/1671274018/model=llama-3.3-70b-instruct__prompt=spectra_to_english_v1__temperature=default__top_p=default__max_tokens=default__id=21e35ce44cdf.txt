The system, called Deliveries, involves two lamps and two robots operating within a defined area of 5x5 dimensions. The environment controls the positions of the two lamps, specified by their x and y coordinates (lamp0X, lamp0Y, lamp1X, lamp1Y), while the system controls the positions of the two robots, also specified by their x and y coordinates (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: the lamps are never placed on the ground floor, meaning their y-coordinates (lamp0Y and lamp1Y) are always non-zero. Additionally, the lamps do not change their positions until they are correctly picked up by the corresponding robots. This means that unless a lamp's position matches the position of its corresponding robot, the lamp's position remains the same from one moment to the next. Conversely, when a lamp is correctly picked up (i.e., its position matches the robot's position), its position can change, indicating it is being moved by the robot.

There are no specified guarantees or requirements for the system's behavior beyond the constraints placed on the environment's behavior. The system's initial conditions and the positions of the robots and lamps at the start are not defined. The focus of the specification is on the constraints and assumptions related to the environment's behavior, particularly how the lamps are controlled and interact with the robots.
