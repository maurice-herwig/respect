### **System Description: Elevator and Robot Coordination**

This system models the interaction between an elevator and two robots in a building with a grid layout. The building has a fixed width (`DimX`) of 5 units and a height (`DimY`) of 5 units, where the elevator moves vertically and the robots operate within this grid.

#### **Environment-Controlled Inputs**
- The environment controls the vertical position of the elevator (`elevatorY`), which ranges from **0 (ground floor)** to **5 (top floor)**. The elevator's horizontal position (`elevatorX`) is fixed at **0**.

#### **System-Controlled Outputs**
- The system controls the positions of two robots:
  - **Robot 0** has coordinates (`robotX0`, `robotY0`).
  - **Robot 1** has coordinates (`robotX1`, `robotY1`).
  Each robot's position is constrained within the same 5×5 grid.

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
1. **Movement Constraints on the Elevator**
   - The elevator can move at most one floor at a time—either staying in place, moving up one floor, or moving down one floor.

2. **Liveness Requirement on the Elevator**
   - The elevator must **infinitely often** visit the **ground floor (position 0)** and the **top floor (position 5)**. This ensures that the elevator does not get stuck on intermediate floors indefinitely.

#### **System Guarantees**
*(The current specification does not include explicit guarantees for the robots, so only the environment assumptions are defined.)*
