The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of robots and lamps within a defined space. The environment controls the position of the elevator and the initial positions of two lamps, while the system controls the positions of two robots.

The environment is expected to behave in certain ways. The elevator's vertical position changes by at most one unit at a time. The elevator visits both the ground floor and the top floor infinitely often. Additionally, the lamps are never initially placed on the ground floor. The lamps do not change their positions until they are picked up by the correct robot. When a lamp is not picked up by its corresponding robot, its position remains the same. However, when a lamp is picked up, its vertical position must change.

The system's behavior is defined by the positions of the two robots. The initial positions of the robots are defined by the variables robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1. The system must ensure that the robots move in a way that allows them to pick up the lamps, but the specific movement rules are not defined in this specification.

The space in which the robots and lamps move is defined by a 5x5 grid, with the elevator fixed at position 0 on the x-axis. The positions of the robots and lamps are constrained to within this grid, with the x-coordinate ranging from 0 to 5 and the y-coordinate ranging from 0 to 5.
