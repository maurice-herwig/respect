The system consists of a robot and a guard in a 2D environment with dimensions 0 to 7. The robot's position is controlled by the system, with its x and y coordinates represented by `robotX` and `robotY`, respectively. The guard's position is controlled by the environment, with its x and y coordinates represented by `mx` and `my`, respectively. There are also fixed obstacles at specific locations.

Initially, the robot is positioned at the top right corner (x=7, y=0), and the guard is positioned at the top left corner (x=0, y=0).

The environment is assumed to move the guard in a way that its x and y coordinates can either increase, decrease, or stay the same by 1 unit at each step. The same assumption applies to the robot's movement, which is controlled by the system.

The system guarantees that the robot will visit three targets, located at (x=0, y=0), (x=7, y=7), and (x=0, y=7), infinitely often. 

The robot and the guard can move horizontally or vertically by 1 unit or stay in the same position at each step. The system must ensure that the robot's movement follows this rule, and the environment is assumed to move the guard according to the same rule.
