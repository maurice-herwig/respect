The system consists of a robot that moves towards targets in a 2D space. The environment controls the locations of three targets, represented by their x and y coordinates. The system controls the robot's position, also represented by its x and y coordinates.

The environment is assumed to keep the targets stationary, meaning their locations do not change over time. 

The system guarantees that the robot starts at the top left corner of the space, with its x and y coordinates both being 0. 

The system also guarantees that the robot will infinitely often reach each of the three targets and return to the top left corner. In other words, the robot will repeatedly visit each target and then go back to its initial position. 

The robot's movement is restricted to a space with a maximum x coordinate of 4 and a maximum y coordinate of 4. The targets are also restricted to a space with a maximum coordinate of 4. 

Note that the system does not specify how the robot moves or the exact path it takes to reach the targets, only that it will eventually reach each target and return to the top left corner.
