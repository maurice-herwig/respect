The system is a robot that moves towards targets in a 2D space. The environment controls the locations of three targets, represented by their x and y coordinates. The system controls the robot's position, also represented by its x and y coordinates.

The environment is assumed to keep the targets stationary, meaning their locations do not change over time. 

The system must satisfy the following requirements: 
- The robot starts at the top left corner of the space, with its x and y coordinates both being 0.
- The robot must eventually reach each of the three targets, meaning it must infinitely often visit the exact locations of target1, target2, and target3.
- After visiting the targets, the robot must eventually return to the top left corner of the space, with its x and y coordinates both being 0. 

The space in which the robot moves is defined by a 4x4 grid, with the x coordinate ranging from 0 to 4 and the y coordinate also ranging from 0 to 4.
