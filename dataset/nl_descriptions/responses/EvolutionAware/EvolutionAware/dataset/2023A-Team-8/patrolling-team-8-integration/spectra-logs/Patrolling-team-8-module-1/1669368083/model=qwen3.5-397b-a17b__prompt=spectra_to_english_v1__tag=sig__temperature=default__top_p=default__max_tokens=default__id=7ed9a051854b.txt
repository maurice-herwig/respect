

Specification name: PatrollingTeam8Module1

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- fixedObstacle1: Dim[2]
- fixedObstacle2: Dim[2]
- fixedObstacle3: Dim[2]
- fixedObstacle4: Dim[2]
- fixedObstacle5: Dim[2]
- movingObstacle: Dim[2]

System variables:
- none

This specification models the behavior of obstacles within a patrolling environment. All variables are controlled by the environment, representing obstacle positions on a grid where coordinates range from 0 to 4. There are no system-controlled outputs defined in this module.

**Environment Assumptions**
The environment must adhere to the following constraints regarding obstacle positions and movements:

*   **Fixed Obstacles:** Five fixed obstacles remain stationary at specific coordinates throughout the system operation:
    *   `fixedObstacle1` remains at coordinates (1, 1).
    *   `fixedObstacle2` remains at coordinates (2, 1).
    *   `fixedObstacle3` remains at coordinates (0, 3).
    *   `fixedObstacle4` remains at coordinates (3, 3).
    *   `fixedObstacle5` remains at coordinates (3, 4).
*   **Moving Obstacle Initial State:** The `movingObstacle` begins at coordinates (0, 2).
*   **Moving Obstacle Movement:** The X-coordinate (`movingObstacle[0]`) changes by at most one unit per step. Specifically, in any transition, the next value of `movingObstacle[0]` must be equal to its current value, one greater than its current value, or one less than its current value.
*   **Moving Obstacle Liveness:** The X-coordinate (`movingObstacle[0]`) must reach the value 0 infinitely often and must reach the value 4 infinitely often.

**System Guarantees**
There are no system guarantees specified in this module.
