The system is a robot that moves towards targets in a 2D space. The environment controls the positions of three targets, denoted as target1, target2, and target3, each with x and y coordinates ranging from 0 to 4. The system controls the robot's position, represented by its x and y coordinates, robotX and robotY, also within the same range.

The environment is assumed to keep the targets stationary, meaning their positions do not change over time. 

The system guarantees that the robot starts at the top left corner, with its initial position being (0,0). Additionally, the system ensures that the robot will eventually reach each of the targets. In other words, the robot will infinitely often be at the same position as target1, target2, and target3. 

Note that the robot's movement is constrained within a 4x4 grid, with the x-coordinate ranging from 0 to 4 (DX) and the y-coordinate also ranging from 0 to 4 (DY).
