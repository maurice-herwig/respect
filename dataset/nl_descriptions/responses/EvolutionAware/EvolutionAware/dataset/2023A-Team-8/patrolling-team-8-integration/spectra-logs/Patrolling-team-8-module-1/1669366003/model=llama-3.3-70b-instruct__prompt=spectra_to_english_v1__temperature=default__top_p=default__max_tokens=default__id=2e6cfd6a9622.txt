The system interacts with an environment that controls the positions of several obstacles. There are five fixed obstacles, each located at a specific position in a 2D space with dimensions 0 to 4, and one moving obstacle. The environment sets the positions of these obstacles.

The fixed obstacles do not change their positions over time. Specifically, the first fixed obstacle is always at position (1,1), the second at (2,1), the third at (0,3), the fourth at (3,3), and the fifth at (3,4).

The moving obstacle starts at position (0,2) and can move horizontally (along the X-axis) to an adjacent position or stay at its current position. The moving obstacle's vertical position (Y-axis) remains constant.

Note that the system does not control any outputs in this specification, as it only describes the environment's behavior and constraints on the obstacles. The specification focuses on defining the allowed behavior of the environment regarding the obstacles' movements. 

There are no system-controlled outputs or guarantees specified in this module, only assumptions about the environment's behavior. The initial condition of the moving obstacle and the safety conditions restricting the movements of the obstacles are defined. However, there is a commented-out assumption that suggests the moving obstacle should not stay at the same position forever, but this is not currently part of the specification.
