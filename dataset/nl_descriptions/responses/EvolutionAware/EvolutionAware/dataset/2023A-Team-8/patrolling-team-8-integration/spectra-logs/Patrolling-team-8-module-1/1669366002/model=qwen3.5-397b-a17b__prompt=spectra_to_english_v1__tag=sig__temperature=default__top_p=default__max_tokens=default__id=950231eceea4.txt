System variables:

**Behavioral Requirements**

This specification defines the behavior of obstacles within a grid environment where coordinate values are integers ranging from 0 to 4. The environment controls the positions of all obstacles.

Five fixed obstacles maintain constant positions throughout operation. Specifically, `fixedObstacle1` remains at coordinates (1, 1), `fixedObstacle2` at (2, 1), `fixedObstacle3` at (0, 3), `fixedObstacle4` at (3, 3), and `fixedObstacle5` at (3, 4).

The `movingObstacle` initializes at coordinates (0, 2). During operation, the first coordinate of the `movingObstacle` must change by no more than one unit at each step; it may increase by one, decrease by one, or remain unchanged.
