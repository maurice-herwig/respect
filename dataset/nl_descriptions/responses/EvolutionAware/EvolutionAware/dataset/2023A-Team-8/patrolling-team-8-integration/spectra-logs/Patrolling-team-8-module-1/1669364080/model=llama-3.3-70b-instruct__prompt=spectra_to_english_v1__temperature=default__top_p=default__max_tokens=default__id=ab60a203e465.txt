The system interacts with an environment that controls the positions of various obstacles. The environment-controlled inputs are the positions of five fixed obstacles (fixedObstacle1 to fixedObstacle5) and one moving obstacle (movingObstacle), each represented by two dimensions (x and y coordinates) within a range of 0 to 4 (defined by D).

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- The fixed obstacles do not change their positions over time, with fixedObstacle1 always at (1,1), fixedObstacle2 at (2,1), fixedObstacle3 at (0,3), fixedObstacle4 at (3,3), and fixedObstacle5 at (3,4).
- The moving obstacle starts at the position (0,2) initially.
- The moving obstacle changes its x-coordinate over time, either incrementing it by 1, decrementing it by 1, or keeping it the same.

The system's guarantees are not explicitly defined in this specification, but the initial conditions and assumptions on the environment's behavior are established. The initial condition specifies the starting position of the moving obstacle. The safety requirement for the environment is that the fixed obstacles remain stationary and the moving obstacle's movement follows the defined rules. There are no liveness requirements or next-state/update rules specified for the system's behavior in this module.
