The system is a robot that operates within a 7x7 grid, where its position is determined by the coordinates (robotX, robotY). The environment controls two obstacles, one located at (obstacleUpX, 0) and the other at (0, obstacleLeftY). 

Initially, both obstacles are placed at the origin (0, 0). The environment is restricted in its control of the obstacles: they must always remain within the grid boundaries, i.e., their coordinates must be less than 7.

The environment can move the obstacles according to certain rules. When a counter, canMove, is 0, the obstacles do not move. When canMove is greater than 0, the obstacles move, with their new positions being either one unit up, down, or staying the same.

The system must ensure that the robot never occupies certain positions: (2, 2), (2, 3), (2, 4), and (2, 5). 

The robot's movement is also governed by rules. When the canMove counter is 0, the robot's position does not change. When canMove is greater than 0, the robot moves, with its new x and y coordinates being either one unit up, down, or staying the same.

The canMove counter starts at 0 and can be incremented, with an overflow behavior that resets it to 0 when it reaches its maximum value of 2.
