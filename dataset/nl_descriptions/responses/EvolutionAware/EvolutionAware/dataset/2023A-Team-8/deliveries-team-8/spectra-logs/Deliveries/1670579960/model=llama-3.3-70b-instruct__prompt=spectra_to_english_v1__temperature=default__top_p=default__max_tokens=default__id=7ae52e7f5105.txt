The system, Deliveries, involves a robot's movement within a defined grid. The environment and system interact, with the system controlling the robot's position. The grid has dimensions defined by DX (5 units) and DY (5 units), establishing the boundaries for the robot's movement.

The system-controlled outputs are the robot's coordinates: robotX0 and robotY0, representing the initial position, and robotX1 and robotY1, representing subsequent positions. 

At the start, the robot's position is defined by the system-controlled variables robotX0 and robotY0, which are within the grid's dimensions (0 to DX for the x-coordinate and 0 to DY for the y-coordinate). Similarly, robotX1 and robotY1 are also within these dimensions, indicating the system's control over the robot's movement within the specified grid.

No specific assumptions about the environment's behavior or guarantees required from the system regarding the robot's movement or interactions are defined in this part of the specification. The initial conditions for the robot's position are established within the defined grid dimensions.
