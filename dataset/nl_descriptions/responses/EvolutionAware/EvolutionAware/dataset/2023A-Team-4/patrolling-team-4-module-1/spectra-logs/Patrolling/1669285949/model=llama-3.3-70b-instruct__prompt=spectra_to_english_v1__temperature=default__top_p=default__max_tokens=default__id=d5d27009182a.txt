The Patrolling system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the positions of three targets, A, B, and C, with coordinates (targetAX, targetAY), (targetBX, targetBY), and (targetCX, targetCY), respectively. The system controls the position of a robot, with coordinates (robotX, robotY).

The environment is assumed to keep the positions of targets A, B, and C constant over time. 

The system must satisfy the following requirements: 
- Initially, the robot must be at the left corner, with coordinates (0, 0).
- The robot must infinitely often visit each of the targets A, B, and C.
- At all times, the robot must move, either staying at its current position or changing its x and y coordinates by either -1, 0, or 1.

In summary, the Patrolling system is required to have its robot continuously move and periodically visit targets A, B, and C, while the environment keeps the targets' positions constant.
