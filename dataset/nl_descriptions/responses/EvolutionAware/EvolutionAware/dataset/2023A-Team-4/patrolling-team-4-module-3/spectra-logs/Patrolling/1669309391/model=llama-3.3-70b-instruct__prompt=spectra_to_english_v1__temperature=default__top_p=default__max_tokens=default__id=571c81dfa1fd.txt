The Patrolling system controls a robot's position, defined by its x and y coordinates (robotX and robotY), within a grid of size 4x4. The environment does not directly control any inputs in this specification. 

The system is assumed to behave correctly when it is not at the charging place for 10 consecutive states, allowing it to move freely. 

The system guarantees that whenever it reaches the charging place, it will remain there for at least 5 states before potentially moving away. Additionally, if the robot is not at the charging place, it will return to the charging place within 10 states. 

The charging place is defined as the location where robotX equals 4 and robotY equals 0. 

Initially, there are no specified constraints on the robot's position. The system's safety and liveness requirements are defined by its ability to return to the charging place and stay there for a specified duration. The next-state relationships are implicitly defined by the system's movement towards or away from the charging place, as dictated by the guarantees.
