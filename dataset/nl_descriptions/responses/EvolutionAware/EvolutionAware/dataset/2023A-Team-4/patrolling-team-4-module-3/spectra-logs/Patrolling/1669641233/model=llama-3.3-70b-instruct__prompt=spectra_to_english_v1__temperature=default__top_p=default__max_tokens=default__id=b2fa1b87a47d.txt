The Patrolling system controls a robot's movement within a grid of size 4x4. The robot's position is determined by its x-coordinate (robotX) and y-coordinate (robotY), both of which are controlled by the system. 

The environment is assumed to allow the robot to function correctly for at least 15 time steps when it is not at the charging place. 

The system guarantees that if the robot reaches the charging place, it will remain there for at least 3 time steps. Additionally, whenever the robot is not at the charging place for 15 consecutive time steps, the system ensures that it will eventually reach the charging place. 

The charging place is defined as the location where the robot's x-coordinate is 4 and its y-coordinate is 0. 

Initially, there are no specific constraints on the robot's position. The system's behavior is solely defined by its ability to maintain the robot at the charging place for a certain duration and to guide it back to the charging place after a specified period of absence.
