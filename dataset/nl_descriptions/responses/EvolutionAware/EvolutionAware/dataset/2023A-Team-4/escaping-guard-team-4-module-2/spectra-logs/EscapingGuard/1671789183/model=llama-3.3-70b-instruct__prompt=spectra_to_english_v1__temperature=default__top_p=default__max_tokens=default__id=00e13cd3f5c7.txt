The system consists of a robot and a moving obstacle within a grid of size 7x7. The robot's position is defined by its top-left corner, represented by the system-controlled variables `robotX` and `robotY`. The obstacle's position is defined by its top-left corner, represented by the environment-controlled variables `obsXL` and `obsYT`.

The environment is assumed to control the obstacle's movement in such a way that it always moves, either staying in the same position or moving one step in any direction. Additionally, the obstacle is assumed to respond to the robot's position by moving towards it. Specifically, if the robot is to the left of the obstacle, the obstacle will move to the left or stay in the same position, and if the robot is to the right of the obstacle, the obstacle will move to the right or stay in the same position. The same rules apply to the vertical movement of the obstacle.

The system guarantees that the robot will always move, either staying in the same position or moving one step in any direction, both horizontally and vertically. 

The initial positions of the robot and the obstacle are not specified, and there are no explicit safety or liveness requirements beyond the movement rules. The system and environment behave according to these movement rules from the start, without any specific initial conditions.
