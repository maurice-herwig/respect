The system consists of a robot and a moving obstacle within a 7x7 grid. The robot's position is defined by its top-left corner, represented by the system-controlled variables `robotX` and `robotY`. The obstacle's position is defined by its left and top edges, represented by the environment-controlled variables `obsXL` and `obsYT`.

The environment is assumed to always move the obstacle by either keeping its position the same or changing it by one unit in either direction, horizontally or vertically. Additionally, the obstacle is assumed to respond to the robot's position by moving towards it, meaning that if the robot is to the left of the obstacle, the obstacle will not move to the right, and if the robot is to the right of the obstacle, the obstacle will not move to the left. The same applies to the vertical direction.

The system guarantees that the robot will always move by either keeping its position the same or changing it by one unit in either direction, horizontally or vertically.

There is also a counter that increments under certain conditions. The environment is assumed to move the obstacle slower when the counter is less than 2, meaning that the obstacle will not change its position when this condition is met.

The initial position of the counter is 0, and it will increment when it is less than 2. When the counter reaches 2, it will overflow, wrapping around to its initial value. 

Note that `obsXL` and `obsYT` are environment-controlled inputs, while `robotX` and `robotY` are system-controlled outputs.
