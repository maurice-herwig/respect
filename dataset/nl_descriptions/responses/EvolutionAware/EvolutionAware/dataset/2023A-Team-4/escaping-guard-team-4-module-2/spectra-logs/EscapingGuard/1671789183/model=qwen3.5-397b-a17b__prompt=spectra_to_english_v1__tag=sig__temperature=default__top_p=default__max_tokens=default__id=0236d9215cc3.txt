

Specification name: EscapingGuard

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- obsXL: Dim
- obsYT: Dim

System variables:
- robotX: Dim
- robotY: Dim

The specification defines the interaction between a robot controlled by the system and a moving obstacle controlled by the environment. Both entities operate on a grid where coordinates are integers ranging from 0 to 7.

The system guarantees that the robot's position updates smoothly. At every step, the robot's horizontal coordinate (`robotX`) and vertical coordinate (`robotY`) must either remain unchanged, increase by one, or decrease by one.

The environment is assumed to adhere to similar movement constraints for the obstacle. The obstacle's horizontal (`obsXL`) and vertical (`obsYT`) coordinates must also change by at most one unit per step. Additionally, the environment is assumed to move the obstacle towards the robot's current position. Specifically, if the robot's horizontal coordinate is less than the obstacle's, the obstacle's next horizontal coordinate must not exceed its current value. If the robot's horizontal coordinate is greater than the obstacle's, the obstacle's next horizontal coordinate must not be less than its current value. The same logic applies to the vertical coordinates: the obstacle must adjust its next vertical position towards the robot's current vertical position or remain stationary in that axis.
