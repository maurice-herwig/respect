

Specification name: CatchingIntruder

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- none

System variables:
- robotX0: Dim
- robotY0: Dim
- robotX1: Dim
- robotY1: Dim

**Requirements Description**

The system controls the positions of two robots, Robot 0 and Robot 1, on a two-dimensional grid. The coordinates for both robots are defined by the domain **Dim**, which consists of integers from 0 to 7. The system variables **robotX0** and **robotY0** represent the X and Y coordinates of Robot 0, while **robotX1** and **robotY1** represent the X and Y coordinates of Robot 1. There are no environment-controlled inputs; the system determines all robot movements.

**Initial Conditions**
Upon initialization, both Robot 0 and Robot 1 must be located at position (0, 0). Specifically, **robotX0** and **robotY0** are initialized to 0, and **robotX1** and **robotY1** are initialized to 0.

**Movement Safety**
At every step of the system operation, each robot must either remain in its current cell or move to an immediately adjacent cell horizontally or vertically. Diagonal moves are not permitted. For each robot, the next position must satisfy one of the following conditions relative to its current position:
- The X-coordinate increases by 1 while the Y-coordinate remains unchanged.
- The X-coordinate decreases by 1 while the Y-coordinate remains unchanged.
- The Y-coordinate increases by 1 while the X-coordinate remains unchanged.
- The Y-coordinate decreases by 1 while the X-coordinate remains unchanged.
- Both coordinates remain unchanged.

**Obstacle Avoidance**
Throughout operation, neither robot is permitted to occupy specific static obstacle cells. Specifically, neither Robot 0 nor Robot 1 may ever occupy a cell where the Y-coordinate is 1 and the X-coordinate is 2, 3, 4, or 5.
