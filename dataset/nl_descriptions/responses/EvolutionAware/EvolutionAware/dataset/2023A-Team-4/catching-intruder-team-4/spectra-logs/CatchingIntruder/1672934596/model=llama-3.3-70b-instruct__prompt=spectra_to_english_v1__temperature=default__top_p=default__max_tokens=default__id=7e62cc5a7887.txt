The system consists of two robots, R0 and R1, operating in a 7x7 grid environment. The robots' positions are controlled by the system, with R0's position defined by coordinates (robotX0, robotY0) and R1's position defined by coordinates (robotX1, robotY1).

Initially, R0 is located at the top-left corner (0, 0) and R1 is located at the top-right corner (7, 0) of the grid.

The robots can move horizontally or vertically to an adjacent empty cell, but they cannot move diagonally. At each step, a robot can either stay in place or move to a neighboring cell. This movement is constrained by the rule that the robot's new position is either one cell to the left, right, above, or below its current position, or the same as its current position.

The system must ensure that the robots avoid certain static obstacles in the environment. Specifically, if a robot is in the second row (row 1), it must not be in columns 2, 3, 4, or 5.

The environment does not have any explicitly defined inputs or constraints in this specification. The system's behavior is defined solely in terms of the robots' movements and the avoidance of static obstacles. 

At all times, the system must satisfy the movement and obstacle-avoidance rules for both robots. The system has complete control over the robots' positions and must ensure that they move according to the specified rules and avoid the designated obstacles.
