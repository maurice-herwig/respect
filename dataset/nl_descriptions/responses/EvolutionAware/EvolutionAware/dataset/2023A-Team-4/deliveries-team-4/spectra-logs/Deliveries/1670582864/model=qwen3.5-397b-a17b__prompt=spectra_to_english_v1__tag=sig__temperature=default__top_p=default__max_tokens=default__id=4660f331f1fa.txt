

Specification name: Deliveries

Named domains:
- DimX: Int(0..DX)
- DimY: Int(0..DY)
- Coord: Int(0..DY)
- DimLambX: Int(1..DX)
- DimLambY: Int(1..DX)

Environment variables:
- lambX0: DimLambX
- lambY0: DimLambY
- lambX1: DimLambX
- lambY1: DimLambY
- elevatorY: DimY

System variables:
- robotX0: DimX
- robotY0: DimY
- robotX1: DimX
- robotY1: DimY

The system manages the interaction between two robots and two packages, referred to as lambs, within a grid environment containing an elevator fixed at horizontal coordinate 0. The environment controls the positions of the two lambs and the vertical position of the elevator. The system controls the horizontal and vertical positions of both robots.

The environment must adhere to the following constraints regarding lamb movement:

1.  **Lamb 0 Movement:** Lamb 0 remains stationary at its current coordinates unless Robot 0 is located at the exact same coordinates as Lamb 0. Specifically, if Robot 0 is not at Lamb 0's location, the coordinates of Lamb 0 must not change in the next state.
2.  **Lamb 1 Movement:** Lamb 1 remains stationary at its current coordinates unless Robot 1 is located at the exact same coordinates as Lamb 1. Specifically, if Robot 1 is not at Lamb 1's location, the coordinates of Lamb 1 must not change in the next state.
