

Specification name: CatchingIntruder

Named domains:
- Dim: Int(0..D)

Environment variables:
- intruderX: Dim
- intruderY: Dim

System variables:
- robotX0: Dim
- robotY0: Dim
- robotX1: Dim
- robotY1: Dim

This specification describes a reactive system that controls the positions of two robots on a two-dimensional grid while interacting with an intruder. The grid coordinates are defined by the domain **Dim**, which consists of integers from 0 to 7.

**Initial Conditions**
At the beginning of operation, the system must ensure the following initial positions:
*   The first robot (**robotX0**, **robotY0**) starts at coordinates (0, 0).
*   The second robot (**robotX1**, **robotY1**) starts at coordinates (7, 0).

The environment is assumed to initialize the intruder such that its Y-coordinate (**intruderY**) starts at 7.

**Movement Constraints**
The system guarantees that both robots adhere to strict movement rules at every step of the execution:
*   A robot may change its X-coordinate by at most 1 (increment, decrement, or stay the same) while keeping its Y-coordinate constant.
*   Alternatively, a robot may change its Y-coordinate by at most 1 while keeping its X-coordinate constant.
*   Alternatively, a robot may remain in its current position with both coordinates unchanged.
*   Diagonal movement is prohibited; only one coordinate may change per step, or neither.

The system operates under the assumption that the environment controls the intruder's position (**intruderX**, **intruderY**) and adheres to the same movement constraints. Specifically, the environment is assumed to move the intruder orthogonally to adjacent cells or keep it stationary at each step, never moving diagonally or jumping more than one unit distance.
