The system consists of two robots and an intruder moving within a 7x7 grid. The robots' positions are controlled by the system, with initial positions at (0,0) and (7,0). The intruder's position is controlled by the environment, with an initial position on the bottom row (y-coordinate 7).

The environment is assumed to move the intruder in a way that is either horizontally or vertically adjacent to its current position, or not at all. This means the intruder can move one cell to the left, right, up, or down, or remain stationary.

The system guarantees that the robots will move in a similar manner, with each robot able to move one cell to the left, right, up, or down, or remain stationary. The robots can only move horizontally or vertically, not diagonally. At any given time, each robot can either move horizontally while keeping its vertical position constant, move vertically while keeping its horizontal position constant, or remain stationary in both directions.

The initial positions of the robots and the intruder are set, with the robots starting at (0,0) and (7,0), and the intruder starting at a position on the bottom row. The system and environment behaviors are constrained to ensure that the robots and intruder move in a predictable and adjacent manner, without any diagonal movement.
