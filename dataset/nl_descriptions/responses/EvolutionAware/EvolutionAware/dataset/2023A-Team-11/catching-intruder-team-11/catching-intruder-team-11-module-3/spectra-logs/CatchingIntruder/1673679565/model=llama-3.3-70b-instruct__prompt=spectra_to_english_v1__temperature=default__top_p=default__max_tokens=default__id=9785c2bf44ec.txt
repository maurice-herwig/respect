The system, called CatchingIntruder, interacts with its environment in a 7x7 grid, where the system controls the positions of two robots (robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, robotY1) and the environment controls the position of an intruder (intruderX, intruderY). There are fixed obstacles at positions (2,1), (3,1), (4,1), and (5,1). The environment also controls a counter, StaysCounter.

Initially, the StaysCounter is set to 0. The environment is assumed to increment the StaysCounter at each step, unless it resets the counter to 0. The StaysCounter will be at least 4 infinitely often. Whenever the StaysCounter reaches 4, the environment will reset the counter to 0 and move the intruder to position (7) on the y-axis at the next step.

The system's behavior is constrained by these environmental assumptions, but its specific guarantees are not defined in this specification. The system's outputs are the positions of the two robots, while the environment's inputs are the position of the intruder and the value of the StaysCounter.
