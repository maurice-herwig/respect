The system consists of two robots and an intruder moving within a 7x7 grid. The environment controls the intruder's position, specified by `intruderX` and `intruderY`, while the system controls the positions of the two robots, specified by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`.

Initially, the first robot is positioned at (0, 0), the second robot is positioned at (7, 0), and the intruder is positioned at an unknown x-coordinate on the bottom row (y-coordinate 7).

The system guarantees that the robots move horizontally or vertically to an adjacent cell, but not diagonally. At each step, a robot can either move one cell to the left or right (while staying in the same row), move one cell up or down (while staying in the same column), or stay in the same position.

The environment is assumed to move the intruder in a similar manner, with the intruder moving horizontally or vertically to an adjacent cell, but not diagonally.

The system must ensure that the two robots never occupy the same position at the same time. 

Note that the intruder's movement is constrained by the environment, and the system does not have control over it. The system's primary goal is to control the movement of the two robots while avoiding collisions between them.
