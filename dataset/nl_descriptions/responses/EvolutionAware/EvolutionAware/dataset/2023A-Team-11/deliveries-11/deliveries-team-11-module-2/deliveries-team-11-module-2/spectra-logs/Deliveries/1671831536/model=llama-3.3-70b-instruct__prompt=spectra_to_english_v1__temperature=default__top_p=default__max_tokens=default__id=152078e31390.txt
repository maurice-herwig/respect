The system, called Deliveries, interacts with an environment to manage the movement of two robots within a defined area. The environment controls the positions of two packages, p0X, p0Y, p1X, and p1Y, while the system controls the positions of two robots, robotX0, robotY0, robotX1, and robotY1.

The environment is assumed to behave in a specific way: when a robot is not at a package and the corresponding counter (r0Count or r1Count) is greater than or equal to 3, the package's position remains the same. However, when a robot is at a package and the corresponding counter is less than 3, the package's vertical position (p0Y or p1Y) changes to a value that is neither its current position nor the maximum value (5).

The system guarantees that it will manage the movement of the robots based on the positions of the packages and the values of the counters. The counters, r0Count and r1Count, are incremented when the corresponding robot is at the package and reset when the robot reaches the corner (position 5, 5). The counters range from 0 to 3 and do not overflow.

Initially, the counters are set to 0. The system's behavior is defined by the interactions between the robots and the packages, with the counters playing a crucial role in determining the allowed movements. The robots' positions are updated based on the environment's inputs and the system's internal state, as defined by the counters and the package positions.
