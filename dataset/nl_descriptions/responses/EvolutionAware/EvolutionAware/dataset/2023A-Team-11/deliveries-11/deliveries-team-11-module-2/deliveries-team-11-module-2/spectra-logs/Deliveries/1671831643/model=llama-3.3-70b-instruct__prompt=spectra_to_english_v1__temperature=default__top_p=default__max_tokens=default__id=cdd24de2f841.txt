The system, called Deliveries, interacts with its environment to manage the movement of two robots within a defined grid. The environment controls the positions of packages, denoted by `p0X`, `p0Y`, `p1X`, and `p1Y`, while the system controls the positions of the robots, denoted by `robotX0`, `robotY0`, `robotX1`, and `robotY1`. 

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- When a robot is not at a package or has been at a package at least three times, the package's position remains unchanged in both the x and y dimensions.
- When a robot is at a package but has been there fewer than three times, the package's y-position changes in the next step, but not to its current position or to the corner position (5).
- The packages' y-positions are never at the corner position (5).
- The packages' x-positions are never at the leftmost position (0).

The system guarantees that each robot will be at its respective package infinitely often. 

Two counters, `r0Count` and `r1Count`, track how many times each robot has been at its package. These counters are incremented when a robot is at its package and reset when the robot reaches the corner position (5, 5). The counters do not overflow.

Initially, the counters are set to 0. 

The system's behavior ensures that the robots will periodically be at the packages, as required, under the given environmental assumptions.
