The system, called Deliveries, interacts with an environment to manage the movement of two robots, robot0 and robot1, within a 5x5 grid. The environment controls the positions of packages p0 and p1, while the system controls the positions of the robots.

The environment is assumed to behave in the following ways: 
- When a robot is not at a package or has been at the package at least three times, the package's position remains the same.
- When a robot is at a package but has been there fewer than three times, the package's vertical position changes, but not to the top or bottom of the grid.
- The packages are never at the top of the grid.
- The packages are never at the left edge of the grid.

The system guarantees that each robot will eventually be at its corresponding package infinitely often.

The system maintains two counters, r0Count and r1Count, to track how many times each robot has been at its package. These counters start at 0, increment when a robot is at its package, reset when a robot is at the corner of the grid, and do not overflow.

Initially, the robots and packages are positioned within the grid, with the robots' positions controlled by the system and the packages' positions controlled by the environment. The system ensures that the robots move to be at their packages, while the environment controls the packages' positions according to the specified assumptions.
