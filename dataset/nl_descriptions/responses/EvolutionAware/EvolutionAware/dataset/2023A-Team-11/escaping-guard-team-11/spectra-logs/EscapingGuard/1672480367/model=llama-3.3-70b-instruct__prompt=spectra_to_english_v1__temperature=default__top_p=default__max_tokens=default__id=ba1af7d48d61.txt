The system, called EscapingGuard, describes the interaction between a robot and its environment, including a guard and fixed obstacles and targets. The environment controls the guard's position, specified by its left corner coordinates, guardLeftCornerX and guardLeftCornerY. The system controls the robot's position, specified by its coordinates, robotX and robotY.

Initially, the robot is positioned at (7, 0), and the guard is positioned at (0, 0). The environment is assumed to start with the guard at this initial position.

The system guarantees that the robot moves according to certain rules: at each step, the robot's x and y coordinates either increase by 1, decrease by 1, or remain the same. 

The system must ensure that the robot infinitely often visits three targets located at (0, 0), (0, 7), and (7, 7). This means that in every run, the robot must repeatedly visit each of these targets.

Note that the specification does not describe how the robot avoids obstacles or interacts with the guard beyond the robot's movement and target visitation requirements. The fixed obstacles are defined at positions (2, 2), (2, 3), (2, 4), and (2, 5), but their effect on the robot's behavior is not specified.
