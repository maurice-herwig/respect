The system is a robot that moves within a 7x7 grid, with its position defined by the system-controlled variables `robotX` and `robotY`. The environment controls the position of a guard, defined by the variables `guardLeftCornerX` and `guardLeftCornerY`. The grid also contains fixed obstacles and targets, with their locations predefined.

Initially, the robot is positioned at (7, 0) and the guard is at the left corner (0, 0). The system guarantees that the robot will start at this position.

The environment is assumed to start with the guard at the left corner (0, 0). 

The system must ensure that the robot visits two targets, located at (0, 0) and (0, 7), infinitely often. 

The robot's movement is restricted to changing its position by either moving one step in any direction (horizontally or vertically) or staying in the same position. The robot's vertical position can also remain unchanged. 

Note that the specification does not define the behavior for the third target, located at (7, 7), beyond the initial definition of the `atT3` predicate.
