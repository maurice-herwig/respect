### **Requirements for the Autonomous Vehicle System**

This specification describes the behavior of an autonomous vehicle that responds to its environment, consisting of pedestrians, crosswalks, and traffic signals.

#### **Environment-Controlled Inputs**
The environment provides the following inputs:
- `pedestrian` – Indicates whether a pedestrian is present.
- `crosswalk` – Indicates whether the vehicle is near a crosswalk.
- `greenLight` – Indicates whether the traffic light is green.

#### **System-Controlled Outputs**
The system controls:
- `speed` – The vehicle’s speed, ranging from 0 to 10 (inclusive).

#### **Assumptions on the Environment**
The environment must ensure that **infinitely often**, the traffic light is green (`greenLight`) and no pedestrian is present.

#### **System Guarantees**
The system must always satisfy the following requirements:

1. **Pedestrian and Traffic Light Safety**
   If either a pedestrian is present **or** the traffic light is not green, the vehicle **must not be driving** (i.e., its speed must be 0).

2. **Crosswalk Speed Limit**
   If the vehicle is near a crosswalk, it **must slow down** (i.e., its speed must be less than 5).

3. **Progress Under Favorable Conditions**
   The vehicle must **infinitely often drive** (i.e., have a speed greater than 0) when the traffic light is green and no pedestrian is present.
