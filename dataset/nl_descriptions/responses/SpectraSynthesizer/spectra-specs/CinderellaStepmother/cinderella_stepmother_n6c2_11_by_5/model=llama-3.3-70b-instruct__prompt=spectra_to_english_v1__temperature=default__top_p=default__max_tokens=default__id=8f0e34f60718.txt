The system, known as Cinderella, interacts with its environment through a set of variables. The environment controls the amount of water added to each of six water fills, represented by the variables `waterFills[0]` through `waterFills[5]`, with each fill having a capacity ranging from 0 to `ADDED_WATER_UNITS` (5 units). The system, on the other hand, controls the amount of water in six buckets, represented by the variables `buckets[0]` through `buckets[5]`, with each bucket having a capacity ranging from 0 to `B` (11 units), and a variable `bucketToEmpty` that indicates which bucket to empty, with a value ranging from 0 to `N-1` (5).

The environment is assumed to always add a total amount of water equal to `ADDED_WATER_UNITS` (5 units) across all six water fills at any given time.

The system guarantees that initially, all buckets are empty. Additionally, the system ensures that the amount of water in each bucket is updated based on the `bucketToEmpty` variable. If `bucketToEmpty` falls within a certain range relative to the current bucket index `i` (specifically, if `bucketToEmpty` is less than or equal to `i` and greater than `i-C`, where `C` is 2, or if `bucketToEmpty` is greater than or equal to `N-C+1+i`), then the amount of water in the bucket at index `i` in the next state is the same as the amount of water added to the corresponding water fill in the next state. Otherwise, the amount of water in the bucket at index `i` in the next state is the current amount of water in the bucket plus the amount of water added to the corresponding water fill in the next state.
