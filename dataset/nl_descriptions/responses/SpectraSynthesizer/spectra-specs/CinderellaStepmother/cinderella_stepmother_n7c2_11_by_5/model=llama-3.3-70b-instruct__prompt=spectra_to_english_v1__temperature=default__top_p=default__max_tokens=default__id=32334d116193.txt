The system, known as Cinderella, interacts with its environment in the following manner. The environment controls the amount of water added to each of seven water fills, represented by the variables waterFills, with values ranging from 0 to ADDED_WATER_UNITS (5 units). The system, on the other hand, controls the amount of water in seven buckets and the index of the bucket to empty, represented by the variables buckets and bucketToEmpty, respectively.

The environment is assumed to always add a total of ADDED_WATER_UNITS (5 units) of water across all seven water fills.

The system guarantees that initially, all buckets are empty. Furthermore, the system ensures that the amount of water in each bucket is updated based on the bucketToEmpty index and the amount of water added to each water fill. Specifically, if the bucketToEmpty index falls within a certain range relative to the current bucket index (i.e., between i-C and i, or greater than or equal to N-C+1+i), the amount of water in the bucket will be equal to the amount of water added to the corresponding water fill in the next step. Otherwise, the amount of water in the bucket will increase by the amount of water added to the corresponding water fill in the next step.

In summary, the environment controls the water added to each fill, and the system controls the water in each bucket and the index of the bucket to empty, ensuring that the buckets are updated according to specific rules based on the bucketToEmpty index and the amount of water added.
