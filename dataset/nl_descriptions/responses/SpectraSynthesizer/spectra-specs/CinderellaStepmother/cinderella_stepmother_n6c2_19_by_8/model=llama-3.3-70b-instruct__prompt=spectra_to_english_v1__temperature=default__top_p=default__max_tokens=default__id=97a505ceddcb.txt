The system, known as Cinderella, interacts with its environment through a set of water fills and buckets. The environment controls the amount of water in each of six water fills, denoted as waterFills, with values ranging from 0 to ADDED_WATER_UNITS (8 units). The system, on the other hand, controls the amount of water in six corresponding buckets and a variable, bucketToEmpty, which determines the order in which buckets are emptied.

The environment is assumed to always maintain a total amount of water across all six water fills equal to ADDED_WATER_UNITS (8 units).

Initially, all buckets are empty, with each containing 0 units of water. 

The system guarantees that the amount of water in each bucket will be updated based on the value of bucketToEmpty and the amount of water in the corresponding water fill. If the bucketToEmpty variable indicates that the current bucket should be emptied (i.e., its index is within a certain range relative to bucketToEmpty), the bucket's next state will be the same as the next state of the corresponding water fill. Otherwise, the bucket's next state will be its current state plus the next state of the corresponding water fill. 

In essence, the system ensures that water is distributed from the water fills to the buckets according to the specified rules, while the environment maintains a constant total amount of water available.
