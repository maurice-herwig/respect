The Cinderella system is a reactive system that interacts with its environment. The environment controls the amount of water added to each of five water fills, represented by the variables `waterFills[0]` through `waterFills[4]`, with each value ranging from 0 to `ADDED_WATER_UNITS` (5 units). The system controls five buckets, represented by the variables `buckets[0]` through `buckets[4]`, with each value ranging from 0 to `B` (9 units), and a variable `bucketToEmpty` that ranges from 0 to `N-1` (4).

The environment is assumed to always maintain a total amount of water added across all five water fills equal to `ADDED_WATER_UNITS` (5 units).

The system guarantees that initially, all buckets are empty, with each `buckets[i]` equal to 0.

The system also guarantees that at each step, the amount of water in each bucket `buckets[i]` is updated based on the value of `bucketToEmpty` and the amount of water added to the corresponding water fill `waterFills[i]`. If `bucketToEmpty` is within a certain range relative to `i` (specifically, `bucketToEmpty` is less than or equal to `i` and greater than `i-C`, or `bucketToEmpty` is greater than or equal to `N-C+1+i`), then the amount of water in `buckets[i]` at the next step is equal to the amount of water added to `waterFills[i]` at the next step. Otherwise, the amount of water in `buckets[i]` at the next step is equal to the current amount of water in `buckets[i]` plus the amount of water added to `waterFills[i]` at the next step.
