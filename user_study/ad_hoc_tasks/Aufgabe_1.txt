A library provides a quiet zone in which electronic devices such as laptops or
phones must not play sound through their speakers. Each device can use GPS
to detect whether it is currently inside the quiet zone. The controller determines
which audio output the device should use.
Environment-Variables:
• inQuietZone: true if the device is currently inside the library quiet zone.
• headphonesConnected: true if headphones are currently connected to the
device.
• musicPlaying: true if the device is currently playing music.
System-Variables:
• audioOutput: current audio output of the device. The possible values are
SPEAKER, HEADPHONES, and MUTED.
The following rules must hold:
• If no music is playing, the device must be muted.
• If the device is inside the quiet zone, music must never be played through
the speaker.
• If the device is inside the quiet zone, music is playing, and headphones are
connected, the music must be played through the headphones.
• If the device is inside the quiet zone, music is playing, and no headphones
are connected, the device must be muted.
• If the device is outside the quiet zone and music is playing, the music must
